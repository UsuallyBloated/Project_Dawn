## 5. Loot tables will roll cascading rarity tiers (decision, no code)

(transcribed locally in a scratch environment, captions refused with HTTP 429). Its closing

plumbing"). One open rule, for the build: what a tier that hits with nothing left in it does.

(draws without replacement, refilled when empty, so streaks are capped). **Rejected by the user
for every roll, procs included:** a bag has memory, so players can count draws and know when the
next crit, proc or rare is due, and a bag that is not persisted resets on relog. Rolls stay
independent and server-side.
