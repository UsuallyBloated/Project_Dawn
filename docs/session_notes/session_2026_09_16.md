## Server (F:\Projects\server, four commits)

- **Replacement `zone_camps.toml`**: 21 camps, 54 spawn points, 16 mob names.
  Every quest target spawns where its giver's dialogue says (Grey Wolves south of
  the east road, Plague Rats behind Brom's, level 3 Gnoll Raiders east, Rotfang's
  den south), the even-con ladder is continuous through 7 (new Boar/Bat/Spider
  camps at 4/4/7), all five named mobs placed as single-spawn dens with long
  respawns, and two renames so loot tables match by substring (Plagued Ghoul to
  Plagued Zombie, Undead Champion to Skeleton Champion). Ring 1 keeps clear of
  the test harness's walking corridors (comment in the file).
- **Five quests in `quests.toml`**: restless_bones (L2), road_toll (L5),
  silk_harvest (L7), champions_crypt (L9), the_undying (L12) — existing items as
  rewards, Flamebrand as the finale unique, `turn_in_npc` set on all five.
- **`npcs.toml`**: Elara's missing row + Hadrik the Blacksmith at [-5, 0, 5].
- **The integration suite's first-ever fully green runs (44/44, three times).**
  The layout change broke seven tests and the triage found the real mechanisms
  behind the months-old "flaky trio": (a) all eight camp-walking tests shared one
  hardcoded walk to the old camp 0; they now aim at the Bonepile's isolated
  single-puller. (b) Casting while a mob swings at you is a ~70% interrupt roll
  per hit at channeling 0 — the AOE test now dev-spawns its victim AFTER the cast
  bar runs (new `send_dev_spawn` + the `is_gm` grant flow), and the two
  unreordered pet tests got pet_command's summon-first reorder. (c) A bare
  multi-second tokio sleep leaves the client socket unserviced while 54 idle
  enemies fan 20 Hz positions, dropping datagrams faster than renet's 150 ms
  resend can land — a server-provably-sent PetSpawn missed a 3 s wait. All eleven
  cast-bar sleeps now pump the transport (`pump_for`), `wait_for` reports a
  mid-wait disconnect, and the harness gained opt-in server tracing (RUST_LOG).
- **A real boot-time spawn bug, found by luck**: `spawn_points` backdated
  `now - respawn_secs`, which underflows the Instant epoch on a freshly booted
  machine; the fallback silently delayed a camp's FIRST spawn by its full timer —
  a 600 s named den would take ten minutes to appear after every R720 start
  (systemd runs seconds after boot). Caught because the desk machine was itself
  freshly booted and exactly the two 600 s dens failed to spawn. The spawner now
  stores the due time directly.
