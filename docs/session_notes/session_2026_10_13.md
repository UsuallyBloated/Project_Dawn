## 1. Food and water: the six decisions, and one correction

The user answered `docs/design/food_and_water.md` §7 in one sitting: EQ's slower pace, EQ's
regen split (food for HP, water for mana, either for stamina), per-race drain, EQ's
hand-eating rules exactly, and the bars in their own small window (two icons beside a
progress bar), to be planned as a note before the client half is built.

On decision 5 the user challenged the draft ("No one was summoning stacks of food or water
at level 1 in EQ. Lets look into this."), and the draft was wrong. Checked against Al'Kabor
spell data, Allakhazam and the P99 wiki: Summon Drink and Summon Food summon ONE item per
cast (10 mana, 4 s); the Magician has both at level 1, Cleric and Shaman get them in the
single digits (drink 2 to 5, food 9), Druid at 10 to 14; stacks arrive with Cornucopia and
Everfount (Magician 21 and 22, twenty each) and Abundant Food/Drink (Cleric 29 to 33, four
each). Recorded as decided for slice 2 at those levels, one item per cast. Lesson kept: a
number from memory is a claim until a source carries it; the user caught the one I did not
check.

Race data found: Human about 3 hours to the final stage, Halfling about 90 minutes (the P99
wiki). The other races have no source; the design starts them at the Human rate and flags
two calls of our own (Ogre and Troll 1.5, Fae 0.75) for the user to keep or drop.

Slice 1 (server half plus the chat lines) is build-ready and aimed at the Oct 22 bump day,
since `HungerUpdate` is a wire addition; the planner carries it.
