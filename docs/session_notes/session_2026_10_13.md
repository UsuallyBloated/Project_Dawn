## 3. The food branch review: one real hole, closed at creation

A review agent read `feat/food-and-water` against the design's ledger. The one real
finding: `create_character` validated only the name, so a modified launcher could send
`race: "Revenant"` (the transformation has a stat block, and under the food model that
race string bought starvation immunity for life). Fix: `char_data::CREATION_RACES` and
`PLAYABLE_CLASSES` (the sixteen and the eighteen), refused at creation with
`invalid_input`; the Revenant's immunity is no longer keyed on the race at all (it waits
for the transformation to be server-side). An auth smoke test sends a Revenant and a
Warlock and is refused, then a Halfling Rogue and is not.

Also from the review: meditate skill-ups rolled on while out of water (gated on the mana
regen rule now); "You are out of food." was said in the same tick as "You eat a Bread
Loaf." (the eat runs first and suppresses it); a character logging in hungry or out was
told nothing (its stage is said once on entering the world); the dead-drain test's bound
could not discriminate (fifteen seconds dead with heartbeats now); the server says "You eat
a X." on a hand-eat and the client no longer narrates it (`6b63ffe`); the migration comment
overstated sqlx's tolerance (a binary lacking an applied migration refuses to boot: deploy
the merged tree). Accepted residual, recorded in the ledger: a crash between the resource
write and the store write restores one loaf while keeping the fullness. Deviations from the
decided text recorded in the design: the race table is a match in `hunger.rs`, Half-Ogre
sits with Ogre and Troll at 1.5, the eat check runs every tick.
