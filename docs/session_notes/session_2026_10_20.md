## 2. Bard songs on the server: decisions, research, the build

The second of the audit's item 6 builds (after active skills), worked through as questions
first. **Research (Project 1999):** every song takes 3 s to sing; a song pulses every 6 s (the
server tick); one song at a time; a song's effect lasts about 12 to 18 s after the last pulse;
twisting is cycling songs on those 3 s sings, so three never drop, four rarely, five do; most
songs cost no mana and a few cost it per tick (the P99 forum's mechanics thread and the wiki's
guides). **Decisions:** EQ's shape (3 s sing, 6 s pulse, 12 s linger, the low end so four songs
is the cap); one song at a time; the heal song as a heal-over-time; effects as the ordinary
buffs; Aria pulsed on the current target; re-singing, `/stopsong`, a non-song cast and death end
a song, movement never does.

**"Mostly free" needed clarifying** (user: "The spells shouldn't be FREE. You said mostly
free, doesn't this mean the cost is so low that running out of mana isn't a constant threat?").
It meant zero: the large majority of P99's songs cost no mana at all; a handful cost it per tick.
Three options were put up (EQ's zero, cheap at the sing, free now with per-tick costs reserved
for big songs); the user's next worry reframed it: **"a level 1 character should not be as
capable as a level 10 character"**, and was EQ's free song a level 60 bard's? No: EQ's levers
were learn levels (ours already gate the six songs at 1, 4, 6, 8, 10 and 16), **each song's
effect growing with level** (Selo's +20% at 5 to +65% at 50; Hymn of Restoration 2 a tick at 6
to 13 at 60), and instruments. Ours were flat. **Decided: free, and every song scales with
level**, 0.4 of the listed effect at the learn level to full at 50 (`SONG_SCALE_AT_LEARN`,
`SONG_FULL_AT_LEVEL`, knobs; the data holds the level-50 values).

**Inflation flagged, a standing rule now** (user: "A lot of our spells, skills, effects, etc are
pretty over inflated. If there is something else that you come across that has such a
significant difference between our game and EQ please let me know"; saved to memory). The six
songs checked against P99: **Mana Weave was 12 mana a second to the bard against P99's mana
song at 6 mana per 6 s tick, to the group and not the bard**, twelve times EQ's; set to P99's
number (to the bard for now, since there are no group songs; EQ's rule when they come). The
other five sit at or under EQ: Selos' +35% ceiling against Selo's +65% (the user may raise it),
Poet's 8 against Hymn's 13 at 60, Wanderer's +12% haste against Anthem de Arms' +10%, Aria 8%
against Largo's 15 to 35%, Anthem of the Hunt with no EQ equivalent. A broader inflation pass
(every spell line and skill against P99 in one table) was offered as the next research job.
