## Name collisions — ruled (same session)

The four collisions flagged by the binding review were ruled rather than renamed:

- **Vrekka & Threkka canonized as a standing in-world dispute.** New lore doc `docs/concepts/world/vrekka_and_threkka.md`: the two pairs learned of each other via a Year 839 CR Counting House ledger error; the Ogres loudly claim to be "the real" pair, the Kobolds deny everything at the Junction and hope never to meet them. Three interpretations kept in tension (coincidence / theft / Dominion work-roster titles both cultures inherited — nobody is the real one). Hard rule: **the pairs never meet on-screen**. Roster briefs updated in IX-B (Vrekka of the Iron Eye, Threkka the Stone-Singer) and IX-D (Vrekka of the Black Bellows, Threkka of the Quiet-Step, plus Krzlik's standing "no one has heard of her" instruction). Quest hook sketched: *The Wrong Vrekka* (mid-level Foreign Quarter letter chain). Bard hook: the Laughing Company has a song about it that names neither party.
- **Mireth — ruled convergent, closed.** Checked `deity_mireth.md` (goddess of luck/trickery/thresholds, Rogue/Bard patron): the Troll healer's name is an old Troll given-name, no pantheon connection (Troll healing is already canon as non-pantheon). One-line note added to her IX-B roster brief. The Laughing Company finds the coincidence "exactly the kind of joke she would make."
- **Kethri — ruled true coincidence, closed.**
- Corrections sheet (`maps/corrections_847.md`) carries a same-season ruling addendum recording all three dispositions.
