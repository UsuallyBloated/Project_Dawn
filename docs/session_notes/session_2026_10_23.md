## 3. The suite made whole before the merge

Three integration tests on `feat/server-speed` had become timing-dependent through the EQ
parity pass's smaller numbers; all three were test faults, root-caused and fixed (server
`b25a19d`), and the suite then ran 134 of 134 six full runs in a row.

- **`pet_pulls_aggro_via_threat_reaggro`** failed every run: the camp's Decrepit Skeleton has
  16 HP since the parity pass, and the pet killed it before its threat passed the owner's. The
  test now fights its own 500 HP dev spawn and sends the pet in after the player's swing.
  Setting the switch multiplier to 1000 makes it fail at the switch, so it still tests the rule.
- **`owner_keeps_seeing_a_pet_parked_two_cells_away`** failed about one full run in four. A
  trace of the pet's positions showed it walking back and stopping 0.15 m short of the
  threshold. The owner's walk on row z = 60 ended 7.4 m from a Cave Bat (aggro 12); a level 6
  Necromancer can die to the bats now, and a player at 0 HP leaves the AI's target list, so the
  pet stands still as if its owner were offline. The walk moved to row z = 80, clear of every
  camp's aggro and spawn jitter by 14 m.
- **`a_cast_can_fizzle_on_a_low_casting_skill`** failed once in six: it read the first mana
  update in its window, and the regen fan's periodic full-mana update can land just before the
  fizzle's own. It now reads the update after the fizzle line on the same ordered channel.

Worth knowing for later tests: the regen fan sends resource updates every 500 ms even when
nothing changed, so a test reading "the" mana update must anchor it to the message it follows.
