# Session 2026-10-15

## 1. Resurrection sickness rebuilt to the P99 design

The morning's question was whether EverQuest had res sickness at all; it did, as Resurrection
Effects, and the user chose it whole: "Lets keep the design you found on the P99 wiki page.
Difficult is what we are looking for." Server `528ae23`, client `9c5e333`, `res_sickness_checklist.md`
rewritten (13 rows). Yesterday's build took one point per four levels off each primary and
halved regen, sized on a wrong claim about our stat scale that the user caught the same
evening (the real scale: base 10, race and class, then a class's main stats gain 1 to 4 a level
for life while the rest never move).

What exists now: on accept the player lands at a quarter of max HP with no mana (one-shot,
announced at once with its own HealthUpdate and ManaUpdate; not damage, so no stand or camp
cancel rides on it), and STR, AGI, INT, WIS and CON lose a level-scaled fraction of their
INTRINSIC value (race, class and level via `char_data::compute`, never gear or buffs), a tenth at
level 1 to a half at 60, which is P99's shape: about half a level 60's STR. Five minutes and
EQ's two lines as before. The regen halving was never P99's and is gone with its test.

Intrinsic is what makes the client honest: the snapshot carries names and durations only, so
the sheet can show the cut only if the client computes the same number. `PlayerStats` now has
`intrinsic_character(race, class, level)`, the race-class-level math `apply_character` always
ran, callable on its own (`apply_character` starts from it, unchanged in effect), and
`BuffManager`'s reconcile applies the server's formula to it. The fraction is f64 in the same
order of operations on both sides (GDScript's float is f64), so the two round the same product;
a one-point disagreement on the sheet would otherwise have been possible at half-point
products. Kept in lockstep by hand, like the level tables.

Tests: unit (the fraction at 1, 20, 60 and past 60; the cut on sample stats and on a level 60
Human Warrior, half off STR 142 and nothing below zero); the resurrection integration test now
also checks the landing (a HealthUpdate at a quarter of max, a ManaUpdate at zero; its first
draft read the last update in the window and regen had ticked one HP, so it looks for the
landing values instead); a headless probe for the client half (a Human Warrior at 1, 20 and 60:
2, 14 and 70 off STR, WIS never below zero). Full suite green (276 unit, 98 integration).

Unchanged by the 2026-06-22 lock: the XP refund tiers (25/50/75%). Flagged: buffs are not
persisted, so a relog clears the sickness, as camping did in classic; a knob if it should
persist.

## 2. Summon Food and Summon Drink, food slice 2

The user brought the numbers from Monsters and Memories: "conjure 1 to 4 slices of bread /
flasks of water, scaling by level." Server `4577350`, client `ec4fff2`, `summon_food_checklist.md` (15
rows); both sides move together, since an old client knows neither the spell nor the item.

What exists: a new `SUMMON_ITEM` target type. The pre-flight checks the item exists and that the
whole batch fits (the placer's own `can_accept`, so "Your bags are full." costs nothing); the arm
conjures `summon_count` of the spell's item into the bags through the real placer and fans the
inventory deltas and "You conjure X xN.". The count is one at the level the class learns the
spell and one more per ten levels, four at most (`SUMMON_LEVELS_PER_EXTRA`, a knob: M&M does
not publish its steps). The design's per-class levels are honoured by a new `class_min_levels`
table on a spell, read by the cast gate's level check and the client's spell book through
`required_level(class)`; the lockstep check learned inline tables and dictionaries for it. Two
new items on both sides, Conjured Bread and Conjured Water, a full meal and a full drink with no
vendor value, so the existing SellItem rule refuses them: free food must not be a coin faucet.
Item lockstep green, 174 on each side; spell lockstep green, 158 client and 142 server.

Not built, flagged: EQ landed summoned items on the cursor (ours go to the bags), and EQ's were
NO RENT and vanished on logout (ours persist; the no-drop flag item is the home for that).

Tests: a unit test on the count and the per-class levels; an integration test where a Magician
1 conjures one bread and is told, and a Magician 35 conjures four water; a headless probe of
the client's data and the two item resources. Full suite green (277 unit, 99 integration).
