## 09-30: the scale-readiness pair (Track D)

With the decided queue built and the audit done, the next work that needed neither the user
nor a desk was Track D of `phase5_plan.md`: the two scale-readiness prerequisites. Both
server-only, both on the deploy branch, no protocol change, no client export. Playtest:
`scale_readiness_checklist.md`.

- **Idle enemies no longer fan Position at 20 Hz** (server `ac38cc2`). Step 6b fanned every
  living enemy's position to every AOI-visible player every tick; idle mobs never move. The
  gate is regen.rs's change-or-keepalive pattern applied to position: `Entity` remembers the
  pos / yaw / time of its last send and fans on a move past 0.005 m, a turn past 0.001 rad,
  or the 500 ms `ENEMY_POSITION_KEEPALIVE`. **Measured, as the To-Do asked:** the new
  integration test counts one idle mob's Position messages to one player over 2 s, **32
  before, 4 after**. A new `enemy position fan` log line (sends, window, alive enemies,
  players in world) reports once a minute while anyone is online, so the R720 shows the same
  before/after from one deploy. `AiEvents.moved`, whose comment claimed to gate exactly this,
  turned out to be dead code (computed every tick, read nowhere) and is deleted.
- **Enemy and pet cell crossings fan spawn/despawn** (server `c121341`). The enemy cell-change
  loop called `aoi.update()` and discarded the `(gained, lost)` cells it returns, so a mob
  chasing into view streamed Position for an id the client had no spawn for; the client
  drops those on the floor (no name, level or hp to build a placeholder from). Now a mirror
  of the step 5b player handler: players in the gained cells get EnemySpawn or PetSpawn,
  players in the lost cells get EntityDespawn.
- **A third gap, found while verifying the second and never recorded:** the player crossing
  handler tested ids against `LOOT_BAG_ID_BASE` before anything else, and the id partitions
  stack (player < enemy < bag < pet), so a pet matched the bag arm, found neither a bag nor
  a corpse, and was skipped. A player walking into view of a friend's existing pet never saw
  it. The handler gains a pet arm ahead of the bag arm.
- **Tests.** Three integration tests seed the second client two cells from spawn through the
  `characters` table (the `set_char_level` pattern; EnterWorld derives the AOI cell from the
  loaded position), so no 240 m walk: a dev-spawned mob chased across the x=240 line spawns
  on the watcher and despawns when led back out; a skeleton following its Necromancer across
  the line spawns on the watcher; a player walking into view of an already-summoned pet gets
  its PetSpawn. All three were run against the previous `tick.rs` first and failed with their
  asserted messages. Suite: 239 unit + 52 integration green.
- **Exploit lens.** Neither change gives the client a new input. The gate is server state;
  a dropped keepalive leaves a view stale by at most 500 ms and every range check is
  server-side. The crossing fan sends the same payloads to the same audience the EnterWorld
  seed and the player crossing already send; kiting a mob over a cell edge costs one
  reliable message per crossing to the players in three cells, bounded by mob speed.
- Also: `server_design.md`'s AOI paragraph described a 100 m bubble; it has been 120 m cells
  with a 3x3 neighbourhood since Track 7. Corrected while there.

The session-workflow review on the two commits returned eight findings; six are folded in,
two recorded. Fixed: the EnterWorld pet seed keyed on the owner's cell instead of the pet's
(every other pet path keys on the pet's; a joiner could hold a ghost pet with no Positions,

connection) and fail loudly on a transport disconnect; the Border Runner spawns at the aggro
cap so a slow test loop cannot leash it home. Recorded, not built: a soft start of up to

owner's HUD dismissing a live pet two cells away (its own To-Do item; theoretical with
today's pet AI). `charm_converts_enemy_to_pet` varied once in the full parallel run and
passed alone twice, the documented load-sensitivity.
