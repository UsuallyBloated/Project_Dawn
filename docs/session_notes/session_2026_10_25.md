**Facing DECIDED: (a)**, the client sends its facing with each Move, after the user asked how
other games do it and what it costs. EQ (EQEmu's `PlayerPositionUpdateClient_Struct`: a 12-bit
`heading` and a 10-bit `delta_heading` beside the position, relayed as sent) and WoW
(TrinityCore's `MovementInfo` carrying a `Position` with `m_orientation`, plus
`MSG_MOVE_SET_FACING` and the turn opcodes) both do (a); neither derives facing from movement
or anti-cheats it. For us: one number on each 20 Hz Move, about 80 bytes a second upstream per

per account and item, the sum of `delta` against the count across the account's inventories
and both vaults; per account, the sum of `copper` against its wallets; empty when the books
balance. The daily sweep (`ledger::sweep`, at boot and every 24 h, on its own task like the
ban sweep) folds rows older than 30 days into one `carry` row per character and item holding
