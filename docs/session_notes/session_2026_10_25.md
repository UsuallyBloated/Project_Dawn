**Facing DECIDED: (a)**, the client sends its facing with each Move, after the user asked how
other games do it and what it costs. EQ (EQEmu's `PlayerPositionUpdateClient_Struct`: a 12-bit
`heading` and a 10-bit `delta_heading` beside the position, relayed as sent) and WoW
(TrinityCore's `MovementInfo` carrying a `Position` with `m_orientation`, plus
`MSG_MOVE_SET_FACING` and the turn opcodes) both do (a); neither derives facing from movement
or anti-cheats it. For us: one number on each 20 Hz Move, about 80 bytes a second upstream per

per account and item, the sum of `delta` against the count across the account's inventories
and both vaults; per account, the sum of `copper` against its wallets; empty when the books
balance. The daily sweep (`ledger::sweep`, at boot and every 24 h, on its own task like the
ban sweep) folds rows older than 30 days into one `carry` row per character and item holding

## 52. Falls on the server: height model slice 2 (2026-11-04, afternoon)

The user's go on the shape laid out that morning ("Sounds excellent. nice work. Go ahead."):
the fall design's slice 1 (`docs/design/server_fall_damage.md` §3.2, decided 2026-10-24),
built on `feat/height-model` in both repos (server `351e732`, client `763a4ab`), riding the

**What exists.** `world/fall.rs` holds the rules and `tick/movement.rs` applies them. A player
carries a vertical state: standing, or in the air with a speed and the height it left the
ground at. After the XZ step the body follows the ground down as far as the client's own floor
snap reaches (`step x tan 55° + 0.5 m`) and only onto a floor (ground no steeper than the
client's 55°); past that, or onto a face, it has left the ground and falls at 20 m/s². In the
air there is no slope rule and no uphill shrink. A face the body would meet is a wall it
slides down, as the client's body slides off one: the movement step pushes it down the
gradient until the ground is below it or the slide reaches a floor, where it lands with the
whole drop charged; a floor above the body blocks the step instead. The landing is measured
from the height the body left the ground at (exact at any tick rate; the end-of-tick speed
under-read a 10 m fall by 5%) and costs a share of max HP: nothing to 2 m, all of it at 42 m,

attacker (the user's calls of 10-24); "You hit the ground hard for N damage."; the ordinary
damage marker stands the player and breaks a `/camp`; a death goes through the death sweep,
the corpse where the body landed. Leaving is no way out of a fall: a linkdead body reads no
heading but still falls and lands; a Quit from mid-air is judged unclean where the transport
drops, so the body lingers, lands and is saved hurt at the reap; a port or a resurrection
summon settles the fall first and does not move a mover it kills; a respawn lands grounded
whatever its bind was saved with; a dropped bag lands on the ground under the dropper. The
jump stays cosmetic; mobs and pets never fall. Client: `player.gd` names its floor angle and
