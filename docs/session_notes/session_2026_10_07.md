## 10. Step 3 built: an interrupted cast costs mana

Server `4303459` (trade branch `f89a1fd`), server-only; `interrupt_charge_checklist.md` (13
rows) is the playtest and needs only the redeploy. The user's call: a hit that interrupts a
cast charges the spell's mana times the fraction of the bar that had run, capped at the mana
held; a deliberate cancel stays free (it never reaches the server); a hit that did no damage
charges nothing, or a damage shield and a zero-damage swing would be mana-drain weapons. The
fraction uses the server's own cast time, never the client's bar length, so a forged long bar
cannot shrink the charge. `cast_gate::interrupt_charge` is the pure decision with a unit
test; `roll_cast_interrupt` takes the applied damage, charges and reports the amount; the
five interrupt sites (enemy melee, PvP melee, PvP spell, both damage-shield reflects) pass
their damage and tell the caster alone, the true ManaUpdate right behind the CastFail plus
"Your concentration breaks: N mana lost." The client's local refund on the CastFail is then
settled a frame later by the ManaUpdate, so no client change; `spells.gd`'s cancel comment
says so. One integration test: two dummies beat on a Cleric casting Bind Affinity until the
real channeling roll interrupts it, and the line, the charge and the settled mana agree; it
fails on the previous code. No integration test for the zero-damage case: the dev dummy's
minimum swing is 1 damage, so the unit test carries that rule. 261 unit, 84 integration
green. Also caught up: `shaman.md` gets the dated Torpor note the step 1 paperwork promised.
