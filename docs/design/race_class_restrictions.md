# Race / Class Restrictions

*Restrictions are cultural, physiological, or both. A blocked combination means character creation will not offer it — not that no individual of that race has ever done it.*

*See `LOCKED_COMBOS` in `data/character_data.gd` for current code state. Full restrictions are design targets, not yet fully implemented.*

| Alignment | Races |

| **Evil** | Dark Elf, Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Kobold |

## Restrictions by Class

**Warrior** — Available to all races.

**Paladin** — Human, Elf, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Felhari, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: all Evil-aligned races. Fae follow no mortal religious hierarchies.

**Shadow Knight** — Human, Dark Elf, Half-Elf, Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Minotaur, Kobold, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: all Good-aligned races. Requires a genuine darkness that cannot be performed.

**Cleric** — Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Ogre, Troll, Minotaur, Felhari, Kobold, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: Fae (no mortal gods). Kel`varath follow necromantic tradition rather than clerical orders.

**Druid** — Human, Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Minotaur, Fae, Felhari.
Blocked: Dark Elf (severed from living nature by generations underground), Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Kobold, Half-Ogre.

**Shaman** — Human, Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Minotaur, Felhari, Kobold, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: races without active ancestor-spirit traditions. Gnome ancestor spirits actively resist the summons.

**Rogue** — Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Kel`varath, Fae, Felhari, Kobold.
Blocked: Ogre, Troll, Minotaur, Half-Ogre — size and gait incompatible with the craft.

**Monk** — Human, Elf, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Kel`varath, Felhari.
Blocked: Ogre, Troll, Minotaur, Half-Ogre (mass and temperament); Fae (size); Dark Elf (the arrogance the training cannot overcome); Gnome (the body will not cooperate with what the mind attempts).

**Ranger** — Human, Elf, Wood Elf, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Minotaur, Fae, Felhari.
Blocked: Dark Elf, Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Gnome, Kobold, Half-Ogre.

**Witch Hunter** — Human, Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Felhari, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: all Evil-aligned races (they are the hunted), Minotaur, Fae, Ogre, Troll.

**Bard** — Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Minotaur, Fae, Felhari, Kobold.
Blocked: Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Half-Ogre.

**Magician** — All races except Ogre (INT floor too low for arcane study).

**Wizard** — Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Gnome, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Kel`varath, Fae.
Blocked: Ogre, Troll (cannot hold the concepts), Wood Elf (oral tradition only), Halfling (no institutional arcane tradition), Minotaur (displaced people, no fixed schools), Felhari (no tradition), Kobold (lifespan too short for meaningful academic completion), Half-Ogre.

**Sorcerer** — Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Troll, Kel`varath, Fae, Felhari, Kobold, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: Ogre, Minotaur — the bloodline power exists but manifests as uncontrolled destructive episodes rather than usable magic.

**Enchanter** — Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Half-Elf, Fae.
Blocked: Dwarf, Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Minotaur, Felhari, Kobold, Half-Ogre — requires sustained concentration and social intelligence.

**Necromancer** — Human, Dark Elf, Gnome, Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Kobold.
Blocked: Elf, Halfling, Dwarf (the prohibition is cultural bedrock, not law), Wood Elf, Half-Elf, Minotaur, Fae, Felhari, Half-Ogre.

**Blood Mage** — Human, Dark Elf, Half-Elf, Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Kobold, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: all Good-aligned races, Gnome, Fae (biology incompatible), Minotaur, Felhari, Wood Elf.

**Beast Master** — Human, Elf, Wood Elf, Half-Elf, Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Minotaur, Felhari, Kobold, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: Dark Elf (severed from living nature; the warder-bond cannot form in soil that no longer answers them), Gnome (the analytical frame that forecloses the instinctual surrender the bond requires), Halfling (relationship to animals is domestic and agrarian — the warder-bond draws on predator instinct, not keeper instinct), Dwarf (stone culture; deep respect for the living world but no tradition of spirit-bonding with beasts), Fae (magic is illusory and mind-based; the beast-spirit bond operates on an incompatible channel, and their form is not suited to the melee demands of the class).
