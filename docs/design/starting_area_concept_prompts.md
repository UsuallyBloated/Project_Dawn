# Starting area concept art: the prompt pack

Written 2026-11-13 for the 4 km starting area proposal
(`starting_area_comp_sketches.html`). Eight scenes. Four have a composition sketch
(`Project_Dawn_Source/concept_art/starting_area/comps/`), sent to Meshy's image-to-image as
the layout reference; four are text only. Each full prompt is: the composition line (only
when a sketch is attached), then the scene, then the style line.

Every named place comes from `zone_camps.toml`, `terrain_features.toml` and the NPC dialogue.
The architecture, vegetation and each scene's accent colour are first proposals: no document
describes them. Nothing here is approved art direction until the user says so.

## Style line (every prompt ends with it)

> Fantasy MMORPG environment concept art, wide establishing shot, clean digital painting with
> soft even shading, matte materials, muted earthy palette with a single saturated accent,
> simple readable shapes that suit low-poly models with painted textures, classic
> EverQuest-era mood, semi-realistic, late afternoon light. No oil-painting brushwork, no
> anime, no glossy CG sheen, no text, no labels, no watermark, no border, no UI.

It is the style bible's look (section 1) restated for environments; the bible's own template
is written for character sheets.

## Composition line (only with a sketch attached)

> Use the attached flat-colour sketch as the composition: keep its camera, horizon line, and
> the placement and relative size of every element. Replace the flat shapes with a finished
> painting; do not keep the flat vector look.

### 1. The town from the east road

> A small frontier town at the edge of the settled lands, a year ago only a watchtower and
> three buildings, seen from the dirt road approaching its east gate, looking west. A low wall
> of warm grey-tan stone with a single arched gate, one old square stone watchtower rising
> above it flying a faded green banner (the only saturated colour), a handful of timber and
> stone roofs behind the wall, thin chimney smoke. Strip farms outside the walls on the left.
> Far behind the town, a broad range of bare hills a mile off with a pale bone-white ruin on
> the crest and the low sun setting behind them; on the right horizon a long dark wooded
> ridge. A lone traveller on the road for scale, a wooden signpost. The land is wide and open.

### 2. The town square

> Inside a small frontier town: a packed-earth and cobble market square at the foot of an old
> square stone watchtower. Around it: a dwarf provisioner's stone-walled shop with loaves on
> the sill and salt barrels by the door, a blacksmith's open smithy with a glowing forge (the
> only saturated colour, orange), a general merchant's stall heaped with rope, tools and bolts
> of cloth, a stout stone counting house, and a robed priestess standing by a small weathered
> shrine stone. A town guard in a faded green and brown tabard near the gate arch. Few
> buildings, new timber beside old stone: a town still growing.

### 3. The Bonepile, west of the walls

> Just outside the west wall of a small stone-walled frontier town: farm plots where the dead
> have clawed out of the ground. Heaps of old bones and disturbed earth among the furrows, a
> few slow decrepit skeletons shambling, an abandoned hoe, a broken fence, a scarecrow with a
> red scarf (the only saturated colour). The town wall and its watchtower close behind on the
> right; far to the west a broad range of bare hills under a low sun.

### 4. The east road and the Gnoll Flats

> A rutted dirt caravan road running east across a wide rocky plain: scattered boulders, dry
> grass, low scrub. Beside the road an overturned supply cart. In the middle distance a gnoll
> raider camp: hide tents, a bone-and-feather totem, a fire pit with thin smoke (its orange
> the only saturated colour), upright dog-headed raiders. Grey wolves loping through the grass
> south of the road. On the far eastern horizon a high flat-topped plateau ending in a sheer
> cliff.

### 5. The Greywood ridge and the pass

> Looking north along a dirt road toward a long ridge of grey rock 70 metres high with sheer
> faces, cut by a single pass 60 metres wide. Through the gap, a dark old conifer forest with
> mist lying in the pass. Sparse pines on the grass in front, bats wheeling over a hollow on
> the left. A lone traveller carrying a torch on the road for scale (the torch the only
> saturated colour). Overcast late light, foreboding.

### 6. The Broken Crypt

> A ruined crypt far older than the road beside it, in a shallow grassy bowl inside a dark
> conifer forest: a broken stone entrance half sunk in the earth, toppled columns, cracked
> flagstones, worn carvings. Skeleton champions in corroded plate armour standing guard in
> disciplined ranks, soldiers of a forgotten war. Cold misty light through the trees. A
> tattered crimson war banner (the only saturated colour).

### 7. The Sunken Barrow

> High on broad bare hills, looking down from the rim into a huge terraced pit 320 metres
> across and 45 metres deep, dug to hold something. At the bottom a low stone barrow door
> leaking a sickly green light (the only saturated colour). A ring of tall rune-carved ward
> stones around the rim, several fallen and cracked. In the foreground a field of scattered
> old bones in the grass. On the hill crest behind the pit, a pale ossuary built of giant
> bones. Slow zombies on the pit's terraces, a lone adventurer on the rim for scale. Dusk sky.

### 8. The Wraith Gate

> Looking east and up at a sheer cliff 60 metres high running across the whole frame, the
> edge of a plateau. On top, at the centre, a single ancient free-standing stone arch filled
> with cold cyan light (the only saturated colour), pale wraiths drifting beside it. At the
> cliff's foot a grassy flat with a tiny lone adventurer for scale; at the left edge dark
> trees hung with giant spider webs. A darkening eastern sky.
