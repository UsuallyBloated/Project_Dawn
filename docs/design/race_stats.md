## Racial Abilities & Starting Skill Bonuses

### Human
**Racial abilities:**
- **Adaptability** — Class advancement penalties reduced by 20%; XP gain +5%
- **Generalist Bonus** — Choose any two tradeskills at character creation; start with +10 skill in each
- **Faction Neutral** — No innate faction bonuses or penalties; start at 0 with all neutral-default races

**Starting skill bonuses:** +5 to any one weapon skill of your choice

---

### Elf
**Racial abilities:**
- **Low-Light Vision** — See clearly in near-darkness; visibility unimpaired down to moonlit conditions
- **Arcane Attunement** — Arcane and holy spell mana costs reduced by 5%; mana regenerates 10% faster out of combat
- **Ancient Memory** — +5% skill gain rate for all learned spells and arcane disciplines

**Starting skill bonuses:** +5 Foraging; +5 to your primary casting school at level 1

---

### Dark Elf
**Racial abilities:**
- **True Darkvision** — Full unimpaired vision in complete darkness; the best low-light capability of any surface-origin race
- **Innate Magic Resistance** — +15 resist vs Enchantment-school spells; harder to charm, mesmerize, or confuse
- **Shadow Step** — Passive: when standing still in darkness, aggro radius reduced by 30%

**Starting skill bonuses:** +5 Hide; +5 to any INT-based casting school

---

### Wood Elf
**Racial abilities:**
- **Low-Light Vision** — See clearly in near-darkness and heavy foliage cover
- **Woodland Camouflage** — While stationary in forest terrain, aggro radius reduced by 40%; counts as partial Hide
- **Forage** — Can gather food, herbs, and useful materials from the environment; success rate scales with WIS
- **Sure-Footed** — No movement penalty in forest or uneven terrain; immune to tripping effects

**Starting skill bonuses:** +10 Archery; +5 Foraging; +5 Tracking

---

### Gnome
**Racial abilities:**
- **Low-Light Vision** — See clearly in near-darkness; useful in workshop conditions
- **Tinkering Aptitude** — +15 starting skill in Tinkering; tinkered items have a 10% bonus to quality
- **Clockwork Eye** — Can identify mechanical traps and lock complexity before attempting; +10 to Disarm Trap
- **Small Profile** — Harder to target at range; ranged attacks against Gnomes have −10% accuracy

**Starting skill bonuses:** +15 Tinkering; +5 Alchemy; +5 Picking Locks

---

### Halfling
**Racial abilities:**
- **Lucky** — Once per combat: automatically avoid a single attack that would have dealt lethal damage (leaves you at 1 HP instead); 5-minute cooldown
- **Natural Stealth** — Innate Sneak and Hide; can use both without class prerequisite; +10 starting value in each
- **Forage** — Can gather food and useful plants from the environment; excellent food quality bonus
- **Hearth-Sense** — +10 to all social skill checks; passive read on creature intent (friendly/neutral/hostile aura visible)
- **Small Profile** — −10% accuracy on ranged attacks against Halflings; +5% evasion vs melee

**Starting skill bonuses:** +10 Sneak; +5 Hide; +5 Foraging; +5 Pick Locks

---

### Dwarf
**Racial abilities:**
- **Infravision** — See heat signatures in complete darkness; no darkvision penalty underground
- **Stoneblood** — +20 resist vs poison and disease; Dwarves endure toxins that fell other races
- **Stonecunning** — +10 to detecting traps, assessing structural stability, and identifying crafted item quality
- **Sturdy Frame** — Knockback effects reduced by 60%; cannot be stunned by non-boss abilities
- **Forge-Rite** — +10 starting skill in Smithing; crafted metal items have +5 to quality floor

**Starting skill bonuses:** +10 Smithing; +5 Mining; +5 Defense

---

### Half-Elf
**Racial abilities:**
- **Low-Light Vision** — Inherited from Elven parent; see clearly in near-darkness
- **Perceptive** — +10 to detecting lies, reading NPC intent, and noticing environmental details
- **Versatile Heritage** — Class advancement penalties reduced by 10%; slightly faster skill-gain rate in second-tier disciplines
- **Diplomatic** — +5 to faction standing checks when opening negotiations; can sometimes smooth hostile encounters to neutral

**Starting skill bonuses:** +5 to one weapon skill; +5 to one arcane or divine skill of your choice

---

### Ogre
**Racial abilities:**
- **Thick Hide** — Natural 5 AC; passive 5% physical damage reduction regardless of armor worn
- **Slam** — Natural attack: 15 base damage, no weapon required; can be used as an off-hand attack
- **Regeneration** — +1 HP/s at all times; not combat-gated; always active
- **Intimidating Presence** — Humanoid enemies have a 10% chance to hesitate (lose first attack) when engaging an Ogre

**Starting skill bonuses:** +10 2H Blunt; +5 1H Blunt; +5 Defense

---

### Troll
**Racial abilities:**
- **Regeneration** — Fastest natural HP regen of any playable race: +4 HP/s out of combat, +1 HP/s in combat; suppressed by fire or acid damage for 10s after being hit
- **Darkvision** — Bioluminescent eyes; see clearly in complete darkness
- **Scent** — Passive detection of hidden creatures and players within 15m; cannot be blocked by Hide or Sneak above a skill of 75
- **Thick Skin** — Natural 3 AC; minor passive physical damage reduction

**Starting skill bonuses:** +10 2H Blunt; +5 Defense; +5 Swimming

---

### Kel`varath
**Racial abilities:**
- **Natural Armor** — Scales provide 5 AC at all times, regardless of equipment worn
- **Infravision** — See in complete darkness; heat-signature detection in low-light conditions
- **Forage** — Can gather food and useful materials from wilderness environments
- **Claws** — Natural attack: 10 base damage; can strike as an off-hand attack without a weapon
- **Cold Resistance** — +15 resist to cold-based effects; −10 resist to fire-based effects (cold-blooded tradeoff)
- **Minor Regeneration** — +1 HP/s out of combat only (scales regrow slowly)

**Starting skill bonuses:** +5 H2H (Hand-to-Hand); +5 Sneak; +5 Swimming

---

### Minotaur
**Racial abilities:**
- **Infravision** — See clearly in near-darkness and underground environments
- **Gore** — Natural horn attack: 20 base damage; can be used as a primary or secondary attack
- **Stampede** — Active ability (60s cooldown): charge forward 5m and knock a single target back 5m; target stunned 2s on landing
- **Endurance** — Stamina pool 25% larger than other races; fatigue effects take longer to apply
- **Ancestor's Memory** — +10 to all WIS-based checks involving history, lore, and ancestor-spirit interaction

**Starting skill bonuses:** +10 2H Weapons; +5 Defense; +5 1H Weapons

---

### Fae
**Racial abilities:**
- **True Flight** — Functional flight; immune to fall damage; can navigate vertical terrain and bypass ground-level obstacles; flight speed equals run speed
- **Glamour** — Passive minor illusion aura: NPCs make a WIS check (DC 15) before attacking; failure causes 2s hesitation; active once per 30s
- **Fae Sight** — Can see through illusions (DC 30 check to see through Enchanter-tier illusions); detect invisible creatures within 10m
- **Tiny Profile** — −20% accuracy on all attacks against Fae; requires Fae-scaled equipment; some heavy armor categories unavailable
- **Ancient Clarity** — Immune to fear and panic effects; WIS-based saves get +10

**Starting skill bonuses:** +10 Illusion school; +5 CHA-based skills; +5 Perception

---

### Felhari
**Racial abilities:**
- **Low-Light Vision** — Predator night sight; see clearly in near-darkness and dim conditions
- **Claws** — Natural attack: 12 base damage; can be used as off-hand attacks or in H2H combat
- **Natural Stealth** — +10 Sneak at start; passive sound dampening while moving slowly
- **Keen Senses** — +15 to perception and hearing checks; detect hidden creatures within 20m by scent
- **Leap** — Active (30s cooldown): leap up to 6m horizontally or 3m vertically; can reach elevated terrain other races cannot

**Starting skill bonuses:** +10 H2H (Hand-to-Hand); +5 Sneak; +5 Tracking

---

### Kobold
**Racial abilities:**
- **Low-Light Vision** — Excellent underground sight; mild −5 penalty in direct sunlight conditions
- **Pack Tactics** — When fighting alongside at least one other Kobold: +8% attack, +5% evasion; bonus scales with pack size (max +20% at 4+ Kobolds)
- **Tunnelcraft** — +10 to underground navigation, detecting tunnels and structural weaknesses, and trap-setting
- **Small Frame** — −15% accuracy on attacks made by Large or larger enemies; +5% evasion bonus
- **Dragon-Kin Fortitude** — Passive: +10 resist vs fire-based effects

**Starting skill bonuses:** +5 Rogue skills (Pick Locks, Sneak); +5 Trap Setting; +5 Underground Navigation

---

### Half-Ogre
**Racial abilities:**
- **Thick Build** — Natural 3 AC; passive 3% physical damage reduction; knockback effects reduced by 40%
- **Slam** — Natural attack: 12 base damage; usable as an off-hand attack without a weapon
- **Imposing Presence** — Humanoid enemies have a 7% chance to hesitate (lose first swing) when engaging; CHA not penalized
- **Endurance** — Fatigue takes 20% longer to accumulate

**Starting skill bonuses:** +5 2H Weapons; +5 1H Weapons; +5 Defense

### Aerathi (draft)
**Racial abilities:**
- **Glide** — While airborne, spread wings to glide: no fall damage, ~3m forward per 1m of descent
- **Raptor Sight** — Visual detection range doubled; +10 to perception checks at range
- **Talons** — Natural attack: 12 base damage; usable as an off-hand attack
- **Stoop** — Active (60s cooldown): diving strike while gliding; +5% weapon damage per meter of descent, capped at +100%
- **Hollow Bones** — Knockback distance against Aerathi +40%; carry capacity −15%

**Starting skill bonuses:** +5 H2H; +5 Archery; +5 Tracking

---

### Vesperin (draft)
**Racial abilities:**
- **Glide** — While airborne, spread wings to glide: no fall damage, ~3m forward per 1m of descent
- **Mimicry** — Can reproduce any voice or sound heard; Languages skill-up rate +50%
- **Songblood** — +10 starting skill in all Bard performance disciplines; own song effects last 10% longer
- **Vivid Plumage** — +10 to social/CHA-based checks; plumage as readable as a Felhari tail to those who know the flock
- **Hollow Bones** — Knockback distance against Vesperin +40%; carry capacity −15%

**Starting skill bonuses:** +10 Performance (Bard disciplines); +5 to any one casting school; +5 Foraging

---

### Sylphari (draft)
**Racial abilities:**
- **Photosynthesis** — +2 HP/s out-of-combat regen in direct sunlight; no bonus at night, indoors, or underground (interaction with planned food/water regen gating TBD)
- **Barkskin** — Natural 4 AC at all times, regardless of equipment
- **Sapblood** — +20 resist vs poison and disease; −15 resist vs fire (sap ignites)
- **Rooted** — While stationary: knockback reduced 60%; immune to trip effects
- **Seasonal Aspect** — Appearance shifts with the world calendar (spring blossom → summer green → autumn amber → winter dormant); cosmetic only in v1

**Starting skill bonuses:** +10 Foraging; +5 Alchemy; +5 to any one WIS-based casting school
