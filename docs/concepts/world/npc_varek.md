# NPC Profile — Varek
*Varek's Spire. Level range 25–35. The wizard who has been sitting in the basement.*

---

## Identity

**Full name:** Varek Lenn *(VAR-eck LEN)*
**Race:** Human
**Class:** Wizard (level 35)
**Age:** 133 (appears: mid-sixties; biological age is approximately 65–70 due to the Residual's temporal effect)
**Alignment:** Neutral

**Physical description:** Tall and slightly stooped — the stoop is from decades of ducking through the Spire's lower-floor doorways, which he built too short and has never corrected. White hair, long but not unkempt. The hands of someone who has been taking extremely careful notes for ninety years — ink stains that have become permanent, nails cut very short. His eyes are clear but have a specific quality of someone who has been staring at something that doesn't stay still for a very long time.

He does not dress in wizard robes. He dresses in the practical clothes of someone who spends most of their time in a basement — layered, worn, functional. The one concession to his profession is a ring on his left hand: a spell-anchor ring he had made at forty and wears constantly, which allows him to cast without a focus component. He has been wearing it for ninety years and it has not lost function. He is uncertain whether this is because of the ring's quality or the Residual's effect on time.

---

## Personality

Varek is intelligent, methodical, and gradually becoming less certain about things. This last quality has been developing for ninety years and is now notable enough that players will encounter it in their first conversation with him.

He is not confused. He is not losing his memory. He is experiencing a specific erosion of epistemic confidence — the conviction attached to facts he knows is wearing thin. He knows his name. He is less certain than he was that Varek Lenn is the right name for himself. He knows he built the Spire. He is less certain that the Spire is something he built, as opposed to something he has simply always been in.

This quality makes him unusual to talk to. He does not say wrong things. He says correct things with insufficient certainty, and occasionally says very uncertain things that turn out to be more accurate than confident statements.

He is not unhappy. He is present, engaged, and genuinely interested in the players who come to see him — partly because visitors are uncommon and partly because he has been trying to solve a problem for ninety years and new perspectives have theoretical value. He will ask questions. His questions are better than most people's.

**Verbal tic:** He ends uncertain statements with a pause and then "I think" — not as a filler phrase but as a genuine epistemological qualifier. "The tower has seven floors. I think." (He built seven floors. He is not wrong. He is just less certain than he should be.)

---

## History

**Age 20–43:** Varek Lenn was a practicing Wizard in Greyveil. Journeyman Rank, later Senior. He specialized in spatial theory — the mathematics of how magic interacts with physical space. He was considered excellent but not exceptional; the Wizards' Guild remembers him fondly, which is more than they say about most practitioners.

**Age 43:** He took a survey contract for the Ashen Coast region — foundation site assessment for what he described as "a private research facility." The survey found the Residual in the rock shelf. He recognized it as significant. He spent three months in Greyveil trying to find documentation of anything like it in the Guild archives and found nothing.

**Age 44:** He began construction of the Spire, funded through the liquidation of his Greyveil assets and a loan from a merchant he repaid with enchanting work over the following decade. The Residual was not mentioned in the construction permits.

**Age 44–54:** The first decade. He studied the Residual in an organized manner, publishing four papers to the Guild on theoretical grounds — he did not mention the Residual by name, describing it as a "theoretical construct" in papers about spatial anomalies. The papers were received with polite disinterest. He stopped publishing after the fourth paper when he realized the Guild did not have the context to evaluate what he was writing about.

**Age 55–90:** The middle decades. He added the lower floors to the Spire (he had originally planned five; he added B1 and B2 as the study expanded; B3 emerged from a survey of B2's depth that revealed more rock than he had planned for). He began spending nights in B3 as well as days. The certainty erosion began somewhere in this period; he noticed it at approximately age 70 and began documenting it.

**Age 90–133:** Current state. He is primarily in B3. He comes upstairs for meals, which the golem prepares according to a schedule he set in year 12 and has not modified. He occasionally goes to his study on Floor 7 to review his upper-floor notes. He has not left the Spire in forty-one years.

## Relationships

**The Golem (Floor 1):** Varek built it in Year 6. He named it Desk, which is not a dignified name but is accurate. Desk has been running the tower's administrative functions since then. Their relationship is the relationship between someone who built a tool and has had it for eighty-seven years — functional, comfortable, and occasionally surprising. Desk will not go to B3. Varek stopped giving it orders to do so after Year 20.

**The Wizards' Guild (Greyveil):** Formal estrangement. The Guild considers Varek a practitioner who went into private study and stopped communicating. They did not expel him — his dues are paid through a standing arrangement he set up before leaving Greyveil — but they would not know him in a crowd. The Guild does not know about the Residual or the Spire's basement.

**Former students:** Varek took three apprentices over his career, all before the Spire. The first is dead. The second is a senior Guild Wizard in Aelindra who occasionally thinks about Varek and does not know why. The third came to the Spire once, twelve years ago, to check on him. She left after two days and has not returned; she told her colleagues Varek was "fine but unusual" and declined to elaborate.

---

## Mechanics

**As a trainer:** Varek trains Wizard spells and theory. He has access to spell ranks and theory knowledge that the Guild does not have, because he has had ninety years without the Guild's curriculum constraints. Spells learned from Varek may have slightly unusual properties — functional but calibrated for someone who has been working in close proximity to the Residual.

**As a vendor:** Floor 2 library. Books available for purchase cover magical theory, spatial mathematics, and several subjects that are not available elsewhere.

**As a quest giver:** The Varek and the Residual questline initiates when players reach B3 and have their first extended conversation with him. He does not frame it as a quest; he frames it as "I have been trying to understand this for ninety years and I would value a second opinion, or more precisely a second set of certainties, because mine are becoming unreliable."

**Combat:** If provoked, Varek fights at full Wizard effectiveness. He has had ninety years to refine his combat spells and is not rusty — the certainty erosion does not affect procedural memory, only propositional certainty. He will use every tool available, including the Spire's prepared defenses on each floor. He is not aggressive and will not pursue fleeing players. He will not use Floor 4 in combat. He will not explain why.

## Dialogue Samples

*On the Residual:*
"It does not have a shape. Or it has several. The shapes are not consistent — I think. I have notes from Year 12 that describe a specific configuration. The notes from Year 43 describe something different. I cannot tell you if the Residual changed or if my perception changed. Both seem possible."

*On how long he has been there:*
"Long enough that I stopped counting years and started counting observations. I have... a great many observations. I think the number is in the high thousands. Time is less reliable down here than it is upstairs."

*On whether he is okay:*
"That is a question with a complicated answer. I am alive. I am working. The work is important. Whether I am okay in the way you mean — the social meaning — I am less certain. I was certain, once. I think it was comfortable."

*On Floor 4:*
"The experiment on Floor 4 is ongoing. It should not be disturbed. I would rather not discuss what would happen if it were disturbed, because I am less certain of the specifics than I would like to be, and imprecision about that particular subject seems inadvisable."

*If asked to leave the Spire:*
"I have thought about leaving. I think about it fairly regularly. The difficulty is that I have been watching the Residual for — a long time. If I leave, no one watches it. I don't know what it does when it's not being watched. I have been afraid to find out. I think that's the accurate word. Afraid. I'm more certain about that word than most."
