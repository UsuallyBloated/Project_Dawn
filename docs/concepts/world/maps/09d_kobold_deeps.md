## Prefatory Note

This plate is the fourth and final starting-zone plate. It zooms upon the **Kobold Deeps** — the Pack tunnel network beneath Cogsworth, originally Dominion supply construction, now Pack-occupied; the only underground starter-zone in the network; the surface entry at the contested junction at Cogsworth's Tier VI is rendered at the plate's top, the Pack territory proper at the plate's centre, the Probe tier and the Expansion face at the plate's south, and the descent to the Underdark Crossing at the plate's bottom.

The Kobold Deeps differ from the other three starter zones in three respects. First, the Deeps are *underground* — there is no sky, no weather, no daylight; the principal light sources are the **Glow-Vein** (a bioluminescent fungus chamber the Pack tends as a working light-source), portable lanterns, and the small fires the Pack Council Chamber maintains for ceremony. Second, the Deeps are *Pack territory* — the four Packs (Long-Nose, Iron-Whisker, Coppertooth, Smokeback) maintain distinct camps and patrol distinct sectors, and Pack-internal politics are the principal social structure rather than clan or guild structure. Third, the Deeps are *the working interface between Cogsworth's surface and the deeper Underdark* — the Compact-licensed escort traffic that passes through the Deeps to the Underdark Crossing makes the Deeps a passage as well as a settlement, and the Pack's escort licence (held currently by **Krzlik of the Whisper-Tongue** of the Long-Nose Pack) is the working political instrument of the Pack-Compact relationship.

The plate is rendered as a hybrid plan-and-section view because the Deeps' chambers are not arranged on a single horizontal plane. Tier-position is shown by vertical stratification: the Pack tier's two camps (Long-Nose and Iron-Whisker) are rendered at the upper section, the Inner tier's two camps (Coppertooth and Smokeback) at the middle, the Deep tier's chambers (Pack Council, Quartzfall, Glow-Vein) at the lower-middle, and the Probe tier (Old Dominion Trace and Expansion Tunnels) at the bottom. Lateral position approximates the directional layout the Pack uses in working speech (Long-Nose to the west, Iron-Whisker to the east, Coppertooth to the south-west, Smokeback to the south-east, Probe tier extending east toward the inferred Quiet Dig).

The visual character of the Deeps is the underground palette: the warm dark-brown of stone walls under lanternlight; the cool blue-green of Glow-Vein bioluminescence; the deeper black of unlit passages; the wet glint of Quartzfall's water at the lantern's edge; the smoke-grey of the Pack Council Chamber's small fires; the pale gleam of quartz veins in the deeper rock; the iron-and-brass of Pack tools and Compact lanterns. Lanternlight is warm and unsteady; Glow-Vein light is cool and steady; the difference is what the Pack uses to know which territory they stand in without looking.

The chief artist's hand is asked to render the Pack as a community of working scale. Kobolds are smaller than Humans (approximately three-quarters Halfling height); doorways, ceilings, tools, and chairs are scaled accordingly. A Human standing in a Pack camp must duck or sit. Visitors who do not duck are not given a second invitation. The plate notes the scaling. The artist's renderings should reflect Kobold-scale architecture throughout.

## Marginalia

### Visual Character — for the Chief Artist

The Kobold Deeps' palette is layered light against deep dark. The principal tones:

- **Stone-warm under lanternlight** — the dominant tone in the Pack and Inner tier camps. Warm browns, ochres, and burnt-amber. Lanternlight is unsteady, casts moving shadows, and gives the Pack's working chambers a domestic, almost hearth-like character against the deep-rock cold.
- **Glow-Vein cool blue-green** — the dominant tone of the Glow-Vein chamber and a secondary tone in passages near it. The cool blue-green is steady, even, and has none of lanternlight's flicker. The Smokeback Pack's Den is partially Glow-Vein-lit and shows the working duality — warm lanterns at the sleeping niches, cool fungal light at the chamber's ceiling.
- **Quartzfall reflective whites and clears** — the Quartzfall chamber's quartz crystals catch both lanternlight and Glow-Vein light, refracting them across the chamber's walls and ceiling in a working dance the Pack considers a sacred sight; the Crystal Cave beyond the Pool concentrates this effect.
- **Old Dominion Trace stone-grey** — the dominant tone of the Probe tier's principal artery. Smoothed Dominion masonry, even and cold under lantern stations, with the only colour variation in the iron-and-brass of the Pack's lantern-fixtures.
- **Council Fire smoke-grey-with-orange** — the Pack Council Chamber's principal colour during session; the imported Cogsworth wood burns warmer than any other fuel in the Deeps and the firelight is the chamber's signature visual; off-session the chamber is lantern-lit and cooler in tone.

Signature visual elements — for the artist's notebook:

- **The Junction Lantern** — a single iron-and-glass lantern at the licensing post, set in a stone alcove under a carved relief of the Compact's wheel-mark on one side and the Long-Nose Pack's banner-mark on the other; the lantern has been continuously lit since the licensing post was established in Year 638 CR (relit through every shift change without extinguishment, by standing custom); its flame is the working symbol of the Pack-Compact accord.
- **The four Pack-Stones** — distinctive mineral-types corresponding to each Pack: Long-Nose's polished black-quartz, Iron-Whisker's iron-streaked stone, Coppertooth's polished copper-and-malachite, Smokeback's black-quartz-with-Glow-Vein-blue-green-calcite-veining. Each stone is approximately fist-sized (Kobold-fist), set on a low pedestal at the Den's entrance, polished by the chiefs' touch over generations. The stones travel to the Pack Council Chamber for formal sessions and return to their camps after.
- **The Pack Council Chamber's high vault** — the largest single architectural feature in the Deeps; an oval Dominion-cut hall sixty feet at the apex, lit by eight lantern stations around the chamber wall and (in session) the central council fire. The chamber is *quiet* by design — neutral ground; sound carries; the chamber's working acoustic is part of the council's protocol.
- **Quartzfall** — a working underground stream cuts through quartz-bearing rock and falls a short drop into a clear pool; the sound of falling water is the Deeps' most distinctive ambient sound and is audible from the Council Chamber's western passage. Quartz crystals line the stream bed and the pool's edges.
- **The Glow-Vein** — a domed chamber forty feet across with bioluminescent fungus colonising the entire ceiling and partial walls. The fungus's blue-green light is the closest thing to natural daylight in the Deeps. The Smokeback Pack's working tending — pruning and seeding on a regular schedule — is visible as small scaffolding-and-pole structures positioned against the walls.
- **The Old Dominion Trace** — a long straight Dominion-cut artery; smoothed grey-stone masonry, lantern stations every fifty paces, the Probe-team's working tools visible at the active face; the Trace's unblinking straightness is its signature against the Deeps' otherwise organic-curved cave passages.

Costume notes: Kobolds wear hide-and-fibre working dress — short tunics belted at the waist, hide-bound feet, hide-bound forearms. Pack identity is shown by sash-colour: Long-Nose black, Iron-Whisker rust-red, Coppertooth copper-green, Smokeback dark blue-green. Rank within a Pack is shown by sash-knot pattern (chiefs single-knot, lieutenants double, members triple). Shamans wear an additional sash crossways of bleached hide; the chief wears their Pack-Stone's mineral as a fist-sized pendant on a hide thong. Tools are belt-hung. Lanterns are Pack-issue iron-and-glass at four sizes (chief-size, member-size, pup-size, working-station-size).

### The Pack Council and Its Workings

For the artist working on tutorial flow and ceremonial scenes, the **Pack Council** is the Deeps' principal political body. It is not a permanent institution; it is a quarterly working session of the four Pack chiefs in the Pack Council Chamber.

**Convocation**: First Restday of every season (four times yearly).
**Procedure**: Each Pack's chief enters the chamber from their camp's approach passage carrying their Pack-Stone. The four stones are placed on stone pedestals at the four compass-positions. The council fire is lit at the chamber's centre by the senior shaman in attendance (Pessrik holds the senior position currently). The chiefs speak in turn, beginning with the Pack of the longest-serving current chief (Vrissil — sixty-one years a Coppertooth, twenty-eight as chief). The agenda is set by the chiefs in succession; any chief may raise a matter. Resolution requires three of four agreement; matters that cannot be resolved are deferred and the Pack-Stones are returned to their camps with the matter pending until next council.

**Standing matters typically before council**:
- The Compact escort licence's terms (renewed by Krzlik's working renegotiation between sessions; reviewed at council)
- The Glow-Vein tending priority (the historical Long-Nose / Smokeback dispute; currently at working accord)
- The Iron-Whisker's iron-trade volume (a quarterly report Mokra gives without comment)
- The Coppertooth carriage-trade (Phossit's working capacity is the limiting factor; council reviews the apprenticeship pipeline)
- The Probe-tier's working pace (Trissel's reports come up at council; the Long-Nose chief alone speaks to it; the other chiefs do not vote on Probe matters by standing custom — the Probe is Long-Nose-only ground)
- Cross-Pack pup-fostering (Veshrik's pupil count and pipeline)

**Tutorial-relevance**: A new Kobold who reaches sufficient working standing in their Pack will be invited to attend a council session as an observer (sitting at one of the eight lantern stations around the chamber wall, not at the central fire). This is the Pack's tutorial culmination — the moment at which the new Kobold is recognised as a Pack member with potential for council-attending standing. The first-attendance ceremony involves the new Kobold's sash being marked with a single small Pack-Stone-mineral chip (a black-quartz chip for Long-Nose, an iron chip for Iron-Whisker, etc.), set into the sash by the Pack's chief during the chamber's silent moments before the council formally convenes.

### The Compact-Pack Working Relationship

For the artist's broader visualisation, the Compact-Pack relationship is the Deeps' principal external interface and is rendered in the plate as the **Junction**. The relationship is treaty-governed (the working accord of Year 638 CR, renewed every fifty years by working renegotiation; the next renewal is due Year 888 CR) and works in practice through three channels:

1. **The escort licence** — held by Krzlik since Year 842 CR; permits Compact-licensed traffic to pass through the Pack tier and Inner tier of the Deeps under Pack escort. Traffic is logged at the Junction Office. The escort licence is not transferable; it is held by a single Pack member at any time and the holder is the Pack's working ambassador to the Compact at the Junction.
2. **The iron trade** — Iron-Whisker forging supplies finished iron to Cogsworth's working trades (the Wireworks, the Bell Foundry, the Smelteries) under quarterly contracts; the contracts are Compact-administered and Mokra of the Iron-Bloom is the Pack's standing signatory.
3. **The carriage trade** — Coppertooth carriage-and-cart construction supplies the Cogsworth Pneumatic Switchyard, the Lower Markets, and the Foundry District under standing arrangements; Phossit of the Six-Strands designs and Coppertooth Pack labour builds; transit between the Deeps and Cogsworth's surface uses the Compact-escorted Pack-tier traffic the licence permits.

Three channels, three Pack chiefs principally involved, the full set of the Deeps' working economy with the wider world. The fourth Pack — Smokeback — is the principal *internal* working tradition (the Glow-Vein), with the smallest external trade footprint, and is accordingly the most insulated of the four from the Compact-Pack relationship's daily working.

### Tutorial Geography — Quest Hooks

For the artist preparing visualisations of the tutorial flow at the Kobold Deeps:

**A new Kobold begins life in their Pack's Den.** Most new Kobolds in the Deeps are pups of the Pack — children rather than newcomers. The tutorial accordingly differs from the other three starter zones in that the Pack's standing pup-curriculum is the working tutorial; new Kobolds enter the formal world of the Deeps at the conclusion of their pup-curriculum (around Kobold age fourteen — equivalent to a Halfling early teen) rather than at first arrival.

**The pup-curriculum** runs from age six to age fourteen and is taught at the Long-Nose Pup Cave (for Long-Nose pups), the equivalent caves at the other three camps, and at the cross-Pack Pup Trainer Cave (for inter-Pack pups). The curriculum covers:
- **Pack-language** (the working trade-tongue between the four Packs)
- **Glow-Vein-tending basics** (every Pack member must know not to disturb the fungus)
- **Lantern-craft** (every Pack member maintains their own lantern; lantern-craft is a working skill)
- **Junction-protocol** (every Pack member must understand the Compact accord's working structure even if they will never serve at the Junction)
- **Pack-history** (the four Pack-Stones, the Pack's foundation in Year 559 CR, the Compact accord of Year 638 CR, the four Packs' splits)
- **Working trade specialisation** (chosen by the pup at age twelve from the Pack's available trades — for Long-Nose: small-tools, escort-warrior, Probe-team apprentice; for Iron-Whisker: forge-trade, iron-trade-courier; for Coppertooth: carriage-trade, copper-extraction; for Smokeback: Glow-Vein-tending, water-keeping)

**The first-recognition ceremony** is the tutorial culmination. Pups who complete the curriculum to the Pup-Tender's satisfaction are presented at the next Pack Council session (this is one of the Council's standing functions) and receive their Pack-sash with the chief's chip-and-knot working at the silent moments before the formal session convenes. The new Pack member is then a recognised apprentice in their chosen trade.

**For non-Kobold tutorial-aged characters arriving at the Deeps as visitors** — through the Junction with Compact licence, or by some other path — the working tutorial is different. They are interviewed by Krzlik at the Junction Office, given an escorted tour of the Pack tier (one chief's Den, the Junction Office's working areas, the Long-Nose Watchpost), and offered limited working assignments depending on their reasons for visiting: trade-related work at the Iron-Whisker Forge or the Coppertooth Workshop, escort-relevant work patrolling the Inner-tier passages, Glow-Vein-tending apprenticeship in the Smokeback Camp's working programmes (the Smokeback Pack will accept non-Kobold apprentices for limited terms — Eshreth Mushroom-Speaker has had two Halfling apprentices and one Wood Elf apprentice in her tenure).

**The Probe tier is restricted.** Tutorial-aged characters do not visit the Probe tier under standing custom. The restriction is a Pack-internal matter and is the principal Pack-internal political question of the Deeps; the Long-Nose chief's decisions about Probe-tier access are the most-watched political signals in the Deeps and the question of whether and when to admit non-Long-Nose Pack members (let alone non-Kobold visitors) to the Expansion's working face is an ongoing standing matter in council.

### What This Plate Does Not Resolve

- **The Iron-Whisker Pack-Stone's foundation inscription.** Mokra has not allowed the Hall to read the Stone directly. The Hall's working position is that the inscription is the Pack's foundation date (Year 559 CR), but the source for that date is Pack tradition rather than Hall reading. The Stone may carry additional inscription detail. The Hall will not press.
- **The Probe tier's working face position.** The face advances at three feet per working day; the position renderable on this plate is current to the date of Cellis Vereth's Year 845 CR visit, when the face was approximately three-quarters of a league east of the Pack Council Chamber. The current face position (Stillmonth 847 CR) is approximately one league east — the plate's rendered position is therefore behind the current face by approximately a quarter-league. The Walking Name Order's inferred terminus distance from the current face is not on Compact record.
- **Whether the Long-Nose Pack knows the Expansion's terminus.** The standing position is that the Pack does not know — that the Expansion follows the Old Dominion Trace's natural extension and the Pack does not have a destination, only a working programme. The Walking Name Order's working position is that this is unlikely — that the Pack knows what they are digging toward at some level even if not at chief-level — but the Order has no field evidence to confirm or deny. The Hall files both positions without endorsement.
- **The Probe-tier descent's destination.** The descent at the Probe-tier's eastern face is sealed; the descent's destination is not on Compact record; the Walking Name Order's working position is that this descent is the Pack's reserve route to the Quiet Dig should the Expansion reach it, but again the Order has no field evidence. The Pack's working position on this descent is silence.
- **The Compact accord's renewal terms.** The accord is renewed every fifty years by working renegotiation; the next renewal is Year 888 CR. The renewal terms are not on Compact-published record — they are working terms held in the Pack-Compact working file which is not a Compact-public document. The Hall's working position is that publishing the terms would not improve the relationship and the Hall does not press.
