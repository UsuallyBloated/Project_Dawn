---

## Prefatory Note

This plate is the maritime sheet. It renders Valdis as a coastal outline with the seas, sea-lanes, hazards, ports, lighthouses, the named buoys of the Compact charts, the storm-shelters maintained for licensed traffic, the off-shore Architect sites, and the trans-Bone-Reach approach to the Maren coast. The continent's interior is rendered without detail because the interior is the subject of the regional plates and is not maritime.

The Compact's authority over maritime traffic is documented in the Compact Charter, Volumes III (sea-lanes) and IV (hazard register). The plate reflects the Charter's working state at Stillmonth 847 CR. Seasonal lane availability is shown alongside the lane glyph; lanes that operate only in summer are marked accordingly. The Greymere Summer Shipping Lane is the strictest seasonal — it is closed Frostmonth through Plowsmonth; the Vah'tharan Passage is the next strictest, with practical traffic only Harvestmonth through Stillmonth.

The four offshore Architect sites are shown at their charted positions. The Sunken Ziggurat is rendered offshore on this plate even though it is geographically interior to the Southeastern Marshlands, because it is reached by punt-and-causeway and the Bonewrights treat its relationship to the Marshlands' tidal estuary as a maritime relationship. The Hall has filed the Bonewrights' position without endorsement.

## The Lanes, Connected — Working Schematic

## Marginalia

### The Six Ports Inside Harrowmere — Maritime View

Plate III renders Harrowmere's ports as city districts. This plate renders them as the maritime apparatus they are. The **Compact Docks** is the main commercial port; the **Old Mole** is the oldest dock and now primarily fishing; the **Stranger's Quay** is where unregistered vessels berth pending Compact review; the **Drowned Hold Approach** is the licensed expedition pier; the **Hollowgate quay** is the river-mouth small-vessel harbour and the Fish Market; the **Lighthouse on the Grey Spit** is, as noted on Plate III, technically a port authority body in Compact charter and refuses to be removed from the maritime register.

For licensing purposes, the **Drowned Hold Approach** is the only Harrowmere pier that operates outside the Compact's standard tier system — it is governed by expedition licence rather than tier, and the licence is non-transferable. The Compact has approved twenty-three expedition licences in the past fifty years; three have been used.

### The Cinder Deep — Three Vessels and a Policy

The Compact's policy not to survey below 40 fathoms in the Cinder Deep was adopted Year 798 CR after the **Stove Wreck** brought the Compact's Cinder Deep loss count to three. The earlier two — the *Hollow Wave* (Year 762 CR) and the *Iron Crow* (Year 781 CR) — are charted at approximate positions; their actual positions are not on record because the survey vessels sent to find the wrecks did not return either. The *Iron Crow*'s timbers ran the Cinder Docks beach for two seasons before sinking, which is the source of the Cinder Coves Magicians' Guild's standing collection of *Iron Crow* fragments; the Guild treats the fragments as working material.

The **Stove Wreck** is the only Cinder Deep wreck visible at low tide. It is on the strand south of Cinder Docks and the Magicians' Guild maintains it. The Compact's official position is that the wreck is a charted hazard. The Magicians' Guild's official position is that the wreck is a memorial. Neither position is contested by the other; the wreck is both.

### The Bone Reach — Tidal Timing and the Window Buoy

The Bone Reach is shallow, tidal, and unmaintained by any single faction. The seasonal shallowing makes large-draft passage possible only at a half-watch window during summer high tide; the **Tidal Window Buoy** is the only buoy in the Reach the Compact maintains and is the marker for that window. Outside the window, the wreck concentration in the Reach is the result of vessels that misjudged timing.

The Reach's wrecks are not salvaged. The Compact's standing position — and the Bonewrights', and the Khala Spirit-Walkers' (in their southerly perimeter capacity), and the few captains who run the lane regularly — is that the salvage operation would put salvors in the same position the wrecks were in. The wrecks are charted but are not retrieved.

### The Vah'tharan Passage — Three Round-Trips and a Counter-Current

The Vah'tharan Passage to Maren is the only documented open-ocean route from Valdis. Three Compact captains have completed round-trips: **Ostin Vrell** (Year 801 CR), **Maris Holler** (Year 822 CR), and **Idis Marn** (Year 836 CR — outbound only at filing; the return is not yet logged because Captain Marn turned back due to supply concerns and her words at the turn-back, *"we have not found a corner"*, are still the most-cited single field observation about Maren's extent the Hall has on file).

The seasonal westerly required for the return runs Harvestmonth through Stillmonth. Outbound traffic uses the southern margin of the Passage where the counter-current runs east-bound; return traffic sails north of the counter-current in the westerly. The Passage is deep, warm, and the headwinds are seasonal and severe; the return leg is documented as significantly harder than the outbound by all three captains.

### Why the Drowned Hold Is the Access Gate

The Compact's standing position on Maren approach licensing is that the licence requires Tier 1+ Compact standing **and** completion of the Drowned Hold questline. The harbormaster's office does not explain the Drowned Hold linkage. The Compact's published licence form lists the Drowned Hold completion as a prerequisite without further detail. The Walking Name Order's working theory is that the Drowned Hold's Architect notation references the Maren approach in some operational way that the Order has not yet recovered the full text of; the Order's three Remnant Sea survey licences are aimed in part at finding the cross-reference. The Hall does not endorse the theory but does file it.

### The Remnant Sea — Three Licences and an Objection

The Walking Name Order holds **three active survey licences** for Architect shelf sites in the Remnant Sea. The Kel'varath Elder Council has objected to all three on the ground that the shelf sites are Kel'varath territorial inheritance; the Compact has filed the objection and not enforced it. The licences remain active. The Order's working position is that the shelf sites' Architect provenance precedes any Kel'varath claim and the Order's authority to survey precedes the Council's authority to object; the Council's working position is that this is the same argument the Dominion made and is no more correct now than it was then.

The shelf sites' positions are on Walking Name Order survey charts and are not published. The plate marks three ✧ positions at approximately the right offshore distances; the actual positions are reserved.

### The Bonewrights' Maritime Position

The Bonewrights of the Southeastern Marshlands maintain the **Bonewright Wharves**, the **Cypress Mile** (which runs from the Wharves to the Sunken Ziggurat's surface marker), and the **Drowned Causeway** (the older Dominion-era stonework that the Cypress Mile partially overlies). The Bonewrights treat the Marshlands' tidal estuary as a maritime zone and themselves as a maritime council, and have historically negotiated with the Compact's Harrowmere office on terms equivalent to a port authority.

The Compact's standing position is that the Bonewright Wharves are a recognised port. The Compact does not licence Bonewright vessels for sea-going traffic and does not require Bonewrights to seek Compact licence for their estuary navigation; the Bonewrights' working chart of the Black Channels is held by the Bonewright Council and is not a Compact chart. The Compact has not asked for it.

### Visual Character — for the Chief Artist

Plate VII is the portolan: the one plate where the sea is the subject and the land is an outline. The continent's interior is bare parchment with a single fine coastline; everything that matters is in the water — lane-lines, buoys, lighthouses, wrecks, storm-cells, and the long empty run of the Vah'tharan Passage south to a coast that is mostly conjecture. The palette is parchment, sea-green washes deepening with charted depth, and sepia rhumb lines radiating from the wind rose.

The signature visuals — for the artist's notebook:

- **The lanes as drawn routes** — the painted impression should connect what the broadsheet schedule lists: Harrowmere to Cinder Docks to the Bonewright Wharves to Cape Bonewright to the Bone Reach window, in dashed seasonal line-work (see the lane schematic below).
- **The wreck fields** — the Bone Reach's massed ⚒ glyphs and the Cinder Deep's three named losses; the Stove Wreck alone is drawn visible above water.
- **The named winds** — the wind rose may carry small wind-faces at the quarters; the Khala's winds have personal names and the artist is permitted to give them expressions, provided the expressions are not friendly.
- **The Maren stub** — the second continent enters the plate only as a partial northern coastline that fades to nothing, with Captain Marn's line — *"we have not found a corner"* — lettered where the coast gives out.
- **The empty south** — most of the plate's lower third is open water and should stay that way; the Passage's loneliness is the crossing's character.

### What This Plate Does Not Resolve

- **The Walking Name Order's three Remnant shelf sites at chart detail.** Reserved by the Order.
- **The Drowned Hold's role in Maren licensing.** The Compact does not explain. The Hall does not press.
- **Maren's interior beyond the coastal stub.** Reserved for Plate X if and when issued. Archivist Valex has not been to Maren.
- **The Cinder Deep below 40 fathoms.** Compact policy. The plate marks the depth limit and does not chart below it.
- **The Bone Reach wreck register.** The wrecks are charted; the register is the Compact's hazard register (Charter Vol. IV); the plate renders the wreck concentration as a glyph density and does not list individual entries.
