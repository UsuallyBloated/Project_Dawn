# Cartography Hall — Survey Plates

These plates are higher-detail companions to the continental and underground maps in [cartographers_atlas.md](../cartographers_atlas.md). The Atlas plate remains the canonical single-page rendering for the Repository; these plates are the working sheets of the High Cartography Hall, prepared at the larger format the Hall now permits and signed individually by their surveying authority.

Glyph conventions vary slightly between plates where additional distinctions are useful. The legend block on each plate is authoritative for that plate.

The series is filed in two volumes:

- **Volume I — Regional and topical plates (I–VIII)** — continental survey, four regional regions, the Underdark cross-section, the maritime routes, and the Architect site network.
- **Volume II — Starting-zone plates (IX-A through IX-D)** — the four 1–10 zones at survey detail (≈ ½ league per grid square; ¼ league for the Kobold Deeps), prepared with the chief artist's rendering hand in mind.
