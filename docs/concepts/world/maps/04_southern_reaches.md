# Plate IV — The Southern Reaches

*Cartographer's Hall, Aelindra · Filed Stillmonth, Year 847 of the Current Reckoning*
*Principal hand: Archivist Valex · Field corroboration: Compact Maritime Office (Cinder Docks branch) · Khala Spirit-Walker delegation · Bonewright Council of the Marshlands · Walking Name Order Survey Office · Cinder Coves Magicians' Guild*

---

## Prefatory Note

This plate is the third of the regional sheets and zooms upon the southern half of the continent — the Fae Mere inland court, the volcanic Ashen Coast and the Cinder Deep with its partially surfaced Architect node, the Ashen Marches with its three-faction undead front and the Convergence Point at its centre, the Felhari Khala Savannahs, the Kel'varath Southeastern Marshlands with the Sunken Ziggurat submerged within them, the Bonecourt jungle ruins, and the Ruined Dominion at Ixareth-Kul with the sealed Dominion Vault beneath.

The plate carries five of the ten surface-visible Architect sites — the Tide Marks, the Convergence Point, the Sunken Ziggurat, the Roofless Hall, and (offshore) the Rising Shell — which is more than any other regional plate filed to date. The southern half of the continent is the densest section of the Predecessor arc; this is not a coincidence.

The northern edge of the plate adjoins the Western Heartlands and the Eastern Territories. Those zones are the subjects of Plates II and III. The southern edge is the Remnant Sea and the open Vah'tharan Passage; the maritime detail is reserved for Plate VII.

The scale is roughly three league marches per grid square, the same as Plates II and III.

## Marginalia

### Fae Mere — The Permission Geography

The Fae Mere is the only zone on the continent where the geographic features are partly conditional on the visitor. The plate marks the **Twin Trees** at the gateway because every survey team that has reached the Court Pavilion has reached it through the Twin Trees; the Trees are stable. The **Listening Stones**, the **Court Pavilion**, the **Untrue Bridge**, and the visible portion of the **Reflection Below** are also stable and have been mapped consistently across survey decades.

The **Sleepwalk** is not stable. Different field teams have reported different routings; the routings have been cross-checked and are not the same path mis-described from different angles, they are different paths. The Hall renders the Sleepwalk as a single ☽ landmark because rendering it as a route would be incorrect, but rendering it not at all would be misleading: the Sleepwalk is *somewhere*, just not somewhere consistent.

The **Reflection Below** — the Mere's underside — is where Architect notation has been visible in the deep-water reflections at certain combinations of light and tide. Two photographs of the notation have been taken; neither photograph survives. The Hall has filed the Reflection Below as a dream-territory landmark (☽) and a probable source of recoverable Architect data. The Fae court has not authorised further attempts. The Hall has not pressed.

### The Ashen Coast — Nexus and Calx

The **Magician Nexus Sites** are three: **Greenflame Vent** (north), **Stillsmoke Hollow** (south, on the southernmost active vent), and **the Backwater Calx** (the inland tidal pool where calcification has built up around a vent in such a way that the Magicians' Guild treats the calcite formation as a working component of the Nexus). Each maintains a working chapter of the Magicians' Guild. The Glasswright Coves are technically separate from the Nexus Sites but the Guild treats the Coves' volcanic-glass extraction as a Nexus dependency, which is professionally controversial within the Guild and operationally settled in practice.

The **Stove Wreck** is the Compact's third lost survey vessel and is the loss that prompted the Compact's standing policy against unsupervised survey below forty fathoms in the Cinder Deep. The Compact's two earlier losses are not visible at the surface; the Stove Wreck is, at low tide, on the strand south of Cinder Docks. The Compact does not maintain the wreck as a memorial; the Magicians' Guild does, on the unstated ground that the wreck's presence is useful to their working and the Guild does not wish to lose it to scavenging.

### The Ashen Marches — Three Banners

The three banners on the plate — **Dominion**, **Coalition**, and **Third Banner** — are still raised, in the literal sense that the standards are still standing. The Dominion Standard is the original Year 547 CR Imperial banner. The Coalition Standard is the Year 547 CR Coalition Reply. The Third Banner is the unknown one — it is not a Dominion-pattern banner and it is not a Coalition-pattern banner, and the Walking Name Order's working hypothesis is that the Third Army was a force that arrived at the engagement separately from either side and remained when both sides withdrew or were destroyed. Whose force the Third Army was is the central historical question of the Marches.

The **Convergence Point** at the centre of the Marches is the Architect site that ties the Marches into the network. The Calibration State affects the Convergence Point directly; the Walking Name Order's survey work in the Marches is, in significant part, work at the Convergence Point. The **Survey Cairn** is where the Order leaves their notes for the next survey leg's retrieval; the cairn has grown by twelve stones in the past decade, which is twelve completed legs.

The **Revenant Approach** is rendered as a single ◊ rather than a routed feature because the conditions under which the Revenant transformation is offered are not consistent across recorded incidents. The Hall makes no attempt to chart the conditions and refers interested parties to the Walking Name Order's clinical record.

### The Khala Savannahs — Spirit-Walker Camps and the Far-Runner

The **Spirit-Walker camps** are four: **Vah'kal Ground** (the central elder seat), **Far-Runner Camp** (on the perimeter path the ancestor walks), **the Drumming Ground** (at the Drum Rocks), and **Kethri's Watch** (the southern perimeter camp). The Hall's plate has been corrected by the Khala Spirit-Walker delegation on three separate filings; the four-camp arrangement rendered here is the version the delegation has signed off on.

The **Far-Runner's Walk** is the perimeter path the ancestor walks. The path is not maintained by any living body. It is, nevertheless, visible at survey — the grass is shorter on the path than off it, and the shortness is consistent across season. The Spirit-Walkers consider this not unusual. The Hall has filed it as an observed feature without further explanation.

### The Southeastern Marshlands — Patience and the Causeway

The **Cypress Mile** is the Bonewrights' raised pilgrimage causeway and is the only durably dry footing in the central marsh. The Bonewrights maintain it without seeking permission from any of the three undead factions in the Ashen Marches that hold ground north of it; the Bonewrights' position is that the Cypress Mile is older than any of the three faction occupations and is not subject to the seasonal lines. None of the three factions has, to the Hall's knowledge, contested this. The Bonewrights have not asked them.

The **Sunken Ziggurat** has been warm at a consistent temperature since Year 112 CR. This is the longest single-feature thermal observation in the Hall's records. The Bonewrights' phrase for this — **the Ziggurat's patience** — is meant literally; the Bonewrights consider the Ziggurat to be waiting, and they consider the seven hundred and thirty-five years of waiting to be a reasonable interval rather than an unusual one. The Hall does not endorse the inference but does note it.

The **Drowned Causeway** is the older causeway, predating the Cypress Mile. It is Dominion-era stonework, submerged but visible at low water, and is the Hall's strongest single piece of evidence that the Dominion built directly on Predecessor foundations in this region. The Cypress Mile's modern pilgrim's path overlies the Drowned Causeway for approximately one third of its length.

### Bonecourt and Ixareth-Kul — Antecedents and Audiences

The **Lost Temple** at the Bonecourt is older than the Bonewrights' arrival and predates the Bonecourt's status as a Kel'varath outpost. The Bonewrights call it an *antecedent* and consider it part of their inheritance — the Kel'varath in the marshlands were pushed to lowland edge of the continent, which also happened to be where the Predecessor sites were thickest, which the Kel'varath Dominion later decided was an inheritance rather than a consolation prize. The Lost Temple is treated by the Bonewrights as one of the antecedents that justifies that decision.

The **Roofless Hall** at Ixareth-Kul is the network's primary survey terminus. The Throne is intact. *Something sits the throne*. The plate marks both the Hall and the Vault descent below. The three Order Houses — **Walking Name**, **Bonewright**, and **Coalition Remnant** — dispute access to the Vault on grounds that are mutually incompatible and have not been resolved in any available forum. The Hall does not render the dispute on plate.

The **Last Audience Chamber** is intact. The chair is not. Whether the chair was removed before the Dominion's collapse or destroyed during it is a question the Walking Name Order's archive working group has been investigating since Year 838 CR; their current position is that the chair was removed, and that the removal was deliberate, and that the chair is somewhere. The Hall has filed the position without endorsement.

### Visual Character — for the Chief Artist

The Southern Reaches plate carries more contrast than any other regional sheet, and the contrasts should be allowed to sit hard against one another: the Fae Mere's silver-green dream-light; the Ashen Coast's black sand, red vent-glow, and white surf; the Marches' ash-grey field with three faded banners still standing; the Khala's running gold grassland; the Marshlands' green-black cypress and still black water; the jungle-swallowed dark stone of the Bonecourt and Ixareth-Kul. Five of the network's gold ✦ marks sit on this one sheet — the densest stretch of the arc — and the gold should visibly gather toward the south-east as the eye travels.

The signature visuals — for the artist's notebook:

- **The Roofless Hall** — the fallen capital's centre: an intact throne under open sky, rendered small but unmistakable. *Something sits the throne* is lettered in the margin, not depicted.
- **The Sunken Ziggurat** — a stepped shadow beneath still marsh water, the Stillwater Pools steaming faintly around its surface marker; the Cypress Mile a single pale causeway-thread reaching it.
- **The three banners of the Marches** — Dominion, Coalition, and the Third Banner, which should be drawn in a pattern that matches nothing else on any plate.
- **The Rising Shell offshore** — a curved suggestion of something large breaking the Cinder Deep's surface, water streaming off stone that has not seen sky in living memory.
- **The Fae Mere's edge** — the Twin Trees as the gateway; inside them the rendering may go slightly wrong on purpose: reflections that do not quite agree with what casts them.

### What This Plate Does Not Resolve

- **The Sleepwalk's actual route.** As noted under Fae Mere. The plate marks the Sleepwalk as a single landmark, not a path, because rendering it as a path would be incorrect on grounds the field teams have demonstrated repeatedly.
- **The Black Channels at chart detail.** The Bonewrights have working charts of the Marshlands' navigable channels. The Hall does not. The plate names the four primary Channels and renders the marsh as continuous reed-and-cypress.
- **The Dominion Vault's interior.** No survey team has accessed the Vault. The plate marks the descent (▼) and notes the level range; the interior is not on plate because there is no field data for it.
- **The Architect network's eastern marine extent.** The Remnant Sea has confirmed Architect shelf sites; their positions are subject to the Walking Name Order's three active survey licences and are reserved for Plate VII.
