# Appendix — The Hall's Table of Leagues

*Cartographer's Hall, Aelindra · Filed Stillmonth, Year 847 of the Current Reckoning*
*Principal hand: Archivist Valex · Compiled from the waystone engravings, the Compact lane logs, the Halfway Hostel's ledger, and the field teams' march records across forty-one seasons*

---

## Prefatory Note

The plates carry the shapes; this table carries the distances. League-figures are road-leagues (the distance walked, not the distance a crow ignores), rounded to the nearest league the Hall can defend. Travel times assume the working paces below, fair weather, and a traveller who stops at dusk. The Hall reminds the reader that every figure in this table has been wrong for somebody.

**Working paces:**

| Mode | Pace | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| On foot, laden | ~10 leagues/day | The field teams' standard march |
| Wagon, kept road | ~8 leagues/day | Slower than walking; carries more than walking |
| Mounted | ~18 leagues/day | Where mounts are available at all; see the note below |
| River barge, downstream | ~24 leagues/day | The Greymere river route's working pace |
| River barge, upstream | ~14 leagues/day | Poled and hauled; storm-season excepted |
| Punt, marsh channels | ~6 leagues/day | Bonewright waters; dry season |

## Marginalia

### On the Mounted Column

The mounted pace is listed and the Hall acknowledges it is the table's least useful column at filing: stabled mounts are available at Greengate (Compact courier stock), Greyveil, Harrowmere, and Auroch's Rest, and almost nowhere else. The Minotaur do not ride and find the question funny. The figure is filed against the day the column is worth more.

### On the Table's Errors

The waystone engravings, the Hostel ledger, and the Compact logs disagree with one another by a league or two on most legs. The Hall has chosen the figure with the best provenance in each case and notes that a traveller who plans to the league deserves the surprise they will get.

### Working Notes (for the designers' bench)

Out-of-world: these figures give the world a consistent travel-time fabric for quest design and for the mount system when it lands. Anchors to preserve: *foot ≈ 10 leagues/day*, *Greenshire→Greyveil = 3 days*, *Greyveil⇋Harrowmere river = 2½ down / 4½ up*, *Bonecourt→Ixareth-Kul = 3 days* (already canon on Plate IX-C). Spirit of Wolf / mount speed multipliers can be expressed directly against the pace table (e.g. SoW ≈ foot ×1.3; mounts ≈ foot ×1.8), which keeps buff stacking arguments anchored to fiction rather than to raw multipliers.
