# The Hall's Drafting Standards — for the Chief Artist's Hand

*Cartographer's Hall, Aelindra · Filed Stillmonth, Year 847 of the Current Reckoning*
*Principal hand: Archivist Valex · Prepared at the request of the Repository for the painted impressions of the second-edition Atlas's broadsheet annex*

---

## Prefatory Note

The twelve plates of the annex were drawn by several field hands over forty-one survey seasons and harmonised by one transcribing hand. The painted impressions will be made by yet another hand, and the Repository has asked the Hall to state, in one sheet, the conventions that make twelve plates read as one atlas. This is that sheet.

Where a plate's own legend or marginalia conflicts with this sheet, the plate is authoritative for that plate. This sheet governs everything the plates do not say: the border, the rose, the seal, the lettering, and the translation of the broadsheet's working glyphs into the painter's icons.

---

## The Sheet Itself

- **The ground** is aged broadsheet parchment — warm ivory, darkening slightly toward the edges, with the working life of the sheet visible: a fold-shadow is permitted; a stain is not.
- **The border** is a double rule in iron-gall sepia: a heavy outer line, a fine inner line, and between them the plate's running title in letterspaced capitals along the top edge and the Hall's filing line along the bottom.
- **The aspect** is broadsheet landscape for Volume I (Plates I–VIII) and Volume II's zone sheets (IX-A through IX-D). The vignette renderings (the IX-series `.i` volumes) are portrait or square at the artist's judgement — they are working sketches, not plates.
- **Inks and washes:** sepia and iron-gall for all line work; muted regional washes for terrain (each plate's *Visual Character* section names its palette). Saturation is rationed — the plates should look surveyed, not illuminated.
- **Gold is reserved.** Gold leaf appears on exactly one class of mark: the Architect sites. Nothing else on any plate is gold. A reader flipping the bound annex should be able to follow the Predecessor Arc by the gold alone.

---

## The Six-Fingered Rose

The Hall's compass rose for the painted impressions is the **six-fingered rose**: a compass star whose pointers are drawn as six slender fingers radiating from an open palm at the hub, the north finger longest. The device quotes the six-fingered handprint at the Greyfen's North Outcrop — the only place on the surface of Valdis where an Architect's hand is recorded at body scale — and the Hall adopted it in this edition with the reasoning that a cartographer's rose should point toward what the cartographer is actually looking for.

- Drawn in sepia line with a single gold point at the north finger's tip.
- Placed in open water or open ground; never over terrain detail.
- Plates VI and VIII carry **no rose** (the Underdark does not keep a north; the network diagram is not geography). Plate VI carries the depth gauge in its place; Plate VII carries the wind rose (below).

### The Wind Rose (Plate VII only)

The maritime plate's rose carries the four quarter-winds as small wind-faces in the old chart convention. The Compact borrows the quarter-winds' names from the Khala's named winds; the artist is permitted to give the faces expressions, provided the expressions are not friendly. The seasonal westerly that brings ships home from Maren is not depicted — it answers to no name the Hall has on record, and an empty cartouche where its face would go is the correct rendering.

### Terrain conventions

- **Water:** horizontal flowline with depth-graded wash; storm-cells (※) as small spiral marks; currents as fine arrowed threads.
- **Mountains:** pictorial hachured ridges in the old style, lit from the north-west.
- **Forest:** massed crowns in two greens; canopy villages (♠) as small platforms within the mass, visible only on close reading — which is accurate.
- **Marsh / fen:** reed-tick clusters over a flat wash; black-water channels in lacquer-dark line.
- **Volcanic ground:** black stipple with vent-plumes; the Cinder Deep's uncharted depths fade to bare parchment below the forty-fathom line, per Compact policy.
- **Blight:** a grey-violet wash with a **hard western edge and a feathered interior** — the advance is sharp, the rot is gradual.
- **The uncharted:** bare parchment with the Hall's note in the Archivist's hand. Blank parchment is a feature. The painter who fills it has misread the assignment.

---

## Lettering

- **Zone names:** letterspaced capitals, sized by zone extent.
- **Landmark names:** the Archivist's italic — a clear, slightly forward-leaning book-hand.
- **Marginal quotes:** each plate's marginalia nominates lines worth lettering into the border (the commission briefs collect them). These are set in the Archivist's italic with a fine rule above and below, as if filed. The quotes carry what the paint must not; do not illustrate them.
- **The filing line:** every plate closes with *Filed under the seal of the High Cartography Hall — Archivist Valex, Hall of Records, Aelindra · Stillmonth, Year 847 CR* along the bottom border.

---

## The Hall's Seal

The seal is a **quartered circle**: an open book (the Record), a surveyor's chain (the Measure), a tree (Aelindra), and an empty quarter (the Unsurveyed — kept empty by charter since the Hall's founding; the empty quarter is the Hall's standing admission and the only part of the seal members are sworn to defend). Impressed in dull red wax at the lower right of every plate, overlapping the border by design — the seal is allowed to break the frame; nothing else is.

---

## The Two Voices

Everything above governs **Hall plates**. Documents reproduced from other authorities are rendered in their own conventions, deliberately distinct, so a reader knows at sight whose hand they are reading:

- **Compact charts** (lane schedules, hazard registers): ruled tables, clerk's round-hand, no terrain art, the Compact's wheel-mark.
- **Bonewright documents** (the Black Channels chart, if ever shared): cypress-bark ground, the ritual cadence's tall script.
- **Breach Warden operational sheets:** field-pencil over printed grid; positions as pinned overlays.
- **The Maren sketch** (the Atlas's second-continent section): rendered *rougher on purpose* — a second-hand chart from logs and one letter; partial coastline, blank interior, Captain Marn's line lettered where the coast gives out. Source reliability must be visible as style.
- **The Drathis Vehn appendix pages** (the two pages the Hall cannot interpret): reproduced as-is, in the Seeker's travelling hand, with the Hall's marginal note *filed, not incorporated*. The Hall does not redraw what it does not understand.
