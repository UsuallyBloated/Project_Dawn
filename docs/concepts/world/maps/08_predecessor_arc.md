# Plate VIII — The Predecessor Arc · Architect Site Network

*Cartographer's Hall, Aelindra · Filed Stillmonth, Year 847 of the Current Reckoning*
*Principal hand: Archivist Valex · Field corroboration: Walking Name Order Survey Office (network file) · Aelindra Sealed Archive (records access reserved) · Dreaming Warden's reception clerk · Kethara Hold Astronomical Observatory (Year 407 CR Calibration State logs) · Compact Drowned Hold Expedition records*

---

## Prefatory Note

This plate is the closing sheet of the regional series and the only plate that does not render geography directly. It renders the network. The Architects built eleven confirmed sites and at least one speculative site; the sites are connected as a working system; the system has roles, signal flows, and a trigger condition that the network has been waiting on for four hundred and forty years. This plate is the Hall's working diagram of the network as currently understood.

Three views are filed here. **Plate VIII A** renders the geographic arc — the positions of all twelve sites overlaid on a simplified continental outline. **Plate VIII B** renders the network topology — the nodes and the cross-references between them, drawn as a graph rather than a map. **Plate VIII C** renders the Calibration State flow and the Survey Chain — the sequence of operations the network performs when a complete survey is delivered, and the dependency order that opens the Dominion Vault.

The plate is a synthesis of field data from the Walking Name Order's network file, the Compact's Drowned Hold expedition records, the Kethara Hold Astronomical Observatory's Year 407 CR Calibration State log, the Dreaming Warden's reception correspondence, and the Aelindra Sealed Archive's restricted records (which the Hall has access to in summary only). Where the field data are partial, the plate marks the partiality and does not extrapolate.

The Hall will revise this plate as new survey returns. The plate is, by its nature, the most likely of the eight to need re-issue.

## Marginalia

### What Is on This Plate That Is Not on Plate I

Plate I marks the Architect sites at their positions and lists their roles in margin notes. This plate goes further:

1. **Network-level roles.** Plate I notes that Sunken Ziggurat is "deep archive" and Roofless Hall is "primary survey terminus" but does not relate them to each other. Plate VIII renders the network as a graph, with the cross-references between nodes drawn as edges. The Convergence Point's role as "the only node that names every other" is on Plate VIII; it is a margin note on Plate I.
2. **The Calibration State sequence.** Plate I notes that the State is the network's self-check. Plate VIII renders the five-phase sequence (trigger · remainders intensify · Warden transmits · terminus processes · conclusion rendered) and the concurrent astronomical anomaly observations.
3. **The Survey Chain.** Plate I lists the sites; Plate VIII orders them by access dependency and identifies the Drowned Hold as the transition node and the Roofless Hall–Dominion Vault as the terminal sequence.
4. **The Deep Rooms' status.** Plate I marks the Deep Rooms as ✧ probable. Plate VIII commits to "referenced in Sunken Ziggurat notation only · no surface entry identified" and notes the cross-reference is the *only* basis for the site's existence on plate.

### The Holds Are Not a Node

The Astronomical Observatory at Kethara Hold filed Year 407 CR Calibration State logs that the Hall cross-references against the Aelindra "sky was wrong" event. The Holds are *correlated* — they observed the State concurrently — but they are not a node. There is no Architect site at the Holds at survey. The Hall's working position is that the Holds are an observation post that happens to have detected the network's most recent firing, and that the Holds' value to the network (if any) is observational rather than operational.

The Holds' position on the Holds-as-observatory question is unstated. The Hall has not pressed.

### The Convergence Point's Role

The Convergence Point is the only confirmed node whose notation cross-references *every* other confirmed node. The Hall's working position is that the Convergence Point is the network's status board — the surface marker at which an outside observer can read the network's current state without having to visit each node individually. The Walking Name Order's working position is similar but with the additional claim that the Convergence Point's notation, fully decoded, would constitute the Architect chain's survey form — the document the surveyor delivers to the terminus to trigger the State.

The Convergence Point's notation has not been fully decoded. The Walking Name Order has been working on it for forty-one years.

### The Dominion Vault's Conclusion

The Dominion Vault contains the conclusion of the network's purpose. The conclusion's content is not specified in any accessible notation. The Hall has stated this elsewhere and states it again here because the question gets asked.

The conclusion is not, the Hall takes pains to clarify, *the Architects' wish*. The Architects designed a system that returns a conclusion derived from the survey data — what the Architects left, what state it is in, whether it has achieved what it was designed to achieve. The conclusion is the system's reading of itself, presented to the surveyor who triggered the reading. The Architects did not editorialise. They designed an instrument and let the instrument's readings speak.

What the readings will say is unknown. The Hall files no prediction.

### Why This Plate Will Be Re-Issued

The Hall expects this plate to require re-issue more often than the other seven combined. The reasons:

1. **The Calibration State could fire** during the plate's working life. If it does, the plate becomes a snapshot of the pre-State condition; the post-State condition is a different document.
2. **The Quiet Dig's seal could break.** If the Third Digger's construction yields, the survey of the Dig becomes possible and the Hall can survey the Notation Wall fully. The cross-reference graph would expand.
3. **The Deep Rooms could be located.** If a surface entry is identified, the speculative ✧ becomes a confirmed ✦ and the network has twelve confirmed nodes rather than eleven.
4. **Vel'Sharath could authorise survey access.** This has not happened in a hundred years and the Hall does not expect it. If it does, the Underdark plate (VI) and this plate both change.
5. **The Maren survey could return Architect data.** The Walking Name Order's three Remnant Sea licences and the Compact's Maren-approach licensing both suggest the network may extend across the Vah'tharan Passage. If it does, this plate's geographic arc extends.

The Hall accepts the re-issue burden as a courtesy of the work.

### What This Plate Does Not Resolve

- **The Architects' identity, fate, or motive.** No data. The Hall files the absence.
- **The conclusion's content.** No data. The Hall files the absence and waits.
- **The trigger condition's exact specification.** Partial decoding only. The trigger is "complete survey," but what the network counts as "complete" — whether all eleven confirmed nodes plus the speculative twelfth, or only the eleven, or some subset that excludes (say) the Foundation Ring on grounds of inaccessibility — is not on plate. The Hall plots the eleven and the one and lets the network decide.
- **The next firing's date.** The interval is curiosity-based, not time-based. The Architects designed the network to fire when its successors completed the surveys, not at any given calendar interval. The next firing is therefore not a date the Hall can plot. It is an event the Hall plots when it has happened.
