*Cartographer's Hall, Aelindra · Filed Stillmonth, Year 847 of the Current Reckoning*
*Principal hand: Archivist Valex · Field corroboration: Compact Underdark Office (Cogsworth branch) · Long-Nose Pack negotiator (Compact escort) · Grey Market arbitration panel · Walking Name Order (Quiet Dig file) · Dreaming Warden's reception clerk*

## Prefatory Note

This plate is the fifth of the regional sheets and renders the Underdark — the underground network connecting the surface entries at Cogsworth, the Dwarven Holds, Aelindra root, and Ixareth-Kul into a single continuous sub-surface system. The plate is drawn as a vertical cross-section because the surface plate's plan view does not render the network's depth stratification usefully; the Underdark's defining feature is its *layering*, and a plan view loses that information.

Depth is the vertical axis. Horizontal extent is the lateral axis and is approximate; the Underdark spans most of the continent's width by direct passage, but the network is not a straight line, and the lateral positions on this plate are working approximations rather than measured offsets. Where a passage's actual horizontal extent is documented (the Dreaming Grove descent from Aelindra, the Dominion Vault descent from Ixareth-Kul) the plate respects the documented direction; where the passage's extent is inferred only (the Kobold Expansion Tunnels' direction toward the Quiet Dig) the plate marks the inference as inferred.

The southern continuation — *what is below Vel'Sharath* — is rendered at the bottom of the plate as the **Lit Space** based on Grix of the Long-Nose Pack's Year 844 CR statement to the Grey Market arbitration panel. That space is uncharted. It is rendered here because the statement is the only field datum available and the Hall would rather plot the inferred space than leave the bottom of the network blank.

The scale of the plate is **not constant**. Depth is compressed at the upper layers and expanded at the lower; horizontal distance is uniformly approximate. This is necessary because the actual depth-to-extent ratio of the Underdark would render the plate as a two-foot-tall narrow column unsuitable for the broadsheet.

## Marginalia

### The Five Surface Entries

The Underdark has four formally documented surface entries plus one descent that the Hall treats as a separate node (the Dominion Vault, which is reached through Ixareth-Kul rather than through the Underdark trunk). The four trunk entries are:

- **Cogsworth lower levels** — the contested junction at Tier VI of Cogsworth's vertical structure, continuous with the Kobold Deeps. Compact-licensed escort required for non-Kobold passage; the Long-Nose Pack negotiator currently holds the escort licence.
- **The Dwarven Holds deep tunnels** — the Hold Stair from Kethara and the Deep Forge Stair from Deepforge. Sealed at Hold-authority level for non-Dwarves; the seal is selective in practice rather than absolute, but the Hall does not contradict the Holds' formal position in print.
- **Aelindra root level (the Dreaming Grove descent)** — the sub-surface descent from the Inner Glade beneath the Heartwood. This is a node, not a transit point; the descent terminates at the Warden's Court and does not continue further into the network.
- **Ixareth-Kul sub-surface (the Dominion Vault descent)** — the sub-surface descent from beneath the Roofless Hall. This is a node, not a transit point; the Vault Seal is the access condition and the Seal opens only when the Architect chain survey delivers a complete report.

The Vel'Sharath surface entry — if there is one outside the Spiderweave Gates' control — is not on Compact record and is not on this plate. The Hall's working position is that the Dark Elf administration prefers it that way.

### The Upper Layers — Pack Tunnels and Hold Tunnels

The Kobold Deeps tier structure carries from Plate V's Cogsworth cross-section into this plate as the upper-left compartment. The Pack tier, Inner tier, Deep tier, and Probe tier are the same tiers; their content is not duplicated here. The Probe tier's **Old Dominion Trace** and the Long-Nose Pack's **Expansion Tunnels** are the relevant features for this plate, because both are heading east toward the Quiet Dig.

The Dwarven Hold tunnels are a parallel system. The Hall Stair and the Deep Forge Stair are the two named trunk descents; the Holds' position on file is that the deep tunnels descend to a separate set of forge levels (the **Inner Hold Forges**) and do not connect to the Underdark Crossing. Hold record is consistent on this point. Compact observation is consistent that Dwarven traffic patterns at the Crossing's centre suggest a connection that the Hold record does not mention. The Hall has not adjudicated the discrepancy and does not propose to.

### The Underdark Crossing — Three Sectors

The Crossing is divided into three sectors by mutual practice rather than any formal authority:

- **The Kobold sector** in the north. Patrolled by the Long-Nose and Iron-Whisker outer watches. The Probe Patrol escorts traffic between the Kobold sector and the Crossing's centre.
- **The Grey Market sector** in the centre. Neutral by mutual restraint, not by treaty. The Compact's clearing-house operates here under six longshore licences that the Compact's published register lists as *six unspecified licences*; the unspecificity is the point. The **Arbitration Chamber** is where the Grey Market settles its own disputes; the panel that took **Grix of the Long-Nose Pack's** statement in Year 844 CR sat in this chamber.
- **The Dark Elf sector** in the south. The Vel'Sharath Tribute Hall accepts tribute from the Crossing's traffic; the Spiderweave Approach is the gate-shaft down to Vel'Sharath proper. The Black Market relay handles cargo from Vel'Sharath that is destined for the surface but cannot pass through the Grey Market openly.

The three sectors maintain their boundaries by the discipline of mutual patrol restraint. There is no neutral force at the boundaries. Encounters at the sector boundaries that escalate are handled on the spot, by whichever sectoral patrol is present, and are reported to the relevant Compact desk after the fact rather than during.

### The Quiet Dig — The Hall's Eleventh Year of Pending

The Quiet Dig is the off-route Architect site beneath the Crossing. It is the site at which the **Third Digger** — a Dominion-era construct using Architect terminology in its operational parameters — terminates. The Digger's working seal at the off-route entry is documented Year 836 CR; the seal has not been breached.

Eleven years of Compact survey requests have been filed with the Hall. The Hall has not issued a survey authorisation. The reasons the Hall gives publicly are procedural; the reasons the Hall gives privately are that the Notation Wall's partial transcriptions describe the Dig's seal-breaking conditions in language the Hall is not willing to commit a survey team to, and the Hall would rather not authorise a survey it does not expect a return from. This is not the Hall's official position and will not be filed as such.

### The Dreaming Grove and the Dominion Vault — Nodes, Not Transit

Both the Dreaming Grove (from Aelindra) and the Dominion Vault (from Ixareth-Kul) are *nodes* in the Underdark — they connect to the surface via descents from their respective surface zones, but they do not pass through the Underdark Crossing or share its trunk. The descents render on this plate as separate side-channels because that is what they are.

The **Dreaming Grove** is at the Aelindra root level and is the network's calibration station. The **Dreaming Warden** is in residence; visitors petition through the Hall of Records. The Reading Pool is where Calibration State transmissions are received. The Grove does not continue past the Warden's Court — it is a destination, not a passage.

The **Dominion Vault** is the network's primary survey terminus, sealed and pending the Architect chain's complete report. The Vault is reached from beneath the Roofless Hall at Ixareth-Kul. The **Vault Seal** is intact. The three Order positions (Walking Name, Bonewright, Coalition Remnant) maintain pressure on the Seal but none has reached past it; the Seal opens by its own conditions, not by force.

### Vel'Sharath — Wards and Authority

Vel'Sharath proper has four named wards:

- **The Outer Wards** — the city boundary; the Spiderweave Gates control entry; tribute received at the Tribute Hall (above the Gates, in the Crossing's south sector).
- **The Inner Wards** — residential; the Black Market local node; Necromancer apothecaries.
- **The Inner Citadel** — governance, with the Speaker's Hall and the Necromancer Guild. The Speaker is hereditary; the seat has not been contested in three generations.
- **The Deep Ward** — the deepest level confirmed by Compact-licensed survey. Compact survey access to Vel'Sharath ended Year 731 CR; the Hall has submitted four survey requests since and received no acknowledged response.

The Vel'Sharath administration's standing position on the Underdark Crossing is that the Crossing is the surface's problem; on the Quiet Dig, that the Dig is the Crossing's problem; and on what is below the Deep Ward, that the matter is not survey data and is not for discussion outside Vel'Sharath. The Hall records all three positions and notes that none of them is a denial.

### The Lit Space — Why It Is on the Plate

**Grix of the Long-Nose Pack** was a Grey Market runner documented in Compact cargo manifests through Year 843 CR. He gave a single statement to the Grey Market arbitration panel in Year 844 CR following an unexplained three-week absence in the passages east of Vel'Sharath's Deep Ward. He stated:

> *the passages east of the Deep Ward open into a space too large to see across, and the far side of it is lit from below*

The arbitration panel did not pursue the matter further. Grix's pack documented his subsequent behavioural changes and filed the documentation with the Compact; the Hall does not consider those changes geographic data and does not include them on this plate.

The plate plots the **Lit Space** because the statement is the only field datum the Hall has for the volume east of Vel'Sharath's Deep Ward, and rendering the volume blank would be wrong: the volume is *there*, regardless of the Hall's confidence in any particular feature within it. The dimensions, the light source, the floor's nature, and any inhabitation are unknown. The Hall has filed the rendering as a working approximation and will revise it when (if) Vel'Sharath issues a survey authorisation or another statement of comparable specificity reaches the Hall.

### What This Plate Does Not Resolve

- **The Quiet Dig's interior.** The seal is intact. The Notation Wall's full reading requires breaking the seal, which the Hall has not authorised.
- **The Dwarven Hold tunnels' connection (or lack thereof) to the Crossing.** Hold record and Compact observation disagree. The Hall does not adjudicate.
- **The actual layout of Vel'Sharath's Inner Citadel.** Compact survey access ended Year 731 CR; the four ward names and their general arrangement are the survivable summary from pre-731 surveys, with no street-level resolution.
- **The Lit Space's dimensions, light source, and contents.** Single statement; no return survey. The Hall plots the space and notes that everything within it is not on plate.
- **Whatever is below the Lit Space.** Survey teams have not returned with usable data. The Vel'Sharath administration does not respond. The Hall does not press.
