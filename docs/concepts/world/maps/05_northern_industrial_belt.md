# Plate V — The Northern Industrial Belt

*Cartographer's Hall, Aelindra · Filed Stillmonth, Year 847 of the Current Reckoning*
*Principal hand: Archivist Valex · Field corroboration: Cogsworth Council Cogwheel · Kethara Hold Records Office · Deepforge Hall Master Smiths' Roll · Halfway Hostel keeper · Long-Nose Pack negotiator (under Compact escort)*

---

## Prefatory Note

This plate is the fourth of the regional sheets and zooms upon the northern stretch of the continent — the Dwarven Mountain Holds along the high range, the Gnome-and-Kobold industrial city of Cogsworth nestled at the southern foothills, the Iron Way that runs between them, and the surface entries to the Kobold Deeps that lie beneath Cogsworth's lower levels.

The Holds are an underground civilisation by inhabitation. Their named halls are interior to the mountain rather than upon it. The plate accordingly renders the Holds as named hold-gates on the surface, with a summary hall-list at each gate; the hall-by-hall interior layouts are a Plate VI matter and will appear on the Underdark plate when issued. The Kobold Deeps are likewise interior; the surface entries are shown here, the tunnel network is shown on Plate VI.

The cross-section inset on the right of the plate renders Cogsworth's vertical structure — the city is built on terraced foothills and runs deeper than its surface footprint suggests, with the Kobold Deeps continuous with Cogsworth's lower levels at the contested junction the Compact licences and the Long-Nose Pack patrol.

The scale is roughly three league marches per grid square, the same as Plates II–IV.

## Marginalia

### The Three Holds — Surface Survey

The Holds are interior structures. The plate renders them as named hold-gates on the mountain surface and lists each hold's principal halls in summary. The hall-by-hall layouts are interior to the mountain and will appear on Plate VI when the Underdark plate is filed. The three named holds the Hall has confirmed at survey date are:

- **Kethara Hold** — the senior hold; the king's seat. The Kethara Vault holds the royal treasury; the Reading Room holds the ancient texts the Hold keeps under restricted access; the **Astronomical Observatory** has filed Calibration State logs since Year 311 CR and is the only Hold facility the Hall maintains a working correspondence with. The Observatory's Year 407 CR log entries are the Calibration State documentation the Hall cross-references against the Aelindra archive's "sky was wrong" event.
- **Stoneholt** — the lesser hold; the Mason-Quarter and the Hall of Lineages are housed here. Stoneholt's working speciality is masonry and Runeforge stonework; the Mason-Hold is where the Runeforge masons live. **The Old Hold** above is a former hall now in ceremonial use only; it has been abandoned for current operations since Year 612 CR and is kept open for funerary purposes.
- **Deepforge Hall** — the working hold. The **Greatforge** sits here, with the **Anvil-of-Ages** at its heart. The Anvil was set in Year 122 CR and has not been moved since. The Smelter Pits are deep-furnace operations under the Greatforge; the **Runeforge Vault** is where rune-inscribed work is finished and kept until the receiving party collects it. Most of the runeforging trade outside the Hold passes through the Vault.

The deep-tunnel descent to the Underdark Crossing departs from both Kethara and Deepforge. Stoneholt does not maintain a deep-tunnel link. The Hold authority's working position is that the deep-tunnel passage is sealed at Hold-authority level; the Hall does not contradict this in print but notes that the Underdark Crossing's contested junction has Dwarven traffic patterns that suggest the seal is selective rather than absolute.

### The Iron Way — Stations Along the Road

The Iron Way runs from Kethara Hold and Deepforge Hall down through the central pass and meets Cogsworth's Foundry District at the Foundry Gate. The road is Dwarven-maintained and is the primary trade artery between the Holds and the city; it carries finished metalwork south, raw timber and grain north, and apprentice traffic in both directions year-round.

- **The Iron Bridge** is the only point at which the Iron Way crosses open water — the Sable's Run gulley between the foothills. The bridge was set in Year 388 CR and has been re-decked twice. The Dwarven masonry on the abutments has not been touched since installation.
- **The Halfway Hostel** is the rest station at the road's midpoint. The current keeper has been the keeper for forty-seven consecutive years; the Compact's licence is renewed annually and the renewal has been a formality at every renewal. The Hostel's records are not Compact records — the Hall has not asked for them.
- **The Snow-Watchers' Hut** is the winter monitor at the southern pass. It is staffed from Frostmonth to Stillmonth; the staffing is by Compact-Cogsworth contract and the staff are usually Halflings from the Greenshire side rather than Cogsworth gnomes, on the ground that the Halflings tolerate the cold better.
- **The Old Marker** is the boundary stone between Hold authority and Compact authority. It has been at its current position since Year 312 CR, when the Hold authority and the Cogsworth Council settled the boundary in formal session. The Marker has been re-cut three times — the inscription weathers — but the position has not moved.

### Cogsworth — Vertical Structure

The plate renders Cogsworth as a city box on the surface plan and as a vertical cross-section to the right. The cross-section is the more accurate working sheet for visiting officials; the surface plan is the more accurate working sheet for traders.

The vertical structure runs **six tiers** above the Kobold Deeps junction, with the contested junction itself counted as Tier VI. The tiers are not equal in size — Tier I is the largest by volume, the Tinker's Quarter and the Brassworks share the bulk of Tier II, the Foundry District's drainage tier (Tier III) is mostly working space rather than living space, Pipefall Square at Tier IV is the largest single open space below the surface, and Tier V's Disputed Valve and the Kobold Outers' residential district are the deepest formally-Cogsworth tiers.

**The Disputed Valve** is at the Tier V/VI junction and is the formal subject of the dispute. The Valve has not been operated in either direction in eighty years. The **Sister Valve** at Tier IV is the matching valve and is operated monthly without dispute; the Council's standing position is that the Sister Valve's monthly operation is evidence the Disputed Valve is operable. The Long-Nose Pack's standing position is that the Disputed Valve's operation is the Pack's call to make and the call is currently no. The Sub-Committee Chamber at Cogsworth is the formal governance space for the dispute; minutes are published quarterly; resolution is not on any near-term schedule.

The bells of the **Grand Clock Tower** have been slightly off for eighty years. Whether the drift began with the Disputed Valve's last operation eighty years ago is the kind of pattern the Hall files and does not endorse.

### The Kobold Deeps — Surface Entry and Pack Distribution

The Deeps are an underground civilisation; the surface entry from Cogsworth is the contested junction at Tier VI. The plate's cross-section continues into the Deeps as far as Compact-Long-Nose negotiated access permits — the Pack tier with the Long-Nose and Iron-Whisker camps, the Inner tier with the Coppertooth and Smokeback camps, and the Deep tier with the Pack Council Chamber, Quartzfall, and the Glow-Vein.

The **Probe tier** is where the Expansion Tunnels begin. The **Old Dominion Trace** is the original Dominion construction beneath Cogsworth — the tunnels were Dominion supply lines before passing to Kobold independence after the Year 558 CR collapse. The **Expansion Tunnels** are the current Kobold-only digging programme; the Long-Nose Pack maintains exclusive access. The tunnels' direction has been inferred from compact-licensed surface readings; the inferred terminus is somewhere in the direction of the Quiet Dig, which is on Plate VI. Whether the Pack knows what the inferred terminus is, or whether the Pack is digging toward it without knowing, is the question the Compact's intelligence office has been pursuing for nineteen years without an answer that the office considers usable.

### What This Plate Does Not Resolve

- **The Hold interior layouts.** Each Hold's halls are listed in summary on plate; the hall-by-hall plans are an Underdark plate matter and are reserved for Plate VI. Stonemason Vell of Stoneholt's plan submission has been received and is in the queue.
- **The Underdark Crossing proper.** The deep-tunnel descents from Kethara and Deepforge are marked here as ▲ entries. The tunnels themselves are on Plate VI when issued.
- **The Expansion Tunnels' actual route.** As above. The plate marks the entry and notes the inferred terminus; the route between is not surveyed and is not on plate.
- **The Astronomical Observatory's Calibration State logs.** The Observatory's Year 407 CR log is the longest single Calibration State observation in the Hall's records, and a Plate VIII (the Architect Site Network) is in preparation to render the Observatory's correspondences against the network's confirmed sites. The Observatory's logs themselves are not the Hall's to publish.
