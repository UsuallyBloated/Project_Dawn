---

## Prefatory Note

The plate that follows is a finer-scale rendering of the continental surface map carried in the second-edition Atlas. The Atlas plate was prepared under hall constraints that permitted only a single sheet per facing-page, and several features — interior landmarks, road and river traces, and the named features that field teams use to navigate in practice — were omitted there for legibility. The Repository now permits the larger sheet, and the Hall has redrawn.

This third impression is on the broadsheet — the largest single sheet the Repository's binding accepts. The earlier impressions filed in this same season were judged to crowd their content; the canvas has been extended east-and-west to give the eastern frontier zones the lateral room their actual extent demands, and to give the western coast and the offshore waters the open sea they deserve.

The conventions of the Atlas plate remain canonical for that document. The legend below applies to this plate only. The orientation is north. The scale is approximate. One grid square is, very roughly, eight league marches.

## Marginalia

### Roads, Rivers, and the Shape of Travel

The plate shows the working road network as the Hall has surveyed it. Three routes carry the bulk of overland traffic:

- **The Iron Way** descends from Kethara Hold through the central pass and meets Cogsworth's Foundry District at the gates. From Cogsworth the **Northern Road** runs east to Aelindra; this is the only kept road between the Industrial Belt and the Heartlands' caster-tradition north. Both segments are Dwarven-maintained.
- **The East Trunk Road** is the king's way out of Greyveil. It carries the river-confluence's overland traffic east through Auroch's Rest and on to the Eastern Badlands frontier. Auroch's Rest's section is patrolled by Minotaur earthwork sentries; the Badlands section is not patrolled at all.
- **The Greenshire–Greyveil east road** is the primary western artery and the road most travelers begin their first journey on. It joins the East Trunk at Greyveil's western gate.

The river system carries the secondary maritime traffic. **Sable's Run** descends from the Dwarven Holds through a long western valley, fed by tributaries off Cogsworth's industrial drainage; it runs south through the Greenshire farmlands to the river confluence at Greyveil. From Greyveil the river runs east to Harrowmere as the **Greymere river route**, which is the trade artery the Compact uses for grain, timber, and finished goods between the central hub and the port.

The mountain pass between Greenshire and Cogsworth is the only ground crossing between Western Heartland and Northern Industrial Belt; in winter it closes for approximately six weeks, and the Iron Way becomes the only land route into Cogsworth from any direction.

### The Architect Arc, Visualised

Eight surface-visible Architect sites are marked on this plate, plus the Drowned Hold offshore and the Rising Shell offshore — ten in all. Read west-to-east, north-to-south, the arc emerges:

1. **The Drowned Hold** ✦ — Harrow Bay, offshore (harbour-anchor node)
2. **The Foundation Ring** ✦ — beneath Greyveil (city-anchor node, structurally inaccessible)
3. **The Dreaming Grove** ✦ — Aelindra root level (calibration station; surface entry shown)
4. **Varek's Spire** ✦ — between Greyveil and Fae Mere (secondary terminus)
5. **The Tide Marks** ✦ — Ashen Coast cliffs (calibration reference)
6. **The Calibration Tower** ✦ — Eastern Badlands (signal relay; partially collapsed)
7. **The Convergence Point** ✦ — Ashen Marches centre (surface marker)
8. **The Sunken Ziggurat** ✦ — Southeastern Marshlands (deep archive; submerged)
9. **The Roofless Hall** ✦ — Ixareth-Kul (primary survey terminus)
10. **The Rising Shell** ✦ — Cinder Deep, offshore (terminal manifestation node)

A single speculative site — **The Deep Rooms** ✧ — is marked beneath the Heartwood in the Blighted Wood. Its presence is referenced in notation recovered from the Sunken Ziggurat. No surface entry has been identified.

The two underground sites — the **Quiet Dig** (Underdark Crossing) and the **Dreaming Grove** (subsurface from Aelindra) — are noted on Plate VI. The Dreaming Grove's surface descent point is shown here for the convenience of overland surveyors.

### Notes by Zone

**Cogsworth.** The Foundry District sits at the Iron Way terminus and is the source of the river drainage that feeds Sable's Run; the river is industrially warm by the time it reaches the Greenshire farmlands and the Halfling smallholders downstream have been negotiating with the Cogsworth City Council about this for forty years. The Disputed Valve is in the lower levels and remains, as the title indicates, disputed.

**Aelindra.** The Sealed Archive's surface position is given on the plate. The Dreaming Grove descent is at root level and is shown with a ▼ glyph; the Grove itself is treated cartographically as a dungeon within Aelindra's bounds. The Hall of Records is where this plate is filed.

**Harrowmere.** The two empty Compact council seats at the Cinder Hall are not vacancies — they are kept empty by Compact policy for representatives who have not arrived. The Hall has not investigated what would happen if someone arrived.

**The Greyfen.** The corridor's western and eastern tree-lines are notional; the fen merges into the Ardenmoor canopy on its northern edge and into the Greyveil river meadows on its southern. The ✦ Foundation Ring marker is at the position where Predecessor stonework has surfaced from peat in the highest concentration. The actual Foundation Ring is beneath Greyveil; what surfaces here is the structure's edge.

**The Blighted Wood.** The blight glyphs (▓) on the plate are positioned at the eastern edge of the Ardenmoor treeline because that is where the blight is currently advancing. The position of the ▓ on this plate is therefore current to Stillmonth 847 CR and will be wrong by an expected two grid-cells eastward in approximately five years.

**Auroch's Rest.** The Earthwork Halls and the Horn-Marker Cemeteries are shown as adjacent because they are. The Minotaur did not separate the living quarter and the burial ground when they laid out the settlement, and have given the Hall to understand that they are not going to. The Long Furrow is the Minotaur's working name for the broad cattle-track that crosses the zone east-to-west; the Hall has adopted the Minotaur's name in preference to its earlier and less accurate label. Auroch's Cairn is the central memorial stone marking where the herd-elders settled the founding question.

**The Eastern Badlands.** The Bone Citadel is shown at the western edge per the western-fronting position the Ogre clans have maintained since at least Year 300 CR. The Calibration Tower is shown distinct from it; the two are about three days' march apart and the Ogre clans treat both as territorial markers for separate purposes. Durrath's Mesa Temple is carved into the north mesa face and predates the current Ogre communities. The Calibration Tower's interior remains unsurveyed.

**The Ashen Marches.** The Convergence Point is at the Marches centre. The Field of Standards is the burial ground from the Year 558 CR collapse engagement and is plotted slightly north-east of the Point, though it spans considerable ground and the precise extent is contested between the three undead factions currently occupying it. The Nameless Commander's pavilion is approximately central to the Coalition Remnant's holding. The Three Lines are the current front positions of the three undead factions and are mobile by season; the position rendered here is the Stillmonth 847 CR snapshot.

**The Khala Savannahs.** The Ancestor's Mouth is shown as a single ◊ glyph but is in fact a cave system with multiple entrances; the entrance most relevant to outsiders is the one the Spirit-Walkers are willing to receive visitors at, which is on the western face. The other entrances are the Spirit-Walkers' business. The Singing Stones are a cluster of standing stones on the eastern uplands where the Khala's named winds gather and intersect; the Felhari say the stones name the winds, not the other way around. The Long Wallow is the kopje cluster the warders fall back to when their territorial circuit is interrupted.

**The Ashen Coast.** The Tide Marks are carved into the sea cliffs at the position shown; they are visible from a vessel passing through the Cinder Deep at low tide and are below the waterline at high tide. The calibration timing they encode is no longer accurate to current tides — either the sea has changed since the marks were cut, or the marks were not cut for tides that exist on this world. Both possibilities are recorded. The Glasswright Coves on the southern coast are the source of the volcanic glass the Magician Nexus Sites work — geographically adjacent, professionally distinct.

**The Southeastern Marshlands.** The Cypress Mile is the raised pilgrimage causeway the Bonewrights walk between the Wharves and the Sunken Ziggurat's surface marker. It is the only durably dry footing in the central marsh and is, as a consequence, the only practical approach from the north. The Bonewrights maintain it; they do not ask permission to do so and they do not stop maintaining it during seasons when other factions occupy the surrounding ground.

**Ixareth-Kul and the Dominion Vault.** The Vault's ▼ glyph indicates the sub-surface descent from the inner ring. The Vault is accessed from beneath the Roofless Hall and is, as of filing, sealed. No survey team has reached it. The three Dominion Orders dispute access; this plate does not record their positions in any detail because the Hall does not consider their dispute a cartographic matter.

### Visual Character — for the Chief Artist

Plate I is the continental statement and should read as one. The ground is aged broadsheet parchment; the inks are sepia and iron-gall; the washes are muted and regional — cold grey-blue for the Greymere, warm blue-green for the Remnant, honey-and-green for the Heartlands, rust-red for the Badlands, black-stippled grey for the volcanic coast, grey-violet for the blight. No single region may shout; the plate's argument is the whole.

The signature visuals — for the artist's notebook:

- **The Architect arc** is the only gold on the plate. Ten ✦ sites rendered as small gold-leaf six-fingered handprints (per the Drafting Standards); a reader's eye sweeping the plate should find the arc before they know they are looking for it.
- **The two offshore sites** — the Drowned Hold in Harrow Bay and the Rising Shell in the Cinder Deep — sit in open water with room around them; their isolation is the point.
- **The road and river net** is fine-lined and quiet: the Iron Way, the Northern Road, the East Trunk, Sable's Run, and the Greymere river are the strings that hold the regions together.
- **The blight edge** at Ardenmoor's east is the plate's one hard line — a wash boundary that does not feather.
- **The empty east** — the uncharted scrub past the Badlands fades to bare parchment with the Hall's *no field data* note in the Archivist's hand. Blank parchment is a feature, not a failure.

### What the Plate Does Not Show

- **The Underdark.** Plate VI carries the underground network. The surface entry points (▲) at Cogsworth lower, Dwarven Holds deep, Aelindra root, and Ixareth-Kul sub-surface are shown here for reference; everything below the entry is on Plate VI.
- **The second continent.** The Vah'tharan Passage runs south-southeast of the Bone Reach into open water and is not shown on this plate. The Maren / Vah'thara sketch carried in the Atlas remains the working document for the second continent; the maritime detail is carried on Plate VII.
- **Faction territory overlays.** Where a zone's de facto control is disputed (the Breach, the Ashen Marches, sections of the Underdark Crossing), the plate marks the contest with ⊕ but does not attempt to render shifting territorial lines. The Breach Warden's operational documents are the reference for current control state and are updated more often than this plate is.
