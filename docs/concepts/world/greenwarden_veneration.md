# The Greenwarden — a Regional Veneration

**Status:** deliberately unresolved. The Greenwarden is *not* a member of the canonical pantheon (Solrath, Erindra, Vethis, Aracheth, Mireth, Durrath). It is a Greenshire-local veneration that surfaced in the Plate IX-A survey, and its identity is an open mystery by design. This doc records what is canon, fences off what is not, and lists the interpretations that are allowed to stay in tension.

---

## What is canon (established on Plate IX-A)

- **The Greenwarden Shrine** at the Three Ponds is a small lime-washed chapel with **three altars in a row: Solrath, Erindra, and the Greenwarden**. Sister Rosalind Pell (Cleric trainer) officiates at all three without apparent doctrinal difficulty.
- **The Bridge-Foot Shrine** at the south end of Greenstone Bridge is a small wayside shrine to the Greenwarden. Offerings are **bread and small coins**.
- The Halfmark Chapel (Halfmark proper) has altars to Solrath and Erindra only — **no Greenwarden altar**. The veneration is strongest at the Three Ponds and along the river, weaker in the eastern hamlets.
- The **High Cartography Hall has no entry on the Greenwarden** in the canonical pantheon and formally treats the figure as "a regional veneration."
- Nobody in Greenshire considers any of this strange. That is itself a data point.

## What is *not* yet canon

- Any image, idol, or physical description of the Greenwarden. The altars are plain. No statue exists anywhere in Greenshire — worth keeping: a god with no face is cheap to maintain and expensive to disprove.
- Any clerical magic attributed specifically to the Greenwarden.
- Any origin story told by a Greenshire local. If asked, Halflings change the subject pleasantly, the way they change the subject about the smugglers' cove.

## The three interpretations (keep all alive)

1. **A genuine local folk spirit** — a small god of hedgerow, harvest, and safe crossings, real at exactly the scale Greenshire needs it to be. The bread-and-coins offering pattern (food + toll) fits a god of *getting safely from one place to another*, which suits a zone defined by roads and a bridge.
2. **A masked aspect of Erindra** — the veneration as honest syncretism; Sister Rosalind's untroubled triple officiating supports this, and the church hierarchy at Aelindra has never objected, which either means it agrees or means it knows something.
3. **Something older wearing a comfortable name** — the Foundation Ring's edge reaches almost to the Greenshire border (Plate IX-A's prefatory note), and Greenshire is "not entirely honest about what comes next." A warden of the green. A warden *in* the green. The blank fourth face of the Old Oak waystone and the Greenwarden's faceless altars rhyme; the docs should never confirm whether that rhyme is meaningful.

## Design hooks

- **Tutorial-level:** an offering quest at the Bridge-Foot Shrine (bread + a copper) as part of first travel to Greyveil — teaches the offering loop and the currency system in one beat. No reward beyond a flavor buff ("the Greenwarden's Toll": +1% movement speed on roads for an hour — small enough to be folklore, real enough to be noticed).
- **Mid-level:** a Hall of Records side-quest — Archivist Valex pays for a transcription of the Greenwarden liturgy, because the Hall files everything; the liturgy turns out to be agricultural calendar verse with one stanza that scans wrong and predates the rest.
- **Never:** a quest that answers what the Greenwarden is. The payoff of this content is that the question stays warm.
