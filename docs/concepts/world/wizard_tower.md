# Varek's Spire — The Wizard Tower
*Levels 25–35 — Isolated / Vertical Dungeon*

## Overview
A single tower on a rock shelf above the eastern Greyveil lowlands, visible from thirty miles in clear weather and routinely mistaken for a lighthouse by travelers who don't know the coast. There is no lighthouse at that location. The light that sometimes appears at the top is not a signal — it is a side effect of whatever Varek is working on.

Varek has been in the tower for an estimated ninety years. He is not a recluse — he receives visitors, responds to correspondence (eventually), and has sold enchantments to half a dozen factions. He simply has not left. He says he is waiting for something. He has said this for ninety years. Visitors who ask what he is waiting for receive different answers each time, which suggests either that he is working through the question or that he finds the question boring.

The tower has seven floors. Varek lives on floors five through seven. Floors one through four are accessible to visitors and considered safe. The basement is not discussed.

## Setting
Windswept rock shelf, no road. The path is two hours of goat track from the nearest village. The tower itself is stone, old, and visibly maintained — Varek patches it periodically. The exterior has runes at every window and door frame; they glow faintly in rain and very brightly during lightning storms, which draws people who want to watch and drives away people with sense.

## Floors

**Floor 1 — Reception:** A waiting room. Chairs. A golem behind a desk that takes names and asks the nature of the visit. The golem is extremely literal. It has rejected visitors for "unclear purpose" for ninety years. Varek built it this way on purpose.

**Floor 2 — Library (Public):** Bookshelves floor to ceiling. Accessible to visitors. Contains primarily magical theory — the practice sections have been removed. Varek pulled them when visitors started attempting the experiments in the library. Two of those experiments are why the east wall has a slightly different color stone than the rest.

**Floor 3 — Experiment Staging:** Benches with equipment that has not been touched in years. Either Varek no longer uses this floor or he does and tidies it obsessively. Both possibilities exist.

**Floor 4 — The First Locked Door:** Varek's work begins here. Players without permission cannot pass. Getting permission is a questline.

**Floors 5–7 — Varek's Domain:** Active magical research. Three simultaneous projects at any given time. Varek can describe all of them in detail. He will. At length. He is lonely in a way he does not acknowledge and being asked about his work is the most reliable way to get an hour of his time.

**The Basement:** Varek says there is no basement. The golem on Floor 1 has been instructed to agree. The stairs are in the library behind a shelf that Varek clearly moved at some point and moved back imprecisely — the drag marks in the dust are legible.

## Enemies
No standard enemy spawns. The tower is inhabited and warded. Unauthorized entry above Floor 4 triggers guardian constructs — not hostile by default, but with a specific definition of "authorized" that is Varek's definition, which changes.

**The Basement:** Whatever is down there is not a construct. It has been there longer than the tower. Varek built the tower on top of it, which he will not discuss.

## Dungeon — The Basement
*Levels 30–35. Access requires completing Varek's full questline.*

Three levels below the tower floor. The first two are storage and old experiments — Varek's work from decades past, some of it still active. The third level is what he built the tower to contain, study, or communicate with. He has not decided which of those three it is yet. Ninety years in.

**Named:** The Residual (level 35) — not alive in the standard sense. A magical echo of something that happened here before Varek arrived. Varek is disappointed when players kill it. He mentions this. He was learning things from it.

## Services
- Varek (Wizard class trainer levels 20–40; requires questline access)
- Varek (Enchanting trainer to skill 175; same requirement)
- Library (Floor 2, free access; passive lore; one hidden recipe scroll per week)
- The golem (vendor for basic spell components; no questline required; extremely unhelpful but technically functional)

## Faction Notes
No faction system. Standing with Varek is personal — he remembers every visit and every conversation. Players who interrupt him while he is explaining something earn a specific notation in whatever record he keeps. Players who listen completely earn something else. He does not say what.

Varek is not affiliated with any city faction. Greyveil considers him a useful independent. Aelindra considers him an interesting problem. The Gnomes of Cogsworth have been sending correspondence about a joint project for thirty years. He has not replied.
