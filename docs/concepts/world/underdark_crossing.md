# The Underdark Crossing
*Levels 25–40 — Underground Transit / Contested Tunnels*

## Overview
The tunnel network beneath the central mountain range connecting Cogsworth's lower levels to the outer reaches of Vel'Sharath's Gate District. The Kobolds have known about this connection since before Cogsworth was built — the tunnels predate both cities. The Dark Elves discovered it approximately two hundred years ago and have been managing its existence carefully ever since. Both sides maintain the fiction that the other doesn't know. Both sides know this fiction is mutual. The Grey Market in the contested middle section is where both sides pretend not to be acknowledging each other.

The zone is also home to something that neither side has identified or wants to fully investigate: a third digger, creating new passages through the contested section weekly.

## Setting
Three distinct tunnel environments. The Kobold deep tunnels: four-foot passages, packed earth and rough stone, trap-rigged at intervals, lit by cultivated bioluminescent moss. The contested middle: wider, older, clearly pre-Kobold and pre-Dark Elf construction, strange geometry (passages that don't connect the way they should), magic unstable in a specific and consistent way. The Dark Elf approach: obsidian-lined, well-maintained, patrol routes with set schedules, a checkpoint before the Gate District connection.

The Grey Market at the center is a genuinely neutral space — both sides enforce its neutrality because both sides benefit from it. Weapons drawn in the market result in both factions stopping their separate business and focusing entirely on the person who drew them. This has happened twice. It was the last time for both who drew.

## Dungeon — The Digger's Warren
*Levels 35–40.*

A new tunnel complex, accessible from the contested section if players can find the correct new passage — it changes location weekly, which is the mechanic that makes it challenging to locate. Inside: a branching labyrinth of freshly-cut tunnels, still warm from whatever cut them, and at the center, the Third Digger itself.

The Third Digger is not hostile by default. It is mining. What it is mining for is the dungeon's mystery. The boss encounter is triggered only if players enter the central chamber and interfere with the mining operation. If they observe without interfering, they can learn what it is, where it came from, and what it's looking for — which opens a separate questline that threads back into the Ruined Dominion Capital.

**Named:** The Third Digger (level 40) — a Dominion-era construct, still executing its original survey orders. What it's surveying for is in the Dominion Vault.

## Services
- Grey Market vendors (Kobold side: ore, maps, trap components; Dark Elf side: alchemicals, enchanted obsidian, passage permits)
- Matron Zikka (if Kobold Deeps reputation is Tier 3+; unlocks Grey Market full access)
- Dark Elf passage permit vendor (expensive; allows authorized transit to Vel'Sharath Gate District)
- No inn or regen service — the Underdark Crossing is a transit zone

## Faction Notes
No primary faction in the Crossing itself. Kobold Deeps reputation carries over directly — Kobold characters with high Ashfang standing are recognized and treated as known contacts in the Kobold tunnels. Dark Elf Vel'Sharath reputation carries over similarly in the Dark Elf approach.

The Grey Market operates on transaction reputation — what you've bought, sold, and not stolen. It has no formal faction name. Matron Zikka tracks it personally.

The Third Digger questline, if completed without combat, opens a reputation track with the construct itself — it recognizes players who observed without attacking and offers survey data that functions as a map bonus in the Ruined Dominion Capital zone.
