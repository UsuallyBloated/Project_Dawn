# NPC Profile — Archivist Valex
*Order of the Sealed Record. Ixareth-Kul. Levels 35–50.*

---

## Identity

**Full name:** Valex Dethras *(VAH-lex DETH-ras)*
**Race:** Kel`varath
**Class:** Necromancer (level 38, though she has not cast offensively in eleven years)
**Age:** 60
**Alignment:** Neutral Evil (she would say Neutral; she is not wrong that she is neutral about most things)

**Physical description:** Short for an Kel`varath, which means she is approximately Human height and finds this noted about her every time she meets someone for the first time. Scales faded from what was once deep green to a weathered grey-green — the coloration of someone who has spent thirty years primarily underground with artificial light. Eyes still sharp. She wears the Sealed Record's archival grey over practical Kel`varath clothing. Her hands are ink-stained in the specific pattern of someone who takes extensive notes by hand and always has.

She carries two things on her person at all times: a small leather-bound volume that is her personal research journal (currently volume 47 in a series; volumes 1-46 are catalogued in the Sealed Record's archive), and a steel stylus she has been using for thirty-one years that she has had repaired seventeen times.

---

## Personality

Valex is precise, patient, and completely comfortable with silence. She has spent thirty-one years in the ruins of Ixareth-Kul doing detailed work that requires sustained attention and produces results on a timescale of years, not conversations. She is used to being the person who knows more about what she is looking at than anyone in the room, and she is correct about this more often than is comfortable.

She is not unkind. She is economical — she says what needs to be said and does not add warmth that the situation does not require. Players who interpret this as coldness are making an error; players who come back with good information find she has been thinking about their last conversation and has questions ready.

She dislikes being wrong. She is wrong occasionally, handles it with a specific flat expression and a note in the journal, and does not discuss it further. She does not apologize for errors — she corrects them.

Her laugh is rare and happens for reasons that are not always immediately apparent.

---

## What She Knows

Valex has been in the Ixareth-Kul ruins for thirty-one years. The Sealed Record's controlled sections are the best-documented portions of the city. She can tell a player more about specific rooms, their original functions, and their current state than anyone else alive — or any of the dead in the vicinity, who she has also consulted.

**On the Dominion:** She has read every surviving Dominion administrative record in the Sealed Record's possession. She has opinions on the Dominion War (the Empire's collapse was avoidable; specific, named poor decisions made it inevitable; she has identified which ones). She knows who made those decisions. They have been dead for three hundred years and she still finds them frustrating.

**On the Architects:** The Sealed Record's position is that the Architects were a precursor Kel`varath civilization. Valex personally finds this hypothesis attractive but insufficiently supported. She holds it as the working theory because better evidence points that direction than any alternative. She will say: "We believe they were ancestral. The evidence is consistent with this. The evidence is also consistent with two other explanations, which I find slightly less likely. I am monitoring for data that would let me distinguish."

**On the other Orders:** She knows Seeker Drathis (Walking Name) by reputation and finds his recent reports about Architect responses interesting enough to want to discuss with him, which she has not done because the Orders do not discuss things. She finds Director Sathren (Final Survey) genuinely alarming — not because the Final Survey's goal is wrong, but because Sathren has not fully modeled what happens if the system completion is bad.

**On the Vault:** The Sealed Record has partial documentation of the Vault's structure. Valex believes the correct authorization mechanism involves an Architect-standard credential, not the Gate-Key — but she acknowledges she cannot rule the Gate-Key out because she has not been able to examine it. She wants to examine it. She has been quietly pursuing research toward obtaining it for eight years.

## Relationship to Players

**First meeting:** Hostile by default (Sealed Record faction standing starts hostile). She will not speak to players with insufficient standing. She will not explain why — the standing threshold is listed in the faction documentation and she considers literacy a prerequisite for engagement.

**Improving standing:** Bring her things that improve the archive. Documented ruins of Kel`varath-era construction from other zones (she has a standing list of items she is seeking, available from a Sealed Record junior archivist at the camp perimeter). Help with documentation work in the ruins. Correctly identify architectural periods when asked. She notices when players demonstrate genuine knowledge.

**High standing:** Valex becomes genuinely forthcoming — not warm, but frank. She will share what she knows, ask good questions, and occasionally request player assistance with things she cannot do herself. One thing she cannot do herself: access the areas of the ruins controlled by the Walking Name, who would not let a Sealed Record scholar in. She is interested in what Seeker Drathis has been experiencing. She will ask a player with good relations with both Orders to observe and report back. She will be specific about what she wants observed.

---

## Quest Hooks from Valex

- **The Missing Volume** — A Dominion administrative record that should be in the Sealed Record's catalogue is not there. It was signed out sixty years ago by a Sealed Record scholar who died in the ruins. The record covers a specific period Valex needs for the Vault access research. She needs someone to find it — it is either in the section of the ruins where the scholar died or it was taken by someone before or after.
- **The Drathis Problem** — Valex wants a report on what Seeker Drathis has been experiencing in the Walking Name's deep sections. She is not asking for espionage (she will be specific: she wants to know what the Warden has been saying, not what the Walking Name's operational plans are). She will pay in documented archive access that players would not otherwise have.
- **The Authorization Question** — Valex's eight-year Vault access research has produced a hypothesis. She needs one piece of evidence she cannot obtain from inside the ruins: a record from the Greyveil city archives covering the Breach Gate's original construction. She does not have contacts in Greyveil. She needs a player to request access to the relevant archive section on her behalf. What the record says when obtained changes the Vault access puzzle significantly.

## Dialogue Samples

*On the Architects:*
"The evidence for ancestral origin is as follows. I will be brief because the full summary is thirty pages and you have not asked for the full summary. The proportions are consistent with Kel`varath anatomy. The structural mathematics uses a base-12 counting system, which the Kel`varath used before the Dominion standardized to base-10. The load distribution in the original foundation prioritizes the same support points an Kel`varath engineer would prioritize. These facts are consistent with ancestral origin. They are also consistent with independent convergent development. I am waiting for a fact that would distinguish between the two."

*On the Walking Name:*
"Their methodology is imprecise but their observations are good. I do not enjoy admitting this. The Dreaming Warden — the construct in Aelindra — has said things in the last three years that I would like to compare to our records. The problem is that Drathis will not share his notes with me and I will not share mine with him because the Orders do not do that and neither of us is willing to be first. This is a structural problem."

*On Director Sathren:*
"I find her troubling for a specific reason. She has correctly identified that not knowing is worse than knowing. Where I disagree is her implicit assumption that knowing and doing are the same thing. I believe the Vault should be opened. I believe the opening should be preceded by substantially more documentation than currently exists. Sathren believes the opening should be preceded by obtaining access. These are different priorities. Hers is faster. I cannot tell you which of us is right."

*If asked what she does for enjoyment:*
A pause. "I read."
