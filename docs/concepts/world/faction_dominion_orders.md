# The Three Dominion Orders — Faction Profile
*Kel`varath factions contesting Ixareth-Kul. Levels 35–50.*

---

## Overview

Three hundred years after the collapse of the Kel`varath Dominion, three organizations maintain competing claims to the ruins of Ixareth-Kul. None of them control the city. All of them control portions of it. The three orders are not military — they are scholarly and doctrinal — but they have armed forces because the ruins are actively dangerous and other parties want access to them.

The orders did not exist during the Dominion. They formed in the aftermath of the collapse, as three different groups of Kel`varath scholars tried to understand what had happened and what should be done about it. Their conclusions were different. Their conclusions have been different for three hundred years. They have not resolved this.

---

## Order of the Sealed Record

**Core belief:** The Architects were a precursor Kel`varath civilization, possibly a pre-Kel`varath ancestor population. The knowledge in the Dominion Vault is Kel`varath inheritance — the Kel`varath are the rightful heirs to the Architects' work, and the Sealed Record's purpose is to ensure that inheritance is protected until the Kel`varath are in a position to properly receive it.

**What "properly receive" means to them:** Political reunification of the Kel`varath people under a structure capable of understanding and applying Architect knowledge responsibly. Currently: not happening; the Kel`varath are fragmented into tribal and city-state structures. The Sealed Record is waiting.

**Approach to the ruins:** Conservative. Their controlled sections of Ixareth-Kul are meticulously documented and carefully maintained. They remove nothing that has not been catalogued. They allow no outside access. Their archives in the ruins are arguably the best historical records of Dominion-era Ixareth-Kul.

**Military posture:** Defensive. They hold three major sectors of the ruins, including the primary access route to the Dominion Vault entrance. They do not initiate conflict with the other orders; they do not yield ground.

**Standing with players:** Hostile by default. Improves through assisting their documentation work, bringing them Dominion-era artifacts found in other zones, or demonstrating alignment with Kel`varath cultural preservation goals.

**Notable NPC:** Archivist Valex — Senior scholar, Sealed Record. Sixty years old. Has been in the ruins for thirty-one years. Knows where more bodies are buried (literally and politically) than the other orders combined. Rarely leaves the archives. When she has something to say, it is worth hearing.

---

## Order of the Walking Name

**Core belief:** The Architects are still present in some form. Not alive in the biological sense, but persisting — their "remainder protocol" is active, the Residual structures at Architect sites are their continued existence in a different mode, and the Dominion Vault contains the Architects' communication record — what they were trying to tell whoever came after them.

**What they want:** To receive the communication. To understand what the Architects are saying through their remaining structures. The Walking Name does not want to unlock Architect power or claim Architect inheritance — they want to listen.

**Approach to the ruins:** Active and exploratory. Their controlled sections include the most structurally dangerous areas of Ixareth-Kul — the areas the Sealed Record considers too unstable to work in. They have lost people to collapses and encounters with things they did not expect to find. They continue.

**On the throne in the roofless hall:** Something sits the throne in the inner palace's roofless great hall. The Sealed Record says it is a relic; they want it catalogued. The Walking Name says it is the Architects communicating; they have been trying to communicate back for eighty years with no confirmed response.

**Military posture:** Aggressive when their access routes are threatened; not expansionist beyond that. They want to be in the dangerous parts of the ruins and will fight to stay there.

**Standing with players:** Neutral by default. Improves through exploration assistance, finding Architect notation they have not documented, or surviving situations in the ruins that their own scholars did not.

**Notable NPC:** Seeker Drathis — Mid-rank Walking Name, young (32), has been in the ruins for eight years and is increasingly convinced the Architects are responding to their communication attempts. Not delusional — there is real evidence that something has changed in the past three years. His superiors are cautiously interested. He is less cautious.

---

## Order of the Final Survey

**Core belief:** The Architects' system is unfinished. The sites are components of something that was never completed. The Dominion Vault contains the Final Survey — the Architect documentation of what the system does, what state it is in, and what is required to complete it. The completion of the system is either catastrophic or necessary. The Final Survey cannot be understood without opening the Vault. The Vault cannot be opened without the Final Survey.

This is the position the other orders find most concerning.

**What they actually want:** They want to know. The Final Survey's theology is that not knowing whether completing the Architect system is catastrophic or necessary is worse than knowing. A catastrophic outcome you understand can be prepared for. A necessary outcome you do not pursue is a failure of responsibility. Not knowing is simply ignorance dressed up as caution.

**On the Gate-Warden at the Breach:** The Final Survey has a document — origin disputed — that suggests the Gate-Warden's registry lists a specific location as authorized passage. That location is within Ixareth-Kul. They want the Gate-Key. They have tried twice to obtain it from the Underbreach and failed.

**Military posture:** The most aggressive of the three orders. They want the Vault open. Anyone who delays that goal is an obstacle.

**Standing with players:** Hostile by default. Improves only through direct assistance with Vault access — bringing them relevant documentation, Architect notation, the Gate-Key, or credentials from other zones. Does not improve through cultural preservation work.

**Notable NPC:** Director Sathren — The Final Survey's current head. Kel`varath female, 55. Has held the position for nine years. Is probably right that not knowing is worse than knowing. Has not fully thought through what happens if the system completion is catastrophic and she caused it.

## The Dominion Vault

All three orders want access to the Vault. None have achieved it. The Vault is sealed with mechanisms that require:
1. Architect-level authorization (none of the orders have this; they believe the Gate-Key is part of the mechanism)
2. Physical presence of multiple individuals simultaneously at specific locations within the inner palace
3. Something that the orders disagree about — the Sealed Record says a specific artifact; the Walking Name says the correct communication attempt; the Final Survey says the Third Digger's survey completion

The Vault is the endgame content of Ixareth-Kul (levels 44–50). Players can align with any of the three orders, achieve the access requirements, and open the Vault. What is inside is designed to be discovered in-game rather than documented here — the answer to the Architect question. The three orders have three different answers ready for when the Vault opens. At most one of them is correct.

---

## Inter-Order Relations

The three orders have a standing agreement: they do not openly war within Ixareth-Kul because fighting each other attracts outside parties. They maintain this agreement by mutual self-interest, not trust.

The Sealed Record and the Walking Name have an occasional working relationship when their interests align. Both consider the Final Survey dangerous.

The Final Survey considers the other two orders as obstacles to an answer that the world needs, and is willing to be temporarily cooperative if it advances Vault access.

Players who advance to maximum standing with one order will find their standing with the others reduced. Achieving high standing with all three is possible but requires careful navigation of situations where they directly conflict.

---

## Breach Standing Connection

Players with high Breach Standing can obtain information from the Warden-Captain that relates to the Final Survey's Gate-Key interest. The Warden-Captain does not know who is asking through her commissioned researchers; the Final Survey does not know the Warden-Captain is investigating. The information flow, if players recognize it, gives them significant leverage with the Final Survey — leverage they will use carefully or not at all, depending on their judgment of Director Sathren.
