# Ixareth-Kul — The Ruined Dominion Capital
*Levels 35–50 — Open World Dungeon / Kel`varath Lore Zone*

## Overview
The fallen capital of the Kel`varath Dominion. Three hundred years ago, a coalition of races finally succeeded in breaking the Dominion's military power here, at its center. The battle was catastrophic on both sides. The city did not fall cleanly — it was abandoned under siege, burned from inside and outside simultaneously, and left to the jungle when the coalition couldn't agree on who would garrison what remained.

The jungle has taken most of it back. Three Kel`varath necromantic orders — the Bonewrights, the Soulcarvers, and the Ash Scribes — have been quietly contesting control of what remains, each convinced that what the last High Commander sealed in the Dominion Vault before the fall belongs to their order specifically. None of them agree on what it is. None of them have gotten to it yet.

## Setting
Three concentric rings of ruins surrounding the fallen palace at the center. The outermost ring is largely reclaimed jungle — recognizable as city only by the stone paving pushing up through roots and the aqueduct remnants overhead. The middle ring is more intact and more contested, with active order operations on both sides. The inner ring centers on the palace itself: one wing partially standing (Ash Scribe headquarters), one wing open to the sky and thoroughly overgrown, and the Dominion Vault beneath.

The grand avenue through the outer ring is still walkable — the stone was laid to last and it did. The scale of the city is disorienting. This was not a small empire.

## Dungeon — The Dominion Vault
*Levels 44–50.*

Beneath the palace, sealed by the last High Commander three hours before she died. The three orders know it exists. None have cracked the seal. The dungeon is documented fully in [dominion_vault.md](dominion_vault.md), which supersedes the summary below.

In brief: the Vault descends through Dominion-era archive levels before reaching an Architect foundation below the Dominion's own construction. Access requires the Gate-Key (from the Gate-Warden at the Breach) and completion of the Architect network survey chain. The three Dominion Orders each maintain a presence and are in active conflict over access and what the Vault's contents mean. The last High Commander personally sealed Level 3's sanctum. The survey terminus — the Architect network's destination — is at the lowest level.

**Named:** The Last Seal (level 50 encounter, not a single mob) — a layered puzzle-combat encounter involving the Vault's final security system, designed to be fought as a full group with players from each role.

## Services
- Ash Scribe Scriptorium (faction-gated; Necromancer / Wizard high-level trainers)
- Order camp vendors (each faction has a camp in their controlled section; accessible based on faction standing)
- Bonewright field surgeon (accessible at Bonewright Neutral or better)
- Dungeon access to the Vault (requires Dominion Seal token from The Bonecourt starting zone, or equivalent questline)

## Faction Notes
Three-way faction system. Players begin at neutral with all three orders and must actively choose alignment through quest decisions. The factions do not forgive betrayal — moving from one order to another after committing requires a costly reconciliation quest.

All three orders are Evil-aligned institutions, but their specific goals, methods, and internal cultures differ significantly. The Ash Scribes value preservation of knowledge above all; the Bonewrights value practical application; the Soulcarvers value power accumulation. Quest tone and rewards differ accordingly.

Non-Kel`varath characters are not excluded from this zone but face automatic CHA penalties with all three orders until reputation reaches Tier 2. An Kel`varath character with high Bonewright standing from The Bonecourt starting zone begins this zone with a significant faction head-start.
