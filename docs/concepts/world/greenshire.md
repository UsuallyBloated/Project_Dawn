# Greenshire
*Starting Area — Levels 1–10 — Human / Halfling*

## Overview
The first place most characters see. Greenshire is a patchwork of farmland, small rivers, modest forests, and the market town of Millhaven at its center. The threats here are proportionate to new characters: wild animals, bandits on the road, the occasional goblin camp in the hills. The zone exists to teach the game without killing anyone unfairly.

Greenshire is the homeland of the Halflings and one of the main Human settlement zones. It is explicitly Good-aligned territory — guards patrol the roads, the temple of the local Cleric order maintains the town square, and the Witch Hunter chapter house has an office near the east gate. Evil-aligned characters can pass through but draw attention.

## Setting
Rolling green hills giving way to dense hedgerows and the occasional stand of old oak. The town of Millhaven sits at a crossroads: one road north toward Ardenmoor, one road east toward Greyveil, one road south toward the river docks. Population is a mix of Human farmers, Halfling innkeepers and bakers, traveling merchants, and the small permanent contingent of guards.

The zone's feel is pastoral and unhurried. This does not make it safe at night — the hills have wolves, the river has trouble, and the Witch Hunter chapter house is not staffed for nothing.

## Dungeon — The Millhaven Cellars
*Levels 6–10.*

Beneath the town, the old storage tunnels have been occupied by something. What started as a rat problem became a goblin problem, which became something worse when the goblins found what was already down there. A two-level dungeon: the upper level is navigable solo at level 8+; the lower level requires a group and features the zone's named enemy.

**Named:** Foreman Grix (level 10 goblin caster) — drops a wand usable by Magician/Wizard/Sorcerer at level 8.

## Services
- General vendor (Millhaven market)
- Inn with regen bonus (Millhaven, The Crossed Keys)
- Temple of the Radiant Order (Cleric/Paladin quests; donation for alignment)
- Witch Hunter chapter house (Witch Hunter class trainer; quests against goblin casters)
- Tradeskill trainers: Farming, Baking, Cooking, Leatherworking (basic)

## Faction Notes
The Radiant Order (temple) is the dominant faction in Greenshire. Completing their quests raises Good alignment. Antagonizing them, stealing from temple-adjacent NPCs, or completing the bandit questline for the bandit camp rather than against them all lower standing here.

The Halfling community in Millhaven is a secondary faction — friendly to nearly anyone neutral or better; they remember faces and hold grudges.
