# Greyveil
*Human City — Levels 20–30 — Merchants / Rogues / Bards*

## Overview
Greyveil is the largest city in the game and the only fully neutral major settlement. Faction doesn't open or close Greyveil's doors — coin does, reputation does, connections do. A Necromancer in Exalted-tier armor who has just come from slaughtering a holy order can walk into Greyveil if they have the money and don't make a scene. A Good-aligned Paladin who has publicly embarrassed a merchant guild will find doors quietly closed.

This makes Greyveil the mid-game social hub. Players use it to trade, to learn skills from trainers who operate outside the usual faction restrictions, to pick up quests that cross alignment lines, and to do the kind of business that the more ideologically organized cities don't offer. The Bard colleges are here. The Rogue guild maintains a visible presence (the Thieves' Guild is an open secret). The merchant exchange that controls pricing in the surrounding region is headquartered in the Trade Quarter.

## Setting
A walled city on a river, with districts organized by function rather than race. The River Quarter is where goods arrive and leave. The Trade Quarter is where they're priced and sold. The Old Quarter is where the money that has been here for generations lives. The New Quarter is where everyone else lives, which makes it the most interesting part of the city.

The city guard is well-paid and professionally neutral. They do not care about alignment. They care about whether you are visibly breaking Greyveil's laws in Greyveil. Outside those laws, they do not follow you.

## Dungeon — The Greyveil Undercity
*Levels 23–30.*

The city was built on an older city. The Undercity is the older city, partially flooded by the river, partially occupied by things that moved in when the original residents moved up. It has three distinct sections: the Drowned Market (flooded; swimming and underwater combat), the Catacombs (undead; Necromancer and Cleric content), and the Old Vault (dungeon boss area; the thing guarding the vault has been there since before the new city was built).

**Named:** Vault Keeper Aldren (level 30 undead former city official) — drops a merchant ring (CHA bonus); rare banker's ledger that starts a chain quest.

## Services
- Trade Quarter exchange: best player-to-player trading infrastructure in the game; auction house design target; pricing information for all zones
- Bard Colleges (three of them, each with a different specialty): Bard class trainers; Twist technique instruction; instrument-crafting workshop
- Rogue Guild (formally "The Greyveil Commerce Association"): Rogue trainers; Lockpicking advanced; Poison Making for eligible classes; fence for looted goods
- The Neutral Arcane Society: Enchanter and Magician trainers available to any alignment; does not ask questions
- General trainers across all standard tradeskills; no faction gate for most services

## Faction Notes
Greyveil uses a standing system rather than alignment tiers. Characters accumulate Greyveil Standing through trade volume, quest completion, and notable contributions to the city. High standing unlocks the Old Quarter, private services, and senior guild access.

Alignment is almost irrelevant here — an Evil character with high Greyveil Standing gets better access than a Good character who has never done business in the city. The one exception: characters at Evil tier who have attacked Greyveil-faction NPCs are flagged and the guard will remove them. The city tolerates everything except direct threats to the city's interests.
