# World — Zone Index

The world of Project Dawn is divided into named zones. Each zone is a discrete area with its own ecology, faction presence, enemy types, and level range. Zones connect to each other via zone transitions (load screens, portals, or geographic passages).

## Zone Design Principles

**Level range is a guideline, not a hard gate.** Solo characters at the low end of a range struggle; groups near the high end find the content easy. The overlap between zones is intentional — players move when they are ready, not when a number tells them to.

**Faction matters at zone level.** Good-aligned characters face hostility in Vel'Sharath. Evil-aligned characters are unwelcome in Aelindra or the Dwarven Holds. Greyveil is the major neutral city — almost anyone can operate there, which makes it the trade and social hub of the mid-game.

**Each zone has at least one dungeon.** The overworld content of each zone provides leveling experience. The dungeon inside or adjacent provides the group content, named enemies, and best loot for that zone's tier.

**Zones have day/night ecology.** Undead content spikes at night. Certain rare spawns only appear at specific times. Safe resting spots matter because the world changes around you.
