# Ashfang Hold
*Evil Starting Area — Levels 1–10 — Ogre / Troll / Half-Ogre*

## Overview
The proving ground for the eastern races. Ashfang Hold is a fortified Ogre warlord camp in the western edge of the Eastern Badlands — close enough to human territory to raid, far enough in to be left alone. Trolls and Half-Ogres pass through and are tolerated; the Hold is Ogre-run, but the eastern badlands are shared territory and Warlord Kurash is practical enough to know that allies are worth more than enemies at close range.

New characters start here because this is where the eastern races end up: born into the Hold, captured and integrated into it, or pointed here by whoever raised them as the place where you learn what you need to survive. The Hold does not coddle. It evaluates.

## Setting
Bone-post perimeter on high ground overlooking a broken scrubland of red dirt, dry creek beds, and scattered rock formations. The Hold smells of fire, rendered fat, and old blood. At night the fire pit at the camp center is visible for miles, which is deliberate — the Hold wants to be found by things that think they can take it. Those things are usually wrong.

The proving grounds run along the south face. The shaman circle occupies the north edge, away from the noise. Between them is the clan camp: the warlord's tent at center, supply pits dug into the earth, a trophy wall on the east face that is taken seriously by everyone in the camp.

## Dungeon — The Bone Arroyo
*Levels 6–10.*

A deep-cut canyon to the northeast that the Stoneback clan has fortified into a layered ambush zone. The Hold has been trading skirmishes with the Stoneback for three seasons. The dungeon is the Stoneback's home ground — going in to clear it out is the zone's capstone quest. A three-section dungeon: arroyo approach (solo viable at 8+), the canyon fort (group content), and the Stoneback Warlord's chamber at the back.

**Named:** Stoneback Warlord Drokk (level 10) — drops a two-handed maul and a clan-mark amulet that opens dialogue options in the Eastern Badlands.

## Services

- Shaman trainer (Grartha; Shaman class skills levels 1–10)
- Combat trainer (Brekka the Scarred; Warrior / Shadow Knight skills)
- Beast Master trainer (available; no NPC name established)
- Cooking fire (regen bonus for food crafted here)

## Faction Notes
The Hold operates on a single internal faction: Ironmaw Clan standing. It rises through quests and proven combat, falls if you lose fights or steal from other Hold members. High standing unlocks Grartha's deeper ancestor-spirit quests and Kurash's personal storyline.

External factions: Stoneback Clan (hostile by default, can shift via specific quest resolution), Eastern Badlands Neutral Traders (accessible at Hold reputation Tier 2+). Good-aligned city factions (Greenshire, Aelindra) are automatically hostile to Ogre and Troll characters regardless of alignment choices — this is a known limitation of those factions, not a character failing.
