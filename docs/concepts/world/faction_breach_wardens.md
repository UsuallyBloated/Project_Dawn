# The Breach Wardens — Faction Profile
*Neutral faction. The Breach gate and point-tracking authority.*

---

## Overview

The Breach Wardens are a neutral organization that administers the Breach gate — the fortified pass through the central mountain range that serves as the only navigable land route between the western Good-aligned territories and the eastern Evil-aligned badlands. They manage the six-hour control windows, maintain the point ledger, operate the toll booth, and ensure that the fighting between the Vanguard and Iron Camp factions stays within the agreed-upon rules of engagement.

They were not always a formal organization. They began as a collective of merchants who needed the pass open regardless of who controlled it, hired guards, and gradually codified their role into something that both warring factions found useful enough to not destroy.

---

## Structure

The Wardens have no formal chain of command that either major faction has been shown. Internally:

**The Ledger-Keepers** — The clerks who run the point board. They are unarmed and protected by treaty from both sides. Harming a Ledger-Keeper is the fastest way to be ejected from the Breach by both factions simultaneously.

**The Gate-Guard** — Warden soldiers who enforce the toll and manage traffic through the gate. They are armed and permitted to use force against anyone who attempts to rush the gate or harm Warden personnel. Their neutrality extends to their weapons — they will fight anyone who violates the gate's terms, regardless of faction.

**The Warden-Captain** — The current Warden-Captain is a Half-Elf named Sereth Dolan who has held the position for eleven years. She has survived three assassination attempts, two of which were by parties whose faction affiliation she did not confirm publicly. She sleeps lightly.

**The Arbitrators** — Senior Wardens who handle disputes that arise in the Breach. If a player claims their kill was stolen, or a faction claims the other is violating the terms of engagement, an Arbitrator investigates and rules. Their rulings are enforced by both factions' commanders through an agreement the Wardens enforce with the point ledger: factions that ignore Arbitrator rulings lose accumulated points.

---

## The Treaty

The Breach Wardens operate under a standing treaty with both factions that predates the current commanders on either side. Key terms:

- The Wardens administer point-tracking in good faith with access to both camps for verification
- Neither faction may attack Warden personnel; a Warden who is killed triggers automatic point forfeiture and a 24-hour ceasefire period
- The toll gate is Warden territory; all traffic through it submits to Warden jurisdiction regardless of faction
- The Underbreach access hatch is Warden-controlled; the controlling faction gets access; this is determined by the point ledger and is not subject to dispute
- Both factions acknowledge the Wardens' right to expel individual players from the Breach for terms violations

The treaty does not say anything about what happens if a third faction appears. This omission is deliberate on the Wardens' part — they find hypotheticals unhelpful and prefer to negotiate the specific situation when it arises.

---

## Economics

The Wardens run the Breach as a business. Income sources:
- **Toll fees** — Opposing-faction travelers pay significant tolls to pass; controlling faction passes free; neutral travelers pay a standard fee
- **Supply vendor** — The Warden post runs a basic supplies vendor accessible to all factions at standard (not faction-adjusted) prices
- **Arbitration fees** — Dispute resolution carries a small fee paid by the losing party
- **Information** — The Wardens know everything that happens in the Breach and occasionally sell summary intelligence to parties who ask correctly; they do not sell tactical information that would affect the current control window; they do sell historical and logistical information

The Wardens are profitable. This is intentional — profitable neutrals are harder to destroy than principled ones.

---

## Breach Standing (Player Reputation)

Breach Standing is a separate reputation track from faction alignment or city standing. It rises through:
- Completing Breach dailies (Warden-issued, available to both factions)
- Winning control windows (the controlling faction gains passive standing)
- Positive Arbitrator rulings (resolving disputes correctly)
- Extended time at the Breach (passive gain for active presence)

High Breach Standing unlocks:
- **Rank 1 (500):** Access to the Warden neutral vendor (non-faction-gated Breach gear)
- **Rank 2 (1500):** The questline about the Gate-Warden's registry
- **Rank 3 (3000):** The Warden-Captain will speak candidly; access to certain Arbitrator-level information
- **Rank 4 (5000):** The Wardens offer a title: "Recognized." This means nothing mechanically and everything socially within the Breach community.

---

## The Gate-Warden Question

The Wardens know the Gate-Warden is in the Underbreach. They know it is not a Warden construct — it predates their organization by an unknown amount of time. They found it when they first established the Underbreach access policy and discovered that something was already down there.

The Wardens' position: the Gate-Warden is the Underbreach's problem. Their jurisdiction ends at the hatch. What happens below the hatch is the controlling faction's concern.

The Warden-Captain privately believes the Gate-Warden is a more significant problem than the treaty's structure allows her to officially acknowledge. She has been quietly funding research into what it is. She has not found anything conclusive. The questline that unlocks at Breach Standing 1500 is, functionally, her commissioning players to do this research for her without having to put it in the official ledger.

## Quest Hooks

- **The Missing Ledger Entry** — A kill was registered in the point system that the Ledger-Keepers cannot account for. Neither faction claims it. The point was added by someone with Warden credentials. The Arbitrator needs to know how.
- **The Expelled Player** — A player was expelled from the Breach last season and is trying to get back in. They claim the Arbitrator's ruling was based on false testimony. They might be right. The Warden-Captain needs someone who was not there to look at the evidence.
- **The Third Camp** — Someone has set up a small camp inside the Breach's boundaries that doesn't belong to either faction. The Wardens cannot expel them — the treaty only covers the two factions, and this camp claims to represent neither. The Warden-Captain needs this resolved before both factions notice.
