# The Bonecourt
*Evil Starting Area — Levels 1–10 — Kel`varath*

## Overview
A partially reclaimed Dominion outpost in the southeastern jungle, occupied by the Bonewrights — the most field-oriented of the three surviving Kel`varath necromantic orders. The Bonecourt is not a city or a settlement in any comfortable sense; it is a training site with walls, staffed by scholars who happen to also be effective at killing things.

New Kel`varath characters arrive here through one of several routes: born to a family with Bonewright ties, sent by a surviving Dominion-lineage elder, or simply found in the jungle by a Bonewright patrol and assessed as worth keeping. The Bonewrights are not sentimental about the intake process. They are thorough.

## Setting
The outpost sits in a clearing maintained by regular clearing of fast-growing jungle growth — a constant task shared among students. The outer wall is original Dominion stonework: grey-black basalt, carved with old Dominion script that students are expected to be able to read by the end of their first month. The temple base at center is structurally sound through the first two floors; the upper levels collapsed sometime before the Bonewrights arrived and have not been rebuilt.

The jungle presses close on all sides. At night it is audible — insects, predators, the specific silence that predators make when they're close. The Bonewrights consider this a useful part of the training environment.

## Dungeon — The Collapsed East Wing
*Levels 6–10.*

The fallen section of the original Dominion outpost has been slowly occupied from below. Something moved in through the deep foundation cracks — the Bonewrights call it the Basement Problem and have been studying it for two years without resolving it. The dungeon is the foundation network beneath the east wing: tight corridors, Dominion-era traps still active, and the Basement Problem itself at the lowest level.

**Named:** The Unbriefed (level 10) — an animated construct from the Dominion era operating without orders, which means it defaults to the last standing orders it received three centuries ago. Those orders are hostile to all non-Kel`varath. It drops an Old Dominion Seal usable as a faction token in the Ruined Dominion Capital zone.

## Services
- Bone-Tender Ixara (supplies, basic healing items, jungle herb knowledge)
- Bonewright Teaching Hall (Necromancer / Shaman class trainer, levels 1–10)
- Draskrix the Patient (Warrior / Monk / Rogue combat trainer)
- Specimen altar (Necromancer: animate practice skeleton, daily use)
- Jungle forage site (Alchemy / Shaman herb gathering nearby)

## Faction Notes
The Bonewrights are the primary faction here. Standing rises through quests and demonstrated competence, falls through failure or theft. High Bonewright standing unlocks Vethkar's deeper Dominion-history questline and access to the locked archive on the upper floor.

External factions: the Soulcarvers (rival Kel`varath order, hostile by default, specific quest can shift to cold neutral), the Ash Scribes (distant, neutral, become relevant at level 25+ in the Ruined Dominion Capital), Southeastern Marshlands Troll tribes (hostile; territorial overlap; no diplomatic resolution available at this level range).

Most Good-aligned and many Neutral-aligned city factions are automatically hostile to Kel`varath characters. Greyveil is an exception — the city is neutral enough to tolerate Kel`varath if reputation is managed carefully.
