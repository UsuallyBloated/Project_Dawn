# The Sealed Archive — Aelindra Mystery Document
*The section of the Aelindra Root Level archive sealed four hundred years ago. The reason given: "the material is not safe to read in its current state."*

---

## What Is Known

The Aelindra archive contains approximately three thousand years of records — the most continuous historical record in the western territories. All of it is accessible to researchers with sufficient standing except one section: twelve volumes covering a period of approximately forty years, four centuries ago, during which something happened that the Elf scholars of the time decided should not be available for general reading.

The seal is a physical one — the relevant shelves are behind a door in the archive's deepest level, the door is locked with a mechanism that requires a specific key, and the key was given to the Grove-Keeper of the time with instructions to hold it until the material was safe. The current Grove-Keeper Silindra has the key. The current Grove-Keeper Silindra has had the key for fifty-two years and has not opened the seal. She was not told when the material would be safe. She was told she would know.

She does not know. She is increasingly uncertain whether "you will know" was sincere guidance or a way of avoiding the question.

---

## The Forty-Year Period

The sealed section covers approximately Year 403 to Year 443 of the Common Reckoning — four hundred to four hundred and forty years ago. The events of this period that are recorded in *other* archives (Greyveil's civic records, Dwarven Hold transaction ledgers, Dominion War predecessors) show nothing particularly remarkable. A drought in the eastern territories. A succession dispute in a Gnome city-state that was resolved peacefully. Trade fluctuations consistent with a mild recession.

The Aelindra archive's sealed section covers the same period. Forty years of Aelindra's records, from a time when nothing remarkable was happening anywhere else, have been deemed unsafe to read.

This is either an enormous coincidence or evidence that something happened in or around Aelindra during those forty years that left no trace in any external record.

---

## The Dreaming Grove Connection

Players who complete the Dreaming Grove dungeon and reach the core chamber receive, among other things, a fragment of the Dreaming Warden's knowledge. The fragment includes a reference: "the visitors who came when the sky was wrong." The phrase does not expand when asked. The Dreaming Warden does not elaborate on things it has already said.

The phrase "the sky was wrong" appears once in the accessible portions of the Aelindra archive — in a marginal notation in a botanical survey dated Year 407, which notes that the survey team could not work during a period when "the sky overhead was wrong in a manner we lacked language to describe." The notation is four words long and is not referenced again. The botanist's subsequent records show normal fieldwork resuming in Year 408.

The date: Year 407. Within the sealed period.

---

## Grove-Keeper Silindra

Silindra has been Grove-Keeper for fifty-two years. She inherited the sealed archive key from her predecessor, who inherited it from his predecessor, who was the one the original seal instructions were given to. The chain of custody is four people long. Each of them held the key, was told they would know when it was safe, and passed it on without opening it.

Silindra's position: she is not going to be the one who decides on her own that four centuries of her predecessors' caution is no longer warranted. She requires either a clear indicator that the situation has changed or authorization from a body with legitimate authority over the question. The legitimate authority, in Aelindra's governance, is the Elder Council, and the Elder Council has not discussed the sealed archive in two hundred years because the topic was tabled in Year 647 CR pending "additional information about the status of the matter."

The "additional information" was never provided. The topic was never reopened. There is now a governing body that has formally tabled a topic and has no memory of having done so.

## The Starwatch Connection

The Starwatch Platform in Aelindra's canopy level has a calibration railing with marks dating back to the tower's construction — over two thousand years old, used for astronomical alignment. Players who examine it carefully find one mark that is different: recently made, in a notation style that matches neither the original marks nor anything in the current Elf tradition.

It is Architect notation. It was placed within the last fifty years, which is determinable from the weathering of the mark against the older ones.

What the notation says: it is a date in the Architect system. Cross-referencing with Varek's decoded Architect notation (available to players who have completed Varek's Spire content): the date corresponds to approximately forty years before the Common Reckoning's Year 403. Forty years before the beginning of the sealed period.

Someone marked the Starwatch Platform in Architect notation with a date approximately forty years before something happened that the Elves sealed for four hundred years. The mark is recent. The mark uses Architect notation. No living Elf uses Architect notation.

## The Unsealing Quest

**Prerequisites:** Completion of the Dreaming Grove dungeon and access to the core chamber.

**Quest giver:** The Dreaming Warden, indirectly — the fragment of knowledge provided in the core chamber contains a phrase that Silindra, when shown it, recognizes as the indicator she has been waiting for. She does not say this immediately. She sits with it for a moment. Then she says: "I think this is it. I think this is how I know."

**The unsealing:** Silindra opens the archive door with the key she has carried for fifty-two years. The lock works. The door opens. Inside: twelve volumes in good condition because the archive's preservation is excellent, and one additional item — a sealed letter, addressed in a hand that does not match any known historical Elf writing style, to "whoever finds this first, after."

**The volumes:** Forty years of Aelindra's records, covering what happened when "the sky was wrong." The contents unlock the sealed archive as readable material for any character with appropriate archive standing. The full text is accessible; summarized highlights are available to players without the full archive standing.

**What the volumes contain (summary):**
- The "sky wrong" event lasted approximately eighteen months, centered on Year 407
- During this period, several things happened in and around the Dreaming Grove that the archivists of the time documented carefully and then sealed
- The Dreaming Warden was more active during this period than in any other recorded window — it spoke to multiple parties, unprompted, with messages that were recorded verbatim
- At least two people went into the Dreaming Grove during the eighteen months and did not come back out in the way they went in; the records describe this without fully explaining it
- The sealed period ends when the "sky wrong" event ended; the final entry is a note that the Dreaming Grove has returned to normal and the archivists are sealing the record "until the circumstances recur, at which point this documentation will be needed"

**The letter:** Addressed "to whoever finds this first, after." Written in the Architect notation that the Starwatch Platform uses. When decoded (requires Architect knowledge from the Third Digger chain or Varek's notes): a single paragraph describing what the sky-wrong event was, what caused it, and what the correct response is when it happens again.

The paragraph ends: "It will happen again. The interval is not fixed. When the sky is wrong, open the Grove and read this. We have left what you need in the usual place."

The usual place is not specified. "The usual place" implies the reader should know. Players who have completed the Architect site chain will have encountered this phrase before — it appears in the Architect notation at the Sunken Ziggurat's deepest level.

---

## What the Sky-Wrong Event Was

The full answer is in the sealed volumes and the letter. It is not documented here — it is designed to be discovered in-game through the quest chain. Sufficient context for writers/designers:

The sky-wrong event was an observable astronomical anomaly — something visible to anyone looking up, not a metaphor. It lasted eighteen months. It was caused by the Architect system operating in a mode the Architects called the Calibration State. The Calibration State occurs at irregular intervals (the letter is correct that it is not fixed) and is the Architect system checking its own status — the equivalent of the machinery running a diagnostic.

During Calibration State: the Dreaming Grove becomes more active (the Grove is an Architect site — the Dreaming Warden is an Architect construct, not an Elf one). Certain connections between the Architect sites strengthen. The remainder protocol becomes legible in ways it normally is not.

The "correct response" in the letter: document what the Dreaming Warden says. Bring it to the survey terminus (in the Dominion Vault). The terminus will know what to do with it.

The last Calibration State was four hundred years ago. The next one is not scheduled — or rather, its schedule is in the survey terminus, and the terminus has not been accessible since before the Dominion War.
