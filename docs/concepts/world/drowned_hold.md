# The Drowned Hold
*Maritime Dungeon — Levels 20–30 — Dockmasters' Compact · Harrowmere*

## Overview

The Drowned Hold is visible from Harrowmere's docks on clear days: a dark structure on a seamount in Harrow Bay, a half-mile offshore, built of the same coursing and buttress style as Ixareth-Kul's harbor district but three centuries older in its abandonment. The Dockmasters' Compact operates licensed expedition boats from the maritime quarter. All three expeditions on record returned. What makes the incident logs read differently from most dungeon records is what the expeditions chose not to say.

The structure has five levels that Compact survey records acknowledge and one level below that they don't. The first licensed expedition turned back at Level 4 due to losses. The second turned back at Level 3 on account of the garrison's behavioral patterns, which the expedition's Witch Hunter described as *"purposeful in a way that undead are not, ordinarily, purposeful."* The third expedition filed no narrative account. The harbormaster's incident records note only that they came back short of supplies and short of conversation.

The undead garrison is Dominion-era military. It does not respond to standard necromantic override signals. Its last confirmed orders were dated Year 547 CR — the first year of the Dominion War — and nothing in those orders appears to have included a provision for the war ending.

## Setting

From the water, the Drowned Hold sits on a seamount that rises cleanly from the bay floor, as if placed. The Dominion construction begins at the waterline and ascends two floors above it, heavy dark stone with iron-framed emplacements that have not corroded in three centuries of salt air in the way iron normally corrodes. The pirate fortress built on top of the Dominion structure has corroded at the expected rate. The contrast in weathering is visible from the boat approach: two different things happened here, at different times, and they did not happen to the same building.

Inside, it is dry below the waterline. No current theory accounts for this.

At the deepest accessible level, the Dominion construction transitions to something older. The stone is fitted to tolerances that differ from Dominion work in the same way that Dominion work differs from everything built in the last three hundred years. The temperature at this level is consistent and warm in a way that has nothing to do with Harrow Bay's volcanic shelf, which is inactive.

## Dungeon — The Drowned Hold

*Levels 22–30. Maritime access from Harrowmere. Compact standing required for expedition boat licensing. Full dungeon access opens after Captain Morreth's questline has been initiated in the Harrowmere zone.*

The dungeon has six levels. The first is the pirate fortress the Harrowmere zone records. The other five are what the licensed expeditions found underneath it.

**Level 1 — The Upper Fortification** (22–24):

The fortification that Captain Morreth the Returned and her crew have occupied since the sea brought them back approximately a decade ago. This level is documented in the Harrowmere zone account — the three-part structure of the above-ground fort, the sea cave system beneath, and the Hold's vault where Morreth waits. Players who resolve Morreth's questline through Harrowmere and choose the full resolution (rather than the destroy outcome) find this level navigable without combat.

The access to Level 2 is in the Hold's deepest vault, behind the pirate captain's position, under flagstones that the pirates evidently knew about and equally evidently never investigated. The passage is Dominion stonework — older, different, more carefully made than anything above it.

**Level 2 — The Entry Works** (24–26):

The Dominion installation begins. The transition from pirate construction to Dominion construction is abrupt — not architectural blending, but material discontinuity. The Dominion built downward through the seamount; the pirate fortress was built upward from what the Dominion left above the waterline.

Dominion undead patrol in watch rotations that have not been adjusted in three hundred years. Necromantic override signals function partially on this level — a Necromancer with high casting skill can measure the interference. The frequency distortion is partial here and complete one level down. The expedition Witch Hunter's personal notes, available from Harbormaster Crost's incident file at sufficient standing, contain the earliest recorded observation of this effect and a specific phrase about its character that the official Compact record declined to reproduce.

**Level 3 — The Command Chambers** (26–28):

The garrison at Level 3 responds to intrusion as a unit rather than as individuals. Not individual alert cascading through proximity — collective tactical adjustment, distributed and simultaneous. Players who observe carefully before engaging will notice: the garrison's members do not respond based on line-of-sight proximity. They respond based on information being shared between them through a mechanism that is not visible. The necromantic frequency interference at this level is complete — standard Necromancer command spells function normally at Level 2 and fail silently here.

The command chambers contain operational records: patrol assignments, supply manifests, the garrison roster. The roster lists one hundred and twelve personnel. Across all three expeditions and all Compact survey attempts, the active undead count has never exceeded forty-three. The discrepancy has not been explained by any expedition team. No expedition team has proposed an explanation in a filed report.

The first expedition turned back here. Four of six returned. The expedition leader's personal account — available from Crost's files at high Compact standing — reads: *"They didn't rush us. They took positions and waited for us to come to them, and one of them — the tall one, with the rank marks — said something in old Kel`varath. I don't speak old Kel`varath. Nobody did. We had an interpreter two levels up. By the time we understood that we needed her, three of ours were down."*

First Captain Voss issues the formal Dominion military challenge before engaging: four words in old Kel`varath followed by one word a translator renders as "authorization." Players with Kel`varath language skill (50+) may respond with a delay request; this extends pre-combat dialogue by one exchange. Voss identifies himself, states his standing orders, and provides the date those orders were last confirmed: Year 547 CR. He does not appear to know the war ended.

The Dominion Officer's Seal, dropped by Voss, is the key to Level 5.

**Level 5 — The Approach** (29–30):

A single corridor, forty feet long, ending at a sealed threshold. The threshold is Dominion construction on the far side of the doorframe; Architect construction on the near side. Two different things, built at different times, sharing a doorway.

The garrison does not follow players into this corridor. Not because they cannot — nothing visible prevents them. Their patrol routes terminate at the corridor entrance. Whatever their orders say about this level, the orders apparently say: *do not proceed further.* They have been not proceeding further for three hundred years.

This is the observation the third expedition would not discuss. Not the garrison's combat effectiveness. Not the depth. This: the garrison that had been tactically adaptive on every prior level stood at the corridor entrance and stopped, and the officer — First Captain Voss — faced the threshold and did not cross it.

The Dominion Officer's Seal satisfies the threshold's Dominion access condition. The threshold opens. On the far side: Architect construction, and warmth, and something the third expedition's scout would describe later at the Grey Anchor in terms that have nothing to do with fire.

**Level 6 — The Monitoring Station** (30):

```
    │         THE MONITORING STATION              │
    │                                              │
    │  [six-sided chamber; Architect stonework]    │
    │  [notation: all six walls, floor, ceiling —  │
    │   continuous; no visible seams]              │
    │  [the remainder — center of the chamber;     │
    │   warm; consistent; active]                  │
    │                                              │
    │  THE THRESHOLD WATCHER — level 30            │
    │  Architect construct; neutral unless the     │
    │  notation space is physically damaged        │
    │                                              │
    │  loot: Foundation Survey Fragment            │
    │        Monitoring Station Record             │
    │        Network Status Entry (partial read)   │
    └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The stone here has not been touched by three centuries of Dominion occupation above it. No scratches, no deposits, no wear of any kind. Whatever process maintains this surface has been running continuously for longer than the Dominion has been gone.

The notation covering all six walls, the floor, and the ceiling is a monitoring station record: the Drowned Hold's function in the Architect network was to track the status of other nodes and transmit that status toward the survey terminus. Players with Architect notation skill (50+) can read partial entries — each corresponds to a network node. Nodes players have visited show statuses consistent with what they observed there. Nodes players haven't visited are listed as active.

One entry differs from the others. A node to the southwest, in deep water, is listed with a designation and a status indicator in a different format from every other entry: *PENDING: FINAL CALIBRATION.* The designation, fully decoded, translates as something like "anchor" or "the closing condition." Players who have encountered Architect notation at other sites find this designation familiar — it appears in cross-references at multiple sites, always pointing toward something the notation never defines at the site where it appears.

The remainder in the chamber's center is warm in the way that all the remainders are warm — but where every other remainder has the quality of waiting, this one is doing something while waiting. It is transmitting. Slowly, continuously, toward the survey terminus. Players who have visited other remainder sites find the difference in character immediately legible, and find it uncomfortable in a way that requires thought to locate: it is not threatening. It simply changes the quality of presence from patience to purpose.

## Services

**Compact Expedition Office** (Harrowmere maritime quarter; not on the island): Books licensed expeditions; requires Compact standing at Working Relationship tier or above. Harbormaster Crost operates from here. Expedition equipment — climbing gear, Enduring Breath components, Kel`varath language aids — is available for purchase. The incident records are filed here.

No services on the island. The structure has no defensible rest point. Players must return to Harrowmere to recover. All three expedition accounts cite this as a significant operational constraint.

## Faction Notes

The **Dockmasters' Compact** is the only faction with standing access to the Drowned Hold. Expedition licenses require Compact standing at Working Relationship tier or above. The maritime office manages the licensing process without comment on expedition findings; Harbormaster Crost manages the incident records and shares their contents at high standing.

Players who reach Level 6 and read the monitoring station notation — specifically the Network Status Entry — trigger the Compact's maritime office to issue Maren sailing instructions. This does not happen through a formal quest flag. Harbormaster Crost receives a summary of what was found, takes it to the maritime office, and the maritime office releases instructions they have apparently been holding until this condition was met. Neither Crost nor the maritime office explains the connection between the Drowned Hold's Architect foundation and Maren access. The Compact's maritime office has declined to explain the policy. The policy exists. Both the Drowned Hold and the Kel'Maren Ruin are on Architect foundations. The cartographer's atlas records this adjacency without concluding from it.

## Key NPCs

**Harbormaster Jeven Crost** — Human, former Compact maritime officer, late fifties. He has been keeping the expedition incident records longer than he expected to be keeping them, and they have changed his understanding of the structure in ways he will share only with people he trusts. He knows the Drowned Hold better than anyone who hasn't been inside it. At Compact standing 600, he shares the official incident files — the expedition leader's formal account and the Witch Hunter's report summary. At standing 1000, he shares his personal notes: the expedition leader's private account of Level 4, and the full Witch Hunter's field notes rather than the summary the Compact accepted.

He is also navigating a separate problem that he cannot resolve through official channels. Warden-Captain Sereth Dolan informed him that a Compact Dockmaster has been selling shipping intelligence to the Iron Camp. He has confirmed this independently. Moving it through Compact internal channels would alert the compromised Dockmaster. He needs a player he trusts who can carry information between him and Dolan without the information passing through compromised hands. This quest becomes available once players have encountered both Crost and Dolan's questlines.

**Dessa Vorn** — Half-Elf Rogue, level 28. Third expedition's scout. She has been at the Grey Anchor in Harrowmere's Tavern Row since the expedition returned. Not dramatically haunted — quieter than she was before, and she charges for information, and the charges scale with depth. Upper levels: standard fee. Level 3: costs more, with specific detail about the garrison's collective response that matches no Dominion doctrine the expedition's scholars could place. Level 5: she takes the money, and then she says: *"The garrison stopped. Just stopped, at the corridor entry. And the room past the door was warm. Not fire-warm. Not body-warm. The temperature of something that has been waiting, and is still waiting, and doesn't mind that you're there."* She will not describe the remainder directly. Players who ask what she means will get: *"You'll know when you're there."*

She is willing to return if players ask. She says this with certainty and without apparent hesitation. She is the only member of the third expedition who will say this.

## Cross-Zone Connections

- **Harrowmere** — The zone this is accessed from. Captain Morreth's questline (documented in the Harrowmere zone) resolves Level 1. Harbormaster Crost's information is the expedition context layer. Compact standing required for expedition licensing is built entirely in Harrowmere.
- **Architect questline** — Level 6's monitoring station is a network node. The Foundation Survey Fragment and the Network Status Entry advance the Architect chain. The PENDING: FINAL CALIBRATION entry for the anchor node is the first Architect-authored documentation linking the Drowned Hold to the Rising Shell — the notation does not name the anchor node, but the position matches.
- **The Breach** — Crost's intelligence leak problem connects to Warden-Captain Dolan's standing concern about the compromised Dockmaster. Players who have encountered both NPCs can serve as the channel between them without the information passing through the Compact's internal structure.
- **Maren — The Varethian Shore** — Completing the Drowned Hold's Architect questline (reaching Level 6, reading the Network Status Entry) triggers the Compact maritime office to release Maren sailing instructions. The reason is not documented. The implication is present.
- **The Dominion Vault** — The Drowned Hold's monitoring station is one of the node survey reports the survey terminus requires. Its transmission has been reaching the terminus continuously for the full period the Calibration State has been absent.
- **The Rising Shell** — The PENDING: FINAL CALIBRATION entry identifies the anchor node. Players who subsequently reach the Rising Shell's Anchor Chamber recognize the node designation and understand they are standing inside what the monitoring station was watching.
