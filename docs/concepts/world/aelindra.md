# Aelindra
*Elf City / Enchanted Forest — Levels 10–20 — High Elf / Arcane Schools*

## Overview
Aelindra is built where the trees agreed to stop growing and the stone agreed to hold the light. The city is organic architecture and deliberate scholarship in the same building — spires of pale stone grown through with living wood, libraries that extend into the canopy, garden districts that function as outdoor classrooms. It is beautiful in the way that places become beautiful when people with long lives and deep aesthetics have been working on them for several centuries.

The Arcane Schools of Aelindra are the most prestigious magic institutions in the game. Wizards come here to study. Enchanters and Magicians can advance their training here in ways the smaller guilds can't offer. The city is Good-aligned — not aggressively so, but the guard presence is real and the standards for admission to the inner districts are quietly enforced.

## Setting
The city occupies a valley in a section of old-growth forest that predates the Ardenmoor by perhaps a century. The outer districts are open to travelers; the inner districts — where the Schools operate and the senior Elven families maintain their halls — require standing with the city faction or a formal introduction. The zone includes the forest surrounding the valley, which provides the leveling content. The city itself is a hub with services.

## Dungeon — The Shattered Archives
*Levels 15–20.*

Something got into the deep library. The Shattered Archives were the oldest section of the Arcane Schools' collection — texts that the living faculty hadn't catalogued in decades because the section had been sealed after an incident thirty years ago. The incident apparently wasn't resolved, just sealed. The dungeon is pure caster content: most enemies here use magic, most traps are magical, and the Witch Hunter and Enchanter classes have specific advantages here that pure melee classes don't.

**Named:** The Index Specter (level 20 arcane construct) — drops a staff and an INT-boosting ring; rare Enchanter focus item on alternate drop table.

## Services
- Outer district vendors: general goods, spell components, cloth armor (good variety)
- Arcane Schools (inner district, faction-gated): Wizard/Magician/Sorcerer/Enchanter trainers at maximum rank; spellbook copying service; scroll library with lore quests
- Temple of Aelindra: Cleric and Druid trainers; healing services
- Rune Market: Runecarving materials; Dark Elf Runecarving trainers (exiled scholars, operating in the outer district with complicated standing)

## Faction Notes
The Aelindra City faction governs access to the inner districts and Schools. Elves start with a bonus to this faction. Humans, Half-Elves, and Gnomes begin neutral and work toward access. Dark Elves start unfavorable and need significant quest work before the inner districts become available — the history between the High Elf and Dark Elf traditions is present here in NPC dialogue and in guard behavior.

Evil-aligned characters can be in the outer district. They cannot access the inner district regardless of race. The Schools do not negotiate this.
