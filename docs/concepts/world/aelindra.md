# Aelindra
*Elf City / Enchanted Forest — Levels 10–25 — High Elf / Arcane Schools*

## Overview

Aelindra is built where the trees agreed to stop growing and the stone agreed to hold the light. The city is organic architecture and deliberate scholarship in the same building — spires of pale stone grown through with living wood, libraries that extend into the canopy, garden districts that function as outdoor classrooms. It is beautiful in the way that places become beautiful when people with long lives and deep aesthetics have been working on them for several centuries.

The Arcane Schools of Aelindra are the most prestigious magic institutions in the game. Wizards come here to study. Enchanters and Magicians can advance their training here in ways the smaller guilds can't offer. The city is Good-aligned — not aggressively so, but the guard presence is real and the standards for admission to the inner districts are quietly enforced.

Below the city's working root system is the Root Quarter, managed by Grove-Keeper Silindra. Below the Root Quarter is the Dreaming Grove. The city was built above these things. The Elves know this and have a considered relationship with it that mostly involves respectful distance and one Grove-Keeper who manages the interface.

## Setting

The city occupies a valley in a section of old-growth forest that predates Ardenmoor by perhaps a century. The outer districts are open to travelers; the inner districts — where the Schools operate and the senior Elven families maintain their halls — require standing with the city faction or a formal introduction. The zone includes the forest surrounding the valley, which provides the leveling content. The city itself is a hub with services.

**The Outer District:** Open to all who arrive without obvious hostile intent. Vendors, the Rune Market, guest accommodations, the Temple. Also where the Dark Elf exile community operates — a population of scholars who left Vel'Sharath over the last century for reasons they describe as academic and which the Aelindra guard describes as "present and monitored."

**The Inner District:** Arcane Schools, senior Elven halls, the library levels above the Shattered Archives. Faction-gated. The standards are maintained consistently enough that what looks like courtesy is actually policy.

**The Starwatch Platform:** A canopy-level observation deck on Aelindra's northern face, used for astronomical record-keeping. In Year 812, Grove-Keeper Silindra found Architect notation carved into its upper corner during vine-clearing maintenance. She filed a report with the Sealed Archive. The Archive confirmed receipt. This was thirty-five years ago. The report has not been acted on.

**The Root Quarter:** The zone's lowest accessible level above the Dreaming Grove. Silindra Vaethas manages it — the walking paths, the marker trees, the transition zones. It is quieter than the rest of the city in a way that is not just a function of depth. Something about the Root Quarter is paying attention. Players who spend time there notice this and then stop noticing it, which is the Root Quarter doing what it is supposed to do.

**The Sealed Archive:** A section of the inner library sealed four hundred years ago, Year 447 CR. The four keys required to open it exist somewhere in the world; the Archive's own documentation says "you will know" when the time is right. The sealing was justified in writing as "material not safe to read in its current state." Four centuries of continued sealing suggests either the material's state has not changed or the evaluation has not been revisited. Silindra has opinions about this.

## Dungeon 1 — The Shattered Archives
*Levels 15–20.*

Something got into the deep library thirty years ago. The Shattered Archives were the oldest section of the Arcane Schools' collection — texts that the living faculty hadn't catalogued in decades because access required a senior credential. Thirty years ago, a scholar named Aveth Malorn descended alone with that credential, spent six days in the lowest level, and came back changed. The faculty sealed the section two weeks after his return, when it became clear what changed meant.

Aveth Malorn completed the Dreaming Grove questline twelve years before he descended into the Archives. What the Warden showed him is not documented — he did not speak of it after the Grove, which is unusual but not unheard of. What the faculty documented after the Archive sealing is that he brought something back from the deep library level that was not in the catalogue and that he could not describe, and that three weeks later the Archive's lowest level was producing effects that were not magical in any classifiable sense. The effects were sealed with the section. Aveth Malorn left Aelindra the night before the sealing. His current location is unknown.

The dungeon is pure caster content: enemies here use magic, traps are magical, and Witch Hunter and Enchanter classes have specific advantages. Corrupted Scholars — former students who went too deep before the sealing — are the primary humanoid enemy type. The Index Specter at the bottom is what Aveth Malorn left behind. Not Aveth himself. The impression of what he learned, externalized.

**Named:** The Index Specter (level 20 arcane construct) — drops a staff and an INT-boosting ring; rare Enchanter focus item on alternate drop table. Players who have completed the Dreaming Grove questline receive additional dialogue from the Specter that changes the encounter's character without changing its mechanics.

## Dungeon 2 — The Dreaming Grove
*Levels 18–25. See [dreaming_grove.md in ASCII reference](../ascii/dreaming_grove.md) for full layout.*

Below the Root Quarter. The Grove predates the city. It is Architect-built, which the Aelindra Archivists know and have not advertised, and which Silindra confirmed from the construction proportions in Year 784. The Dreaming Warden has been in the lowest chamber for as long as the Grove has existed. It watches how visitors descend before it speaks.

Access is managed by Silindra — she speaks to every party that wants to go in. She does not refuse entry. She does prepare visitors, in the way that a conversation that gives you accurate information prepares you even when it cannot prepare you for the experience itself.

The Grove's completion reward is a node survey fragment — a partial Architect report on the Dreaming Grove site. Combined with reports from other sites, it contributes to the Calibration State trigger condition documented in the Architect master records.

**Connections:** The Index Specter in the Shattered Archives and the Dreaming Warden in the Grove are not connected — but players who complete both in the same session find that the Specter dialogue (if they have Grove completion) references something the Warden said, which the Specter should not know. Neither dungeon explains this. The Sealed Archive may.

## The Dark Elf Presence

Three Dark Elf scholars have been in Aelindra's outer district for the last fourteen months, operating at the Rune Market under cover of legitimate academic interest. Two are active Aracheth operatives. The third was sent as an operative and has been in Aelindra long enough to have become genuinely uncertain about what she is doing here.

**What they want:** The Sealed Archive. Specifically, they believe the Archive contains records of Architect notation that the Aelindra scholars obtained before the sealing and that Vel'Sharath has been unable to access through other means. The Aracheth web interprets fate as architecture — the Architect network's design philosophy overlaps with Aracheth theology in ways the Vel'Sharath temple hierarchy finds significant. Whether the overlap is coincidence or ancestry is one of the Aracheth inner circle's open questions.

**What players can do:** High-standing players who have completed the poacher questline in Ardenmoor or the Dark Speech questline in Vel'Sharath can speak with the third operative — the uncertain one — before the confrontation. She has been here long enough to have read things in the Rune Market's public collection that her mission briefing didn't account for. She does not defect. She does answer questions she would not answer a month ago.

**Resolution:** Players who expose the operation to the Aelindra guard resolve it with faction standing and a Vel'Sharath penalty. Players who complete the conversation with the third operative and allow her to depart receive a contact in Vel'Sharath who will acknowledge them, once, as having acted with more nuance than expected. The standing with the inner Aracheth hierarchy that flows from this is small but not nothing.

## Key NPCs

**Grove-Keeper Silindra Vaethas** — Elf Druid, level 28, 340 years old. Manages the Root Quarter and the Dreaming Grove interface. Has been inside the Grove twice. Will not go again. Has filed a report about the Starwatch notation and not received a response in thirty-five years. Full profile: [npc_silindra.md](npc_silindra.md).

**Archivist Tessen** — Elf Wizard, level 25, senior inner-district faculty. Holds one of the theoretical keys to the Sealed Archive (the key is a credential, not a physical object). Does not confirm this. Will discuss the Archive's existence in academic terms while denying that the academic terms imply anything practically accessible. He has read Silindra's Starwatch report. He knows what it describes. His "under assessment" responses to her follow-up inquiries are deliberate.

**Rune Market Manager Ceth** — Dark Elf Ranger, level 18, exiled from Vel'Sharath twelve years ago on grounds he describes as "a disagreement about research methods." Runs the Rune Market with correct-seeming neutrality. He knows two of the three Aracheth operatives are in his market and has not reported them because they have not done anything in his market that he can document and because reporting Dark Elf scholars to the Aelindra guard feels like a form of collaboration he is not prepared for. He will tell players they are there if players already know and are asking, which he finds a relief.

## Services

- **Outer district vendors:** general goods, spell components, cloth armor
- **Arcane Schools** (inner district, faction-gated): Wizard/Magician/Sorcerer/Enchanter trainers at maximum rank; spellbook copying; scroll library with lore quests; access to Aveth Malorn's pre-Archive research notes (partial, the rest went with him)
- **Temple of Aelindra:** Cleric and Druid trainers; healing services; Elder Elvish reference texts (cannot be trained here; can be studied toward derivation)
- **Rune Market:** Runecarving materials; exiled Dark Elf scholars as trainers; mixed standing implications
- **Root Quarter (Silindra's office):** Dreaming Grove briefing and access; Starwatch report on request at high standing; Grove-Keeper's mark after sufficient service

## Faction Notes

The Aelindra City faction governs access to the inner districts and Schools. Elves start with a bonus. Humans, Half-Elves, and Gnomes begin neutral and work toward access. Dark Elves start unfavorable and require significant quest work before the inner districts become available — the city's history with Vel'Sharath is present in NPC dialogue and guard behavior.

Evil-aligned characters can operate in the outer district. The inner district is closed to them regardless of race. The Schools do not negotiate this.

**The Sealed Archive and faction:** Players who advance the Architect questline far enough — specifically, who have the Dreaming Grove's node survey fragment and can demonstrate what it represents — unlock a unique faction track with Archivist Tessen. He does not formally open the Archive. He does begin having different conversations. The track ends with him providing a specific piece of information about what the Archive contains and what conditions the sealing document described as the appropriate time to reopen it. The conditions are not time-based. The Architects designed systems that way.
