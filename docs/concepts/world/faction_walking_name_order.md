# The Walking Name Order — Faction Profile
*Neutral-leaning scholarly faction. Ixareth-Kul · Aelindra · The Greyfen · cross-zone.*

---

## Overview

The Walking Name Order was founded in the decades after the Dominion War's end. The collapse produced casualties beyond the military dead: administrative records burned, oral traditions interrupted, a generation of scholars lost, and an unknown quantity of names — of places, families, practices, events — becoming unavailable as the people who held them died without heirs or record. The founders understood that unnamed things are not merely forgotten. They become inaccessible. The work of the Order was originally archival recovery: walking forgotten names back into existence by finding and documenting them.

Forty years of that work produced a pattern no one had anticipated. Every significant recovery eventually pointed toward the same sites.

The Architect focus was not a decision. It was an observation. The surviving fragments of pre-Dominion scholarship, the old ritual scripts, the structural terminology that appeared in documents predating the Dominion by centuries — all of them used a notation system that shared deep structural features with the geometric markings at the Architect sites. The Walking Name's conclusion, reached independently by three of its founding-generation scholars: the things the Order had been recovering were themselves records of something earlier. The archive they had been restoring was a translation layer for something older.

## Identity and Purpose

**Name origin:** "Walking a name" is pre-Dominion Kel`varath — to walk a name back is to restore it to existence through documentation and use. An unnamed death becomes a named one; an unnamed site becomes mapped; an unnamed practice becomes teachable. The founders chose it because they understood that what they were doing was not research in the academic sense. It was retrieval. A name only exists if someone walks it.

The same logic applies to the Architects. The Walking Name's position is that the Architect network is not a historical artifact. It is an addressed communication. *"Whoever finds this first, after"* is not past tense. It is present tense addressed to whoever is currently reading it. The Walking Name takes the word *after* seriously: after means now, for the person reading it. The Architects designed the network to wait for its recipients, not for a date.

**What they are not saying:** The Walking Name does not believe the Architects are physically present and waiting to be awakened. This is a common misreading of their position. Their position is more precise: the network is the Architects in the only form they designed to persist. The communication is real and ongoing. The remainders are warm because something is maintaining the transmission. The Calibration State trigger is completion-driven because the Architects designed it to wait for an engaged recipient, not a patient one. Reading the letter is the activation condition. Mechanical triggering without genuine understanding is what the Final Survey proposes and what the Walking Name considers a category error about what the letter is for.

**What they want:** To receive the communication. To complete the survey correctly, in full, with understanding intact at each node, and to hear what the Architects designed this system to say to whoever found it. Not to activate, not to accelerate, not to extract a result from a system whose process they have not completed. The process is the point.

## Structure

The Walking Name Order has no conventional hierarchy. Rank is not conveyed by time served, seniority, or position. It is tracked by the number of names walked back — recoveries documented and placed in the Order's archive. A Seeker who has recovered three hundred names outranks a Seeker who has recovered twenty, regardless of age or tenure. The single title for all members is **Seeker**.

In practice, this produces an organization that looks flat to outsiders and is more complex than it appears. High-recovery Seekers tend to be experienced, so seniority and rank correlate, but neither formally produces the other. A young Seeker who has done exceptional recovery work will be listened to. An old Seeker doing routine work will not.

The Order has no fixed headquarters. It operates through a network of field stations at or near significant research sites:

- **Ixareth-Kul ruins station** — The primary Dominion archive operation; the Order's largest presence, in converted lower survey complex space
- **Aelindra approach station** — The Dreaming Warden observation program; ongoing since Year 801 CR
- **Greyfen field station** — Foundation Ring perimeter documentation; operated by Herath-Vix
- **Harrowmere maritime station** — Management of the three Remnant Sea survey licenses; correspondence with Compact maritime authority and Elder Council response teams

Administrative decisions are made through written correspondence. All Seekers receive copies of major decision documents. Consensus is the operating standard; unresolved disputes are addressed by having the highest-recovery-count Seekers present make a ruling, which is the closest the Order gets to formal governance.

## The Remnant Sea Licenses

The Walking Name Order holds three active survey licenses in the Remnant Sea, granted by the Dockmasters' Compact maritime authority. The Kel`varath Elder Council has formally objected to all three.

**The three licenses:**
1. The outer reef survey at the Sunken Ziggurat's eastern maritime approach — studying Architect construction patterns at the ziggurat from water positions that land-based surveys cannot access.
2. The Southeastern Coastal Shelf Survey — charting Architect construction signatures in the shallow water adjacent to the Southeastern Marshlands.
3. The Remnant Sea Eastern Route Survey — mapping potential Architect construction along the deep maritime route toward the Bone Reach, related to the Walking Name's research interest in the Maren approach.

**The Elder Council's formal objection:** The survey licenses approach maritime areas adjacent to Kel`varath ancestral territory. The Compact maritime authority has no jurisdiction over Kel`varath territorial waters. All three licenses were granted without Kel`varath consultation. The Elder Council has requested suspension pending a formal territorial jurisdiction review.

**The Walking Name's internal assessment of the real objection:** The Elder Council endorses the Sealed Record's hypothesis that the Architects were ancestral Kel`varath. The Walking Name's surveys may produce findings that complicate this — specifically, that the Architect sites' construction signatures are inconsistent with Kel`varath architectural lineage. If the Architects were not Kel`varath ancestral predecessors, the Elder Council's claim to Architect inheritance becomes politically vulnerable. The Elder Council has declined to review the Walking Name's published findings, which the Walking Name notes is an unusual position for an organization whose stated interest is the accuracy of the ancestral record.

**Current status:** The licenses are active. The Compact has acknowledged the Elder Council's objection but has not suspended the licenses, describing the jurisdictional question as "a matter requiring extended review." The review has been underway for six years. Maret Seln, the Harrowmere station's liaison, manages this correspondence and has filed seventy-three documents in the ongoing proceeding. She has noted that the Compact's lawyers appear in no hurry.

## Walking Name Standing (Player Reputation)

Walking Name standing starts at neutral with all players. It rises through:
- Completing Walking Name survey contracts (documenting sites, bringing notation fragments)
- Surviving situations in the ruins that Order scholars did not
- Sharing Architect notation from sites the Order has not yet documented
- Delivering the Dreaming Warden's question to Drathis (with or without the answer — both are meaningful in different ways)

| Tier | Standing | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Recognized** | 500 | Field access; Herath-Vix's partial notation guide (*Notation Skill unlock to 25*); Walking Name survey contracts |
| **Trusted Seeker** | 1500 | Drathis's partial site archive; access to the Dreaming Grove station documentation; *Notation Skill unlock to 75* |
| **Named** | 3000 | Full Drathis archive; the Architect notation decode cipher (*unlocks full notation reading*); the Walking Name's working conclusions about what the Calibration State is designed to produce |
| **Walking Name** | 5000 | The title. Passive: Architect notation at all sites becomes slightly more legible. The Dreaming Warden asks a different question of players who have achieved this standing — the only documented case of the Warden modifying its questionnaire based on an observable condition other than language. |

**The notation cipher:** At standing 3000, the Walking Name provides the Architect notation decode key — the cipher that unlocks reading comprehension of the full notation system. This is the same cipher Director Sathren's quest hook (*The Credential Alternative*) is seeking, and which Archivist Valex will not give to a Final Survey agent. The Walking Name provides it at this standing level because they trust that a player who has completed this much of the survey understands what the notation is for. The cipher is also available through the Millhaven Cellars dungeon and the Sunken Ziggurat's deepest accessible level, both requiring substantially more effort.

## Cross-Faction Relationships

**Cartography Hall / Archivist Valex:** The productive relationship. Valex is Sealed Record, but her personal research methodology frequently aligns with Walking Name practice — she documents carefully, she shares findings when she judges sharing appropriate, and she has communicated things to Drathis that she has not told her own Order. The Walking Name and the Cartography Hall collaborate on sites where neither organization holds exclusive access. This is the best working relationship the Order maintains with any institution, and both parties treat it with corresponding care.

**Order of the Sealed Record:** Uneasy. The disagreement is doctrinal: the Sealed Record believes the Architects are ancestral Kel`varath; the Walking Name believes they are something other, and that the Kel`varath pre-Dominion ritual script's shared structural features with Architect notation indicate cultural contact rather than lineage. Both Orders are Kel`varath-majority organizations reaching incompatible conclusions from similar evidence. The relationship is scholarly friction maintained by mutual awareness that open conflict would damage both organizations' research access in the ruins. They do not fight. They do not share data. Both find this suboptimal and neither has moved to change it first.

**Order of the Final Survey:** Mutual distrust. Sathren's action-priority approach is incompatible with the Walking Name's documentation-completeness standard at a fundamental level: they disagree on what the survey completion condition *is*, not just on how to meet it. Drathis has explained the Walking Name's objection to Sathren three times in written form. Sathren has acknowledged it each time and not updated her approach. The Walking Name considers this the most dangerous relationship they have — not because Sathren is hostile, but because she is organized, capable, and wrong about something specific that matters, and she is probably going to act before they can demonstrate the error.

## Quest Hooks

- **The Survey Contract** — Herath-Vix in the Greyfen or Drathis in Ixareth-Kul issues a documentation request: specific notation to record, specific observations to make, specific conditions to note. The quality of what players bring back is assessed and discussed. Incomplete or imprecise documentation is noted; the Order appreciates when players ask what they missed.
- **The Warden's Pattern** — Drathis asks players to share what the Dreaming Warden asked them. He is assembling a cross-visitor pattern from twelve previous accounts. Sharing produces a specific response: Drathis shows players the pattern, which reveals that the Warden is assessing for something that most visitors have not identified as what the Warden is actually measuring.
- **The Millhaven Node** — Drathis suspects an active Architect node in the Crossed Keys Inn cellar in Millhaven. He asks players to gain access and confirm it. He can specify what to look for. This quest directly links the Bess Holloway questline to the Architect network.
- **The Objection Brief** — At higher standing, the Order asks players to investigate the actual basis for the Elder Council's survey license objections — specifically, what the Elder Council's own research findings say about the Architect sites' construction lineage that they have not published. The formal objection is jurisdictional. What the Elder Council knows is not.
