# Harrowmere
*Levels 15–30 — Coastal Port City / Maritime Zone*

## Overview
Built where the Greyveil River meets the sea. Harrowmere is technically independent — it pays tithe to no kingdom and answers to no faction beyond the Dockmasters' Compact, a council of seven that governs the city with a mixture of genuine pragmatism and spectacular self-interest. The city works. It is not clean. It is not safe. It is the only place in the world where you can sell almost anything to almost anyone without a faction check — which makes it the trade hub for everyone who can't operate in more respectable cities.

The zone covers both the city itself and Harrow Bay, which requires a boat and offers underwater content, wreck exploration, and an island dungeon.

## Setting
River mouth city built on multiple levels: the river docks and warehouse district at water level, the market and Compact Hall on the first rise, the residential quarters (new and old money separated by visible distance) above that, and the Shallows — poorest quarter, built on stilts over the water at the river's edge. The bay opens to the east; the river runs west toward Greyveil.

The architecture is practical — stone where it needs to last, timber where it can be replaced, everything a little salt-worn. The Grey Anchor tavern on Tavern Row is the center of Harrowmere's social and criminal life in the same way that a drain is the center of a floor.

## Dungeon — The Drowned Hold
*Levels 22–28.*

Harrow Island, a half-mile offshore, was used as a pirate anchorage for decades before the pirates were killed or scattered. The Hold — their fortified base — is still there. So are they. The sea brought them back about ten years ago, and they've been inside ever since, neither leaving nor decaying as expected. Three levels: the above-ground fort (accessible, moderate difficulty), the sea cave system beneath (flooded sections, requires Enduring Breath or equivalent), and the Hold's vault where the pirate captain is waiting.

**Named:** Captain Morreth the Returned (level 28) — the captain knows she's dead. She's not angry about it. She's angry about something else, and completing the dungeon involves finding out what. Resolution options: destroy her (standard loot), fulfill the unfinished business she's waiting for (rare outcome, significant reward, unlocks a follow-up questline in Greyveil).

## Services
- Harrowmere harbormaster (maritime travel: Ashen Coast route, open sea exploration)
- The Ember Hearth (inn; regen; no questions)
- Dockmasters' Compact Hall (faction quests; political questline for empty council seats)
- Open Market (all races, all goods; no faction check; prices are what they are)
- Shipwright Ironhull & Sons (boat rental, boat purchase, sea content access)
- Temple District (three faiths sharing one building; none of them happy about it)

## Faction Notes
Harrowmere Dockmasters' Compact is the primary faction. Standing rises through city quests, Compact contracts, and the political questline around the two empty council seats. High Compact standing unlocks back-room access in the Compact Hall and information about Warehouse 4.

Secondary factions: Fishermen's Guild (route to Ashen Coast Vareth chain); the Shallows community (independent, suspicious, powerful if befriended); Harrow Island Pirates (hostile until the Drowned Hold questline reaches a specific resolution point).

Harrowmere is the most genuinely neutral zone in the game for alignment purposes — Good and Evil characters face no automatic hostility here. Faction standing in Harrowmere is entirely determined by actions within the city. This makes it a natural second hub for Evil-aligned characters who can't operate freely in Greyveil.
