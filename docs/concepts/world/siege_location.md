# The Breach — Contested Siege Location
*Levels 25–40 — PvP / PvE Contested Zone*

## Overview
A fortified pass through the central mountain range — the only navigable land route between the western Good-aligned territories and the eastern Evil-aligned badlands that doesn't go through Greyveil or underground. Both sides have always wanted it. Both sides have held it at various points in the last four hundred years. Currently: contested, which means both sides have forces there and neither one controls the gate.

The Breach operates on a rotating control system. Every six hours, control of the central gate is re-evaluated based on faction activity during that window. Whichever faction controls the gate controls the pass — and the economic, military, and political weight that comes with it.

This is the game's primary contested content location. It works in both PvP and PvE modes and scales to whichever the server population supports.

## Setting
A narrow mountain pass with a fortified gate structure at its center. The pass is five hundred yards from eastern approach to western approach, with walls on both sides cut into the cliff face. The gate itself is a substantial stone and iron construction that would take a siege engine to damage — both sides know this, which is why the fighting focuses on the approaches rather than the gate.

Two siege camps flank the central gate: the Vanguard Camp (western approach, Good-aligned) and the Iron Camp (eastern approach, Evil-aligned). A neutral Breach Wardens post sits at the gate itself, managing the control mechanics.

## Control Mechanics

**Control window:** Six hours. At the end of each window, control passes to whichever faction has accumulated more Breach Points during that period.

**Breach Points gained by:**
- Killing enemy faction soldiers (1 point each)
- Completing Breach dailies (5–20 points)
- Holding the forward outpost positions (1 point per minute while held)
- Killing the opposing faction's field commander (25 points)

**Control benefits for the winning faction:**
- Pass access: controlled faction's characters move freely through the Breach
- Opposing faction characters must pay a heavy toll or fight through
- Winning faction vendors at the gate sell unique Breach-exclusive gear
- Dungeon access: the Underbreach dungeon only opens for the controlling faction's characters

**Tie:** If points are equal at window end, the Breach Wardens declare a ceasefire — both factions have access, neither has the vendor bonus or dungeon access, and the next window starts immediately.

## Dungeon — The Underbreach
*Levels 35–40. Controlling faction only.*

A tunnel complex beneath the gate structure, used historically for supply runs and emergency exits. Currently occupied by something that came up from below when the fighting intensified three years ago. Both factions have tried to clear it. Neither has succeeded. The controlling faction gets access; the controlled period is rarely long enough for a complete run.

**Named:** The Gate-Warden (level 40) — a construct built by whoever originally fortified the pass, running its original security protocol. The protocol is: allow authorized passage, destroy unauthorized passage, maintain the gate. The Gate-Warden does not distinguish between the two factions — both are unauthorized by its ancient registry. Killing it drops the Gate-Key, which allows permanent uncontested passage for one character. One per server. The key does not reset.

## Services
**Both factions, always:**
- Breach Wardens (neutral; manage point tracking; sell basic supplies)
- Breach Wardens (quest giver for PvE-flagged Breach dailies)

**Controlling faction only:**
- Faction commander (vendor; Breach-exclusive gear; requires 500 accumulated Breach Points across sessions)
- Dungeon access (Underbreach)
- Reduced toll on pass transit

## Faction Notes
The Breach has its own standalone reputation track — Breach Standing — separate from alignment and city factions. It rises through participation regardless of which faction wins. High Breach Standing unlocks a third vendor (neutral, sells the best non-faction-gated Breach gear) and a questline that reveals why the Gate-Warden's registry has no authorized entries.

PvP flagging: players in the Breach are automatically flagged for PvP against the opposing faction. The Breach Wardens enforce this by territory — there is a marked boundary at each approach. Inside it, you are flagged. Outside it, you are not. The boundary is clearly posted. Crossing it accidentally is possible. Claiming it was an accident after the second time is not credible.
