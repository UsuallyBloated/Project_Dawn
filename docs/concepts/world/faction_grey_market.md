# The Grey Market
*Neutral trading ground — Underdark Crossing, contested middle section — All levels.*

---

## Overview

The Grey Market has no charter, no flag, and no year of founding in any formal record. It has been operating in the contested middle section of the Underdark Crossing for longer than most of its current vendors have been alive, in a space that both the Kobold Deep-Warrens and the Dark Elf Vel'Sharath approach have informally agreed to not formally acknowledge. The non-acknowledgment is the policy. The policy has worked.

What makes the Grey Market unusual among underground neutral markets is not its inventory — which is what draws most visitors — but its operational discipline. Markets in contested spaces typically collapse when either dominant power decides to stop tolerating the other's presence. The Grey Market has survived four significant Kobold-Dark Elf confrontations in the Crossing without closing once. The single factor that distinguishes it from markets that did not survive is Matron Zikka, who has been operating it for thirty-seven years and whose understanding of what makes the market useful to both sides has kept those two things aligned through every crisis.

---

## The Market

The market occupies a natural chamber in the Underdark Crossing's contested middle section — pre-Kobold and pre-Dark Elf construction, older than both cities, with a ceiling high enough that the Kobolds and the Dark Elves do not need to notice each other unless they choose to. The original geometry that makes the contested section disorienting is present here too, but the chamber itself is stable. Vendor stalls ring the walls: permanent stone fixtures on the Kobold side, lacquered wood screens on the Dark Elf side, and an unclaimed strip in the center where traders who affiliate with neither put their tables.

The lighting is mixed: bioluminescent Kobold moss on the floor sections, Dark Elf phosphor-globe fixtures at the wall junctions, and in the center, a standing iron brazier of debated provenance that Zikka lights personally at market open and extinguishes at market close. The brazier has been in the same location for thirty-seven years. Neither side has claimed it.

Weapons are peace-bonded at the market entrance by Zikka's two assistants. Violation of peace-bond results in both factions immediately ceasing their separate business and focusing entirely on the person who violated it. This has happened twice. It was the last time for both who violated.

## What Is Traded

**Underdark materials:** The market's primary inventory distinction. Deep ore grades not available in surface markets, refined fungal components, bioluminescent cultivation stock, mineral samples from contested passage depths that neither Kobold nor Dark Elf traders regularly reach.

**Faction crafts:** Kobold trap-mechanism components sold openly, which does not happen in Cogsworth surface markets. Dark Elf alchemical compounds that Vel'Sharath export controls do not technically apply to in a space neither party controls. Both sides have decided the revenue is worth the exposure.

**Goods of unclear provenance:** A category Zikka does not officially acknowledge but which accounts for roughly a third of the market's floor space. Items arrive, items are sold, the chain of custody does not appear in her records. She applies one test: the item cannot have been taken from a vendor in this market, from a Kobold city, or from Vel'Sharath directly. Everything else is merchandise.

**Intelligence:** Sold at Zikka's table specifically. Routes, schedules, personnel, locations, and events that have not yet occurred but that someone has paid for someone else to know. She does not sell to the same party twice on the same subject, which she describes as "conflict prevention." The parties who understand why this is conflict prevention are the ones she continues to sell to.

---

## How It Operates

**Transaction reputation:** No formal faction. Zikka tracks what each regular customer has bought, sold, returned, disputed, and not stolen. This is the Grey Market's version of standing. High transaction reputation unlocks vendor tiers that are not accessible to unknown parties. It is built through consistent, uncomplicated trades over time. It is lost through one incident of bad faith faster than it was built.

Players with Kobold Deeps reputation at Tier 3 or above receive entry-level Grey Market access without building the transaction track from zero — Zikka knows what her suppliers know, and a player trusted in the Kobold Deeps has been vouched for in a form she accepts.

**The Arbitration Panel:** Disputes that cannot be resolved at the vendor level come to the Panel: Zikka, one Kobold-side representative (currently **Crak of the Long-Root Pack**, Zikka's nephew — she is aware this creates a perception problem and has told him he will rule against Long-Root on every borderline case to compensate), and one Dark Elf-side representative (**Seravin Doth-Xan**, a junior Vel'Sharath trade agent two years at the Crossing, one year on the Panel). The Panel rules by majority. Zikka has not cast a deciding vote in thirty-seven years of operation; she describes this as luck and it is not.

Panel rulings are final. The two vendors who subsequently appealed to outside faction authority found the market's availability closed to them, by coincidence, indefinitely.

## Grey Market Standing

Standing is personal to Matron Zikka and tracked in a ledger she keeps behind her table. There is no display, no announcement, no tier name. You find out your standing by what Zikka offers you and what she does not.

| Access Level | How Gained | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Entry** | First transaction completed without incident — OR — Kobold Deeps Tier 3 voucher | Kobold-side and Dark Elf-side standard vendor access; Zikka's standard intelligence offerings |
| **Regular** | Three completed transactions, no disputes | Full vendor floor access; goods-of-unclear-provenance tier; Zikka's second intelligence tier |
| **Known** | Five completed transactions; one Arbitration Panel interaction without bad faith | Zikka's full intelligence tier; passage map fragments (Crossing routes, contested section); the direct conversation about Grix |
| **Trusted** | Extended trade history; a demonstrated service to the market's continued operation | Zikka's personal inventory (not on the floor); the observation she has not filed with either faction — about the contested passages and what returns from them |

## Named Personnel

**Matron Zikka** — Kobold, Long-Root Pack, approximately eighty years old. She has the specific stillness of someone who learned that stillness is safer than movement in contested spaces, and the specific alertness of someone for whom that lesson came from an incident rather than from advice. She is shorter than she used to be. Her claws are maintained with the care of someone who stopped needing them for work but kept the habit. She does not explain decisions; she describes the market. If you understand it, you are useful. If you do not, you are a new customer who will either become useful or become a lesson.

She founded the Grey Market in Year 810 CR at age fifty-two, which is not young to start a thirty-seven-year project, and which she describes as evidence that the market was a practical necessity rather than an ambition. She has survived six Arbitration Panel representatives, two confrontations in which both factions tried to use the market's neutrality as a staging point, and one attempt by Vel'Sharath trade authority to bring her operation within city licensing. She is politely clear about how each ended.

**Grix** — Kobold, Long-Nose Pack, level 30. The Long-Nose Pack operates a mapping service out of the market: passage routes through the contested section, updated monthly, priced by depth and currency. Grix is their best tunnel-reader and has been at the market for six years.

In Year 844 CR, Grix was absent from the market for three weeks without notice. He returned physically unharmed and said the following, once, to Zikka: *"Those passages open into a space too large to see across, and the far side of it is lit from below."* He has not elaborated. He has not returned to those passages. His mapping updates since his return are accurate and more detailed than before, but they do not cover the section those passages lead into — he marks it UNCHARTED and does not say more.

What Zikka has observed since his return: Grix marks distances on surfaces with claw-scratches before entering any unfamiliar space. He works with his back to the wall. He files his passage maps with more careful reference notation than before — as if building a record for someone who will need to understand it without him present to explain. He has not told her what he saw. She has not pressed.

**Crak** — Kobold, Long-Root Pack, level 28. Zikka's nephew. Arbitration Panel representative, Kobold side. Running out of borderline Long-Root cases in which to demonstrate his impartiality and beginning to find the problem interesting in a way he did not anticipate.

**Seravin Doth-Xan** — Dark Elf, level 34. Vel'Sharath trade agent; Arbitration Panel representative, Dark Elf side. Arrived with the standard assignment posture (observe, document, report, commit to nothing). Has been on the Panel long enough to understand that its authority depends on decisions being genuinely contested rather than administered. Her reports to Vel'Sharath are accurate and do not include her reasoning, which she keeps separate.

## Quest Hooks

- **The Passage Map** — Grix's Long-Nose Pack maps cover the contested section with the exception of the UNCHARTED area. Players who have explored the Third Digger's Warren and the wrong-geometry passages can provide him with observations from those areas. He will not react to most of it. He will react — specifically and without covering it — to any mention of open space, light from below, or the far side. He is not ready to discuss what he saw. He is ready to hear that someone else was there.
- **The Provision Problem** — A specific cargo is simultaneously claimed by vendors on both sides, which is not supposed to be possible, and both claims appear valid. Zikka asks players with standing to find out how this happened without either vendor losing face. The answer involves a contested-section passage that connects two nominally separate delivery routes through geometry that should not permit it.
- **Zikka's Private Stock** — At Trusted standing, Zikka places something on her table without explaining what it is or where it came from. "I'll tell you what I know about it. You tell me what you know about it. We'll see if the exchange is fair." What she found — and where it was found in the contested section — connects to the wider question of what the wrong-geometry passages are a remnant of.

## Cross-Zone Notes

- **Kobold Deeps** — Long-Root Pack has operated at the market since before Zikka took over; Kobold Deeps reputation at Tier 3 grants immediate entry-level access.
- **Vel'Sharath** — The market's Dark Elf-side connection routes through the Gate District. High Vel'Sharath reputation does not grant Grey Market access directly — Zikka maintains separation from Vel'Sharath's formal trade structure deliberately — but it smooths the initial interaction.
- **Third Digger chain** — Grix's three-week absence and what he found in the contested section is a data point in the Third Digger questline. Players who have followed that chain and read the Digger's survey orders understand what Grix was describing before Grix knows there is anything to understand it *as*.
- **Architect network** — The cartographer's atlas footnotes the contested section's "wrong geometry" as sharing structural features with Architect construction signatures. Zikka has noted the footnote. She has found two people in thirty-seven years who understood what it meant. She kept records of both conversations.
