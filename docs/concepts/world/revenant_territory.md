# The Ashen Marches — Revenant Territory
*Levels 30–45 — Undead / Transformation Zone*

## Overview
A contested borderland between the Eastern Badlands and the Southeastern Marshlands where three major battles were fought during the Dominion War. The dead from all three battles are still here — not as feral undead, but as something more organized. The Revenants who rose from this ground remember the war. They remember which side they were on. They have not stopped.

The Ashen Marches is the primary zone for earned Revenant transformation content. Players approaching the Revenant transformation threshold (Necromancer / Shadow Knight / Blood Mage, Evil alignment, Level 20) will find quest threads pulling them here. The zone is also a high-level hunting ground for players who want undead content without alignment requirements.

The Revenants of the Marches are not mindless. They are organized, factional, and in some cases approachable. The three battle groups — Dominion soldiers, Coalition forces, and a third army whose affiliation nobody has been able to determine — maintain their wartime alignments and have been slowly re-fighting the same campaign for three hundred years with an intensity that suggests they believe they can still win.

## Setting
Scorched plain transitioning to grey-ash lowland. The ground is wrong here — it doesn't grow things the way it should, and the ash is not the remnant of fire but of something that happened to the soil at a deeper level. Ruins of three field encampments are still standing, which is impossible given the weather and the time, which suggests the camps are maintained.

At night the zone becomes dramatically more dangerous — the Revenant patrols extend further, the third army becomes visible where it wasn't during the day, and the sky above the Marches takes on a color that nearby zone residents have learned to avoid looking at for too long.

## Dungeon — The Final Encampment
*Levels 40–45.*

The third army's base. Unlike the Dominion and Coalition camps, this one has no identifying markers, no flags, no unit insignia. The soldiers inside are Revenants who look like every race — Human, Kel`varath, Dwarf, Elf — but wearing no recognizable armor. The camp has been here since the battle. The battle was three hundred years ago.

The commander of the Final Encampment is a Revenant who does not remember their name, their race, or their side. They remember only the objective. The dungeon is built around discovering what the objective was and whether completing it — or preventing it — ends the battle or continues it.

**Named:** The Nameless Commander (level 45) — can be fought, negotiated with, or given what they were ordered to retrieve (if players can determine what that is from the dungeon's records). Killing them ends the dungeon. Completing their objective changes the Ashen Marches permanently — the third army stops fighting and disperses. What this means for the zone long-term is a server-scale event.

## Revenant Transformation Content

Characters approaching the Revenant threshold receive a vision: the Marches, at night, the three armies frozen mid-battle, and a figure that is their own face but wrong in a way that is hard to name. The vision quest leads to the Marches and to The Battle-Remembering — the oldest Revenants who can answer questions about what the transformation means, what it costs, and whether it can be reversed.

**The answer to whether it can be reversed:** No. The Battle-Remembering are honest about this. They find the question understandable.

**Transformation site:** A specific location in the center of the Marches where all three battle lines converged. Characters who complete the questline at this site undergo the Revenant transformation at the moment of choosing — the game gives a confirmation prompt with full consequences listed. The transformation happens on confirmation.

## Services
- No permanent vendors in the Marches
- Coalition Field Camp (if Coalition reputation is sufficient): basic supplies, Necromancer trainer
- Dominion Field Camp (if Dominion reputation is sufficient): basic supplies, Shadow Knight trainer  
- The Battle-Remembering (questline access): Revenant transformation lore; one will teach Blood Mage skills if alignment matches

## Faction Notes
Three factions: Dominion Remnant, Coalition Remnant, Third Army. All three begin hostile. Coalition Remnant is the most approachable for Good-aligned characters; Dominion Remnant for Evil-aligned. Third Army has no faction diplomacy — they do not recognize alignment, only living vs. dead.

Revenant player characters who have completed the transformation are recognized by all three factions as a fourth category — neither living nor fully of any army. This grants automatic neutral status with all three groups on first entry, and opens a questline not available to living characters.
