# Khala Savannahs
*Savannah / Grassland — Levels 30–45 — Felhari Clans*

## Overview
The Khala Savannahs are the largest continuous open-terrain zone in the game. The grass is waist-height on a Human, chest-height on a Halfling, and the wind moves through it in ways the Felhari have named. Not wind in general — specific winds, at specific times, that have personal names the way weather phenomena get named when the people living with them have been living with them long enough.

The Felhari homeland. The clan structure here is complex and functional: three major clans hold primary territories, six smaller clans affiliate with them in arrangements that shift over generations, and the Gathering (a multi-clan assembly held at seasonal intervals) is the zone's central social event and the place where the Spirit-Walker transformation becomes relevant to other clans. A character who has become a Spirit-Walker and attends the Gathering with that status is a different kind of visitor than a character who has not.

The Beast Master tradition has strong roots in the Savannahs. The warders that Beast Masters form bonds with are frequently savannah fauna — swift, intelligent animals that have been in relationship with Felhari hunters for generations.

## Setting
Open grassland with termite mounds, acacia stands, and rocky kopjes (isolated hills) that serve as navigation landmarks and territorial markers. The horizon is a long way away. Clan settlements are semi-permanent — structures that can be broken down for a seasonal move and reassembled elsewhere, but which have been in their current location long enough to be considered permanent by everyone who lives there.

The Savannahs are a day/night zone in a meaningful way: the predator ecology shifts dramatically after dark. Hunting is a daylight activity by Felhari custom, not because night is more dangerous (though it is) but because the animals being hunted deserve to be hunted in conditions where they can see what is happening.

## Dungeon — The Ancestor's Mouth
*Levels 37–45.*

A cave system at the base of the largest kopje in the zone that the Felhari have maintained as a spirit-communication site for generations. The Mouth is not, technically, a dungeon in the normal sense — the Felhari hold ceremonies here and consider it sacred ground. What makes it a dungeon is what has been pushing inward from the deeper sections: something that the ancestors have been warning about and that the current clans have been managing by having Spirit-Walkers stand watch, and which has begun exceeding the Spirit-Walkers' capacity to manage.

The Mouth rewards players who have high Felhari faction and Spirit-Walker connections: certain passages are accessible only with Felhari clan escort or Spirit-Walker status.

**Named:** The Encroaching Silence (level 45 — the entity the ancestors have been warning about; its nature is ambiguous) — drops Spirit-Walker-relevant transformation items; Shaman highest-tier spirit focus; rare Beast Master warder bond component.

## Named Ancestors

The Spirit-Walkers of the Savannahs work with specific ancestral figures, not a general afterlife. Three are named frequently enough that other clans recognize them by title:

**The Far-Runner** — An ancestor who walked the full perimeter of the Savannahs in a single season as a demonstration that the territory was real and theirs. Now appears to Spirit-Walkers primarily in motion — you see them walking, already at the edge of your vision, always the same direction. What they know is about boundaries and what is beyond them. Characters pursuing the zone's edge-territory content receive The Far-Runner's recognition as a passive buff when deep in unmapped territory.

**The One Who Waited** — An ancestor who stayed at the Mouth during the first significant encroachment from the deep sections, alone, for eleven days. Did not drive it back; survived it. The distinction is considered meaningful. Appears as a stationary figure in complete stillness. What they know is about endurance and the difference between waiting and losing. Spirit-Walker characters with high Khala Clan standing can receive a sustained regen buff called *Waiting Knowledge* by spending time near the Mouth entrance.

**Ash-in-the-Wind** — The most recent ancestor recognized by formal clan consensus, which is unusual (most take generations). A Shaman who died stopping a Thunderhorn stampede from reaching the clan settlement's most vulnerable period, two generations ago. Appears only during the nighttime hours and only to characters who have been hit recently. What they know is immediate and practical. Not a source of wisdom; a source of help when help is needed.

Interacting with all three is a prerequisite for the deeper Spirit-Walker transformation quests.

## Services
- Clan gathering grounds: Beast Master advanced trainers; Shaman ancestor-practice trainers; Spirit-Walker questline accessible here
- The Gathering (seasonal event): cross-clan commerce; unique seasonal items; the zone's social hub; Spirit-Walker reputation carries significant weight
- Hunting lodge network: scattered rest points; Ranger-equivalent tracking training for Beast Masters; Skinning and Trapping trainers

## Faction Notes
The Clan Alliance (representing all Khala clans) is the primary faction. Felhari start at high standing. Others start at wary — the Savannahs are Felhari territory and the Felhari are politely clear about what that means.

The Spirit-Walker transformation changes how the Gathering treats a character regardless of race. A Human Spirit-Walker is something the other Felhari Shamans want to know about. The ancestors communicating with a non-Felhari through Felhari methods is a fact the Gathering will form opinions about — the opinions are not automatically positive, but the standing gained from navigating them well is substantial.

The hostile minor clan (the Clan Raider questline) is the fastest path to Clan Alliance standing for characters who haven't yet built Gathering connections.
