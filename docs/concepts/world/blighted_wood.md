# The Blighted Wood
*Levels 20–30 — Corrupted Forest / Horror Zone*

## Overview
The eastern edge of Ardenmoor where the forest died. Forty years ago, on a moonless night, something pushed up from beneath the eastern ridge and the nearest trees went grey by morning. The blight spreads at roughly six inches per month — slow enough to track, fast enough that the Wood Elves have been watching the marker cord close on their canopy villages for four decades.

The source is not what players will expect. The Heartwood — the oldest tree in Ardenmoor, two hundred feet tall, still technically alive — is not the cause. Something lives inside it. The tree is a symptom, not the origin. The thing at the bottom of the tree roots is what fed until the Heartwood couldn't sustain it alone, and then spread outward.

## Setting
Three zones within the zone. The outer blight is grey-barked forest with no leaf cover, no animal sounds except undead ones, and Wood Elf ranger posts marking the edge and losing ground. The deep blight is fused-tree terrain where canopy blocks all light and sound echoes wrong. At the center: the Heartwood, black and vast, visibly wrong, still growing.

The Corrupted Druid settlement in the deep blight is not hostile until players enter the ritual circle. The druids were Wood Elves. Some still remember it. They face the Heartwood constantly — not in worship, but in the way that people watch something they know is going to move.

## Dungeon — The Hollowed
*Levels 27–30.*

Inside the Heartwood, descending through the tree's root system rather than ascending. Vertical dungeon: players go down through increasingly tight root passages, deeper into what the tree has become. The thing at the bottom is not the final boss — it is what the Heartwood is defending. The Heartwood itself is the final boss, or what remains of it: a massive root-system encounter that fills the dungeon's lowest chamber.

**Named:** The Heartwood (level 30 encounter) — not a standard mob. A zone boss that requires players to damage specific root nodes in a specific order before the central mass can be harmed. Killing it ends the blight's spread. It does not reverse existing damage. The grey trees stay grey.

The thing the Heartwood was defending is a seed. It is dormant. Taking it is a choice with consequences that extend into later zones — it is, among other things, the original Heartwood's offspring, and several factions have strong opinions about what should be done with it.

## Services
- Wood Elf Ranger Post (east Ardenmoor border; zone entrance quest hub)
- Blight Edge camp (temporary; basic supplies; rangers on rotation)
- Corrupted Druid, the one who still talks (quest giver if approached correctly; opens alternate dungeon path)
- No inn — the Blighted Wood has no permanent safe settlement

## Faction Notes
Ardenmoor faction standing carries over — Wood Elf rangers at the blight edge respond to players based on Ardenmoor reputation. High Ardenmoor standing unlocks the ranger post questline which provides a map of known corrupted druid positions and a key item for the alternate dungeon path.

The Corrupted Druids are their own faction — Corrupted Grove. They begin at neutral and can be moved toward allied through the one druid who still communicates. Full alliance with the Corrupted Grove unlocks a fundamentally different dungeon path through The Hollowed where the Heartwood encounter is a dialogue rather than a fight, and the outcome of the seed decision has additional options.

Connection to Ardenmoor: completing the Rotwood Hollow dungeon in Ardenmoor yields a clue pointing toward the Blighted Wood as the source of the rot in that dungeon. Players who follow the breadcrumb find the zone entrance immediately; players who arrive without it must find the blight edge another way.
