# The Architects — Pre-Dominion Builders
*Lore reference — unnamed civilization, referred to colloquially as "the Architects" or "the Builders"*

---

## What Is Known

They built things. The things are still standing. That is the starting point for everything else, because the civilization itself left no name, no written language anyone has successfully decoded, and no living descendants — or none that have identified themselves.

Evidence of their construction exists in seven confirmed locations across the known world, with suspected sites in three others. All confirmed sites share the same construction signature: stone fitted without mortar to tolerances that should require tools the civilizations of today cannot replicate, geometric proportions that repeat across every site regardless of geography, and a specific architectural motif — the six-fingered handprint pressed into wet stone at doorways and foundation corners.

The handprints are not decorative. They appear too consistently, in too structurally load-bearing a position, to be decorative. What they are is not agreed upon.

---

## Physical Evidence

### What the structures reveal
Doorways and passages at all sites are designed for a bipedal form roughly Human in height — shorter than Ogre, taller than Halfling. The proportions suggest a frame broader in the shoulder than most races and with a different hip structure — not wrong, exactly, but optimized for different movement patterns.

The six-fingered handprints measure approximately 20% larger than a Human adult's hand. Six fingers of roughly equal length, with the thumb positioned differently than any known race's.

Optical features built into the structures — windows, sighting alignments, interior light shafts — are calibrated for three light-gathering points per face. Either three eyes, or two eyes and a secondary light-sensitive organ. The alignment is too consistent across sites to be architectural coincidence.

### What the structures don't reveal
No murals depicting the Architects themselves. No statues. No effigies. Every structure that has interior decoration depicts only geometry and what appear to be astronomical or mathematical notations. If there are portraits, they have not been found.

No burial sites have been located. Either the Architects did not bury their dead, disposed of them in ways that left no detectable trace, or the burial sites exist but have not been identified as such.

## The Architect Notations

The geometric markings found at all sites use a consistent system that multiple scholars have partially decoded. It is not a writing system in the conventional sense — it encodes spatial relationships, ratios, and what appear to be conditional instructions ("if X then Y" rendered geometrically) rather than narrative or language.

What has been decoded:
- Astronomical alignment instructions for each site, calibrated to star positions that predate current star charts by an estimated three to five thousand years
- Mathematical constants that appear across sites in ways that suggest the sites were meant to function together — as components of a larger system rather than independent structures
- A recurring notation that translates loosely as "when complete" or "upon completion" — followed by a notation that has not been decoded

What that final notation means is the central question. Every scholar who has worked on it has a different theory. None of them agree. Most of them stopped publishing.

## The Dominion Connection

The Kel`varath Dominion built Ixareth-Kul on top of — and incorporating — an Architect foundation structure. This was deliberate. Dominion records from before the collapse reference the Architect structures explicitly, calling them "the elder works" and treating them as legitimate antecedents to Dominion authority — as if inheriting Architect sites conveyed a legitimacy that the Dominion's own construction could not.

What the Dominion scholars knew about the Architects that has been lost is a significant open question. The Dominion Vault in Ixareth-Kul is widely believed to contain Dominion research on the Architect sites. No one has accessed it.

## The Third Digger

The construct found in the Underdark Crossing — Dominion-era, running survey orders — has orders that point to the Quiet Dig. Its orders reference "the completion survey." The phrase appears in Architect notation. A Dominion-era construct using Architect terminology in its operational orders is either coincidence or evidence that the Dominion's interest in the Architects went further than known.

---

## The Still Throne

The deepest accessible level of the Sunken Ziggurat contains a throne. The throne is carved for a form with the Architect proportions — broader than Human, six-fingered armrests, with a headrest featuring three recesses for three light-gathering organs. The throne is occupied. The pre-Kel`varath builders carved a six-fingered figure. The figure appears to have been carved sitting down, not placed — as if the stone grew around someone already there.

No one has examined it closely. The water in the deepest chamber is not water in the way that water elsewhere is water.

## Faction Interest

**The three Dominion Orders** (at Ixareth-Kul) all want access to the Dominion Vault and its Architect research. Their interpretations differ:
- Order of the Sealed Record believes the Architects were a precursor Kel`varath civilization and the knowledge is Kel`varath inheritance
- Order of the Walking Name believes the Architects are still present in some form and the research is a communication record
- Order of the Final Survey believes the Architects' system is unfinished and completing it is either catastrophic or necessary

**The Breach Gate-Warden** uses the phrase "original protocol" to describe its operational parameters. The word "original" in the Breach Wardens' neutral ledger is a different word than the word used for things the Wardens built. The Gate-Warden may predate the Wardens.

**Varek** — the wizard at Varek's Spire — has a section of his 90 years of notes on the Residual that uses Architect notation. He has not explained this to anyone. He may not remember that he did it.

## What They Were

Unknown. Theories range from:
- A precursor civilization destroyed by the same catastrophe implied by the Architect notations' "upon completion" sequence
- An off-world or extradimensional civilization that used this world as a construction site
- A transformed state of a known race, unrecognizable in that form
- Something that is not gone, only patient

The evidence supports all of these theories equally. The evidence was designed by people who were very good at calibrating things precisely.
