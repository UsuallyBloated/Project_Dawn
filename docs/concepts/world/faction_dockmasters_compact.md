# The Dockmasters' Compact — Faction Profile
*Neutral/mercantile faction. Harrowmere's governing authority.*

---

## Overview

The Dockmasters' Compact is the governing body of Harrowmere, the independent coastal port city. It is not a government in the traditional sense — it is a commercial partnership that has accreted governmental powers over two centuries of being the only organization in the city capable of doing anything consistently. The Compact manages the docks, sets tariff schedules, arbitrates trade disputes, issues sailing licenses, maintains the city guard as a contracted service, and collects the fees that pay for all of this.

It is run by a council of seven Dockmasters. There are currently five, because two seats have been empty for six years following deaths that the official record categorizes as "misadventure at sea" and the unofficial record categorizes as "someone's problem."

---

## Structure

**The Seven Seats** — Each seat represents a major trade interest in Harrowmere. The current distribution:

| Seat | Trade Interest | Current Holder | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| First Dock | Bulk cargo, grain, timber | Margot Hale | 23 years in the seat; eldest Dockmaster |
| Second Dock | Fishing fleet and processing | Cam Oreigh | 8 years; advocates for the fishing community |
| Third Dock | Exotic goods (eastern and underground trade) | Delvar Senne | 3 years; widely considered to be a proxy for an unnamed patron |
| Fourth Dock | Shipbuilding and repair | Wren Ashford | 12 years; controls the dry docks |
| Fifth Dock | **VACANT** | — | Empty 6 years; contested between multiple claimants |
| Sixth Dock | **VACANT** | — | Empty 6 years; the previous holder's estate is suing three parties |
| Seventh Dock | Financial services and currency exchange | Thalia Mourne | 17 years; runs the city's banking functions |

**The Harbormaster** — An appointed administrative officer who runs the day-to-day port operations. Currently: Jeven Crost, a meticulous Half-Elf who has held the post for fourteen years and is the one person in the Compact everyone trusts, because he is not interested in power and everyone knows it.

**The Guard Contract** — The Compact does not have its own military. It contracts the city guard from a mercenary company called the Iron Rope. The Iron Rope has held the contract for eight years. Whether the Iron Rope's loyalty is to the Compact or to their contract rate is an open question the Compact prefers not to test.

## The Two Empty Seats

The Fifth and Sixth Dockmasters died within three months of each other, six years ago. The official cause of death for both was misadventure at sea, which in both cases is technically accurate — both died at sea, in circumstances that were in some sense adventurous.

The relevant context: both Dockmasters had been pushing for a formal trade agreement with Greyveil that would have significantly reduced Harrowmere's independence. The agreement collapsed when they died. The remaining Dockmasters declined to investigate the circumstances publicly and have been declining to fill the seats since, because filling the seats requires agreement from four of the remaining five, and the remaining five cannot agree on candidates.

The two empty seats cast votes as "abstentions" under the Compact charter, which effectively requires four of five active votes to pass anything. The deadlock has been producing gridlock for six years. Some observers believe this is the intent.

## Economics and Services

Harrowmere runs on trade. The Compact's tariff schedule is among the most competitive in the region — lower than Greyveil's, significantly lower than Vel'Sharath, and with fewer political conditions attached than either. This is the city's primary competitive advantage: money spends the same here regardless of who you are.

**Services open to all:**
- Dock rental (daily and seasonal rates)
- Ship repair and provisioning
- Bonded warehouse storage (Compact-certified; goods insured against theft)
- Currency exchange (Seventh Dock rates, updated weekly)
- Sailing licenses (required for captains operating from Harrowmere; one-time fee + renewal)

**Trade goods flowing through Harrowmere:**
- Inbound from western territories: grain, timber, manufactured goods, livestock
- Inbound from eastern routes: exotic goods, underground materials (Kobold and Dark Elf goods go through Third Dock)
- Outbound: processed fish, local craftwork, refinished goods (re-export trade is significant)
- Sea-sourced: salvage from the Drowned Hold wrecks, if anyone survives the retrieval

## The Grey Anchor

The Grey Anchor tavern is the Compact's unofficial meeting space — not owned by the Compact but functionally their second office. The Dockmasters drink there. Deals get made there. The person who is already sitting at the corner table when you arrive is always someone, and they are always there for a reason.

The owner is a Minotaur named Orreth who claims to have retired from a career he does not describe. He runs the Grey Anchor with the specific competence of someone who knows how much trouble can happen in a drinking establishment and has developed systems for preventing most of it. He is not affiliated with the Compact. He charges them the same as everyone else. They consider this neutral; he considers this a business strategy.

## The Compact and the Rest of the World

**Greyveil:** Complicated. Greyveil wants a formal trade agreement that would tie Harrowmere into Greyveil's tariff structure. Harrowmere's independence is economically valuable precisely because it is not Greyveil. The two cities trade anyway; the formal agreement is where the politics live.

**Good-aligned territories:** Generally positive. Harrowmere serves them commercially and takes no political positions. They wish the city were more aligned but accept the value of a neutral port.

**Evil-aligned factions:** Tolerated. The Compact's position: money is money. They do not ask what the eastern goods are for. They do ask for the tariff.

**The Drowned Hold:** The Compact has issued salvage licenses for three expeditions to the island dungeon. None have returned with significant profit. The Compact has not stopped issuing licenses, because the licensing fee is paid upfront.

## Compact Standing (Player Reputation)

Compact Standing rises through:
- Completing Harrowmere trade quests and delivery jobs
- Successful salvage operations (Drowned Hold and sea content)
- Arbitration and dispute resolution for the Compact
- Commercial activity in the city (volume-based, slow)

High Compact Standing unlocks:
- **Rank 1 (300):** Preferred tariff rates; reduced dock fees
- **Rank 2 (1000):** Access to the bonded warehouse's restricted section (goods held under Compact authority, not just commercial goods)
- **Rank 3 (2500):** The Harbormaster speaks candidly about the two empty seats
- **Rank 4 (5000):** A Dockmaster seat is offered. This is social and economic, not mechanical — but the roleplay implications for a player who accepts are significant

## Quest Hooks

- **The Claimant** — Three parties are competing for the Fifth Dock seat. The Compact needs someone to investigate their backgrounds; the investigation cannot be done by someone on the payroll because anyone on the payroll is already committed. Two of the three claimants are legitimate. One is not what they claim to be.
- **The Third Dock Problem** — Delvar Senne (Third Dock) has been importing goods that clear customs without any inspection. Someone in the Iron Rope Guard is being paid to look away. The Harbormaster knows; he cannot prove it; he needs someone who can.
- **The Salvage License** — A player with a Compact salvage license for the Drowned Hold returns from the island with something that is not cargo. It is not alive. It is not obviously dangerous. But the Compact's bonded warehouse manager will not accept it for storage, and the Harbormaster is suddenly very interested in who licensed this expedition.
- **Captain Morreth's Debt** — If Morreth was resolved in the Drowned Hold dungeon (questline version), the resolution has legal consequences in Harrowmere. The Compact was owed money by the ship Morreth was on when it went down. The estate has been in legal limbo for twenty years. If Morreth is back — in any form — the debt question is technically reopened. The Sixth Dock's vacant seat's estate lawyer has noticed.
