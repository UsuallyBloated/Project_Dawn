# Auroch's Rest
*Plains / Minotaur Lands — Levels 20–30 — Minotaur Clans*

## Overview
The Minotaur did not choose this land. They were displaced here. The name "Auroch's Rest" is theirs — what they called the open grassland when they arrived and found that the aurochs had already been here long enough to carve it into a home, and that maybe that was a sign.

Several generations later, the Minotaur have made the plains their own. The clan settlements here are permanent — earthwork construction, carved stone, the horn-markers that designate each clan's territory and the names of its dead. The Shaman tradition is centered here, particularly the ancestor-practice that eventually produces the Ancestor Vessel transformation. The horn carvings are not decorative.

Auroch's Rest is the most culturally rich zone in the mid-tier game. NPCs here have real opinions about history, about other clans, about what the displacement meant. The Shaman quests accessible here extend deeper into lore than almost any other class's quest content.

## Setting
Wide open grassland with rocky outcroppings and river tributaries that cut irregular channels through the plains. Wind is constant. The sky is enormous. Clan settlements cluster near water sources and defensible high ground — earthwork berms and carved-stone halls rather than built-up walls. The largest settlement, Stonemark, sits at the center of the zone where three clan territories meet and agreed, decades ago, not to fight about it.

At night, the plains are different. The aurochs herds move. The ancestors move with them, according to the Shamans.

## Dungeon — The Sunken Hall
*Levels 24–30.*

One of the old displacement-era clan halls is underground — they carved it down when the ground above was too exposed, and then the ground above shifted, and the entrance became the ceiling. The Sunken Hall is accessible only through a shaft in the eastern plains. Inside: the oldest Minotaur settlement remaining, preserved almost intact, currently occupied by something that moved into the abandoned space after the clan above ground stopped using it. The Shaman quests that lead here involve the ancestors requesting something specific be done about what is in their old home.

**Named:** The Hollow Patriarch (level 28 corrupted ancestor spirit) — drops a Shaman spirit-focus; bone carving component usable in Ancestor Vessel-unlocked recipes.

## Services
- Stonemark: Shaman advanced trainers; Warrior and Monk trainers (Minotaur martial tradition has its own techniques); Spirit-Walker questline entry point
- Clan markets: Bone Carving trainer; rare plains-harvested Herbalism reagents
- Ancestor shrine network: donation and ritual points that progress toward the Ancestor Vessel transformation requirements; Shaman lore content

## Faction Notes
The Stonemark Compact governs standing here. Minotaurs start with full access. Other races start neutral — the Minotaur reception is neither warm nor hostile; it is assessing. What you have done elsewhere matters here more than what you are.

The Rogue Clan (the faction that did not accept the Stonemark peace) is an active threat questline. Completing it raises standing substantially. Siding with them instead is an Evil-path option that raises alignment negatively but opens unique questlines about the politics of the displacement.
