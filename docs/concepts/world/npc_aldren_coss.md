# NPC Profile — Aldren Coss
*The Maritime Quarter. Harrowmere. Currently operating as "Kellen Post."*

---

## Identity

**Full name:** Aldren Coss *(AL-dren KOSS)* — currently operating under the name **Kellen Post**
**Race:** Human
**Class:** Wizard (level 21 — active in his Millhaven years; he has not cast a spell in nineteen years and does not intend to resume)
**Age:** 63
**Alignment:** Neutral

**Physical description:** Lean, with the posture of someone who spent his thirties moving rope between docks and discovered the work was better for his back than spell-work had been. Salt-stained hands, calluses that did not come from a scholar's work. Gray at the temples and mostly throughout. He has the specific unremarkability of someone who decided to be unnoticed and practiced the decision for nineteen years.

He looks like a rope-maker in the Maritime Quarter who has been doing this for a long time. He looks like Kellen Post. He has worked at this.

The tell, if you are looking for it: he still reads. Not ledgers or charts — books, carefully maintained, stored in a way that suggests they were not bought in Harrowmere. A former Wizard's library is smaller than people expect and more specific than people notice.

---

## Personality

Aldren Coss made a decision nineteen years ago and has been living inside it. The decision was not: *forget what I know*. He cannot do that and has not tried. The decision was: *don't act on it*. This is a different thing. It requires regular renewal. He renews it every year. He has not missed a year.

He is not an unhappy man. He is a man who made a trade and received what he traded for: a quiet life, a repeatable skill, a dock-town where people leave you alone if you do your work and don't claim more than your share. He has found rope-making satisfying in the way that physical, clear, finished work is satisfying.

What he has not found is the ability to stop noticing things. Twenty years of Wizard training does not leave. He reads inscriptions that most people see as decoration. He notices the properties of buildings he passes. When the Drowned Hold expeditions generated conversation at the Compact dock office — not often, but sometimes — he heard things he recognized and said nothing. He has been living at the place where observation and inaction meet for nineteen years and is better at it than he was, but it is still a place, and he still lives there.

He will not lie about his identity to players who already know it. He will not confirm it to players who are guessing. He has been Kellen Post long enough that the distinction is smaller than it sounds.

---

## The Inn

Aldren Coss owned the Crossed Keys Inn in Millhaven for seven years, from Year 821 to Year 828 CR. He found what he found in Year 827.

What he found: a passage in the back cellar he had not known existed, a chamber below it connecting to tunnels heading south, and in the chamber, something he had no category for at the time. He had a Wizard's training, a specific interest in pre-Dominion construction, and access to a library he had built over twenty years of academic work. He spent a year identifying what he was looking at.

He found a reference to a Year 647 CR engineering record — a document describing a project involving the Greyveil Foundation Ring, the chamber under the Crossed Keys, and something the engineer designated *the prior work*. He had seen that phrase once before, in Dominion Survey Corps administrative records, where it appeared as the contracting authority for their largest classified Architect contract. He recognized that he had found something he could not leave alone if he stayed.

He took one thing from the chamber: a tooth. He sealed it in a jar with everything he understood about why it should not be opened, without looking at it again, and pressed the jar into Bess Holloway's hands on the day of the sale. He told her: "This stays with the building. Don't open it. Don't give it to anyone." He left Millhaven the following morning.

He has not been back. He does not think about this on most days. He is aware this is a choice he has made, every year, for nineteen years.

---

## What He Knows

He knows the passage leads to a chamber connecting to the Greyveil Foundation Ring through tunnels he did not map completely. He knows the Year 647 CR engineering record exists and is in the sealed death-records section of the Harrowmere Vethis temple — he traced the provenance but not the document itself, which was removed from Millhaven's public records before he could read it and transferred under Vethis administrative authority to a sealed archive. He knows the chamber responds to the presence of a Wizard; he noticed this and moved quickly and does not describe the response.

He knows what the tooth is. He knows what the jar does when it is warm. He sealed it anyway because he understood that the alternative was not putting it back but doing something with it, and that everything he knew about what it connected to suggested the something would not end with a quiet life in Harrowmere.

He has known where the engineering record is for nineteen years. He has not retrieved it. This is the center of the decision he renews every year.

## Relationship to Players

Players cannot find him directly. Bess Holloway provides his name at the resolution of her questline. Aldren Coss appears in Compact maritime property records from Year 828 CR under his own name — a detail he left because he needed the Compact authorization and did not believe anyone would look. The alias *Kellen Post* does not appear in the property record. Players who search carefully find the gap, which leads to a rope-maker in the Maritime Quarter who has been recognized by Compact dock staff long enough to be unremarkable.

He will not deny being Aldren Coss to players who call him that name. He will ask, before he responds to anything else, one question: *"Did someone open the jar?"*

**If the jar has not been opened:** He will talk carefully. He confirms who he is, describes why he left, explains that the tooth is in the jar because it should not be elsewhere, and tells players where the engineering record is. He will not go to Millhaven. He will not retrieve the record himself. He tells players what to look for.

**If the jar has been opened:** He goes still. He asks who opened it, in the voice of someone who already knows the answer does not change anything. He explains what the tooth is — the shedding — and what it means that it is now unsealed. He is not angry. He is the specific version of not-angry that means a nineteen-year decision has been made for him, and he is deciding how to continue from the new position. He will tell players everything. He asks, when he is done, whether the Old Thing seemed different after the opening — diminished, changed, or the same. He files the answer without explaining what it means. He gives them the Vethis archive location and adds: *"Tell whoever you find there that Aldren Coss says the prior work is continuous. They'll know what that means if they're the right person."*

---

## Quest Hooks

- **Finding Kellen Post** — Tracing Aldren Coss from Bess's questline resolution through Compact property records to the Harrowmere Maritime Quarter. The investigation rewards players who read carefully — the Compact record is not hidden, only unexamined.
- **The Year 647 Record** — Aldren provides the location: sealed death-records section of the Harrowmere Vethis temple. Access requires Vethis clerical standing, Necromancer temple authorization, or a Rogue with sufficient skill. The record describes the engineering project clearly enough to connect the Crossed Keys chamber to the Foundation Ring and names *the prior work* as the commissioning authority. Bringing it to Aldren changes the shape of the conversation he has been having with himself for nineteen years.
- **The Prior Work Is Continuous** (late questline) — Carrying Aldren's message to the right person in the Greyveil or Ixareth-Kul research community produces a specific response. Who the right person is depends on where the player is in the Architect questline. The message connects the Millhaven node to the larger chain.

## Dialogue Samples

*First approach — jar not opened:*
"Kellen Post. Rope-maker, if you need anything in that line." — [pause] — "You said Aldren Coss. Where did you hear that name?"

*After confirming identity, jar not opened:*
"I left because I found something I couldn't leave alone if I stayed. That's the honest version. The comfortable version is that I sold a good business at a fair price and retired to the coast. Both are true. The first one is the reason for the second one."

*Asked why he took the tooth:*
"Because it was there and it didn't have anywhere else to be. And because if it stayed in that chamber, it was still connected to things I'd found the edges of, and I didn't want to leave it connected. I sealed the jar, didn't look at it again, and asked Bess not to open it. I thought that would hold." A pause. "Nineteen years is not nothing."

*If jar was opened — asked about the tooth directly:*
"The tooth is what the old ones shed when they've been somewhere long enough. It's not the thing itself — it's evidence of duration. What you opened is the record of how long that thing has been in that building, in that chamber, at that depth. The jar being warm is the tooth recognizing it's still near the source." A moment. "Tell me whether the warmth changed after you opened it."

*On the Year 647 record:*
"It describes a project. The engineering language is clinical — a technical record, not a philosophical one — but the purpose is clear if you know what you're reading. There was a Ring, there was a passage, and there was something the engineer called 'the prior work' that preceded both. He didn't know what it was. He knew it was load-bearing in a sense he wasn't sure was structural. I spent a year figuring out what he meant. The record is in the Vethis sealed archive under a classification that shouldn't exist for an engineering report. That classification is the most interesting thing about it. Someone understood what they were filing."
