# Ardenmoor
*Forest / Wilderness — Levels 5–15 — Ranger Guilds / Wood Elf*

## Overview
Ardenmoor is what happens when forest gets old enough that the trees start having opinions. The canopy here is continuous for miles — it filters the light to a permanent amber-green and makes the sky something you remember from before you entered. The Wood Elves have been in these woods since before the word "forest" needed a name, and they treat it accordingly.

This is the primary early wilderness zone and the home base of the Ranger tradition. The Wood Elf settlements are not towns — they are camps that have been camps for two hundred years. The trails are unmarked because the people who use them know where they go. New arrivals find this frustrating. Rangers find it home.

## Setting
Dense deciduous and conifer mixed forest broken by clearings, stream beds, and the occasional granite ridge. No significant settlements — the Wood Elf villages are above the sight line, platform structures in the high canopy that are invisible from the ground unless you know where to look. The Ranger guild maintains a ground-level waystation at the zone's south entrance, which is the primary hub for non-Wood-Elf visitors.

At night, the forest changes character. Nocturnal predators, a higher density of hostile encounters, and the specific quality of a lightless forest under moon that makes experienced players run toward the waystation before dark.

## Dungeon — The Rotwood Hollow
*Levels 10–15.*

A section of Ardenmoor where something corrupted the trees from the root up. The Rotwood is the infected heart of it — an underground network of hollow root systems that something has made into a home. The Wood Elves want it cleared. The dungeon's design rewards nature-aligned classes (snare, root, and entangle work especially well here) and punishes fire users (the poachers outside used fire on the forest once; the zone does not forget).

**Named:** The Bark Matron (level 15 corrupted treant) — drops Wood Elf-aligned druid focus; bark armor piece for Druids and Rangers.

## Services
- Ranger waystation (south entrance): Ranger class trainer; Fletching and Trapping trainers; arrow vendor
- Wood Elf canopy villages: accessible only with Wood Elf faction or Ranger guild standing; offer unique Herbalism recipes and Wood Elf lore quests
- No inn with full regen; bedrolls at the waystation provide partial regen bonus

## Faction Notes
The Wood Elf community in Ardenmoor is a closed faction. They start neutral to most characters and warm slowly through zone quests and completing the poacher questline (the poachers are explicitly anti-Wood-Elf). Characters who are Ranger class, Wood Elf race, or have completed the Ardenmoor questline gain access to the canopy villages.

Druids receive a mild passive bonus to faction here — the nature tradition creates a shared language even when the races aren't aligned.

## Ranger Connection
Ardenmoor is the zone where the Ranger's tracking ability becomes most useful. The forest limits sight lines to approximately 15 meters; the tracking window compensates by showing what's in range even when it's not visible. A Ranger who ignores tracking in Ardenmoor is fighting with one hand tied back.
