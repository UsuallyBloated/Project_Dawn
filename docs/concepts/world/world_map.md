# World Map — Zone Geography and Connections

## Geography Overview

The world is roughly divided into four geographic regions connected by a central access point (the Greyveil basin). Zones within a region share ecological character; cross-region travel requires passing through a hub or taking a maritime route.

---

## Regions

### Western Heartlands
*Starter and early-mid zones; predominantly Good/Neutral-aligned factions.*

- **Greenshire** (Human/Halfling starting area) — flat farmland; gateway zone
- **Ardenmoor** — forested wilderness east of Greenshire; accessible from Greenshire's eastern pass
- **Aelindra** — High Elf city in the deep Ardenmoor forest; accessible from Ardenmoor's northern road
- **Greyveil** — large neutral Human city at the river confluence; accessible from Greenshire (east road), Ardenmoor (south road), and Harrowmere (river route)

### Northern Industrial Belt
*Mid-tier zones; predominantly Gnome/Dwarf/Kobold; underground content.*

- **Cogsworth** — accessible from Greyveil (north road) or Greenshire (mountain pass)
- **Dwarven Mountain Holds** — accessible from Cogsworth (deep tunnels) or Ardenmoor (northern mountain approach)
- **The Kobold Deeps** — beneath Cogsworth; accessible from Cogsworth lower levels (Kobold faction required or combat)

### Eastern Territories
*Mid-to-high tier; mixed alignment; Evil-faction zones present.*

- **Harrowmere** — coastal port; accessible from Greyveil (river route south), Southeastern Marshlands (coast road)
- **Auroch's Rest** — plains west of the Eastern Badlands; accessible from Greyveil (east road) or Eastern Badlands (western edge)
- **Eastern Badlands** — accessible from Auroch's Rest (east), Ashfang Hold (interior)
- **Ashfang Hold** — western edge of Eastern Badlands; Evil starting area; technically accessible to non-Evil characters at significant risk

### Southern Reaches
*High-tier and Evil-faction content; includes most of the Predecessor-thread zones.*

- **Southeastern Marshlands** — swamp territory south of Harrowmere; accessible from Harrowmere (coast road) or Ixareth-Kul (northern approach)
- **Fae Mere** — inland, south of Ardenmoor; accessible from Ardenmoor (deep forest path) or Blighted Wood (border)
- **The Blighted Wood** — eastern Ardenmoor, corrupted; accessible from Ardenmoor (eastern border) or Fae Mere (north edge)
- **The Ashen Coast** — volcanic shoreline south of Harrowmere; accessible from Harrowmere (maritime route) or Southeastern Marshlands (coast)
- **Khala Savannahs** — open grassland south of Eastern Badlands; accessible from Eastern Badlands (southern pass) or Southeastern Marshlands (inland route)
- **Ixareth-Kul — Ruined Dominion** — deep south; accessible from Southeastern Marshlands (jungle approach) or The Bonecourt (ancient road)
- **The Bonecourt** — Kel`varath starting zone; jungle ruins northeast of Ixareth-Kul; accessible from Ixareth-Kul (ancient road) or Southeastern Marshlands (river route)

### Underground Network
*Cross-regional contested tunnels; mid-to-high tier; contested alignment.*

- **The Underdark Crossing** — connects Cogsworth deep levels (north) to Vel'Sharath approaches (south); accessible from Kobold Deeps or Dwarven Holds (contested entry points) or Vel'Sharath (southern approach)
- **Vel'Sharath** — deepest underground; end of the Underdark Crossing's southern terminus; Evil-aligned city

## Maritime Routes

Harrowmere is the primary maritime hub. The Harrowmere harbormaster offers routes to:
- **Ashen Coast** — standard route; available once Harrowmere Compact faction Tier 1+
- **Open sea exploration** — unlocks after Drowned Hold questline; leads to unnamed island content (future zone stub)

The Ashen Coast's Cinder Docks offer a secondary maritime connection back to Harrowmere.

## Travel Notes

**Natural chokepoints:**
- *Greyveil* is the unavoidable hub for Western-to-Eastern travel overland. Its neutrality is structurally load-bearing — Evil characters need it for access to the eastern half of the map.
- *Harrowmere* controls maritime traffic and the southern coast. The Dockmasters' Compact's neutrality serves the same function as Greyveil's.
- *The Underdark Crossing* is the only route to Vel'Sharath for non-Dark Elf characters who don't have exceptional above-ground access. The Kobold/Dark Elf conflict in the tunnels is not purely hostile — the Grey Market operates in the neutral sections.

**Faction-restricted access:**
- Good-aligned characters face guard hostility entering Vel'Sharath
- Evil-aligned characters face guard hostility entering Aelindra and Dwarven Holds
- Ashfang Hold tolerates non-Evil visitors at some risk; the Hold evaluates on utility
- Ixareth-Kul's three factions are internally hostile to each other but all treat outsiders as potential assets before enemies

**The Predecessor thread in geography:**
The Sunken Ziggurat (Southeastern Marshlands), the Bone Citadel (Eastern Badlands), Ixareth-Kul lower levels, and the Fae Mere's Reflection Below are all connected by Predecessor presence. Characters assembling this picture will have traveled across three geographic regions and through two contested underground systems — the world geography enforces the quest's scope.
