# The Third Digger Chain — Cross-Zone Mystery Thread
*A connected questline spanning four zones. The survey that was supposed to complete three hundred years ago.*

---

## Overview

The Third Digger is a Dominion-era construct found in the Underdark Crossing. It has survey orders. The orders point to a location in the Architect site network. The location is Ixareth-Kul. The survey was supposed to be completed before the Dominion War. It was not. The Third Digger has been continuing the survey, alone, in the tunnels, for three hundred years.

This questline connects:
1. **Underdark Crossing** — The Third Digger (discovery and initial contact)
2. **Varek's Spire** — The Residual and the Architect notation (context for what the survey is)
3. **Ashen Marches** — The Third Army's objective at the convergence point (parallel survey mission)
4. **Ixareth-Kul / Ruined Dominion** — The Dominion Vault (the survey's destination)

Players do not need to complete the chain in order. But the chain makes the most sense in this order, and the Vault resolution is richer if players have context from all four zones.

---

## Zone 1 — Underdark Crossing: The Third Digger

**Discovery:** The Third Digger is in the Digger's Warren dungeon (the Underdark Crossing's dungeon content). Players encounter it in a chamber that should not exist — the geometry is wrong; the tunnel is wider than the surrounding rock should allow; the floor has survey markings in Architect notation.

The Third Digger is a Dominion-era construct — stone and iron, roughly humanoid, built for sustained operation in confined spaces. It is still working. It has been widening the same tunnel for three hundred years, because its orders specify minimum clearance for "authorized passage" and the tunnel does not yet meet specification. The specification is for something much larger than a person.

**Initial contact:** The Third Digger does not attack players on sight. It checks them against an authorization registry. Players fail the check — they are not in the registry. The construct issues a warning, then an escalating series of instructions to leave the survey area. If players leave the immediate area, it returns to work. If they stay, it engages.

**The survey orders (recoverable):** If players defeat the Third Digger or find a copy of its orders in the Warren's records area, the orders reveal:
- The construct was built by the Dominion Survey Corps
- Its mission: widen and improve the Architect transit route beneath the mountain range to a specification provided by an Architect-notation document the orders reference but do not reproduce
- The destination of the route: a specific location in Ixareth-Kul that the orders call "the survey terminus"
- Authorization requirement: the construct will grant passage only to bearers of a valid Architect authorization token
- Note at the bottom of the orders, in different ink (added later): "Completion target moved to post-cessation. Resume on cessation of active hostilities."

The cessation of active hostilities (the Dominion War) happened two hundred and eighty-nine years ago. The Third Digger has not received the cessation notice. It has been waiting for the notice before completing the last phase of the survey.

**What players can do:**
- Fight the Third Digger (it returns if not destroyed; it returns because its orders include "resume on resumption of operations")
- Find the cessation notice (it exists; it was issued by the Dominion Survey Corps at war's end; it was sent by courier; the courier died in the Marches; the notice is in the Marches battle records)
- Obtain an Architect authorization token (the Gate-Key from the Underbreach is not the same thing, but the Final Survey order believes it might be; they are wrong, but usefully wrong)

## Zone 2 — Varek's Spire: The Notation

**Connection:** The survey orders reference an Architect-notation document for the tunnel specification. Varek has ninety years of notes that include Architect notation he wrote during "the months when I was not entirely myself." One section of those notes contains what appears to be a transit specification — tunnel clearances, route coordinates in a coordinate system that does not map to current geography, and a phrase that translates as "survey terminus: see remainder protocol."

**Varek's contribution:** He cannot fully decode what he wrote. He can tell players that the notation is genuine — it matches the Architect notation at the Sunken Ziggurat — and that "remainder protocol" is a phrase he has encountered before in connection with the Residual.

**What players learn here:** The transit route the Third Digger is building is not a road. It is a conduit — something designed to allow passage of something that requires minimum clearances far larger than a Dominion military unit. The Third Digger was building something the Dominion was commissioned to build by someone else, for a purpose the Dominion Survey Corps recorded but may not have understood.

**The authorization token question:** Varek has an object on his desk (the floor 7 study) that he describes as "something I found in B2 when I was clearing space in year 23." It is a small stone disc with Architect notation on both faces. He has not decoded it. He has been using it as a paperweight. The notation, if decoded using the cipher key from the Millhaven Cellars or the Sunken Ziggurat, identifies it as an authorization token — one of the Architects' access credentials. This is the genuine article. It will clear the Third Digger's authorization check.

Varek will give it to players if they explain what it is. He will say, "I thought it was interesting. I did not know it was useful. I am glad it is useful. I think." He will want to know what they find.

## Zone 3 — Ashen Marches: The Parallel Mission

**Connection:** The Third Army's objective — the survey assessment at the convergence point — is a different but related mission. The Third Army was sent to assess the survey terminus from above: to reach the convergence point, determine the status of the Architect site there, and report.

The Third Digger was building the approach from below. The Third Army was sent to evaluate the destination from the surface. These were two parts of the same mission, executed by two different organizations, coordinated by the Dominion Survey Corps acting on behalf of a principal whose identity is in the Dominion Vault.

**What players learn here:** The convergence point in the Marches is not where the survey terminus is. It is the surface marker for the survey terminus — an Architect calibration point that marks the location of something underground, directly below the Marches. The Third Digger is building toward it from the Underdark Crossing direction. The survey terminus is the intersection of the two missions.

The cessation notice the Third Digger is waiting for is in the Records Tent of the Final Encampment. The Nameless Commander has it — the courier who was supposed to deliver it to the Underdark Crossing was Third Army, and they died in the Marches before delivery. The Nameless Commander has been sitting on the cessation notice for three hundred years. They do not know it is the cessation notice — it was filed with the battle records and marked with a Survey Corps designator they did not know to cross-reference.

**What players can do:** Find the cessation notice in the Records Tent, understand its significance, and decide whether to deliver it to the Third Digger. This triggers the Third Digger's Phase 3 operation — it completes the tunnel and the route opens.

## Zone 4 — Ixareth-Kul: The Survey Terminus

**Connection:** The survey terminus is beneath Ixareth-Kul, accessible through the Dominion Vault once opened. The Third Dominion Orders all want what is in the Vault. What is in the Vault is partly the Architects' communication record (Walking Name) and partly the Final Survey documentation (Final Survey). What none of them know is that the Vault's deepest level contains the survey terminus itself — the Architect site that the Third Digger's transit route was built to service.

**The transit route, complete:** If players have delivered the cessation notice and the Third Digger has completed the tunnel, the transit route connects the Underdark Crossing to the Dominion Vault's lower levels. This provides a second entrance to the Vault that bypasses the three Orders' contested surface access. Players who have followed the full chain can use this route.

**The terminus itself:** Functionally, it is the same class of object as the Residual in Varek's basement — a remainder, a terminus point in the Architect network. It is larger. It is older. It has been in the Dominion Vault's deepest level for longer than the Vault has existed around it — the Dominion built down to it and built the Vault to protect it.

The terminus's status is the survey assessment the Third Army was supposed to deliver and the Third Digger was building toward. The status: it is warm. It has always been warm. It is waiting for something. What it is waiting for is in the Architect notation in the terminus chamber — notation that cross-references with Varek's B3 notes, with the Still Throne in the Sunken Ziggurat, and with the survey orders of the Third Digger.

The full answer — what the Architect system is, what the remainder protocol does, what the terminus is waiting for — is the server-scale discovery event. It is resolved in the Vault endgame.

## Standalone vs. Chain

Each zone's content stands alone:
- Underdark Crossing: The Third Digger is an interesting dungeon boss regardless of the chain
- Varek's Spire: The Residual mystery is complete without the Third Digger's survey orders
- Ashen Marches: The Nameless Commander's resolution works without knowing the cessation notice's survey significance
- Ixareth-Kul: The Vault is the zone's endgame regardless of how players got there

Players who follow the full chain get: the authorization token (clears Third Digger's check), the alternate Vault entrance, and the full context for what the terminus is and what the system is waiting for. Players who do not follow the chain get a good dungeon and a good endgame zone. The additional layer is for players who read everything.
