# Dwarven Mountain Holds
*Underground / Dungeon — Levels 15–25 — Dwarf Clans*

## Overview
The Holds are not one place. They are a network of connected strongholds cut into a mountain range, each governed by a clan, all connected by the Deep Road — the primary artery that Dwarven civilization has maintained and expanded for several hundred years. What the surface looks like is irrelevant. The surface is where you approach from. The Holds are below.

Dwarven culture here is forge-first. Every conversation eventually comes back to what has been made, what is being made, and whether the person you are talking to is qualified to have an opinion about what is being made. Blacksmithing reaches its highest possible development in the Holds. Runeforging — the fusion of metalwork and runework that produces permanently enchanted items — is taught exclusively here, and the Dwarves are not in a rush to expand access.

The Holds are Good-aligned territory. This is less about divine conviction and more about the fact that the Dwarven clan structure values craft, legacy, and order — and all three of those things tend to push culture toward Good over time.

## Setting
Stone everywhere. Vaulted ceilings, carved reliefs of clan lineages on major corridor walls, the constant sound of the forge districts in the background. Light comes from embedded runestones in the ceiling — a permanent soft amber that mimics midday outside, because the Dwarves decided centuries ago that they preferred not to have their circadian rhythms governed by the sun.

The surface approach is a switchback mountain trail to the main gate. Below the gate: the Great Hall (the civilian and merchant level), the Forge District (gated to guild members and high-standing visitors), the Clan Halls (private), and the Deep Road extending between Holds.

## Dungeon — The Sealed Forges
*Levels 20–25.*

Four generations ago, a section of the Forge District was sealed. The official reason the Dwarves give is structural instability. The actual reason is that what they were making in there started making things back. The Sealed Forges dungeon is industrial-horror aesthetic: functional forge equipment, partially finished constructs that resumed construction on their own, and the understanding that every enemy here was built — not born — and is following a blueprint.

**Named:** Rune-Smith Korath (level 25 construct — the original Rune-Smith, who sealed himself in with his work) — drops Runeforging schematic; plate chest with embedded rune bonus.

## Services
- Great Hall market: best Blacksmithing and Armorsmithing components in the game; Dwarven ales (Brewing tradition buffs); Mining and Smelting trainers
- Forge District (high-standing visitors): Blacksmithing and Weaponsmithing advanced trainers; Runeforging trainer (Dwarf race only at first; unlockable for others at maximum Forge District standing)
- Clan Hall access: quest-gated; Dwarf racial lineage quests; Runeforging recipes that cannot be found anywhere else

## Faction Notes
The Dwarven Clan Council governs all standing here. Dwarves start with maximum access. Other races start at wary — the Holds are not hostile to outsiders, but they are not immediately welcoming either.

The fastest path to Forge District access for non-Dwarves is the Deep Gnome questline: the Deep Gnome Raiders are an active threat to the Holds' lower levels, and clearing that content raises standing faster than any other path.

Runeforging access for non-Dwarves requires maximum standing AND completion of a specific multi-stage quest that the Clan Council assigns only to characters who have already demonstrated their crafting competency. It is deliberately hard to get. The Dwarves are not giving this away.
