# Vel'Sharath
*Dark Elf City / Underground — Levels 35–50 — Dark Elf / Necromancer Guilds*

## Overview
Vel'Sharath is the endgame Evil city. Getting here requires either Evil alignment, Dark Elf race, or a sufficiently neutral standing built through the Necromancer guild network — and even then, the inner districts have their own requirements. The city is not hostile to outsiders by policy; it is hostile by default and can be made less so through demonstrated competence and usefulness.

The Necromancer Guilds maintain their most advanced operations here. Grave Robbing and Necromantic Scribing are taught at their highest levels in the Vault District. The Dark Elf political structure — house politics, shadow alliances, the specific kind of social navigation that operates through implication and consequence rather than declaration — is the dominant texture of the city's quests.

This is the zone where Evil-aligned characters find the game's most concentrated support for their playstyle. The city does not judge alignment. It rewards being useful, and "useful" here means different things than it does in Greyveil.

## Setting
Underground, lit by bioluminescent fungi, captured light from deep crystal formations, and the enchantment infrastructure the Dark Elves have maintained for centuries. The architecture is vertical and precise — every surface that can carry a carving does, and the carvings are historical records in a script that requires specific training to read. The city is beautiful in a way that is entirely clear about not being designed for your comfort.

Five districts: the Gate District (open to anyone who arrives), the Trade District (functional commerce; faction-gated), the Vault District (Necromancer operations; Evil alignment required), the House Halls (Dark Elf house politics; Dark Elf race or maximum faction standing required), and the Deep Quarter (where things happen that the other four districts maintain comfortable distance from).

## Dungeon — The Unlicensed Wing
*Levels 42–50.*

The Vault District has an official section and an unofficial section. The Necromancer Guilds officially do not operate in the Unlicensed Wing. Unofficially, the Unlicensed Wing is where experiments that would be flagged in formal review have been running for somewhere between thirty and eighty years, depending on which Guild member you ask, and none of them are consistent. The dungeon is the highest-difficulty content in the game's current tier: entirely undead, entirely shadow-and-necrotic damage type, entirely designed for groups with coordinated CC, a dedicated tank with Fear Ward (or Ancestor Vessel), and a Cleric who has prepared specifically for undead content.

**Named:** Archivist Vellandras Sinn (level 50) — The Senior Researcher. Vellandras was the lead researcher of the Unlicensed Wing's founding project, which was not the project that has been running there since. The original project was relatively benign: extended animation research, testing whether Animate Dead protocols could be made reversible. That project was completed in its first decade. What Vellandras then discovered in the notes of the Wing's previous unauthorized occupant — someone operating there before the current guilds existed, before Vel'Sharath was Vel'Sharath in its current form — became the second project, which required a third project to contain, which required a fourth project to study. Vellandras never left the Wing. This was eventually formalized as a staffing arrangement by guilds that found the output useful enough to fund without asking questions.

Vellandras is not hostile on sight. The difficulty of the encounter is not that Vellandras is aggressive; it is that the things Vellandras has animated over fifty years of undisclosed research are extremely aggressive, the Wing is their territory, and Vellandras has designed the space such that the most direct route to the deeper chambers requires solving, in real time, three escalating versions of the same containment problem that Vellandras never fully solved. The implication — that the reason Vellandras never left is the same reason no one has successfully broken in — is left for the player to arrive at.

Drops the highest-tier shadow equipment in the game; Lich Form-relevant transformation component for Necromancers; a journal of the original project whose notes, if brought to the Ash Scribes of Ixareth-Kul, trigger a significant Predecessor questline revelation.

## Services
- Gate District vendors: shadow reagents; high-level cloth armor; Necromantic Scribing components
- Trade District (Evil alignment or Vel'Sharath faction): advanced Enchanting (shadow-school); dark tradeskill components not available elsewhere; Black Alchemy advanced trainer
- Vault District (Evil alignment required): Necromancer advanced trainers; Animate Dead instruction; Lich Form questline entry; Grave Robbing master trainer
- House Halls (Dark Elf or maximum faction): political questlines; house affiliation; the most complex faction content in the game

## Faction Notes
Vel'Sharath Standing is the primary faction. Dark Elves start at favorable. Necromancers start at cautious-positive. All others start at wary or hostile depending on alignment.

Good-aligned characters attempting to enter Vel'Sharath face immediate guard hostility. Neutral-aligned characters can enter the Gate District and work from there. The fastest standing gain for non-Evil characters is through the Necromancer Guild questlines, which are not alignment-gated (the Guilds care about what you know, not how you feel about it).

The house politics layer is the deepest social content in the game. Affiliating with one house creates antagonism with at least one other house. The political questlines can result in significant alignment shifts downward — the work the houses assign is not morally neutral.
