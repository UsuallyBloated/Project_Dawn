# The Kobold Deeps
*Evil Starting Area — Levels 1–10 — Kobold*

## Overview
The tunnel network beneath Cogsworth's lowest level, controlled by four Kobold packs in a relationship somewhere between alliance and armed truce. The Deeps are not a city — they are a distribution of pack territory connected by shared tunnels and one neutral market space. New Kobold characters begin here, claimed by the Ashfang pack, the most outward-facing of the four.

The Gnomes above know the Deeps exist. They do not acknowledge this officially, because acknowledging it would require doing something about it, and doing something about it would require going down there, and going down there is a project no one in the Clock Guild or Steam Guild has successfully proposed a budget for. The détente suits both parties.

## Setting
Tunnels cut in two scales: the Kobold-native passages (four feet high, comfortable for small races, a crawl for everyone else) and the older pre-Gnome excavations that both Kobolds and Gnomes use for larger transit. The Deeps smell of packed earth, tallow candles, and whatever the Ashfang pack cooked most recently. Bioluminescent moss has been cultivated on key tunnel walls — navigation aid and light source combined.

The Ashfang pack den is the largest structure: a carved-out chamber with a central fire, sleeping piles, a cache tunnel, a hatchery, and the elder's nook. The Tunnel Market sits at the intersection of all four pack territories, genuinely neutral ground maintained by Matron Zikka. The Dragon Shrine is a short walk from the market, tended by Shaman Rikka, and is the only space in the Deeps where all four packs are explicitly welcome simultaneously.

## Dungeon — The Abandoned Works (Upper Level)
*Levels 6–10.*

The pre-Gnome excavation level directly below the Kobold Deeps. The Kobolds use the upper section for hunting and salvage; the lower section is genuinely unknown — the Kobolds have been marking tunnels down here for thirty years and the map still doesn't close. The dungeon proper begins where the Kobold markings stop.

**Named:** The Mechanism (level 10) — a pre-Gnome automated system still running its original function, which appears to have been mining. It has been mining the same seam for approximately four hundred years. The seam is now a room-sized void. The Mechanism is not hostile to Kobolds specifically, which the Kobolds find deeply suspicious and the Gnomes, when told about it, find deeply alarming.

## Services

- Senior Female Fikka (cultural authority; quests with political weight)
- Shaman Rikka (Shaman class trainer; Beast Master trainer; Dragon Shrine blessing buffs)
- Matron Zikka (inter-pack quests; Rogue / Bard trainer)
- Pack supply cache (basic goods; stolen Gnome components available)

## Faction Notes
The Ashfang Pack is the primary starting faction. Standing rises through pack contribution quests, falls through selfishness (not sharing loot with the pack) or losing the pack territory. High Ashfang standing unlocks Fikka's deeper questline and access to the cache tunnel's best stock.

Three secondary pack factions: Deepclaw, Rustbone, Mudtooth. All begin neutral. Inter-pack quests from Matron Zikka can shift any of them toward allied or hostile. The Tunnel Market is only accessible while at least one non-Ashfang pack is neutral or better.

Gnome factions in Cogsworth above are automatically suspicious of Kobold characters. Access to the upper Cogsworth levels (the Clockgate Market at Level 3) requires specific reputation work — possible, but the Gnomes start from a position of polite hostility and move slowly.
