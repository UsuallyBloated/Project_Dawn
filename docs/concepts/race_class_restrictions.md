# Race / Class Restrictions

*Restrictions are cultural, physiological, or both. A blocked combination means character creation will not offer it — not that no individual of that race has ever done it.*

*See `LOCKED_COMBOS` in `data/character_data.gd` for current code state. Full restrictions are design targets, not yet fully implemented.*

## Restrictions by Class

**Warrior** — Available to all races.

**Paladin** — Human, Elf, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Vah Shir, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: all Evil-aligned races. Fae follow no mortal religious hierarchies.

**Shadow Knight** — Human, Dark Elf, Half-Elf, Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Minotaur, Kobold, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: all Good-aligned races. Requires a genuine darkness that cannot be performed.

**Cleric** — Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Ogre, Troll, Minotaur, Vah Shir, Kobold, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: Fae (no mortal gods). Iksar follow necromantic tradition rather than clerical orders.

**Druid** — Human, Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Minotaur, Fae, Vah Shir.
Blocked: Dark Elf (severed from living nature by generations underground), Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Kobold, Half-Ogre.

**Shaman** — Human, Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Minotaur, Vah Shir, Kobold, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: races without active ancestor-spirit traditions. Gnome ancestor spirits actively resist the summons.

**Rogue** — Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Iksar, Fae, Vah Shir, Kobold.
Blocked: Ogre, Troll, Minotaur, Half-Ogre — size and gait incompatible with the craft.

**Monk** — Human, Elf, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Iksar, Vah Shir.
Blocked: Ogre, Troll, Minotaur, Half-Ogre (mass and temperament); Fae (size); Dark Elf (the arrogance the training cannot overcome); Gnome (the body will not cooperate with what the mind attempts).

**Ranger** — Human, Elf, Wood Elf, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Minotaur, Fae, Vah Shir.
Blocked: Dark Elf, Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Gnome, Kobold, Half-Ogre.

**Witch Hunter** — Human, Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Vah Shir, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: all Evil-aligned races (they are the hunted), Minotaur, Fae, Ogre, Troll.

**Bard** — Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Minotaur, Fae, Vah Shir, Kobold.
Blocked: Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Half-Ogre.

**Magician** — All races except Ogre (INT floor too low for arcane study).

**Wizard** — Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Gnome, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Iksar, Fae.
Blocked: Ogre, Troll (cannot hold the concepts), Wood Elf (oral tradition only), Halfling (no institutional arcane tradition), Minotaur (displaced people, no fixed schools), Vah Shir (no tradition), Kobold (lifespan too short for meaningful academic completion), Half-Ogre.

**Sorcerer** — Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Troll, Iksar, Fae, Vah Shir, Kobold, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: Ogre, Minotaur — the bloodline power exists but manifests as uncontrolled destructive episodes rather than usable magic.

**Enchanter** — Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Half-Elf, Fae.
Blocked: Dwarf, Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Minotaur, Vah Shir, Kobold, Half-Ogre — requires sustained concentration and social intelligence.

**Necromancer** — Human, Dark Elf, Gnome, Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Kobold.
Blocked: Elf, Halfling, Dwarf (the prohibition is cultural bedrock, not law), Wood Elf, Half-Elf, Minotaur, Fae, Vah Shir, Half-Ogre.

**Blood Mage** — Human, Dark Elf, Half-Elf, Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Kobold, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: all Good-aligned races, Gnome, Fae (biology incompatible), Minotaur, Vah Shir, Wood Elf.

**Beast Master** — Human, Elf, Wood Elf, Half-Elf, Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Minotaur, Vah Shir, Kobold, Half-Ogre.
Blocked: Dark Elf (severed from living nature; the warder-bond cannot form in soil that no longer answers them), Gnome (the analytical frame that forecloses the instinctual surrender the bond requires), Halfling (relationship to animals is domestic and agrarian — the warder-bond draws on predator instinct, not keeper instinct), Dwarf (stone culture; deep respect for the living world but no tradition of spirit-bonding with beasts), Fae (magic is illusory and mind-based; the beast-spirit bond operates on an incompatible channel, and their form is not suited to the melee demands of the class).
