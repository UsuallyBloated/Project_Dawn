# Kel`varath
*Evil — Fallen Empire — The Cold Inheritance*

## Overview
The Kel`varath were once an empire. This is not pride or myth — it is documented history in ruins scattered across the eastern continent, in script that surviving Kel`varath scholars can still read. The Kel`varath Dominion built cities that lasted a thousand years, developed necromantic traditions that remain the most advanced on the continent, and fielded armies that multiple races combined forces to destroy. They succeeded. The Dominion fell three hundred years ago, leaving a scattered diaspora of survivors navigating a world that remembers what they were and has complicated feelings about it.

Modern Kel`varath carry this weight with varying responses. Some hold to the old Dominion culture with fierce pride — the necromantic traditions, the rigid hierarchy, the cold assessment of every other race as either useful or inferior. Some actively reject it, trying to build something new in a world that doesn't quite trust them yet. Most land somewhere in between: pragmatic about what the inheritance costs them socially while unwilling to pretend it doesn't exist.

They are not a warm people. Kel`varath emotional expression is subtle to the point of invisibility by mammalian standards — a Troll reads their flatness as contempt; an Elf reads it as arrogance; Humans tend to assume hostility. Kel`varath who navigate successfully in non-Kel`varath company generally develop an intentional overlay of legible signals — not fakery, but translation. Those who don't bother tend to confirm every prejudice their history built.

## Physical Description
| Sex | Height | Weight | Build | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Male | 5'10"–6'6" | 180–270 lbs | Leaner, brighter scale coloration | Display frill at neck flares during aggression or intimidation |
| Female | 6'2"–7'0" | 220–320 lbs | Larger, heavier scale plating | Subtler coloration (better camouflage); slightly broader skull; carry the eggs |

Kel`varath are scaled, cold-blooded, and built with the specific economy of movement that comes from not generating body heat internally — no wasted motion. Scales cover the body completely; color ranges from dark olive to deep blue-black to a vivid jade green, with males typically more vibrant and females more muted (an evolutionary inversion from most races). Eyes are golden or amber with vertical pupils. The display frill along the male neck is a flat, fan-like structure of thin bone and skin that flares involuntarily under emotional stress — Kel`varath males who deal with non-Kel`varath regularly spend considerable effort learning to suppress it.

Reversed sexual dimorphism is a cultural non-issue internally; Kel`varath society built around female size advantage as an evolutionary fact. It confuses outsiders consistently.

## Homeland & Society
The Kel`varath diaspora has no unified homeland — the Dominion capital is ruined, its reconstruction not yet agreed on even in principle. Kel`varath settlements exist in the southeastern jungles (the original Dominion heartland, now mostly reclaimed by vegetation), scattered in Neutral city-states that tolerate them, and in a handful of independent communities where former Dominion subjects have built something new.

What remains of organized Kel`varath culture centers on the necromantic orders — the Bonewrights, the Soulcarvers, and the Ash Scribes — which preserved the most sophisticated elements of Dominion scholarship through the collapse. These orders function as both academic institutions and social infrastructure for the diaspora: they train, they record history, they settle disputes. Membership is not required, but unaffiliated Kel`varath lack the support network the orders provide.

The relationship between Kel`varath and other races is defined by the Dominion's history. Most Evil-aligned races find them acceptable or useful. Most Good and Neutral races range from cautious to actively hostile, depending on how directly their ancestors encountered the Dominion. Some Human trading cities are pragmatically neutral — Kel`varath necromantic services have genuine market value. Wood Elves will not negotiate with them at all.

## Racial Traits
- **Stat profile:** High STR (females especially), INT; CHA penalty with most non-Kel`varath races; natural armor from scales
- **Cold-blooded:** Resistant to heat-based effects; vulnerability to extreme cold
- **Claws:** Natural attack option
- **Regeneration (minor):** Slower than Trolls but scales regrow; minor passive HP regen out of combat
- **Starting region:** Southeastern jungles, Kel`varath settlements
- **Faction standing:** Deeply negative with Elf, Wood Elf, Halfling; cautious neutral or negative with most races; accepted among Evil-aligned factions

## Available Classes
Warrior, Shadow Knight, Shaman, Beast Master, Rogue, Monk, Magician, Wizard, Sorcerer, Necromancer, Blood Mage.

**Blocked:** Paladin (Evil alignment), Cleric (Kel`varath follow necromantic tradition rather than clerical orders), Druid (no connection to living nature), Ranger (no tradition), Witch Hunter (alignment), Bard (the culture doesn't produce them), Enchanter (requires social intelligence the culture doesn't cultivate).

## Naming Conventions
Kel`varath names are sibilant and precise — the language favors long vowels and crisp consonant endings. The old Dominion naming system assigned rank-prefixes (dropped after the collapse); what remains is the personal name, a lineage marker, and optionally an order affiliation suffix. Non-Kel`varath find the lineage markers difficult to pronounce and usually omit them.

**Male examples:** Arix, Drask, Iketh, Kazeth, Raxx, Sareth, Thrix, Uval, Veth, Xariss
**Female examples:** Arissa, Draxis, Ixara, Kazara, Raxxa, Sareth, Thrixa, Uvala, Vetha, Xariss
**Lineage markers:** -kar, -veth, -rix, -ss (appended or prefixed by family tradition)
**Order suffixes:** -wright (Bonewrights), -carver (Soulcarvers), -scribe (Ash Scribes)
