# Half-Ogre
*Neutral — Children of Collision — The Door Between*

## Overview
Half-Ogres exist at the intersection of two worlds that did not plan to produce them. The union of Ogre and Human is almost always the result of circumstances that neither community would have chosen — war, captivity, coercion, or the specific desperation of two people on the margins of their respective worlds who found each other there. The result is a child that belongs fully to neither culture and must build identity from what's available, which turns out to include quite a lot.

They are large enough to intimidate and too Human-featured to be taken entirely seriously among Ogres. They are too Human to be classified Evil by default but carry the Ogre heritage in every physical line of their body. Most Half-Ogres spend their early years navigating this: the Human city that isn't sure what to do with someone that big who can also speak in complete sentences, and the Ogre tribe that isn't sure what to do with someone that weak who has all these opinions about how things should be done.

Those who survive this navigation tend to develop a particular toughness — not the brutal endurance of full Ogres, but a social and psychological resilience built from having been the uncomfortable thing in every room and learning to make it work. The Half-Ogre Paladin exists. The faith required to get there, against the background of both parent cultures' skepticism, is not incidental. It is the whole story.

## Physical Description
| Sex | Height | Weight | Build | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Male | 6'8"–7'4" | 330–450 lbs | Heavy brow, broader jaw, more prominent vestigial tusks | Tusk visibility is the clearest sex indicator; skin tone greener on average |
| Female | 6'4"–7'0" | 270–380 lbs | Slightly softer jaw, tusks often absent or minimal | Human heritage shows more strongly in the face; still towers over most other races |

Half-Ogres are large — substantially larger than the biggest Humans, shorter and lighter than full Ogres. The Ogre heritage shows in the bone structure: heavy brow, wide jaw, broad hands, the characteristic slight forward hunch of the shoulders. Skin tones are human-range but often shifted toward grey-green. Tusks are present in males more often than females; when present they are smaller than Ogre tusks, sometimes only vestigial. The Human heritage softens the features enough that expression is substantially more readable than a full Ogre's.

## Homeland & Society
Half-Ogres have no homeland, which they share with Half-Elves but experience quite differently. A Half-Elf in a Human city is slightly exotic; a Half-Ogre in a Human city requires some adjustment. Most Half-Ogres end up in the frontier settlements and borderland cities — places where the population is used to variety, where physical capability is valued practically, and where nobody's background is too closely examined.

The Colosseum cities of the eastern trade corridor have historically large Half-Ogre populations — the arena culture valued their size and endurance, and over generations a community formed around it. Whether this is origin myth or documented history depends who you ask, but the community is real and now extends well beyond arena work.

Half-Ogre culture is emergent rather than inherited — they have taken pieces from both parent cultures and assembled something new. From the Ogre side: direct hierarchy, respect for demonstrated strength, loyalty to those who've earned it. From the Human side: the possibility of complex social roles, the value of institutions, the concept of earned status through means other than combat. The result is a culture that is more pragmatically flexible than either parent, and somewhat more comfortable with contradiction.

## Racial Traits
- **Stat profile:** High STR, STA; lower INT, AGI
- **Thick build:** Natural damage reduction; harder to knock down or move against their will
- **Presence:** CHA is not penalized — Half-Ogres have learned to use their size socially as well as physically; some find them compelling rather than threatening
- **Starting region:** Frontier settlements, eastern trade corridor cities
- **Faction standing:** Cautious neutral from most; accepted in Neutral trading cities; negative with some Good-aligned settlements; mixed reception from Ogre tribes

**Racial abilities:**
- **Thick Build** — Natural 3 AC; passive 3% physical damage reduction; knockback effects reduced by 40%
- **Slam** — Natural attack: 12 base damage; usable as an off-hand attack without a weapon
- **Imposing Presence** — Humanoid enemies have a 7% chance to hesitate (lose first swing) when engaging; CHA not penalized (learned to make size work socially)
- **Endurance** — Fatigue takes 20% longer to accumulate; useful for prolonged dungeon sessions

**Starting skill bonuses:** +5 2H Weapons; +5 1H Weapons; +5 Defense

## Available Classes
Warrior, Paladin, Shadow Knight, Cleric, Shaman, Beast Master, Witch Hunter, Magician, Sorcerer, Blood Mage.

**Blocked:** Druid (no living-nature tradition), Rogue (size and gait incompatible with the craft), Monk (mass and temperament), Ranger (no tradition), Bard (blocked per the restrictions — the cultural breadth the Bard requires hasn't coalesced in Half-Ogre communities), Wizard (no institutional tradition), Enchanter (requires sustained social intelligence in a specific direction), Necromancer (the taboo carried from Human parent culture holds; the Ogre side doesn't practice it either in any sophisticated form).

## Naming Conventions
Half-Ogre names are a direct reflection of upbringing. Those raised in Human communities have Human names. Those raised in Ogre tribes have Ogre names. Those raised in Half-Ogre communities tend toward human-adjacent names with a heavier sound — longer vowels, harder consonants — that have emerged as a distinct Half-Ogre aesthetic over generations.

**Male examples:** Arvok, Brennak, Dolvrak, Garen, Kolveth, Morrakh, Roggarn, Torval, Undrek, Vorrak
**Female examples:** Arvoka, Brenna, Dolvra, Garra, Kolva, Morra, Rogga, Torva, Undra, Vorra
**Community names:** Bloodstone Crossing, Irongate Quarter, the Reach, Threshold, Wallbreak
