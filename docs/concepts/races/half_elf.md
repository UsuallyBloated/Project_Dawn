# Half-Elf
*Neutral ”” Children of Two Worlds ”” The In-Between*

## Overview
Half-Elves are the product of a union that both parent cultures treat with varying degrees of discomfort, which means Half-Elves spend most of their lives developing a very specific kind of competence: the ability to be comfortable in rooms where not everyone wants them there. This is, it turns out, an extremely transferable skill. Half-Elves are found in every city, every guild, and every adventuring party on the continent ”” not because they are the best at any one thing, but because they are good at almost everything and genuinely at ease in almost any company.

The in-between is not a comfortable place, but it is a useful one. A Half-Elf who grows up in a Human city learns to navigate Human social structures with Elven patience and perception. One who grows up near Elven territory inherits some of the long view without the full weight of Elven obligation. Neither fully belongs, which means both worlds are partially accessible ”” and someone who can walk through two doors carries information and options that people locked to one door cannot.

Their broad class availability reflects this directly. Half-Elves can pursue almost any discipline, which means they must choose ”” and must live with the knowledge that a pureblood of either parent race may eventually surpass them in that specific path. Most Half-Elves stop caring about this around their fortieth year, which is when they finally start being good at whatever they picked.

Half-Elves present in a wide range depending on which parent's traits asserted more strongly. The giveaways are consistent regardless of skew: the ears taper to a slight point ”” not as pronounced as a full Elf, but not Human-flat either. The face carries a quality of agelessness in early adulthood that gradually becomes apparent to careful observers. Heterochromia ”” mismatched eye colors ”” occurs with much higher frequency in Half-Elves than in either parent race. It does not indicate anything magical, though people keep assuming it does.

## Homeland & Society
Half-Elves have no homeland. This is not a euphemism ”” there is no Half-Elf nation, city, or territory. They are born into Human or Elven communities, or into the border settlements between them, and they build their lives in whatever space they can find. The border towns ”” small communities along the Human-Elven frontier ”” have the highest concentrations and the most Half-Elf-friendly social norms, but these remain small.

Most Half-Elves navigate belonging as a continuous project. Some commit fully to one parent's culture and succeed in being accepted, more or less. Some refuse to commit and build identities around motion ”” traveling, trading, working across cultural lines. A notable proportion are adventurers, which is less a career and more a structural solution to not having a fixed place to return to.

The result is a population that is individually resilient and collectively unorganized. There is no Half-Elf political faction, no half-elf institution. What there is: a loose, informal network of Half-Elves in most major cities who know each other, look out for each other's interests with no formal obligation, and share the specific dark humor of people who've navigated the same thing.

## Racial Traits
- **Stat profile:** Balanced and moderate ”” slight bonuses to INT and CHA; no significant weaknesses
- **Perceptive:** Bonus to social reads, detecting deception, and noticing environmental details
- **Elven heritage:** Low-light vision; marginally slower aging than Human
- **Starting region:** Border settlements, Ardenmoor, and Elven frontier territories
- **Faction standing:** Broadly neutral; welcome in most Human and Elven settlements; some Elven traditionalists are cool; generally well-regarded

## Available Classes
Warrior, Paladin, Shadow Knight, Cleric, Druid, Beast Master, Rogue, Monk, Ranger, Witch Hunter, Bard, Magician, Wizard, Sorcerer, Enchanter, Blood Mage.

**Blocked:** Shaman (no ancestor-spirit tradition established in either parent culture's Half-Elf communities), Necromancer (cultural prohibition carried over from both parent cultures ”” the Elven prohibition is cultural bedrock; the Human prohibition is softer but most Half-Elves raised in Human communities absorbed it).

## Naming Conventions
Half-Elf names draw from both parent traditions, sometimes blended and sometimes drawn straight from one. A Half-Elf raised by their Elven parent will often have an Elven name; one raised in a Human city will often have a Human name with an Elven middle name they may or may not use. Surnames are whatever the family passed down from whichever parent acknowledged the child.

**Male examples:** Aldrath, Caelvin, Davan, Ereth, Faelor, Haevyn, Lydric, Maeron, Syldan, Treveth
**Female examples:** Aelind, Brysara, Caevyn, Daven, Eliara, Faerel, Haelith, Lyrindra, Sinaeva, Taelith
**Surnames:** Variable ”” may be Elven (Vaensoth, Caerith), Human (Merrick, Ashford), or wholly original
