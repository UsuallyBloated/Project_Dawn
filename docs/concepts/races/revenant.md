# Revenant
*Evil — The Returned — The Unfinished Dead*

## Overview
Revenants did not choose to be what they are — they died, and something in the nature of their death, their alignment, and their unfinished purpose brought them back. They are not undead in the way a skeleton is undead — animated by external necromancy, operating without self. A Revenant is themselves, fully: memories intact, skills intact, personality intact. The body simply no longer lives in any metabolic sense. It is preserved at the moment of death and will not age further, heal naturally, or respond to food and drink.

Most Revenants select their starting state deliberately — the character creation implies they died at some point before the game begins and have been navigating unlife since. Some came back with no memory of how. Some pursued it. Some were simply in the wrong place at the wrong time with the wrong alignment score and came back changed. The game does not judge which is true. The mechanics are the same either way.

The unlife is not nothing. Revenants are immune to a range of things that kill the living. They see in darkness, sense the undead around them, and are not subject to fear in any ordinary sense — something that has already died has already crossed the threshold that makes fear functional. What they have lost is the infrastructure of living: food, rest, natural healing, the warmth of a body that repairs itself. What they manage instead is older and quieter and does not require rest to function.

## Physical Description
| Sex | Height | Weight | Build | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Male | As in life | Lighter than in life | Preserved at moment of death | Grey-blue pallor; still eyes — present, aware, not moving naturally |
| Female | As in life | Lighter than in life | Preserved at moment of death | Same; the wound that killed them remains as a permanent scar |

A Revenant's appearance is the last living moment, held indefinitely. Everything that was true of their race at the time of death remains: an Ogre Revenant is still Ogre-sized; a Fae Revenant still has wings. The consistent tells are the pallor — grey-blue, shifting toward blue-black in very old Revenants — the temperature (ambient, not body heat), and the eyes, which are present and aware but without the constant micro-movement of living eyes. The wound that triggered the death is preserved as a visible scar regardless of its original severity.

## Homeland & Society
Revenants have no homeland of their own, though the neutral city of Greyveil has a larger Revenant population than most places and has developed the services and social norms to accommodate them. Outside Greyveil, Revenants navigate a world that ranges from alarmed to hostile to grudgingly pragmatic, depending on the city and their track record.

There is no formal Revenant community — the circumstances of their creation are too varied and individual. What exists instead is a scattered recognition: when two Revenants meet, they tend to skip a layer of explanation that takes longer with the living. Revenant-specific vendors in certain cities carry the supplies that work on their physiology (Revenant-compatible consumables, specific healing reagents). Necromancer guilds often employ or collaborate with Revenants in ways that make practical sense.

Evil-aligned factions treat them as unremarkable or useful. Good-aligned communities range from cautious discomfort to open hostility. A Revenant who wants to operate in Good-aligned cities must invest heavily in reputation because the default is not favorable.

## Racial Traits
- **Stat profile:** Moderate STR and STA for their origin race; high INT; penalties to CHA with Good-aligned races
- **Undead Physiology:** Immune to poison, disease, and environmental exposure damage; immune to starvation and thirst
- **Shadow Sight:** Full vision in complete darkness; passively detects other undead within 20m
- **Fear Immunity:** Immune to fear, panic, and compulsion effects; the threshold is already crossed
- **No Natural Regeneration:** Zero out-of-combat natural HP regen; healing must come from magical sources (spells, potions, Revenant-compatible consumables)
- **Magical Healing Reduction:** Healing spells restore 25% less HP than on living targets; Necromancer-school lifetap and Blood Mage life-drain work at full effectiveness
- **Death Magic Resistance:** +20 resist vs death spells and compulsion; these effects have already partially arrived

**Starting skill bonuses:** +5 Shadow-school spells; +5 Defense; +5 Perception (undead sense)

## Available Classes
Warrior, Shadow Knight, Rogue, Magician, Sorcerer, Necromancer, Blood Mage.

**Blocked:** Paladin and Cleric (dead faith — the divine structures that support these classes do not extend to the undead; some have tried; the power simply doesn't answer), Druid (no living-nature connection remains), Shaman (ancestor-spirit tradition requires a living conduit; Revenants blur the line in ways the spirits find uncomfortable), Beast Master (the warder-bond requires a living soul; Revenants retain a soul but it reads wrong to animals), Monk (the discipline is a living-body practice; the Revenant body does not respond), Ranger (no tradition), Witch Hunter (they are the subject of the hunt, not the hunter), Bard (the art requires resonance with living audiences in ways the Revenant's presence disrupts), Enchanter (mind magic requires projecting life energy — Revenants project something else), Wizard (not blocked on principle, but the few who tried report that the spellbook's underlying theory assumes a living caster; the spells work but the mana mathematics are wrong in ways that are difficult to correct).

## Naming Conventions
Revenants keep the name they had in life. Those who died before they had a settled name sometimes adopt a new one — either descriptive of their condition, drawn from the culture of whoever found them when they returned, or simply chosen as a fresh start. There is no Revenant naming tradition because there is no Revenant culture old enough to have one.

**Names:** Whatever they were in life, or whatever they chose after.
