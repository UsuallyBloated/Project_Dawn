## Overview
The Vesperin are songbird-coded avian people — small, vivid, and communal where their
Aerathi cousins are large, muted, and solitary. A Vesperin alone is an unfinished
sentence; the flock is the unit of thought, and decisions of weight are *sung through*
— debated in overlapping melodic argument until the flock settles into harmony, which
is both the method and the verdict.

Their culture runs on voice. History is oral and melodic — the great chronicles are
song-cycles that take days to perform in full, and a Vesperin who carries a chronicle
is a library with opinions. Mimicry is innate: they reproduce voices, birdsong, and
ambient sound effortlessly, which makes them the fastest language-learners in the world
and occasionally insufferable mimics of their friends.

They share the sky-heritage of the Aerathi, and both peoples hold song as the first
gift. Bards are the highest calling in Vesperin culture — not entertainers but
historians, judges, and diplomats — and Vesperin and Aerathi Bards together maintain
the old kinship between canopy and cliff.

## Physical Description
| Sex | Height | Weight | Build | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Male | 4'8"–5'2" | 55–80 lbs | Slight; brighter plumage | Songbird dimorphism — males carry the vivid display colors |
| Female | 4'6"–5'0" | 50–75 lbs | Slight; subtler plumage | Often the stronger singers; most chronicle-keepers are female |

Small and feather-light — the lightest playable race after the Fae. Plumage covers head,
arms, and back in songbird and parrot palettes: jewel greens, blues, sunset oranges,
ink-black corvid sheens. Wing-folds along the arms allow gliding, not true flight.
Plumage pattern is as readable as a Felhari tail once you know a flock's conventions —
preening, fluffing, and crest position all carry meaning.

Society is the flock — twenty to two hundred, nested within the larger canopy city.
Flocks specialize (chroniclers, traders, growers, wardens) and intermarry freely.
Status comes from voice: the ability to carry a chronicle, settle a dispute in song, or
coin a melody the whole canopy ends up humming.

## Racial Traits
- **Stat profile:** High CHA and INT, strong AGI; very low STR and STA (hollow bones, small frame)
- **Glide:** Spread-wing descent — no fall damage while gliding; long horizontal travel
- **Mimicry:** Reproduce any voice or sound heard; languages come unnaturally fast
- **Songblood:** Performance disciplines start trained; their songs carry further and linger longer
- **Vivid Plumage:** Hard to ignore and easy to like — bonuses to social reads and first impressions
- **Hollow Bones:** Light frame is the price of the sky — knockback and carry capacity suffer

## Available Classes
Warrior, Ranger, Rogue, Bard, Magician, Wizard, Sorcerer, Enchanter.

**Under discussion:** Cleric (a song-liturgy temple tradition would fit; not yet
decided), Druid (canopy-dwellers have an obvious claim to the green; not yet decided).

**Blocked:** Paladin and Shadow Knight (faith is song, not oath — no crusading
tradition in either direction), Monk (hollow bones cannot take the conditioning),
Shaman (ancestor-memory lives in the chronicles, not in summoned spirits), Beast Master
(flock-kin do not bind single beasts — kinship is plural or it is nothing), Witch
Hunter (no inquisitorial tradition), Necromancer and Blood Mage (a stilled voice is the
deepest grief; both arts are obscenity).

## Naming Conventions
*Draft.* Melodic, multi-syllable names meant to be sung — the written form is only
notation for a short personal melody, and Vesperin treat the melody as the true name.
Introducing yourself means singing it; close friends improvise variations.

**Examples:** Liraveth, Solienne, Maraveen, Cadenza-called names, Orielle, Tavishen,
Verisette, Lullaby-coded nicknames for children.
**Flock names:** the Dawn Chorus, the Bright Canopy, the Long Chronicle, the Thousand
Greens.
