# Ogre
*Evil ”” Tribal Warlords ”” The Weight of the World*

## Overview
Ogres are the largest and most physically powerful of the playable races, and they know it. Their culture organizes entirely around this fact: might is authority, the strong protect the tribe, the weak serve the strong, and challenges to leadership are resolved directly and usually quickly. This is not moral bankruptcy ”” it is a functional system that has kept Ogre tribes alive through conditions that would have destroyed more nuanced cultures. What it is not, by any reasonable outside standard, is Good.

The Evil alignment designation is accurate, but Ogres are not evil in the way Dark Elves are evil ”” calculating, patient, strategic. Ogre evil is direct. An Ogre tribe raiding a Human settlement is not making a geopolitical statement; it is feeding and supplying itself through the most efficient means available. The moral dimension does not factor in, and an Ogre who stopped to consider it would be seen as dangerously weakened by their fellows. There are exceptions ”” Ogre Paladins and Clerics exist and are marked individuals who carry both genuine faith and the weight of a culture that views that faith as a foreign import ”” but they are genuinely exceptional.

What Ogres offer to a party is simple: they absorb punishment that would kill anything else and deliver force that nothing else can match. Their intellectual limitations are real (INT penalties are significant) but not total ”” Ogres can learn, adapt, and be loyal with a ferocity that their more "intelligent" party members would not think to question.

Ogres are enormous and built with a density that makes their weight hard to guess from appearance alone. Skin tones range from grey-green to a deep forest green, sometimes mottled, rarely uniform. Eyes are small relative to the skull, often dark brown or black, and set beneath a prominent brow ridge that puts the face in permanent partial shadow. Tusks protrude from the lower jaw; in males they curve up, in females they tend outward. Ogres rarely cut their tusks, and the state of the tusks (cracked, carved, adorned) carries tribal meaning.

## Homeland & Society
Ogres hold the eastern badlands ”” a broad stretch of broken terrain between the central human lowlands and the eastern sea cliffs that is too rough for farming and too contested for permanent settlement by any other race. Ogre tribes have claimed this territory since before written history began, and they defend it with enough consistency that other races have largely stopped attempting to take it.

Tribal structure is direct: the strongest individual leads, is challenged periodically, and is either still strong or no longer the leader. The shaman class provides the only real counter-weight to raw physical hierarchy ”” shamans hold spiritual authority that warlords cannot simply overpower, and the negotiations between physical and spiritual leadership define much of Ogre internal politics. Clan shamans hold tribal memory and communicate with ancestors; without them, the tribe loses coherence.

Ogres are distrusted and feared by most races, which they find reasonable and occasionally useful. They trade rarely but will when it benefits the tribe ”” typically exchanging raw materials or labor for things the badlands cannot produce. Human merchants who deal with Ogres report that Ogre negotiators are blunter than ideal but rarely dishonest; they do not have the patience for a con.

## Racial Traits
- **Stat profile:** Very high STR, STA; very low INT, AGI; significant CHA penalty
- **Thick hide:** Natural damage reduction; Ogres are harder to hurt than their armor suggests
- **Slam:** Natural attack option in addition to weapons
- **Magician blocked:** INT floor too low for arcane study
- **Starting region:** Eastern badlands, tribal territories
- **Faction standing:** Negative with most Good-aligned races; accepted in Evil-aligned settlements; tolerated in Neutral trading posts

## Available Classes
Warrior, Shadow Knight, Cleric, Shaman, Beast Master, Necromancer, Blood Mage.

**Blocked:** Paladin (Evil alignment), Druid (no connection to living nature), Rogue (size and gait incompatible), Monk (mass and temperament), Ranger (no tradition), Witch Hunter (alignment), Bard (insufficient range of expression for the art), Magician (INT floor too low), Wizard, Sorcerer (power exists but manifests as uncontrolled destructive episodes), Enchanter (requires sustained concentration and social intelligence), Blood Mage noted as available ”” Ogres have participated in blood-pact traditions that align with the Blood Mage path.

## Naming Conventions
Ogre names are short, guttural, and built for a mouth with tusks. Long vowels are rare; hard stops and nasal consonants are common. Names are personal and tribal ”” no surnames in the Human sense; clan affiliation is appended as a modifier after the name if clarification is needed.

**Male examples:** Brak, Droog, Grath, Krug, Mogg, Runk, Thrag, Ulgrak, Varsh, Zorn
**Female examples:** Bakka, Drega, Grusha, Karma, Mokra, Rukka, Thaga, Ulga, Varsha, Zokka
**Clan designators:** of the Bloodrock, of the Ironmaw, of the Stoneback, of the Ashfang
