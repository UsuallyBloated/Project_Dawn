# Halfling
*Good ”” Pastoral Survivors ”” The Deceptive Hearth*

## Overview
Halflings look soft. This is the most consistent tactical error their enemies make. The round cheeks, the warm eyes, the immediate offer of food ”” all of it is genuine, and none of it is the whole picture. A Halfling who has decided something needs doing will do it with a calm, thorough efficiency that tends to surprise everyone who only knew them at the dinner table. They are not a warrior people in the way Dwarves are or in the way Humans sometimes become; they are a people who survive, reliably and without drama, which requires its own specific kind of toughness.

Halfling communities are built around the shire ”” a cluster of family holdings centered on common land, shared wells, and a tavern that functions as the shire's real governing body. They are collectively resistant to conquest: every Halfling military campaign in recorded history has ended with the invader occupying the land and then, over the following decade, mysteriously failing to extract anything useful from it while their supply lines quietly deteriorated. The Halflings were still there when the invaders left. They are always still there.

Their Good alignment is not philosophical. It's practical. Halflings live close enough together that bad behavior has immediate consequences, and they have a long memory for debt and favor both. They are genuinely warm people who extend genuine hospitality to strangers ”” and they also know exactly who in the region owes them a favor and roughly what it would take to call it in.

Halflings are small, rounded, and built closer to the ground than their height alone suggests ”” they are sturdier per pound than they appear, with strong legs and good balance. Foot hair is thick and coarse, usually dark, sometimes braided or decorated. Faces are expressive and frequently animated; Halflings make eye contact easily and read it easily. Skin tones and hair colors roughly parallel Humans. The feet are bare as often as not ”” Halflings with toughened soles consider shoes an imposition.

## Homeland & Society
The Halfling heartland is the Greenshire ”” a broad agricultural lowland of rolling hills, orchards, and river meadows between the southern Human cities and the edge of the Great Western Forest. It is divided into several dozen independent shires, each with its own traditions and governing tavern, loosely confederated under a council that meets annually and mostly argues about river rights.

Halfling shire society is deeply community-oriented and deceptively well-organized. The Mayor (always the proprietor of the central tavern) manages the shire's external relationships. The Roundtable ”” a rotating council of heads-of-household ”” handles internal disputes. Real power, however, runs through the network of social debts, favors, matchmaking, and long-term reputation that every Halfling tracks passively as a matter of course. Crossing a Halfling community tends to produce no immediate response and then, inexplicably, a great deal of difficulty with things you couldn't quite trace back.

Relations with Humans are warm and mutually beneficial ”” Halfling agricultural output is exceptional, and Human cities buy most of it. Wood Elves and Halflings share forest borders and a cordial understanding. Dwarves respect Halfling resilience and are invariably charmed by Halfling hospitality, which is the correct response.

## Racial Traits
- **Stat profile:** High DEX, AGI; lower STR; natural evasiveness
- **Lucky:** Small passive chance to avoid the worst outcome of a failed roll
- **Hearth-sense:** Bonus to social skill checks; instinctive read on intent
- **Size:** Halfling-sized; some heavy armor and large weapons unavailable
- **Starting region:** The Greenshire and the southern pastoral territories
- **Faction standing:** Positive with most Good and Neutral races; generally welcome everywhere except Evil-aligned settlements

## Available Classes
Warrior, Paladin, Cleric, Druid, Rogue, Monk, Ranger, Witch Hunter, Bard, Magician, Sorcerer, Enchanter.

**Blocked:** Shadow Knight (Good alignment ”” genuine darkness is not available here), Shaman (no ancestor-spirit tradition), Beast Master (domestic animal relationship, not predator bond), Wizard (no institutional arcane tradition in the shires), Necromancer and Blood Mage (the cultural prohibition is as solid as the Dwarf prohibition and possibly more heartfelt).

## Naming Conventions
Halfling names are warm, English-adjacent, often with a slightly old-fashioned feel. Two-syllable first names are most common. Surnames are family names, often nature-adjacent or occupation-derived, passed down for generations and taken seriously.

**Male examples:** Aldric, Barrin, Corvin, Darro, Ferris, Hobb, Milo, Pepin, Tobbin, Wendell
**Female examples:** Alda, Bessa, Corra, Daena, Fenna, Hilla, Mira, Pippa, Rowena, Willa
**Surnames:** Appleseed, Brambleton, Copperpot, Goodbarrel, Heathfield, Merriwick, Stonegarden, Thistledown
