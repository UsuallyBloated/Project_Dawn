# Gnome
*Neutral ”” Arcane Engineers ”” The Tinkering Mind*

## Overview
Gnomes approach every problem ”” magical, mechanical, social, or existential ”” as an engineering challenge. This is not a metaphor. A Gnome who encounters an unfamiliar lock will sketch its probable internal mechanism in the margin of whatever they're currently carrying before deciding whether to pick it or redesign it. A Gnome who receives a philosophical question will ask what inputs the question is accepting and what outputs are acceptable. This is not evasiveness; it is how their minds genuinely work, and most Gnomes find the results faster than whatever method you were using.

They are the foremost artificers and arcane engineers on the continent. The mechanisms that regulate the great city gates of Ardenmoor were Gnomish designs. So was the water distribution system under Aelindra, which the Elves declined to attribute for three decades. So is roughly forty percent of the maritime navigation equipment currently in service across both known oceans. Gnomes do not particularly care about credit, which makes them excellent consultants and somewhat infuriating colleagues.

Gnomish arcane scholarship diverges from Elven practice in one fundamental way: Gnomes want to know *how* magic works at a mechanical level, not simply how to use it. This has produced both genuine theoretical breakthroughs and several notable accidents that are still cited in academic hazard literature. The Gnomish Academy maintains an active list of currently ongoing experiments deemed "inadvisable but not yet stopped."

Gnomes are small and energetic, with expressive faces, oversized eyes, and an almost constant quality of forward motion even when standing still. Hair comes in colors that other races do not produce naturally ”” copper, electric blue, bright green ”” and is typically arranged into the most structurally improbable configuration the owner has time for that morning. Skin is warm-toned, ranging from cream to a deep tan, often carrying a faint iridescence. Burn marks and alchemical stains on hands and forearms are so universal that their absence is the notable condition.

## Homeland & Society
The Gnomish city of Cogsworth sits on a plateau in the northern highlands, reachable by a rail system the Gnomes built when the road proved insufficiently interesting. The city is a vertical maze of workshops, lecture halls, test facilities, and residential warrens that have been added to, modified, and revised continuously for four hundred years without ever being fully torn down. Navigating it for the first time requires a guide, a map, and willingness to climb through at least one access panel.

Gnomish society is organized around the Academies ”” research institutions covering different disciplines, each with its own internal rankings and traditions. Social status tracks expertise; a Gnome who has mastered something is universally respected regardless of how small that something seems to others. The Great Academy of Arcane Mechanics is the largest and most prestigious, but the Academy of Small Systems (devoted to clockwork, locks, and miniaturized engineering) and the Academy of Volatile Transmutations (which has its own dedicated hazard ward) are considered equally serious.

Gnomes get along well with races that appreciate competence and don't ask too many questions about the smell. They have productive if sometimes alarming relationships with Human merchants (who buy everything), Dwarf engineers (with whom they compete aggressively and respectfully), and surface Elves (who find Gnomish methods distasteful but use the results). They avoid Ogres and Trolls on general principle.

## Racial Traits
- **Stat profile:** High INT; lower STR, STA; small physical profile
- **Tinkering:** Bonus to crafting, trap-setting, and lock interaction
- **Arcane attunement:** Bonus to spell research; slightly faster mana regen
- **Size:** Gnome-sized equipment required; some heavy armor unavailable
- **Starting region:** Cogsworth and the northern highlands
- **Faction standing:** Positive with most Neutral and Good races; caution advised near Ogres and Trolls

## Available Classes
Warrior, Cleric, Druid, Rogue, Witch Hunter, Bard, Magician, Wizard, Sorcerer, Enchanter, Necromancer.

**Blocked:** Paladin and Shadow Knight (neither alignment extreme fits the pragmatic Gnomish worldview institutionally), Shaman (ancestor spirits actively resist the summons ”” something in the analytical relationship to the spirit world forecloses the bond), Beast Master (the analytical frame that forecloses the instinctual surrender the warder-bond requires), Monk (the body will not cooperate with what the mind attempts), Ranger (no tradition), Blood Mage (alignment).

## Naming Conventions
Gnomish personal names are compound words smashed together ”” often with a technical or mechanical sound to them. The longer the name, the more parents expected of the child at birth. Surnames are clan-workshop designations passed through the Academy affiliation, often abbreviated in practice.

**Male examples:** Alvirak, Boltwick, Coppwick, Dinrik, Fizzben, Gimwick, Ipwick, Novrak, Quibbin, Zivrak
**Female examples:** Alrissa, Brightgear, Copret, Dindra, Fizzix, Gimbrit, Iprix, Noxia, Quibbra, Zivra
**Surnames:** Brasswheel, Cogsworth, Flickspring, Irongate, Sparkfuse, Steelwick, Tinderbox, Voltsworth
