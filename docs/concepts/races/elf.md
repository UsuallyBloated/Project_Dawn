# Elf
*Good — Ancient Scholars — The Long Memory*

## Overview
Elves do not experience time the way shorter-lived races do. A Human scholar spends a lifetime accumulating knowledge; an Elf scholar spends three Human lifetimes becoming competent in their first discipline, then three more becoming proficient, and considers this a reasonable pace. The result is a race of extraordinary depth — in art, magic, history, and warfare — paired with a patience that other races frequently mistake for arrogance. It is not arrogance, exactly. It is the specific calm of someone who has watched empires rise and collapse and knows approximately how long most things take.

Elves are not passive. They fought wars that ended centuries before Humans built their first cities, and they remember those wars in detail. They maintain standing armies, active intelligence networks, and treaties with nearly every race on the continent — including several they have been at war with within living Elven memory. This capacity to hold grievance and pragmatism simultaneously is one of the race's most disconcerting qualities from the outside.

Their alignment is Good, but Elven Good is not Human Good. Elves protect the natural order, the integrity of accumulated knowledge, and the survival of what they consider irreplaceable. An Elf will spare an enemy who can be reasoned with and execute one who cannot without ceremony. They find Human notions of mercy applied uniformly to be sentimental and slightly dangerous.

Elven features are angular and precise: high cheekbones, pointed ears that extend several inches above the skull line, and eyes in shades that lean toward silver, green, violet, or deep amber. Their skin is pale across all variations, running from alabaster to a cool golden-ivory. Hair is typically dark or silver; red is rare and considered notable. They do not visibly age past early adulthood; the oldest Elves look like well-rested adults in their thirties.

## Homeland & Society
The Elven homeland is the city of Aelindra, built within and around a forest so old the trees predate most recorded history. The city is vertical — platforms, bridges, and halls carved into the living wood, without felling a single trunk. Aelindra is simultaneously a city, a library, and a temple, and the Elves make no distinction between these functions.

Elven society is organized around the great Houses, each of which holds custodianship over a domain of knowledge or a piece of the natural order. House Vaen holds arcane scholarship. House Seren holds the healing arts and natural philosophy. House Dael holds military history and tactical doctrine. Membership in a House is not hereditary — it is earned through study and assessed periodically. An Elf who fails to maintain their discipline loses standing; one who surpasses it may petition for elevation. There are no idle nobles.

Relations with other races are cordial and carefully maintained. Elves trade freely with Humans and Halflings, hold lasting alliances with Dwarves (forged in a war neither side speaks of in detail), and maintain a cold ceasefire with Dark Elves that has held for eleven hundred years. They watch Humans carefully — not with fear, but with the close attention you give to something moving fast in uncertain terrain.

## Racial Traits
- **Stat profile:** High INT, WIS, AGI; lower STR and STA
- **Low-light vision:** Can see clearly in near-darkness
- **Arcane attunement:** Slightly reduced mana cost on arcane spells; faster mana regeneration out of combat
- **Starting region:** Aelindra and the ancient forest territories
- **Faction standing:** Positive with most Good-aligned races; neutral with most Neutral races; negative with Dark Elf, Ogre, Troll, Kobold

## Available Classes
Warrior, Paladin, Cleric, Druid, Beast Master, Rogue, Monk, Ranger, Witch Hunter, Bard, Magician, Wizard, Sorcerer, Enchanter.

**Blocked:** Shadow Knight (Good alignment prohibits it), Shaman (no ancestor-spirit tradition in the Elven practice), Necromancer and Blood Mage (cultural prohibition is absolute — these arts are antithetical to everything the Elven order preserves).

## Naming Conventions
Elven names are long, liquid, and rich with vowels. They flow rather than punch. Many names contain an internal apostrophe marking a tonal shift — this is a real phonetic distinction in the Elven tongue that gets dropped in Human transliteration and bothers educated Elves considerably. Surnames are House-derived and compounded.

**Male examples:** Aethan, Cael'yn, Daerith, Elvan, Faerion, Lor'seth, Miravael, Orineth, Sylaren, Thalindor
**Female examples:** Aelith, Caelara, Dael'vyn, Erindra, Faelith, Lirael, Mireva, Naeris, Soravel, Vy'nara
**Surnames:** Aelindrath, Caerith, Dawnveil, Lightmantle, Moonsilver, Starwarden, Vaensoth, Wyrmbane
