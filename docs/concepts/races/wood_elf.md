# Wood Elf
*Neutral — Forest Guardians — The Living Canopy*

## Overview
Wood Elves and surface Elves share ancestry, but the split happened long enough ago that calling them the same people overstates the case. Where Aelindran Elves built libraries and institutions, Wood Elves built nothing permanent at all — their entire civilization is oral, mobile, and woven into the living structure of the forest itself. They do not write down their history. They carry it. Every elder is a living archive, and the death of an old Wood Elf is treated as a kind of burning.

This is not a primitive arrangement. The accumulated knowledge held in a Wood Elf clan's oral tradition covers centuries of ecological data, magical lore, hunting craft, and relational history that surface scholars have never successfully recorded in full. Wood Elves are simply not interested in helping them try. Outsiders who push on this tend to find themselves politely redirected, then lost in unfamiliar forest, then eventually returned to the edge of the tree line having seen nothing they could accurately describe.

Wood Elves are neutral in the global political sense but intensely local in their loyalties. They will protect their specific forest with lethal efficiency, trade with those who treat the land with respect, and largely ignore the politics of cities and kingdoms that don't threaten the canopy. They do not dislike other races as a rule; they simply have priorities that are legible only from inside the forest.

| Female | 5'0"–5'5" | 88–118 lbs | Lighter, equally lithe | Marginally faster; otherwise nearly indistinct from males at range |

Wood Elves are smaller and more compact than their surface kin, with shorter ears and more mobile facial expressions — they spend more time in close company than Aelindran Elves, and social legibility matters more. Skin tones range from a warm olive to a deeper, almost bark-brown. Hair runs toward auburn, chestnut, black, and occasionally a deep forest green that is not dye. Eyes are most commonly green or amber; a rare few show a mottled pattern that suggests the forest expressing itself through the face.

## Homeland & Society
Wood Elves live throughout the Great Western Forest, a continuous canopy stretching from the northern mountain foothills to the southern coastal cliffs. There is no single Wood Elf city — the population is organized into clans of twenty to a hundred individuals, each occupying a defined territory within the forest. Clan territories are known to everyone, respected by most, and enforced by all.

Social structure within a clan is egalitarian by function: the elder who holds the most memory leads, but that leadership is advisory rather than authoritative. Decisions that affect the whole clan are made by consensus after the elder's counsel. Wood Elves find Human and Dark Elf hierarchies bewildering in equal measure, though for different reasons.

The relationship between Wood Elves and surface Elves is cordial but carefully bounded — a mutual respect between cousins who made incompatible choices and know it. Wood Elves trade forest goods (rare wood, specific herbs, animal hides) with Human merchants and have standing agreements with Halflings, whose pastoral land borders the forest in several places. They have essentially no relationship with underground races and prefer to keep it that way.

## Racial Traits
- **Stat profile:** High AGI, DEX; lower STR, STA
- **Woodland camouflage:** Passive concealment bonus while stationary in forest terrain
- **Foraging:** Can identify and use wilderness consumables more effectively
- **Low-light vision:** Can see clearly in near-darkness
- **Starting region:** The Great Western Forest, clan territories
- **Faction standing:** Positive with Elf, Halfling, Fae; neutral with most; cautious with underground races

## Available Classes
Warrior, Cleric, Druid, Beast Master, Rogue, Ranger, Witch Hunter, Bard, Magician, Sorcerer, Enchanter.

**Blocked:** Paladin (no institutional religious hierarchy to be ordained in), Shadow Knight (alignment), Shaman (no ancestor-spirit tradition), Monk (oral tradition doesn't extend to that martial discipline), Wizard (requires sustained written scholarly study — Wood Elves have no written tradition), Necromancer and Blood Mage (alignment and cultural prohibition).

## Naming Conventions
Wood Elf names are short and nature-adjacent — sounds borrowed from running water, wind through leaves, and animal calls. No apostrophes; the language flows without stops. Clan names are descriptive compound words in their own tongue, usually translated for outsiders.

**Male examples:** Aevo, Brin, Cael, Drev, Fen, Lorn, Maet, Reth, Sylvan, Wren
**Female examples:** Aela, Birch, Ceva, Dria, Fern, Lira, Mavi, Rinna, Syla, Thren
**Clan names (translated):** Deeproot, Greenbough, Ironbark, Morningcanopy, Quietwater, Stoneleaf, Tallgrass, Windwhisper
