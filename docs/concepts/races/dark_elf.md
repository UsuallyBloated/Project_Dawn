# Dark Elf
*Evil ”” Matriarchal Underground Society ”” The Patient Knife*

## Overview
Dark Elves are what happens when a people spends ten generations underground, cut off from the living world above, and decides that survival at any cost is a reasonable philosophy. They are not simply Elves who made different choices. The generations of isolation, the extinction of their original druidic practice, and the rise of a matriarchal power structure built on controlled scarcity have produced something genuinely distinct: a race that is brilliant, cold, and completely comfortable with the idea that power is the only honest currency.

The split from surface Elves is old enough that most Dark Elves have no particular feelings about their surface kin beyond the practical assessment that they are potential threats or potential assets. The hatred runs hotter in the other direction ”” Elves remember the betrayal that drove their kin underground and have not forgiven it. Dark Elves, for their part, consider this a surface Elf problem and are mildly amused by the continued emotional investment.

Their society is matriarchal in the specific sense that females hold all institutional power ”” the great Houses, the military command, the trade guilds, and the temple hierarchies. Males are capable warriors, scholars, and mages, but rarely hold structural authority. This is not a casual arrangement; it is enforced by centuries of tradition, and a male Dark Elf who rises above his station tends to find himself the subject of careful attention from several directions at once. Outside their own society, Dark Elves project a unified front. Inside it, the intrigues never stop.

Dark Elves share the Elven frame ”” slender, angular, long-eared ”” but everything has been translated into a darker key. Skin tones run from deep charcoal grey to a blue-black so deep it reads as color only in direct light. Hair is typically white or silver, sometimes pale gold; it stands out starkly against the skin and Dark Elves are well aware of the effect. Eyes are most commonly red, violet, or a pale silver that catches light in the dark. They see well in full darkness ”” better than surface Elves.

## Homeland & Society
The Dark Elf homeland is the undercity of Vel'Sharath, a vast network of carved tunnels and vaulted chambers deep beneath the central mountain range. The city is beautiful in the specific way that things are beautiful when designed with abundant slave labor and no shortage of time: obsidian columns, bioluminescent fungi cultivated into permanent illumination, bridges over underground lakes. It is also a trap. The city's architecture is explicitly designed to funnel, control, and redirect movement. Visitors rarely see the same path twice.

Society is organized around the matriarchal Houses, which control everything from food production to military contract assignment. The High Matron of the dominant House holds executive power, but that dominance shifts through alliance, assassination, and strategic marriage. The temples of their deity ”” a spider-goddess of fate and power ”” serve as both religious institutions and intelligence networks; the priesthood is all-female and functionally indistinguishable from a secret police. Males serve as soldiers, mages, and artisans, and some achieve genuine renown, but the path to any real authority runs through a female patron.

Dark Elves trade reluctantly and carefully with the surface. Their underground goods ”” refined metals, certain mushroom-derived alchemicals, enchanted obsidian ”” are valuable enough that surface merchants accept the terms, which are always slightly unfavorable and come with surveillance.

## Racial Traits
- **Stat profile:** High INT, AGI; lower STA; CHA penalty with Good-aligned races
- **True darkvision:** Full vision in complete darkness, better than any surface race
- **Innate magic resistance:** Partial resistance to Enchantment-school spells
- **Starting region:** Vel'Sharath and the underdark network
- **Faction standing:** Positive with Evil-aligned races; deeply negative with Elf, Halfling, Dwarf; cautiously neutral with most Neutral races

## Available Classes
Warrior, Shadow Knight, Cleric, Rogue, Bard, Magician, Wizard, Sorcerer, Enchanter, Necromancer, Blood Mage.

**Blocked:** Paladin (Evil alignment prohibits it), Druid and Beast Master (severed from living nature by generations underground ”” the bond cannot form), Shaman (no ancestor-spirit tradition), Monk (the arrogance the training cannot overcome), Ranger (no tradition, no surface connection), Witch Hunter (they are more often the subject of a hunt than the hunter).

## Naming Conventions
Dark Elf names are sharp, sibilant, and precise ”” the language was developed underground where soft sounds get lost in stone, and a name that cannot be spoken clearly is a liability. The apostrophe in Dark Elf names marks a glottal stop, not a tonal shift ”” a distinction that confuses surface scholars. Surnames are House-prefixed with *Vel'* for the great Houses and *Drak'* for lesser ones.

**Male examples:** Arek, Draz'val, Faerin, Keth'sol, Lireth, Mael'drak, Rath, Sev'yn, Thraex, Zar'vik
**Female examples:** Alysara, Caravin, Dar'vyss, Elyxis, Faevyn, Ilryss, Mer'vath, Nyx, Syl'vara, Vel'ara
**Surnames:** Vel'Sharath, Vel'Drask, Vel'Nyx, Drak'Aern, Drak'Sev, House prefixes vary by lineage
