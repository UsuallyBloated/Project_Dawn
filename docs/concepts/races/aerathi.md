*Neutral — Eyrie Hunters — The Stoop-kin*

## Overview
The Aerathi are raptor-coded avian people — broad-winged, keen-eyed, and built around
the hunt from above. Where the Felhari hunt by pursuit across open ground, the Aerathi
hunt by patience and altitude: hold the thermal, read the ground, and commit to the
stoop only when the kill is certain. Other races know them as the **Stoop-kin** — named
for that hunting dive — and the Aerathi wear the name with pride. The culture prizes
the *clean kill* — one strike, decisively placed — and judges waste, cruelty, and
hesitation with equal contempt.

They share a deep heritage with the Vesperin: both peoples hold that the first gift of
the sky was the voice, and song is the oldest thing the two cultures still have in
common. Aerathi song is sparse and carrying — calls that travel between eyries across
miles of cliff face. Bards are honored among them as the keepers of that shared
inheritance, and Aerathi Bards are the natural diplomats between the cliff and canopy
peoples.

## Physical Description
| Sex | Height | Weight | Build | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Female | 6'2"–6'10" | 140–180 lbs | Larger, broader wingspan | Females run larger — true of the raptors they echo; most eyrie-leaders are female |
| Male | 5'10"–6'6" | 120–160 lbs | Lighter, faster in the stoop | Often the longer-range scouts |

Tall but startlingly light — hollow bones keep even the largest Aerathi under two
hundred pounds. Feathered head, arms, and back; taloned feet; wings that fold along the
arm line and spread for the glide (gliding, not true flight — the Fae hold that
distinction alone). Plumage runs to hawk and eagle patterns: barred browns, slate
grays, sea-cliff white. Eyes are oversized and front-set, gold to amber.

Social structure is the eyrie — small, fiercely self-reliant households of two to
twelve, bound by hunting partnership more than blood. Eyries federate loosely for
defense and dispute settlement. Leadership goes to demonstrated judgment: the leader is
whoever the eyrie trusts to call the stoop.

## Racial Traits
- **Stat profile:** Highest AGI of any playable race; strong DEX; low STA (hollow bones)
- **Glide:** Spread-wing descent — no fall damage while gliding; long horizontal travel
- **Raptor Sight:** Visual detection range roughly doubled; little escapes them at distance
- **Talons:** Natural attack option
- **Stoop:** Diving strike from altitude — damage scales with descent
- **Hollow Bones:** Light frame is the price of the sky — knockback and carry capacity suffer

## Available Classes
Warrior, Paladin, Monk, Ranger, Beast Master, Bard, Rogue.

**Under discussion:** Shaman (a wind-ancestor tradition would fit; not yet decided).

**Blocked:** Shadow Knight (the eyrie code is built on the clean kill; the Shadow
Knight's power is carrion-magic), Cleric (no temple structure — the sky itself is the
rite), Druid (reverence belongs to the open sky, not the rooted grove — *debatable,
revisit*), Wizard / Magician / Sorcerer / Enchanter (no written arcane tradition; eyrie
culture is oral and spare), Necromancer and Blood Mage (the dead are given to the sky;
both arts are desecration).

## Naming Conventions
*Draft.* Short, wind-cut names — hard consonants, long open vowels, made to carry
across a cliff face. A child bears a nest-name until their first clean kill, then takes
a blooded name of their own choosing; using someone's nest-name after they are blooded
is a serious insult.

**Examples:** Kaiel, Skarn, Vrael, Orrin, Tessa, Avrik, Sorra, Thane, Kestrel-called
names are common honorifics.
**Eyrie names:** the Shriekspire, the Updraft Throne, the Seventh Ledge, the Pale Aerie.
