# The Sunken Ziggurat — Southeastern Marshlands (Dungeon, Levels 35–40)

*The Ziggurat predates the Kel`varath. This is established fact — the stone is older, the script
on its walls is not Kel`varath script, and the things that live in the lower levels are not
things the Kel`varath put there. The Kel`varath claimed the site anyway. The upper levels are theirs.
The lower levels are a different negotiation.*

## Encounter Notes

**Level 1** — Kel`varath guards, bone scribes (non-hostile if you're not looting their tablets).
Undead shuffle in from the marsh at dusk.

**Level 2** — Kel`varath undead (ordered, patrol routes), some feral undead (chaotic). The older
images painted over on the walls, if fully uncovered, depict a race building the ziggurat —
bipedal, six-fingered, three eyes. No match in any race record.

**Level 3** — The Guardians: stone-and-water constructs, not mechanical. They respond to
intrusion, not alignment. They cannot be reasoned with. They respawn if destroyed and the
intruder remains.

**Level 4** — The Shaper Below is not undead. It is not a construct. The Kel`varath scribes
have forty pages of notes on what it might be. None of the pages agree.

---

## The Still Throne

No Kel`varath will sit in it. They won't say why. Player characters can — the game should react.
What happens is GM-defined, but the throne remembers everyone who has ever sat in it,
and some of them aren't dead.
