# The Ashen Coast — New Zone (Levels 20–35)

*Where the central mountain range meets the eastern sea, the volcanic shelf beneath the water
has been pushing up for ten thousand years. The result is a coastline of black sand, sea-stack
obsidian pillars, lava tube caves, and a permanent haze of sulfur and salt spray. Nothing lives
here that hasn't adapted to it. Everything that has adapted to it is dangerous.*

## Zone Notes

**Ecology:** Ash crawlers, obsidian crabs, fire elementals (bound and unbound), Salamander
packs (reptilian humanoids, non-playable, territorial), sea birds adapted to sulfur air,
blind deep-sea fish that surface in the lava-heated shallows.

**Magician content:** Several natural elemental nexus points where fire elementals
coalesce. Magicians can use these as binding sites for more powerful fire servants than
normal. The nexus points are contested by wild elementals.

**Dungeon:** The Caldera — summit of the Ember Peaks, levels 30–35. A volcanic crater
dungeon with a fire giant at its core. The fire giant is not hostile by default. It is
also not interested in conversation until it decides you are interesting, which requires
a specific quest chain through the Cinder Docks fishermen.
