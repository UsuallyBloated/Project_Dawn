# The Unlicensed Wing — Vel'Sharath (Dungeon, Levels 42–50)

*Every city has a place where the rules stop. In Vel'Sharath, that place is sanctioned —
officially unmapped, officially unpatrolled, officially nonexistent. What happens in the
Unlicensed Wing is not illegal. It simply has no legal category. The Deep Quarter border
guards will tell you there is no door. The door is right behind them.*

## Room Notes

### A — Intake Hall
A featureless anteroom. A slot in the wall accepts coin — House scrip or raw obsidian.
No coin means the door back seals until morning. There is no one to bribe.

### B — Collapsed Tunnels
The geometry is wrong — passages that should connect don't, dead ends that loop, a room
that appears twice with different contents. Magic is unstable here. Spells cast at full
power sometimes arrive at a fraction. Sometimes more.

### C — Experiment Wing
Six sealed lab doors. Three open easily. Two require keys found deeper in.
One has been sealed from the inside.

Contents vary — alchemical, necromantic, enchantment-school experiments that exceeded
their containment parameters. Loot is exceptional. Survival is not guaranteed.

### D — The Deep Wards
Containment cells, floor to ceiling iron-and-obsidian construction. The occupants
are not hostile. They watch visitors pass through the bars with patient attention.
Most of them look like Dark Elves. Not all of them do anymore.

Freeing any occupant triggers the E encounter early and adds the freed occupant
as a second boss. The freed occupant may or may not be grateful.

### E — The Revocation Chamber
The ritual circle at the center is the original construction purpose of the Unlicensed Wing.
It was designed to erase a person — soul, record, name — completely. It worked once.
The Revoked is what happened to the soul after it was revoked.
She knows your name. She knows everyone's name. She collects them.
