# The Underdark Crossing — New Zone (Levels 25–40)

*The tunnel network beneath the central mountain range connects Cogsworth's lower warrens to
the outer reaches of Vel'Sharath's Gate District. The Kobolds have always known it exists. The
Dark Elves have always known it exists. Neither side acknowledges this to outsiders. The section
between them — the Crossing proper — belongs to whoever is standing in it at the time.*

## Zone Notes

**The third digger:** Something is tunneling new passages through the contested section.
The tunnels are too large for Kobolds and the technique is wrong for Dark Elves. The Grey
Market brokers know what it is. Asking costs more than most players can afford at this level.

**Navigation:** The Kobold tunnels require crouching for most non-Kobold races (Halflings,
Gnomes, Fae move freely). The Dark Elf tunnels are built for Dark Elf proportions — tall
races (Ogre, Troll, Minotaur) cannot access them at all without a specific quest unlock.

**Dungeon:** The Digger's Warren — accessible from the contested tunnels if you can find
the correct new passage. Levels 35–40. The digger is inside.
