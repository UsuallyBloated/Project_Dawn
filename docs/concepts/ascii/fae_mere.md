# Fae Mere — Zone Overview (Levels 25–35)

*The Fae homeland. A forest built from the inside out — starting with what it was meant to mean,
then constructing the physical reality around the meaning. The central Mere does not reflect the sky.
The four courts compete for control of its shore. Time moves wrong here: day and night cycle faster
than elsewhere, each court most active in its preferred light. The Crosswater Bazaar is the only
place in the zone where the courts agree on anything.*

## Zone Notes

**Time:** Day and night cycle faster than normal zones. Spring Court is most active at dawn;
Summer at noon; Autumn at dusk; Winter at midnight. Court-specific events and named spawns
follow the cycle. Players who do not track the cycle will miss time-gated content.

**Illusion mechanics:** Enemy appearances are unreliable. Glamour Wisps appear as other
enemy types until damaged past a threshold. Fae Hounds have illusion forms. Enchanters can
identify true forms at skill 50+ without attacking.

**Navigation:** No marked paths. The forest gives directional cues to characters with
Elvish language (100) or Grove-Sense (passive ability from Dreaming Grove questline).
Everyone else navigates by landmark — the Mere is always visible from any elevated position.
The courts can be found by following court-specific ambient sounds: Spring (wind chimes),
Summer (constant warm light-haze at tree level), Autumn (single distant drum), Winter
(the Winterspire is visible above canopy from three zones away in clear weather).

**Court standing:** Standing with one court is independent of others. A character with
maximum Winter standing and minimum Summer standing is in a specific social position
the Fae find interesting. They may take one side or the other. They may make it worse.

**The Mere's reflection:** The image shown is the site as it existed before the Fae.
Characters who can read Architect notation see additional layers in the reflection —
the site is labeled in Architect records as "site-7-secondary-archive-environmental."
The Fae know this and have not told anyone.
