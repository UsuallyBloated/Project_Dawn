# The Dreaming Grove — Dungeon Overview (Levels 18–25)

*Below the root-level of Aelindra, past the sections the city maintains, past the sections anyone
maintains. The Grove predates Aelindra. The city was built above it. The Grove-Keeper manages the
interface; the dungeon itself manages nothing — it is what it has always been, and it does not
accommodate visitors so much as it receives them.*

*The Warden is at the bottom. It is waiting for you, specifically. It has been waiting for
everyone, specifically, which is the kind of thing that is difficult to think about clearly.*

## The Warden — Notes

The Warden is not hostile. It is not friendly. It is old enough that those categories are too small.

It has received visitors since the Calibration State of Year 407 CR (documented in the Aelindra sealed archive). The question it asked Silindra twice was the same. The question it asked Drathis Vehn was different. The questions it asked the three Aelindra archivists who descended before the archive was sealed were different from each other and from any recorded visitor's question. The pattern Drathis is mapping across known visitors' questions has twelve data points. He is trying to find the underlying logic.

**On Elder Elvish:** The Warden speaks primarily in a language older than Elder Elvish. Characters with Elder Elvish receive increased engagement — more of the Warden's communication registers as language rather than as something that is not quite language. Characters with Ghost Tongue receive a different interaction — the Warden's response to Ghost Tongue is documented by exactly two visitors, both Revenants, and the documentation is in sealed Order of the Walking Name records.

**On the node survey fragment:** The fragment the Warden provides as a completion reward is the most complete single-site Architect survey in the player-accessible game. It contains observations the Warden has been accumulating since before Aelindra was built. The full fragment, combined with other site surveys, is required for the Calibration State trigger.

## Zone Notes

**Enemies:** Dreaming Grove enemies are not hostile by default. They respond to aggression, disruption of the Grove's state (certain spells), or players who have poor Grove-standing. The combat here is avoidable for players who understand what the Grove is. It is not avoidable for players who treat it as a standard dungeon.

**The Lost:** Several former explorers who descended and could not find their way back. They are not undead — they are simply still here, in a state between present and elsewhere. They can be guided out by players who pay attention to what each one is looking for. Each one who is successfully guided out reduces the dungeon's ambient difficulty slightly and provides minor faction standing with Silindra.

**No map:** The Dreaming Grove does not have a minimap function in any of the four levels. This is not a bug. The Grove's geometry does not correspond to what the minimap system assumes about geometry.
