# Eastern Badlands — Zone Overview (Levels 25–40)

*Red clay and broken rock. Water is scarce and contested. The Ogre, Troll, and Half-Ogre communities
here were pushed to this territory by the expansion of Good-aligned civilizations. The Badlands are
what was left when everything easier was taken. Durrath's main temple is carved into the largest
mesa. The Calibration Tower stands in the northeast — the Ogre clans use it as a territorial
boundary marker. The Bone Citadel is in the west. Nobody goes in the Bone Citadel.*

## Zone Notes

**Inter-clan neutrality:** The springs in the open inter-clan badlands are contested but not
actively fought over — there is an informal ceasefire near water sources because even
at-war clans need water. Characters can move through contested spring zones without
triggering combat if they do not engage first.

**Dawn and dusk light:** The red clay reflects warm light at these times in a way that makes
the zone briefly beautiful. Experienced players stop moving during this window. New players
learn to do this after they see it once.

**The Bone Carving schematic:** One of two tradeskill schematics in the game that produces
items with properties not fully explained by the crafting system. The other is in the
Unlicensed Wing of Vel'Sharath. Alchemists who combine products of both have observations.

**Durrath faction note:** Durrath temple standing is separate from the three clan standings.
Solen-Vekt's quests raise all three clan standings simultaneously because the temple's
neutrality means his recommendations carry cross-faction weight. This makes his questline
the most efficient standing-gain path in the zone.
