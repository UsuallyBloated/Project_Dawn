## Access and Standing

Aelindra is open to all players at the Ground Level. Higher levels require:
- **Upper Level:** Any Elf or Half-Elf enters freely. Others require Neutral or better Aelindra Standing.
- **Wizard/Enchanter Platforms:** Relevant class or academic credentials from another Guild.
- **Root Level / Archives:** Aelindra Standing 500+. The sealed section requires a quest unlock.
- **Dreaming Grove dungeon:** Ground Level entry; no standing requirement. The dungeon is the content.

Good-aligned players entering Aelindra receive warm treatment. Neutral players are tolerated. Evil-aligned players are permitted at Ground Level only and are watched. Dark Elf players are not welcome — there is a specific conversation at the West Gate that occurs, and it is not short.
