# Ancestor Shrine — Khala Savannahs (Felhari)

*Shrines are not built — they accumulate. A clan stops at a place where an ancestor
died well or lived long and leaves something: a stone, a tooth, a strip of hide. Other
clans stop and leave something. Generations pass. What began as a pause in a migration
route becomes a permanent place. The shrine described here is three hundred years old.
The Felhari who tend it can name every stone.*

## Shrine Tender

*The current tender is an elder named Mirrath. She is not a shaman — shrine-tending is
its own tradition, separate from spiritual practice. She maintains the fire, the bowls,
and the stones. She knows the name of every Felhari whose mark is here.*

*She will speak with travelers. She will not lie to them. She will also not answer
questions she considers poorly formed — she will instead ask you a question back, and
your answer will tell her whether you were worth speaking to.*

*If you bring an offering appropriate to the shrine and you are not Felhari, she will
notice. If it is a good offering, she will remember you. This has consequences,
eventually.*
