# The Kobold Deeps — Cogsworth Underbelly (Evil Starting Zone, Levels 1–10)

*Beneath Cogsworth's lowest level, before the Abandoned Works begin, the tunnel network
branches into a Kobold-controlled sprawl that the Gnomes officially deny exists. The Deeps
have their own economy, their own rules, and their own version of every Gnome city map
(more accurate, for the lower half). New Kobolds start here, in the pack that found them
or the pack that will claim them.*

## NPC Roster

| Name | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Alpha Drix | Pack leader / quest giver | Male; earned the spot two seasons ago; still has the scar; functional paranoia |
| Senior Female Fikka | Cultural authority | Older than Drix; gives him advice he can't ignore; gives you the quests that matter |
| Shaman Rikka | Shrine-tender / skill trainer | The dragon answers her. She is uncomfortable with how often. |
| Matron Zikka | Neutral arbiter | At the market; quest giver for inter-pack storylines |
| Gnix | The one Kobold who has been to the surface | Won't shut up about it; also won't go back; useful for surface zone quests |

## Starting Quests (1–5)

- **Pack Contribution** — Bring 6 pieces of raw ore from the Deep Runs; don't ask where the Deep Runs go; just bring the ore
- **Trap Setting** — Place three traps along the Cogsworth Level 7 approach; the Gnomes have been sending scouts; discourage them
- **Deepclaw Disagreement** — The Deepclaw pack claims a tunnel that belongs to Ashfang; resolve it (combat, negotiation, or evidence — Fikka accepts any outcome that works)
- **Shrine Offering** — Rikka needs a specific bone from a specific creature three tunnels east; she won't tell you what the creature is, only what the bone looks like
- **Gnix's Errand** — Gnix needs something from the surface and won't go himself; deliver it; he owes you and Fikka has decided this debt has value
