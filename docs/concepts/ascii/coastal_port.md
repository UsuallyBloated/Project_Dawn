# Harrowmere — Coastal Port City (New Zone, Levels 15–30)

*Built where the Greyveil River reaches the sea. Harrowmere is technically independent —
it pays no tithe to any kingdom, answers to no faction. In practice it is governed by the
Harrowmere Dockmasters' Compact, a council of seven that has changed membership through
election, purchase, assassination, and marriage in roughly equal measure. The city works.
It is not clean. It is emphatically not safe. It is the only place you can sell anything
to anyone without a faction check.*

## Zone Notes

**The empty Compact seats:** Two of the seven Dockmasters are dead or missing. The remaining
five are negotiating replacement. This is a quest chain — players can back a candidate,
investigate the disappearances, or take a seat themselves (requires level 25+ reputation).

**Warehouse 4:** A different lock. The contents are not cargo. The Dockmasters know. Two of them
would prefer the players not look too closely. One of them put it there.

**The Drowned Hold:** Pirate dungeon on Harrow Island, levels 22–28. The pirates who used it
are dead. Their loot is still there. So are they — the sea brought them back.
