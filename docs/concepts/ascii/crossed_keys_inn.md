# The Crossed Keys Inn — Millhaven, Greenshire

*The largest inn in Millhaven. Two stories, stone ground floor, timber upper. The sign outside
shows two iron keys crossed on a field of green. Known for hot stew, warm beds, and a barkeep
named Bess who has heard every story twice and remembers the lies.*
