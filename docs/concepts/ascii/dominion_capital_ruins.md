# The Dominion Capital — Ruined (New Zone, Levels 35–50)

*Ixareth-Kul. The name translates roughly as "the city that does not sleep." It hasn't slept
in three hundred years — the ruined streets are still patrolled, the crumbled towers still
emanate residual magic, and the necromantic orders that survived the Dominion's fall have been
quietly reconstructing what they can. The jungle has taken most of it back. Most of it.*

## Zone Notes

**Three-way faction tension:** The Bonewrights, Soulcarvers, and Ash Scribes all claim the
ruins. Players can align with any, which determines NPC hostility and quest access. All three
factions want the same thing from the Dominion Vault: whatever the last High Commander locked
inside before the fall.

**The throne occupant:** The thing sitting the throne is not Kel`varath. It is not undead. The
Ash Scribes have records suggesting the throne was already occupied when the Dominion was
built and they simply built around it. They do not publicize this.

**Respawn logic:** The ruins are a living zone — ordered Kel`varath undead patrol set routes,
feral undead (Dominion-era, not Kel`varath-ordered) wander unpredictably. The ordered ones can
be tracked and timed. The feral ones cannot.
