# Ashfang Hold — Eastern Badlands (Evil Starting Zone, Levels 1–10)

*The first thing a new Ogre, Troll, or Half-Ogre sees is the Proving Gate — two Ogre femur
bones crossed over a skull the size of a barrel. This is not decoration. It is information:
the Hold survives because everyone in it has already proven themselves, and if you are
entering for the first time, you have not yet. The proving starts now.*

## NPC Roster

| Name | Race | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warlord Kurash Ironmaw | Ogre | Starting warden | Gives first quests; judges you by your hands |
| Shaman Grartha | Ogre | Skill trainer | Ancient, patient, smells of smoke |
| Brekka the Scarred | Troll | Combat trainer | Lost an eye proving herself; considers this normal |
| Half-Ogre Denn | Half-Ogre | Supply vendor | Shrewd, quieter than the others, watching |

## Starting Quests (1–5)

- **First Blood** — Kill 8 badland scavengers east of the Hold
- **Proving the Spine** — Win two sparring circles in the proving grounds
- **The Shaman's Errand** — Gather three ancestor-stone fragments from the ruined Ogre camp to the north (it was raided; find out by whom)
- **Trophy Collection** — Bring a predator skull from the eastern ridge; the warlord will tell you which predator is worth the effort
- **The Raider's Road** — Escort a supply runner to the Troll arroyo settlement and back; something has been picking off runners
