# Greyveil — City Map (ASCII)

*The largest neutral city in the western territories. Population: ~40,000. Built on a river bend, walled, with an undercity that officially does not exist.*

## Key Locations Summary

| Location | District | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Merchants' Guild | North | Trade licensing, auction, stalls | 40 stall permits; Commission Board |
| Bard Conservatory | North | Music, performance, archive | Archive = intelligence network |
| Market Square | North | Open trade | Erindra's tree in the center; 200 years of logistics problems |
| City Hall | Central | Government, records | Magistrate offices; guard coordination |
| Solrath Temple | Central | Justice faith, Paladin hub | Deliberately prominent; high vaulted ceilings |
| Crossed Avenues | Central | Main intersection | Always busy; pickpocket risk |
| Smiths' Row | West | Weapon/armor crafting | Three blacksmith trainers |
| Witch Hunter Chapter House | West | Anti-corruption, evidence | Interrogation room; evidence lockup; basement wards |
| Iron Flagon | East | Popular tavern | Brawls on Restday; guard knows the layout |
| Vethis Temple | East | Funerary rites, death records | Door always slightly open |
| River Docks | South | Cargo, river transit | Connection to Harrowmere via river (long trip) |
| Undercity | Below | Rogues, black market, prison | Officially does not exist; everyone knows |
