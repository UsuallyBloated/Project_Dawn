# Prison Cell — Greyveil Undercity

*Greyveil's undercity has three sections. The Drowned Market is commerce. The Catacombs
are history. The Old Vault is where they put people the city wants to forget about, in
rooms the city prefers not to have on the official maps. The cells are old. The stones
have absorbed things. The city's position is that no one is held here who isn't here
for good reason. The city has always had a flexible relationship with what constitutes
good reason.*
