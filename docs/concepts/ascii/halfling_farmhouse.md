# Halfling Shire Farmhouse — Greenshire

*Halfling farmhouses are built into hillsides where possible, freestanding where not.
The one described here belongs to the Thistlewick family — four generations on this
particular patch of Greenshire — and is typical of the shire style: low ceilings,
thick walls, a kitchen that takes up more space than is strictly necessary, and a
garden that has been tended so long it has opinions about what grows where.*
