# The Bonecourt — Kel`varath Starting Zone (Levels 1–10)

*A partially reclaimed ruin in the southeastern jungle — one of hundreds of outpost sites
from the old Dominion. The Bonewrights chose this one because the necromantic residue
in the stone is still strong enough to accelerate training, and because the jungle has grown
over it thoroughly enough that outsiders don't look twice. New Kel`varath who don't know where
they belong end up here. The Bonewrights find them useful.*

## Starting Quests (1–5)

- **The First Binding** — Animate a skeleton from the jungle bone-scatter east of the Bonecourt using the altar; bring it back; it must still be following orders when you arrive
- **Dominion Salvage** — Recover three carved stone fragments from the collapsed east wing of the outpost; the Scribe wants the carvings translated, not the stone
- **Jungle Predator Cull** — The approaches to the Bonecourt are watched by apex predators; thin them; the Bone-Tender wants a venom gland from the largest
- **The Disobedient Bones** — Something has been animating the deeper jungle ruins without authorization; find the source and stop it or recruit it, at your discretion
- **Inheritance** — Vethkar gives you a sealed tube; deliver it to the Soulcarver outpost three miles west; do not open it; this is a test of whether you will
