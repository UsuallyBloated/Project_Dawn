# The Greyveil Conservatory — Greyveil

*The oldest institution in Greyveil's Old Quarter. Three stories of dark timber and river stone,
built before the Trade Quarter existed. The Conservatory trains Bards, archives music going back
four centuries, and maintains a polite fiction that it has no connection to the city's intelligence
networks. Everyone in Greyveil knows this is false. No one says so.*
