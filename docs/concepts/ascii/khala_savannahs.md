# Khala Savannahs — Zone Overview (Levels 30–45)

*The largest continuous open-terrain zone in the game. Waist-height grass and specific named winds.
The Felhari have been here long enough that the land has opinions about them, and they have opinions
about the land, and some of those opinions match. Three major clans hold primary territories. Six
smaller clans affiliate in arrangements that shift. The Ancestor's Mouth is in the east, at the
base of the largest kopje, and the Spirit-Walkers have been watching it more carefully than usual
for the past two seasons.*

## Zone Notes

**Day/night:** Night is genuinely more dangerous — the Night Cat spawns are exclusive to
dark hours, and predator aggro radius increases significantly. The Felhari hunt by day as
custom, not just for safety; players who hunt at night receive minor faction penalties until
they understand why.

**Open terrain:** Social aggro radius is wider here than any other zone — 20+ units in open
grass. Trained groups can be enormous. The open sight lines mean trained groups are also
visible from much further. Use the kopjes for line-of-sight breaks. The Felhari use them
for exactly this reason.

**Spirit-Walker and Beast Master synergy:** More Beast Master warder bond components drop
in this zone than any other. The Felhari training traditions produce the highest-tier
Beast Master trainers in the game. Spirit-Walker questline items are exclusively accessible
here — the Gathering is required, the Ancestor's Mouth is required, and all three Named
Ancestors must have been encountered.

**The Gathering:** Seasonal event. Specific in-game calendar dates. When active: all three
clan settlements open full services, unique items available, cross-clan dialogue accessible
that is not available otherwise. A Spirit-Walker attending with that status is a recognized
figure regardless of race. The welcome is complicated but it is a welcome.
