# Ardenmoor — Zone Overview (Levels 5–15)

*What happens when forest gets old enough that the trees start having opinions. The canopy filters
the light to a permanent amber-green. The Wood Elves have been here since before the forest needed
a name. The trails are unmarked because the people who use them know where they go. The Blighted
Wood is to the east. The cord markers are getting closer to the canopy villages. The Wood Elves
are watching and marking.*

## Zone Notes

**Sight lines:** ~15 meters ground level; zero at night. Players who have not been to Ardenmoor
before and enter at night will find nothing visible beyond torch range and will immediately
understand why experienced players sprint toward the waystation at dusk.

**Tracking:** The Ranger tracking window is the practical navigation tool. It shows what is in
range even when it is not visible. A Ranger who does not use tracking in Ardenmoor is fighting
with one hand tied back. A non-Ranger who buys a Tracking herb from Kaevel's vendor gets
a one-hour tracking window — not class quality, but functional.

**The poacher questline:** The fastest path to Wood Elf standing. The poachers (Human, anti-Wood-Elf,
10-13) are running a commercial operation that has the Canopy Council's specific attention. Kaevel
knows who they report to; he will not say until players have done two quests for him first.

**Night:** Nocturnal predators spawn at dusk. Night Stalkers (11-14) are category-two threats —
solo-manageable with good gear, dangerous if multiple are engaged. The waystation fire is visible
at 300 meters in darkness. Experienced players run toward it.
