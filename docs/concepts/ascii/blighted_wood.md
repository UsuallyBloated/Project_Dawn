# The Blighted Wood — New Zone (Levels 20–30)

*Ardenmoor's eastern edge where the forest died. The Wood Elves know the hour it began — 
forty years ago, on a night with no moon, something pushed up from below and the trees nearest
the eastern ridge went grey overnight. The blight spreads roughly six inches per month. The
Wood Elves have been marking the edge with white cord and red paint for four decades. The
cord is very close to their canopy villages now.*

## Zone Notes

**The blight source:** The Heartwood is the answer, not the cause. What lives inside the
Heartwood fed on the tree and is working outward. It is not malicious — it does not have
the capacity for malice. It simply needs and takes. The Wood Elves have no word for it
because they have never encountered anything like it before.

**Dungeon — The Hollowed:** Inside the Heartwood, levels 27–30. The dungeon is vertical —
players climb downward through the tree's root system, not upward. The thing at the bottom
is not the boss — the boss is the Heartwood itself, or what remains of it, defending the
thing at the bottom.

**Connection to Ardenmoor quests:** Ardenmoor questlines reference the blight as ongoing.
Players from Ardenmoor who complete the Rotwood Hollow dungeon will find a clue pointing
toward the Blighted Wood as the source of the rot.
