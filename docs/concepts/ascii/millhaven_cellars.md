# The Millhaven Cellars — Greenshire (Dungeon, Levels 6–10)

*Beneath Millhaven's market square runs a network of old storage tunnels, older than the town
above them. What they were built for is unclear. What they're used for now is worse.*

## Room Notes

### A — Entry Passage
- Stairs down from Millhaven market square, behind a locked grate (key from sheriff or lockpick)
- Torch brackets every 10ft — some still lit, some burnt out
- Scratched graffiti on walls: "don't go past the water" and "Ren was here (he wasn't)" and tally marks

### B — Rat Warrens
- Waist-high tunnels branch off the main corridor
- Giant rat packs, 3–4 per pull
- Webbing in corners — something bigger than rats made it
- Minor loot: chewed coin purses, a lost Millhaven guard badge

### C — Collapsed Storage
- Old town supply cache, ceiling partially fallen
- Safe to explore, no enemies
- Lootable: old ale (undrinkable), iron spikes, a ledger in old script

### D — Flooded Passage
- Water came in from underground spring decades ago, never drained
- Knee to waist depth depending on season
- Cave crawlers lurk in the water — crocodilian, palm-sized, bite in packs
- A body face-down — recent, not old

### E — Cultist Shrine
- Whoever maintains this shrine still visits
- The candles are fresh
- The symbol carved into the altar matches nothing in any public record
- Named mob: The Tender of the Below — humanoid, robed, does not speak, casts shadow magic
- Drop: Candle of Unlight (glows black, illuminates only undead)

### F — Boss Chamber
- The Old Thing has no official name in Millhaven records
- It predates the tunnels — the tunnels were built around it
- It was worshipped once; it may remember
- Kills reward: [Millhaven Seal Key] opens the sheriff's evidence lockup
