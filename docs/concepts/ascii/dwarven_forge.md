# Clan Forge Interior — Dwarven Mountain Holds

*Every clan hold has a forge. The forge is not simply where metal is worked — it is where
the clan's identity is maintained. The Forge District requires a clan-mark pass to enter.
Inside, every forge is the forge-master's domain absolutely. The forge described here
belongs to the Ironmaw clan. The forge-master is Bruga Stonebeard. She has been
forge-master for forty-one years. The forge was her mother's before her.*

## Forge-Master Bruga Stonebeard

*Bruga does not look up when you enter. She gives you time to show whether you know
to wait. If you interrupt her work, she puts the hammer down, turns to face you, and
her expression has a specific quality that apprentices have named among themselves
"the cooling iron look." It is not anger. It is assessment. She is deciding whether
you are worth the interruption.*

*If you are a Dwarf, she will ask your clan before your name. If you have no clan,
she will ask who your parents were. If you don't know that either, she will still
help you — but she will note it, and you will feel it noted.*

*She does not charge Dwarves for basic work. She does not explain this policy.
It is simply true.*
