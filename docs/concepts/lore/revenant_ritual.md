# The Revenant Ritual
*What is asked. What is given. Who asks.*

## Overview

The Revenant transformation is not granted by a class trainer, found in a spellbook, or achieved through sustained practice. It is a pact. Something offers it. The character accepts or does not.

The offer does not come from a person. It comes from a place: the Unmarked Threshold, a site that exists in three locations simultaneously — a specific deep chamber in Vel'Sharath, a specific point in the Sunken Ziggurat, and a point in the ruins of Ixareth-Kul that the Soulcarvers guard without fully understanding why. The Threshold is not a portal. It is a condition — a place where the boundary between alive and ended is thin enough that something on the other side can speak.

What speaks from the Threshold is not a deity, not a demon, and not an ancestor spirit in any sense that Shaman practice would recognize. The Bonewrights of Ixareth-Kul call it the Residue — what remains of a will that outlasted the body and the world that body belonged to. The Ash Scribes believe it is a Predecessor artifact, the last surviving coherence of the civilization that built the ziggurats, now reduced to a single sustained intention: to continue.

---

## The Terms

The Residue offers the Revenant transformation to characters who have demonstrated, through their alignment record, that they understand continuity through darkness — that they have chosen to persist in conditions that would have redirected a weaker will. Evil alignment is not a moral prerequisite; it is evidence of the specific kind of commitment the Residue recognizes.

**What is asked:**
- The character accepts that death, when it comes, will not be what it is for other characters. The Revenant does not become fully dead on death; they return from a state that is not exactly alive and is not exactly dead. The mechanism of this is not explained to the character in advance.
- The character accepts the presence of Ghost Tongue — not the ability to speak it, but the awareness of it. The language of the dead is now legible to the Revenant whether they want it to be or not. This cannot be turned off.
- The character accepts the -CHA consequence. What makes you approachable to other people is, in part, the signal that you are alive in the way they are alive. The Revenant no longer transmits this signal cleanly.

**What is given:**
- The Revenant body does not decay. It does not feel pain the way a living body does. It regenerates from damage that would end a living creature.
- Fear does not operate on the Revenant — not because they have overcome it, but because the thing fear points to (death, ending) has already been partially negotiated.
- Access to Ghost Tongue. The dead have been talking in this world for a long time. Most of it is ambient, fragmentary, and not useful. Some of it is not.

---

## The Cost

The alignment cost (-100) at the moment of transformation is not a punishment. It is the record of what the character agreed to. They agreed to be partly the Residue's in exchange for what the Residue offers. This is not a temporary condition that can be reversed through subsequent good behavior — the alignment will drift back if the character earns it back through action, but the debt is genuine and will be present in every subsequent alignment calculation.

The Residue does not enforce the pact. It does not need to. The Revenant transformation is permanent by nature, not by policing. The character is now what they agreed to become. The Residue offered terms; the character accepted them; the transformation is the result of the agreement having been completed, not the result of a chain being attached.

---

## The Three Sites

A character seeking the Revenant transformation must reach the Unmarked Threshold. This requires one of:
- High standing in Vel'Sharath and knowledge of the specific deep chamber's location (which the Dark Elf houses do not advertise but do not keep from sufficiently trusted characters)
- Access to the deep levels of the Sunken Ziggurat, past the dungeon's named boss
- Access to the specific Ixareth-Kul chamber the Soulcarvers guard — this requires completing enough of their questline that they trust the character with the chamber's location

A character who reaches the Threshold at any of the three sites is reaching the same thing. The Threshold is not spatially located in any one of them; the three sites are places where the geography happens to be thin enough.

The Residue does not compel, threaten, or persuade. It simply makes the offer and waits.
