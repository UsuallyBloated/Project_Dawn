# Tradeskills

Brainstorming list of tradeskills for Project Dawn. Not yet implemented.

---

## Gathering / Harvesting

| Tradeskill | Description |
|---|---|
| **Mining** | Extract ore, gems, and stone from mineral nodes in the world |
| **Logging** | Harvest timber and rare wood from trees |
| **Herbalism** | Forage plants, roots, fungi, and reagents from the environment |
| **Skinning** | Strip hides, pelts, and scales from slain creatures |
| **Fishing** | Catch fish, shellfish, and underwater treasures |
| **Trapping** | Set and collect small-game traps for pelts and meat |
| **Quarrying** | Extract large stone blocks used in construction and masonry |

## Notes

- Some tradeskills have natural prerequisites (e.g. Mining → Smelting → Blacksmithing).
- Skills likely cap at a numerical value that increases with character level or dedicated practice.

### Class Restrictions

- **Lockpicking** — Rogue-type classes only.
- **Poison Making** — available to Necromancers, Shadow-Knights, Blood Mage, andRogue-type classes only. 
- Class access may restrict other skills (e.g. Enchanting gated to Magician or higher-tier casters).

### Alignment Restrictions

- **Lockpicking** and **Poison Making** require evil alignment (or neutral at minimum, TBD).
- Some skills may have inverse restrictions — e.g. a Paladin-adjacent class might be barred from poison crafting entirely regardless of alignment.

## Creative Ideas & Brainstorm

### Race-Specific Unique Tradeskills (possible exclusives)

- **Bone Carving** (Ogre/Troll exclusive) — craft weapons, trinkets, and fetishes from monster bones and tusks. Thematically gritty; produces primitive but surprisingly effective gear.
- **Runeforging** (Dwarf exclusive) — a fusion of Blacksmithing and Runecarving. Engrave runes directly into metal during the forge process for bonuses that are baked into the item, not applied after.
- **Clockwork Engineering** (Gnome/Kobold exclusive) — a specialization of Tinkering focused on automated traps, mechanical companions, and siege-scale gadgets.
- **Shadow Weaving** (Dark Elf exclusive) — weave shadow-infused cloth that has innate stealth properties. Bridges Tailoring and dark magic.
- **Deepwater Fishing** (Troll exclusive) — Trolls know how to fish in dangerous, dark waters and can pull creatures others cannot. Yields rare deep-sea ingredients for Alchemy and Cooking.
- **Spore Cultivation** (Halfling and Wood Elf exclusive) — cultivate exotic fungi for alchemical reagents, poisons, and food. Bridges Herbalism and Alchemy.

### Interesting Skill Chain Ideas

- **Beekeeping → Candlemaking → Incense Crafting** — a full artisan chain that produces ritual consumables useful to caster classes.
- **Trapping → Skinning → Tanning → Leatherworking** — complete self-sufficient ranger/rogue chain.
- **Farming → Baking → Brewing** — the Halfling dream; produces the best food/drink buffs in the game.
- **Mining → Smelting → Blacksmithing → Runeforging** — the Dwarf endgame crafting path.
- **Herbalism → Alchemy → Poison Making** — the Dark Elf / Rogue chain; Herbalism feeds both healing potions and poison base reagents.

### Alignment-Gated Alchemy Branches

- **White Alchemy** (good/neutral) — healing potions, antidotes, stat elixirs.
- **Black Alchemy** (evil/neutral) — poisons, curses-in-a-bottle, plague vials. Same tradeskill, split recipes by alignment.

### Other Creative Tradeskill Ideas

- **Taxidermy** — mount creature heads and bodies as trophies; could also produce trinkets with passive stat effects.
- **Bookbinding** — combine Scribing with Cartography to produce sellable lore books, player-authored texts, or skill manuals.
- **Tideweaving** — a coastal tradeskill using sea plants, shells, and coral to craft light armor and jewelry (unique aesthetic niche).
- **Grave Robbing** (evil-aligned only) — a dark "gathering" skill; loot burial sites for rare reagents, cursed items, and components used in Necromantic Scribing.
- **Necromantic Scribing** (evil, Magician-class) — copy dark tomes and summon scrolls. Evil counterpart to standard Scribing.
- **Animal Husbandry → Breeding** — selectively breed mounts and pack animals for better stats, rare colorations, or combat traits.
