# Kel`varath Ascendant
*Race-Specific — Kel`varath — Evil — The Dominion Remembered*

## Overview
The Kel`varath Dominion fell three hundred years ago. The knowledge did not. The Bonewrights, the Soulcarvers, and the Ash Scribes preserved the most sophisticated necromantic practices in the world through the collapse, in fragments, in oral transmission, in the minds of scholars who died of old age before passing it to the next generation. What they preserved was functional but diminished — a library from which many volumes were missing.

The Kel`varath Ascendant is an Kel`varath who has recovered enough of those missing volumes that the Dominion-era power begins to manifest. Not a reincarnation, not a possession by an ancestor — a living Kel`varath who has trained deeply enough in the old ways that the scale plating thickens, the undead answer more readily, and ruins that were dark for centuries begin to respond.

## Requirements
- **Race:** Kel`varath
- **Alignment:** Evil (≤ -1501)
- **Classes:** Necromancer, Shadow Knight
- **Level:** 28
- **Trigger:** Deep investment in one of the three Bonewrights/Soulcarvers/Ash Scribes orders; completing a research arc within the order that recovers a piece of lost Dominion knowledge

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Enhanced Undead Command** — Maximum controllable undead pets increases by one; they respond faster and hit harder
- **Dominion Authority** — In Kel`varath ruins and Dominion sites: bonus to all abilities; certain dormant mechanisms activate
- **Ancient Scale** — Scale plating visibly thickens and darkens; natural armor increases; minor resistance to physical damage
- **Ancestor's Voice** — Can communicate with the spirits of Dominion-era Kel`varath specifically; provides lore access and certain rare quest options

**Visual:**
- Scale plating visibly heavier and darker than standard Kel`varath
- Eyes gain a faint secondary glow in darkness
- Necromantic and shadow spell effects are more elaborate in visual output

## Narrative Implications

Within Kel`varath communities, the Ascendant is both a figure of reverence and a political flashpoint. The Bonewrights celebrate. The factions trying to build something new without the Dominion's baggage are deeply ambivalent — the Dominion's power and the Dominion's catastrophic collapse are inseparable. The Ascendant represents one side of that argument made flesh.

Other races see the Dominion regaining power in any individual Kel`varath and react according to what their histories taught them about what that means. For most: it means nothing good.
