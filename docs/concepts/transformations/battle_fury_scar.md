# Battle-Fury Scar
*Earned Permanent — Neutral to Evil — The Edge You Stop Fearing*

## Overview
The Battle-Fury Scar is not a magical transformation. There is no spell involved, no ritual, no divine or demonic force at work. It is purely accumulative — the result of a body that has taken an enormous amount of damage over a career, survived it all, and developed a relationship with near-death that most living creatures don't have.

The character has been at the edge so many times that the edge stopped being frightening. Something in the body's crisis-response has been recalibrated by long exposure: at low HP, instead of the fear and impaired function that most creatures experience, the Warrior or Monk enters a state of clarified violence. The scars glow. The damage increases. The fear goes away.

This is not a blessing. The scars represent real accumulated damage, and the transformation is most accessible to characters who consistently fight at the edge of survival rather than managing resources carefully.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Neutral to Evil
- **Classes:** Warrior, Monk
- **Level:** 18
- **Trigger:** Accumulated total damage received over the character's lifetime exceeds a very high threshold; scar tissue reaches a density where something changes

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Crisis Surge** — Passive: when HP drops below 25%, damage output increases by 35% instead of decreasing. The closer to death, the more dangerous.
- **Fear Immunity** — Passive: complete immunity to fear effects; the character has lost the capacity for that particular response
- **Stun Resistance** — Significant passive resistance to stun/CC; the body doesn't register shock the way a normal body does
- **Unbreakable** — Passive: cannot die from a single hit that would take HP from above 25% to zero; minimum survival at 1 HP once per combat

**Visual:**
- The scars themselves are the visible marker — extensive, obviously old, covering exposed skin
- At low HP (below 25%) the scars illuminate briefly in a dull red-orange before fading
- Combat animations are slightly more aggressive in posture

## Narrative Implications

The Battle-Fury Scar is visible history written on the body, and anyone experienced in combat can read it. Veterans respect it — this is the mark of someone who didn't just survive dangerous situations, but survived an extraordinary number of them. Healers view it with the professional discomfort of people who've been shown what their work is fighting against.

NPCs who are afraid of violence will be afraid of this character specifically. NPCs who respect violence will be specifically respectful.
