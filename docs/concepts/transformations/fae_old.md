# Fae-Old
*Race-Specific — Fae — Any Alignment — The Shedding*

## Overview
Fae are already ancient. The Fae-Old transformation is what happens when a Fae lives long enough and practices deeply enough that the individual quality starts to thin, and what was a person begins to become a category. Not a loss of self — a deepening of it past the point where self is the right unit of measurement.

The Fae courts have a name for this stage and considerable institutional anxiety about it, because a Fae-Old in a court position carries authority that is technically theirs but derives from something larger than their individual claim to it. The distinction between "Fae elder" and "Fae-Old" is the distinction between "this person has been here a very long time" and "this person is in some meaningful sense what the Fae are, individuated."

It is disconcerting, and also very useful in negotiations.

## Requirements
- **Race:** Fae
- **Alignment:** Any
- **Classes:** Enchanter, Druid
- **Level:** 30
- **Trigger:** Sustained deep investment in Fae court practice at high level; the transformation arrives when the individual's magic has reached a depth that blurs the boundary between personal and categorical

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Illusion Depth** — Enchantment effects last longer and are harder to dispel; the Fae-Old's illusions have a structural quality that other illusions lack
- **Timeless** — Immune to aging effects, time-based debuffs, and haste/slow effects that don't specifically penetrate this resistance
- **Court Weight** — Social authority in Fae courts is immediately recognized at the highest available level; other Fae defer without requiring the Fae-Old to establish standing
- **Thinning** — Passive partial phase resistance; physical attacks have a small chance to pass through the Fae-Old; the boundary between them and the world is thinner than it used to be

**Visual:**
- The Fae-Old is slightly more luminous than their baseline; the inner glow that all Fae carry is brighter and more constant
- In certain light their edges are not entirely sharp
- Their expression in idle animations carries the specific patience of something that has outlived most of the things it has encountered

## Narrative Implications

Other Fae recognize the Fae-Old state immediately. Non-Fae NPCs perceive something different without being able to categorize it — the specific unease of encountering something that is technically understandable and experientially isn't.

The Fae-Old's relationship to the courts changes: they are no longer a court member navigating court politics. They are something the court navigates around.
