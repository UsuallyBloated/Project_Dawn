# Seraphim Aspect
*Earned Permanent — Exalted — The Divine Has Decided*

## Overview
The Seraphim Aspect is the furthest development of the Exalted transformation available to Paladin and Cleric. Where the [Exalted transformation](exalted.md) represents divine recognition, the Seraphim Aspect represents divine investment — the divine has not simply noticed this character; it has decided they are an expression of something it wants expressed in the world, and it has contributed accordingly.

Wings. The glow expands. The undead proximity damage increases substantially. The character has crossed from saint-candidate into something that older religious texts describe with words that were not originally meant for mortals.

This is the most powerful transformation available to Good-aligned characters and among the most powerful in the game overall. It is also the most demanding to reach — requiring Exalted alignment at level 35, specific quest conditions, and a sustained record of the faith community's most demanding acts.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Exalted (≥ 1500), sustained
- **Classes:** Paladin, Cleric
- **Level:** 35
- **Prerequisites:** Must already hold the [Exalted transformation](exalted.md). Must have resurrected players on multiple occasions (Cleric) or defeated a named undead boss (Paladin). Must have completed a specific divine-call quest that fires after these conditions are met.

## Effects

**Wings:**
- Functional cosmetically; not full flight (design target — potentially moveable to active flight ability)
- Major visual presence: large, feathered, gold-white
- Enemies will visibly react to the wings in proximity

**Enhanced Proximity:**
- **Holy Radiance** — Undead proximity damage increases substantially; larger radius, higher damage per tick; some undead will flee rather than engage
- **Sanctified Ground** — Walking in a zone gradually accumulates a minor buff for all allies nearby (small HoT, minor resistance)

**Divine Shield:**
- Once per combat, when HP would be reduced to zero, an automatic divine shield absorbs one lethal hit at full health
- This is a gift, not an ability — it activates without input and the player is informed it was used

**Visual:**
- Wings are the defining visual
- The glow is more intense than the base Exalted transformation
- In darkness the character becomes a significant light source
- Evil-aligned NPCs at a distance will stop moving and watch with fear

## Narrative Implications

A Seraphim Aspect character in any settlement is an event. Guards stop. Conversations pause. NPCs who are inclined toward faith will approach with reverence; NPCs who are opposed will give way from simple self-preservation. This is not something that blends into a crowd.

The practical consequence is that the Seraphim Aspect character has become a pillar of the world's Good infrastructure — NPCs will treat them as a resource, a symbol, and occasionally a burden, because symbols carry demands. There is no version of this transformation where the character gets to be anonymous.

For those who wanted this: it is the completion of everything the class was built toward.
For those who reached it by accident: welcome to a different kind of adventure.
