# Troll Ancient
*Race-Specific — Troll — Neutral to Evil — The Long Survival*

## Overview
There is no ritual. There is no threshold in any traditional sense. A Troll Ancient is simply a Troll who has survived long enough — long enough that the regeneration has had time to work on scale, that the accumulated damage has calcified into protection, that the warts have hardened into something closer to stone than skin. The transformation is not a moment. It is a recognition: at some point, the Troll stopped being a Troll who had survived everything and became a Troll for whom survival is simply the stable state.

Other races encounter a Troll Ancient and know immediately that something is different. Not from knowledge of the transformation — from the specific quality of being in the presence of something that has not been successfully killed in a very long time.

## Requirements
- **Race:** Troll
- **Alignment:** Neutral to Evil
- **Classes:** Any
- **Level:** 35
- **Trigger:** Sustained survival through high-damage content at high level; the regeneration has had time to work; no specific quest or ritual

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Doubled Regeneration** — Passive HP regen rate approximately doubles; even fire and acid (the standard Troll weaknesses) suppress it for shorter duration
- **Calcified Hide** — Natural armor increases significantly; wart tissue has hardened; physical damage reduction is meaningful
- **Old Scars** — Passive resistance to the damage type that has most frequently reduced the character below 25% HP over their career; the body has learned
- **Ancient Fire Resistance** — Partial resistance to fire (the classic Troll weakness); not immunity, but meaningful reduction — old Trolls develop partial adaptation

**Visual:**
- Size increases fractionally — Troll Ancients are slightly larger than standard
- Skin texture is visibly different: calcified, layered, terrain-map quality from the accumulated scar tissue
- Eyes are still and bright; something in them is very old

## Narrative Implications

Troll Ancient is the transformation with the lowest dramatic moment and the highest long-term presence. An Ancient walking into a territory changes what that territory means — it is now claimed by something very difficult to remove, and everything in the area adjusts accordingly.

Warband culture treats an Ancient with the specific deference of knowing that the Ancient has survived every challenge their culture has ever produced. The Ancient is the test case result. They are proof that the culture works.
