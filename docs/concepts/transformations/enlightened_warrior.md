# Enlightened Warrior
*Alignment Drift — Exalted tier — The Light Has Noticed*

## Overview
Warriors have no magic. They never did. The Enlightened Warrior did not acquire magic — something came to them. After a long career of fighting in the service of Good, of protecting people who couldn't protect themselves, of applying the only skill they have in the service of something that mattered, the accumulated weight of those choices reached a threshold that the divine apparently has an opinion about.

This transformation does not give the Warrior spells. It gives them presence. The holy effects that manifest are passive, aura-based, emerging from what the character is rather than what they can cast. A practitioner who spent decades learning a discipline doesn't suddenly know a different one. But the light notices discipline, and when it notices long enough, it offers something back.

The Enlightened Warrior is the rarest of the Exalted-tier drift transformations because Warriors have the slowest alignment drift of any class — no holy spells generating positive alignment, no divine quests, just the slow accumulation of fighting Evil enemies and protecting others. Players who reach Exalted on a Warrior did so the hardest possible way.

## Requirements
- **Class:** Warrior
- **Trigger:** Alignment score reaches Exalted tier (≥ 1500)
- **Reversibility:** Reverses if alignment drops below Good.

## Effects

**Gained (passive and active):**
- **Righteous Stance** — Passive: auto-attacks deal bonus holy damage to undead and Evil-aligned enemies
- **Unyielding** — Active: brief CC immunity on activation; once per combat
- **Courage Aura** — Passive: nearby allies gain a minor morale bonus (small stat boost while within range)
- **Judgment** — Passive: when below 30% HP, holy energy partially shields incoming damage

**Nothing is lost.** The Warrior's existing skill set is unchanged. What's added is an overlay, not a replacement.

**Visual:**
- A faint gold-white glow at the edges of the armor during combat
- Hit effects against undead show brief holy flare
- The character's bearing is readable — something in them is settled

## Narrative Implications

The Enlightened Warrior receives no institutional recognition. The faiths that produce Paladins and Clerics have no framework for a martial who reached Exalted without any divine training. Temples may receive them with respectful uncertainty. Some clergy see it as validation that the divine works outside their framework. Others see it as a theological problem.

What the Enlightened Warrior gets, unambiguously, is the respect of anyone who understands how hard that climb is without magic.
