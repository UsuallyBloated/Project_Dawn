# Tainted Monk
*Alignment Drift — Evil tier — The Discipline Serves Darkness*

## Overview
The Monk's discipline is one of the hardest things in the game to corrupt, because it is not an external power that can be redirected — it is a trained internal state. A Monk reaching Evil tier did not have their chi hijacked by dark forces. They changed what they chose to do with it. The discipline remains. The application is now predatory.

The Tainted Monk retains everything that made them effective. The speed, the precision, the capacity to strike the exact point that matters — all of it is intact. What changes is the energy that runs through it. Chi is morally neutral; it goes where its practitioner directs it. A Tainted Monk has spent long enough directing it toward cruelty that the energy has taken on a shadow quality, and the abilities that emerge from it reflect that.

This transformation is philosophically the most interesting of the drift group. The Monk tradition is built on the idea that discipline perfects the practitioner toward Good. A Tainted Monk is a refutation of that claim, or a complication of it.

## Requirements
- **Class:** Monk
- **Trigger:** Alignment score reaches Evil tier (≤ -1501)
- **Reversibility:** Reverses if alignment returns to Good or higher.

## Effects

**Lost:**
- Meditative healing passive (the inner calm that enabled it is compromised)
- Positive-alignment chi abilities (if any were gained through leveling)

**Gained:**
- **Shadow Step** — Short-range blink behind the current target; usable once per combat
- **Chi Drain** — Unarmed strike variant: transfers a portion of target's stamina to caster; target is briefly slowed
- **Void Stance** — Toggle: evasion bonus active but damage taken is partially returned as shadow damage to attacker
- **Darkened Reflex** — Passive: counter-attack chance increases; the strike back is shadow-aspected

**Nothing mechanically lost from core Monk combat** — this is an addition, not a replacement. The Tainted Monk is a more dangerous combatant. That is the problem.

**Visual:**
- Chi effects shift from warm gold to a cold blue-black
- Strike trails leave brief shadow afterimages
- Character's face in combat — still composed, still precise; that composure is now unsettling rather than peaceful

## Narrative Implications

The Monk communities that trained them react with the specific grief of an institution confronting a case it didn't prevent. The training is supposed to refine toward Good. A Tainted Monk is evidence that the training works on the practitioner's choices, not their soul — and that a disciplined practitioner who chooses Evil is a more effective Evil than an undisciplined one.

Some Monk masters maintain that the Tainted Monk is a test case for the tradition's limits, and that understanding the failure mode is part of mastering the discipline. The Tainted Monk themselves usually doesn't care about the institution's theological processing of their existence.
