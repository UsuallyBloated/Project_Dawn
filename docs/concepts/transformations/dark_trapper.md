# Dark Trapper
*Alignment Drift — Evil tier — The Hunt Turns Cursed*

## Overview
A Rogue's tools are precision, patience, and the willingness to strike from a position of total advantage. At Evil tier, those same tools — directed by a person with no remaining moral constraints — become something else entirely. The poisons become curses. The careful preparation becomes ritualized predation. The backstab becomes a soul-strike that takes something that doesn't heal easily.

The Dark Trapper is not dramatically different from the Rogue in combat profile. The change is in the kind of harm they can produce. A standard Rogue can kill you. A Dark Trapper can mark you, drain something fundamental, and leave a wound that the healer can close only with difficulty. The efficiency is retained. The moral ceiling is removed.

The name is informal — something that bounty hunters and guild enforcers use when they need to describe what a Rogue looks like after they've crossed certain lines. The Rogue themselves probably doesn't use it.

## Requirements
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Trigger:** Alignment score reaches Evil tier (≤ -1501)
- **Reversibility:** Reverses if alignment returns to Good or higher.

## Effects

**Lost:**
- Certain poison applications that required clean-hands alignment (vendor access, guild membership, etc. — social losses rather than mechanical)

**Gained:**
- **Soul Tap** — Backstab variant: deals standard backstab damage and reduces target's max HP by a small percentage until they rest. Does not stack.
- **Hex Blade** — Imbue weapon with a curse that spreads to the next target the first target attacks after being struck. Curse applies a minor DoT.
- **Shadow Mark** — Mark current target: they take bonus damage from all damage sources for 15 seconds. Usable from stealth.
- **Blood Trail** — Passive: targets affected by the character's DoTs take slightly increased damage from the character's next direct attack

**Visual:**
- Poisons take on a darker, more viscous visual quality
- Backstab impact has a brief shadow flare
- Shadow Mark applies a visible dark outline on the target

## Narrative Implications

Rogue guilds maintain complex stances on alignment. Most operate under neutrality — they'll contract with anyone who pays. A member reaching Evil tier is an internal matter, and some guilds will simply continue the relationship if the member remains useful.

Others will cut ties. A Dark Trapper working without guild backing is a freelance predator, which the guild infrastructure generally regards as bad for business — someone with no institutional accountability and no interest in maintaining the fiction that rogues are merely professional.

The Dark Trapper's social position is uniquely uncomfortable: they're too marked for the clean side of the world and too unaffiliated for most Evil factions to take at face value.
