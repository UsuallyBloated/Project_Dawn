# Plague Bearer
*Earned Permanent — Evil — The Bodies Said Stop*

## Overview
The Plague Bearer is what a Necromancer or dark-path Shaman becomes when they have applied disease and death to enough targets that the process has become part of them. The corruption they distributed is now resident. Their body has become a vector — not suffering from disease but carrying it, housing it, in a way that is deeply uncomfortable for anyone nearby and genuinely lethal at close range.

This is not a glamorous transformation. There is no moment of triumph. There is instead the gradual realization that something about the character's presence is making people sick, and the decision — conscious or unconscious — to lean into that rather than resist it.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Evil (≤ -1501)
- **Classes:** Necromancer, Shaman (dark alignment path)
- **Level:** 24
- **Trigger:** Applying disease/poison DoT effects to a very high lifetime total of targets; the corruption of others eventually corrupts the channel through which it passes

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Disease Aura** — Passive: enemies within melee range accumulate disease stacks each second; stacks deal minor damage and reduce healing received
- **Immune to disease and poison** — completely; the body houses the corruption and is no longer affected by it
- **Plague Bolt** — New ability: ranged disease attack that applies a unique DoT variant stronger than standard abilities
- **Carrion Presence** — Certain undead and disease-linked mobs treat the Plague Bearer as non-threatening (they sense something familiar)

**Complications:**
- **Healing effects reduced on self** — the corruption interferes with clean restoration; holy healing is less effective
- **NPC discomfort** — NPCs in close proximity in cities display visible discomfort animations; some refuse to remain near the character; vendor interactions are brief

**Visual:**
- Skin has a faint mottled quality — not dramatic, but visible in good light
- Breath is visible as a faint miasma even in warm weather
- Disease DoT particle effects are more intense than standard

## Narrative Implications

The Plague Bearer cannot comfortably enter most cities. The passive disease aura, while not lethal to NPCs (the stacks clear quickly outside combat context), produces visible sick reactions at close range. Guards who notice will demand the character leave. Healers and Clerics react with professional alarm. This is a transformation that progressively narrows the social world.

What it opens instead: certain zones, certain quest-givers, and certain factions that specifically deal with plague, disease, and the kind of warfare that uses vectors rather than swords. Necromancers who pursue this path gain access to a dark ecology of their own.
