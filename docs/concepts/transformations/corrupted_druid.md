# Corrupted Druid
*Alignment Drift — Evil tier — The Blight Within*

## Overview
The Druid's power comes from a living relationship with the natural world — the forest, the soil, the water, the slow persistence of growing things. It is a power that requires something in return: care, attention, and a moral alignment that the living world recognizes as trustworthy.

A Druid who reaches Evil has poisoned that relationship. The connection to the natural world persists — it cannot be fully severed — but what comes through it has changed character. The forest that once answered warmly now answers with thorns. The healing energies become corrupting energies. The growth magic becomes decay. Nature does not abandon the Corrupted Druid; it becomes what they've taught it to be.

This is ecologically interesting, narratively. The Corrupted Druid is not separate from nature — they are a part of nature that has gone blighted. Some of the most effective poisons come from the deepest woods.

## Requirements
- **Class:** Druid
- **Trigger:** Alignment score reaches Evil tier (≤ -1501)
- **Reversibility:** Reverses if alignment returns to Good or higher.

## Effects

**Lost:**
- All healing spells (HoTs, direct heals)
- Beneficial growth and nature-enhancement spells
- Movement speed buffs (Spirit of Wolf equivalent)
- Root/snare retain their effect but change school (nature becomes blight)

**Gained:**
- **Blight Bloom** — AoE poison field: ground effect that damages and slows all enemies within radius
- **Wither** — Reverse-HoT applied to target: drains HP each tick, transferred partially to caster
- **Corpse Vine** — Root that deals damage-over-time while holding the target; the vines feed
- **Spore Cloud** — Brief blinding/disorienting cloud; nature-school area debuff
- **Carrion Shift** — Animal companion (if any) becomes a blighted warder variant; visual change

**Visual:**
- Nature magic takes on a sickly green-black tinge
- Bloom and growth particle effects become wilt and rot effects
- Character's appearance shifts subtly: bark texture on skin edges, moss and fungus rather than flowers

## Narrative Implications

The Corrupted Druid's community — Wood Elves, other Druids, Ranger allies — reacts with something between grief and fury. The violation is not just personal; it affects the territory the Druid was steward of. Regions where a Corrupted Druid has operated may show actual ecological damage — blighted zones, diseased trees, poisoned water sources.

Some Corrupted Druids lean into this as a philosophy: nature includes death and decay; they are still part of nature; the beautiful and the terrible are both real. It's not a completely wrong argument. It's also frequently used to justify things that have nothing to do with ecological balance.
