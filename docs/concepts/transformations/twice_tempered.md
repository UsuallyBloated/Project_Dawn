# Twice-Tempered
*Quest Endpoint — Good to Exalted — The Burning Road*

## Overview
A Paladin who fell to Evil and came back. Not through a mechanical shortcut — through the Burning Road: a quest arc that requires confronting what specifically broke the alignment, healing a threshold of damage, freeing imprisoned souls, and completing a culminating act of atonement that the faith community considers sufficient for re-ordination.

The result is not a Paladin who was always going to be Good. It is a Paladin who knows exactly what they are capable of and chose differently, repeatedly, past the point where it would have been easier to stop. The faith trusts that more than it trusts inexperience. So does the divine.

Twice-Tempered is not a second transformation layered on the Paladin — it replaces the standard Paladin state with a version that reflects where they've been. They retain one dark ability as a permanent scar of the fall. Everything else is holy.

## Requirements
- **Class:** Paladin who has experienced the [Fallen Paladin](fallen_paladin.md) drift transformation
- **Trigger:** Completion of the Burning Road quest arc (see below)
- **Alignment:** Must reach at least Good tier during the arc; Exalted recommended for full access to final quest stages
- **Reversibility:** Permanent. The Twice-Tempered state is stable — not a drift state, but a new baseline.

## The Burning Road Arc
A multi-stage atonement quest that begins when a Fallen Paladin demonstrates early signs of return (alignment improving past -1000).

**Stage 1 — The Acknowledgment:**
- Seek out the NPC contact who recognizes what happened (a former faith community member, a Cleric who remembers them, or a blind priest who cannot see what they became)
- Complete a dialogue that requires no deception — the system checks alignment; active Evil cannot access the later stages
- Receive the mission brief

**Stage 2 — The Work:**
- Heal a specified total of HP using Fallen Paladin abilities (the dark tools doing good; this is the point)
- Free a number of imprisoned souls — a specific dungeon encounter where souls are bound by a Necromancer or similar
- These two requirements run concurrently; either can be completed first

**Stage 3 — The Confrontation:**
- Return to a specific location connected to the moment of the fall (a quest flag set when Fallen Paladin was triggered)
- Complete a solo encounter — not necessarily combat; what it is depends on why they fell
- This stage cannot be designed generically; it is the most narratively rich part of the arc and requires implementation per player history or a fixed dramatic equivalent

**Stage 4 — The Return:**
- Present to the original faith community or an equivalent
- The transformation fires; Twice-Tempered state is applied

## Effects

**All standard Paladin holy abilities return,** including full spell and skill access.

**Additionally gained:**
- **Tempered Will** — Passive: complete immunity to mind-control and charm effects; they have been tested and held
- **Scar's Edge** — One retained dark ability (Shadow Flame) remains available; reduced power; a permanent reminder. Cannot be removed or replaced.
- **Twice-Blessed** — Healing spells have slightly increased effectiveness compared to standard Paladin; the arc has deepened the connection

**Visual:**
- Standard Paladin appearance with one persistent mark — a shadow-line scar on one hand that never fully heals
- Holy glow is slightly warmer in tone than a standard Paladin's — more amber, less white
- The Scar's Edge ability, when used, produces the shadow effect against a gold backdrop; it looks like what it is

## Narrative Implications

The Twice-Tempered is the most interesting character in any room they walk into, to anyone who knows what they're looking at. The faith community that re-ordained them has complicated feelings. The Evil factions they left behind have simpler ones. The players who watched the arc unfold have the most investment.

This transformation should be difficult enough that it means something, and rewarding enough that it was worth it.
