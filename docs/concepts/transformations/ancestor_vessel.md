# Ancestor Vessel
*Race-Specific — Minotaur — Any Alignment — The Asking Becomes Unnecessary*

## Overview
A Minotaur Shaman carries the dead. That is the practice: ancestor names carved into the horns, ancestor voices consulted through ritual, ancestor memory kept alive because someone has to carry it. The Ancestor Vessel transformation is the point at which the carrying becomes structural rather than deliberate — the ancestors are not called because they never really left.

The Vessel does not channel the ancestors voluntarily. They are always present. Not controlling, not intrusive — resident. The Vessel's decisions are still their own. The ancestors contribute the weight of collected memory, the occasional urgent prompting when something connects to what they know, and the specific quality of presence that other characters perceive when the Vessel is near: the sense that the person they are talking to has many more perspectives than one body should contain.

For a people whose culture is built on carrying the dead forward because history tried to erase them, the Ancestor Vessel is the most complete expression of that practice.

## Requirements
- **Race:** Minotaur
- **Alignment:** Any
- **Class:** Shaman
- **Level:** 28
- **Trigger:** Sustained deep shamanic practice; the horns are full; the ancestors have been called enough times that returning became more effort than staying

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Collective Memory** — Access to the knowledge held by every ancestor spirit carried; bonus to lore checks, history dialogue, and certain quest paths that require knowing what happened before living memory
- **Always Present** — Shaman passive buffs (those that normally require active song or sustained channeling) pulse at a fraction of their normal power in a radius around the Vessel without being actively cast; the ancestors contribute
- **Fear Ward** — Immune to fear effects; the collective weight of the ancestral presence is not something fear operates on
- **Vessel's Voice** — On death, the character does not die immediately — the ancestors hold them at 1 HP for 5 seconds while the collective decides if the work is finished; if the Vessel is healed in that window, they survive; if not, they fall

**Visual:**
- The ancestor spirits are semi-visible in the Vessel's presence — not dramatic, not distracting, but perceptible to other characters as a faint crowd around the Vessel
- The horn carvings glow faintly during ability use
- The Vessel's eyes in certain light carry a depth that suggests more than one person looking

## Narrative Implications

Within Minotaur culture, the Ancestor Vessel is simultaneously the highest achievement and the most solemn responsibility. The ancestors living in the Vessel are not at rest. They are still here, still carrying. The Vessel has taken on the weight of every name and agreed to carry it further.

Other races who encounter the Vessel and know enough to understand what they are seeing tend to react with a respect that transcends faction alignment. Something about the specific act of carrying the dead forward that long, voluntarily, earns cross-cultural recognition that most other achievements don't produce.
