# Dragon Kin
*Race-Specific — Kobold — Any Alignment — The Blood Was Real*

## Overview
Kobolds believe they are the lesser kin of dragons. Every pack shaman maintains this, every elder repeats it, and most of the races that deal with Kobolds treat it as charming mythology. Dragon Kin is what happens when a specific Kobold, pursuing the right class in the right direction, actualizes the connection and discovers the pack was right.

The transformation does not make the character a dragon. It makes them a Kobold in whom the dragon bloodline has expressed — scales appear on the face and hands, minor fire breath becomes available, and something in the bearing changes: the specific shift of a creature that has always been underestimated suddenly becoming somewhat harder to underestimate.

The confusion on the faces of the enemies who encounter it for the first time is the Kobold's favorite part.

## Requirements
- **Race:** Kobold
- **Alignment:** Any
- **Classes:** Sorcerer, Shaman
- **Level:** 20
- **Trigger:** Shaman: deep ancestor-spirit communion revealing the truth of the lineage. Sorcerer: innate power expressing a bloodline quality that was always there.

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Dragon Breath** — Short-range fire breath; deals AoE fire damage in a cone; limited uses per combat; scales with INT
- **Dragon Scale** — Minor natural armor from the emerging scale patches; physical damage reduction
- **Dragonfear** — Passive: enemies occasionally hesitate briefly when the character uses Dragon Breath (brief fear-adjacent reaction; not a hard CC)
- **Lineage Sight** — Can detect dragon-type enemies or dragon-influenced magic at range; some lore/dialogue access

**Visual:**
- Scale patches on face, hands, and shoulders — iridescent, keyed to the character's primary element (fire = red-gold, shadow = dark purple, etc.)
- Eyes change: vertical pupils, brighter coloration
- Dragon Breath attack has obvious visual

## Narrative Implications

The pack's reaction when a Dragon Kin returns is the closest thing to religious ecstasy Kobold culture produces. The pack shaman, who has been maintaining this belief for decades, has been validated in the most direct possible way. The Alpha's reaction is more complicated — the Dragon Kin is now the most prestigious thing the pack has ever produced, which has implications for hierarchy.

Other races react with a range from academic fascination (Gnomes: immediate research interest) to re-evaluation of assumptions (Humans who dealt with Kobolds as pests reconsidering their position) to zero change whatsoever (most Trolls couldn't care less).
