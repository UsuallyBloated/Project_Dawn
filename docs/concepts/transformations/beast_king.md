# Beast King
*Earned Permanent — Any Alignment — Together Long Enough*

## Overview
The Beast Master's warder is not a pet. That's the first thing trainers tell new Beast Masters, and most of them don't fully understand it until much later. The warder is a second perspective on the same hunt — a spirit-bonded animal that chose the bond as much as the caster did. After enough battles, enough shared danger, enough years of the bond deepening past language: the Beast King transformation. The warder and the Beast Master have been together long enough that the boundary between them has become porous.

The Beast King does not control the warder. The warder is not controlled by the Beast King. What they have instead is a complete and continuous mutual awareness — two bodies, one hunt.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Any
- **Class:** Beast Master
- **Level:** 25
- **Trigger:** Warder survives a very high number of battles without being permanently lost; the bond deepens past its normal threshold

## Effects

**Warder enhancements:**
- The warder's stats grow beyond their normal scaling ceiling
- The warder's appearance changes — larger, marked with the Beast Master's colors, visibly ancient in the way that long-bonded animals become
- When the warder dies, the retreat timer is longer but the warder returns at 50% HP instead of 30%

**Beast Master enhancements:**
- **Predator's Instinct** — Passive: +DEX and +AGI when the warder is within range; the bond sharpens both
- **Shared Wound** — Passive: if the warder takes massive damage in a single hit, the Beast Master absorbs a portion; and vice versa; the bond distributes extremes
- **Fury of Two** — Enhanced Warder's Fury skill: both the Beast Master and the warder deal bonus damage, and the warder's attack speed temporarily increases
- **Grief Rage** — When the warder dies, the Beast Master enters a combat rage for 10 seconds: damage increased, cannot use spells; this cannot be suppressed

**Visual:**
- The warder's appearance is visibly enhanced — larger, more defined, subtly matching the Beast Master's coloring
- The Beast Master's combat animations have a slightly more fluid, predatory quality
- The Fury of Two visual is more dramatic than standard Warder's Fury

## Narrative Implications

Beast King is the transformation that Beast Master trainers recognize on sight and the one they tell trainees will take a very long time. It is the completion of the class fantasy — not a power acquisition, but a deepening. Other Beast Masters respect it without ceremony; it is the thing they are all theoretically working toward.

The Grief Rage is not optional and is not glamorous. Losing the warder temporarily costs the Beast Master control. This is lore-accurate: the bond being severed, even briefly, is the worst thing that happens to a Beast King, and the body responds before the mind can.
