# Exalted
*Earned Permanent — Exalted tier — The Recognized*

## Overview
The Exalted transformation (distinct from the [Exalted alignment tier](../alignment/exalted.md)) is the permanent physical manifestation of sustained divine favor. It is not available to everyone at Exalted tier — it requires Exalted tier plus class and level conditions. It is what happens when a Paladin or Cleric has sustained Exalted alignment long enough, at high enough level, that the divine's noticing becomes structural rather than conditional.

The glow is real. The undead proximity damage is passive and always on. The character has crossed from "someone the divine approves of" into "someone the divine has marked." The distinction matters — the Seraphim Aspect is a further development of this state, and it begins here.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Exalted (≥ 1500)
- **Classes:** Paladin, Cleric
- **Level:** 30
- **Reversibility:** If alignment drops below Exalted, the transformation becomes dormant. The visual fades. The passive effects stop. If Exalted tier is regained, the transformation re-activates. It does not require re-earning — the mark remains; it simply dims.

## Effects

**Passive (always active while at Exalted tier):**
- **Glow aura** — Soft gold-white light emanates from the character; visible to all players and NPCs
- **Holy Proximity** — Undead enemies within a radius take continuous minor holy damage from the character's presence; weak undead may flee
- **Blessed Resilience** — Passive bonus to resistance against fear, charm, and mind-control effects
- **Radiance** — Healing spells cast on allies gain a small bonus effectiveness when the caster is at Exalted

**Visual:**
- Persistent glow effect (can be toggled cosmetically but the underlying state is legible to NPCs regardless)
- Holy symbol glows brighter; if worn armor has divine symbols they illuminate
- Hit effects have brief gold flash

## Narrative Implications

The Exalted transformation is the most socially legible transformation in the game, in the Good/Neutral space. NPCs react to it immediately and clearly. Evil-faction NPCs are hostile at a greater range. Good-faction NPCs may kneel, bow, or approach to ask for assistance.

The character has become, in the world's perception, a living symbol. This has consequences. Enemies specifically seek them out for elimination. Good-aligned communities may lean on them as a resource in ways that require response. There is no neutral ground for someone visibly marked by divine favor — every room they walk into changes.

Some players will find this deeply satisfying. Others will prefer the quieter progress of the [Warden of the Wild](warden_of_the_wild.md) or [Grove Heart](grove_heart.md), which achieve their own forms of Exalted expression without the social spotlight.
