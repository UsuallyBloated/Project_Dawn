# Revenant
*Earned Permanent — Evil — The Unfinished*

## Overview
A Revenant is a person who died and came back. Not as a skeleton, not as a ghost — as themselves, preserved at the moment of death, no longer entirely alive. They retain everything: skills, memories, levels, the specific weight of whatever they left unfinished. What they lose is the body's living quality and, with it, access to the world's healing infrastructure and the ordinary comforts of being alive.

The transformation is available only to Evil-aligned characters. Something in the nature of the return selects for souls already oriented toward darkness. Good and Neutral souls that die simply do not come back this way. Whether this is a property of the afterlife's architecture or a selection effect, the Revenants themselves largely agree it has something to do with unfinished business that the ordinary afterlife couldn't absorb.

This is the most significant physical transformation in the game — it changes what the character fundamentally is.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Evil (≤ -1501)
- **Classes:** Necromancer, Shadow Knight, Blood Mage
- **Level:** 20
- **Trigger:** Death while meeting the above conditions; triggers the return rather than normal death

## Effects

**Gained:**
- Immune to poison, disease, and environmental exposure damage
- Immune to starvation and thirst
- Immune to fear effects
- Resistance to death magic and compulsion
- Bonus to Necromancer and Blood Mage ability effectiveness
- Reduced aggression from undead mobs (not zero — controlled undead obey their master; free undead see something familiar)

**Lost:**
- No out-of-combat natural HP regeneration (no living metabolism; the body does not repair itself)
- Food and drink provide no benefit; out-of-combat regen buffs do not apply
- Healing must come from magical sources
- CON stat effectively frozen — the body does not change

**Visual:**
- Skin shifts to grey-blue; exact shade depends on race
- Eyes become still — present, aware, but without the constant micro-movement of living eyes
- Body temperature is ambient
- The wound that triggered the death remains as a permanent visible scar
- All original race features persist — an Ogre Revenant is still an Ogre

## Social Effects
- Good-aligned NPCs react with unease or hostility
- Some Evil-aligned NPCs react with respect or recognition
- Certain Revenant-specific NPCs and vendors become accessible
- The community of Greyveil (if implemented) treats the Revenant as unremarkable

## Notes
- Reversibility: **not reversible through normal play.** This is a permanent physical change.
- A Revenant who completes specific quests may find ways to accommodate their condition (Revenant-compatible consumables, alternative healing sources) but cannot undo the transformation.
- The transformation is a death event — the character's death animation plays, then they stand up wrong.
