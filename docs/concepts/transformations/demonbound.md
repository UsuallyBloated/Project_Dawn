# Demonbound
*Quest Endpoint — Evil — The Pact*

## Overview
The Pact is offered once, in a specific place, at a specific time. Not to everyone — to characters who are Evil-aligned, at a sufficient level, in the right zone at the right hour. A presence. An offer. The specific terms are negotiable only in the sense that the demon allows you to believe they are negotiable.

What is given: one demonic ability, keyed to the character's class. What is taken: one eye, replaced with something that sees differently. The obligation: ongoing, vague, documented in small print that the character technically agreed to but probably didn't read carefully.

The Demonbound is the mechanical seed for the Warlock class — these are the characters who the world's lore explains Warlocks as emerging from, over generations. An individual Demonbound is not a Warlock, but they are the thing Warlocks are made of.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Evil (≤ -1501)
- **Classes:** Any
- **Level:** 18 minimum
- **Trigger:** The Pact encounter — specific zone, specific time of day (night), specific trigger condition TBD; the encounter is not guaranteed to fire on every visit; it appears when the character is ready by the demon's assessment

## The Pact
The encounter is brief. The demon does not negotiate extensively. The offer is clear: one ability, class-specific. The cost is clear. The obligation is stated in terms that are technically accurate and deliberately difficult to interpret. The character can refuse — the encounter ends, the zone returns to normal, and the character will not be approached again for a significant time.

If the pact is accepted, the transformation fires immediately.

## Effects — Always Applied

- **Demonic Eye** — One eye is visibly changed: vertical pupil, faint red-orange glow, no whites; the changed eye sees certain things others don't (detect hidden, detect Good alignment)
- **Minor resistance to fire** — The demonic resonance provides basic fire resistance
- **Demon-adjacent NPC reactions** — Some Evil-faction NPCs recognize the mark and treat the character with particular respect. Some Good-faction NPCs react with specific alarm beyond standard Evil recognition.

## The Obligation
The demon's terms are vague. During implementation, possible interpretations:
- Occasional quest demands from the demon (refuse at consequence cost)
- The character serves as a beacon for demonic activity in their zone (slightly more demon-type mobs spawn nearby)
- A slow, persistent alignment drain (the pact costs alignment over time, pushing further Evil)
- A specific named quest arc that arrives later in the character's career

*Design note: the obligation should feel real but not burdensome — the player chose this, and the price should be interesting rather than punishing.*

## Notes
- Demonbound is the conceptual seed for the planned Warlock class
- Multiple Demonbound players in a region may have some interaction — the demon network is aware
- Reversibility: the pact can theoretically be broken through a high-level quest involving a specific Witch Hunter or holy-aligned character, at significant cost. Design target only.
