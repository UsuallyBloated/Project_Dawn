# Felhari Spirit-Walker
*Race-Specific — Felhari — Neutral — The Clan Does Not End at the Living*

## Overview
Every Felhari knows the clan does not end at the living. The ancestors are present in the shamanic tradition, in the stories, in the names given at the Gathering. The Spirit-Walker transformation is the Felhari who has practiced deeply enough, listened carefully enough, and maintained sufficient alignment that the ancestors began communicating without being called.

The clan spirit — not a single ancestor but the collective presence of everyone the clan has lost — begins to manifest around the Spirit-Walker in combat. Not as a sustained pet, not as a controllable ability, but as a intermittent presence that acts when it decides to act, when its knowledge and the moment align. The Spirit-Walker has not gained control of something. They have gained a relationship with something that has always been there.

## Requirements
- **Race:** Felhari
- **Alignment:** Neutral (-299 to 299)
- **Classes:** Shaman, Monk
- **Level:** 26
- **Trigger:** Sustained deep shamanic or disciplined practice; sustained Neutral alignment; the clan spirit recognizes the character as someone whose company it prefers

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Spirit Double** — During combat, the clan spirit occasionally manifests as a ghostly Felhari form that makes a single strike alongside the character before fading; not controllable or predictable; functions like a random-proc additional attack
- **Ancestral Warning** — Passive: the clan spirit occasionally communicates danger before it presents; bonus to detecting hidden enemies and traps; occasionally the character knows something is wrong before they know why
- **Two-Way Channel** — The ancestors can contact the Spirit-Walker, not just be called; some quest-related information arrives this way; the character may receive direction without explicitly requesting it

**Visual:**
- In combat, the spirit double appears briefly when it strikes — a translucent Felhari in traditional clan dress, visible for the duration of the strike and then gone
- The Spirit-Walker's eyes in idle moments occasionally focus on something that isn't there

## Narrative Implications

Within the clan, the Spirit-Walker's status changes from accomplished practitioner to something closer to an elder function — they carry active communication with the dead in a direction that normal shaman practice doesn't produce. The clan elder will want to know what the ancestors are saying, which is both an honor and an obligation.

Other clans, at the Gathering, recognize the Spirit-Walker's status and treat them with corresponding weight. The transformation is visible to other Felhari Shamans who know what to look for.
