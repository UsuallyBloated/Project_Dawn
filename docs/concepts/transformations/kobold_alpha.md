# Kobold Alpha
*Race-Specific — Kobold — Evil — The Pack Answers*

## Overview
Kobold hierarchy is simple: the strongest leads. The Kobold Alpha transformation is the formalization of absolute dominance — not just within one pack, but as a quality that other Kobolds recognize across pack lines. The Alpha is not simply the strongest Kobold in their pack. They are the kind of Kobold that packs orient around instinctively, that even rival packs' members hesitate to challenge.

This is not magic. It is the accumulation of victories and survivals reaching a threshold where the species' pack-recognition instinct fires at full intensity. Wild Kobolds in the zone respond differently. Pack members fight harder. Something in how the Alpha moves makes other Kobolds reconsider.

## Requirements
- **Race:** Kobold
- **Alignment:** Evil (≤ -1501)
- **Classes:** Warrior, Shadow Knight
- **Level:** 20
- **Trigger:** High kill count, sustained Evil alignment, and repeated victories against significantly dangerous enemies; the pack-recognition instinct fires when the record is long enough

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Pack Dominance** — Wild Kobold mobs in the current zone become non-hostile and can be briefly commanded (30 seconds, simple commands: attack/follow/stay); they return to neutral after
- **Alpha's Pack Tactics** — The pack tactics bonus (normally requires Kobold allies nearby) becomes a permanent passive; the Alpha fights as if in a pack even alone
- **Intimidating Presence** — Passive: enemies that are significantly weaker than the Alpha (level-gated) occasionally hesitate before engaging — brief delay on initial aggro
- **Size** — The Alpha is slightly larger than standard Kobold; still small; just less small

**Visual:**
- Fur darker and more defined than standard Kobold
- Bearing is different — the hunched, quick-movement quality of standard Kobolds is replaced by something more deliberate
- Eyes are steadier

## Narrative Implications

The Alpha's own pack is the most affected. They now have something that functions as a known quantity in inter-pack negotiations — the Alpha's presence changes how other packs approach their territory. Pack status improves, which means better access to the tunnel network, better trading positions, and more leverage in the inter-pack dynamics that govern Kobold underground politics.

Non-Kobold NPCs respond to the size and bearing change — the Alpha reads as something that requires more assessment than a standard Kobold, and most NPCs update their behavior accordingly. The reaction is not fear, exactly. It is recognition that the initial assessment was wrong.
