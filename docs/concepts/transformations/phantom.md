# Phantom
*Earned Permanent — Neutral to Evil — The Shadow Stuck*

## Overview
A Rogue who spends enough time in stealth, dealing enough damage from the dark, accumulates something the shadow does not give back. The Phantom transformation is not a curse visited from outside — it is the natural consequence of a person spending so much of their life in the spaces between visibility that the boundary between them and that space starts to dissolve.

The Phantom is still themselves. Their skills are intact, their class unchanged. What is different is that some portion of their physical substance has taken on a quality of the places they lived in — a partial incorporeality that functions as an advantage in combat and an unsettling quality in social situations. They are not fully here, in the way that a fully here person is fully here.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Neutral to Evil
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Level:** 20
- **Trigger:** Sustained stealth use combined with a high kill count from stealth; the shadow keeps part of the payment

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Partial Incorporeality** — Physical attacks have a small percentage chance to phase through the Phantom entirely (miss regardless of hit roll); not reliable but real
- **Shadow Absorption** — Shadow-school spells cast at the Phantom deal reduced damage; the void-quality in their body partially absorbs it
- **Fade** — Active ability: brief burst of incorporeality for 3 seconds; cannot attack but cannot be physically hit; higher mana/stamina cost than standard stealth; useful for escape

**Visual:**
- Edges of the character's form occasionally blur and trail in quick motion
- In shadows, the character's outline is slightly less defined than it should be
- In stealth, the stealth quality is more complete — not invisible, but closer to it

## Narrative Implications

The Phantom unsettles people in ways they can't easily explain. There is something slightly wrong about the visual, the way the person moves, the occasional moment where the shadow doesn't match. NPCs with high perception may comment on it directly. Others will simply be uncomfortable without knowing why.

Guild rogues who encounter a Phantom tend to treat them with respect and mild unease in equal measure — the transformation represents an extreme of the path they're on, and not everyone is comfortable with where that path leads.
