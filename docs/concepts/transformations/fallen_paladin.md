# Fallen Paladin
*Alignment Drift — Evil tier — The Faith Inverted*

## Overview
A Paladin who reaches Evil tier has not simply sinned. They have sinned enough, consistently enough, that their accumulated choices have outweighed everything they built in the other direction. The divine did not abandon them — they moved out of range. What fills the space is not nothing. Something else was watching.

The Fallen Paladin retains their martial training, their armor proficiency, their familiarity with the battlefield. What changes is the source of their power. The holy flame that sustained their spells is gone. In its place: shadow, death-adjacency, and the particular cold clarity that comes from having broken the most important promise you've ever made and discovering that you're still standing.

This is not a villain's transformation — or it doesn't have to be. Some Fallen Paladins are exactly what the name implies: people who gave in to cruelty or ambition. Others are people who made choices the faith couldn't sanction, and the alignment system is a blunt instrument that doesn't distinguish between them. Both get the same transformation. The story is theirs.

## Requirements
- **Class:** Paladin
- **Trigger:** Alignment score reaches Evil tier (≤ -1501)
- **Reversibility:** Reverses if alignment returns to Good or higher. See [Twice-Tempered](twice_tempered.md) for the permanent redemption arc.

## Effects

**Lost:**
- All holy spells (Smite, Holy Shield, Lay on Hands, Divine Radiance, and all Paladin-school holy abilities)
- Divine Blow and Holy Shield skills
- Undead-turning passive

**Gained:**
- **Shadow Flame** — Dark damage with a brief burning DoT
- **Death's Embrace** — Absorb shield drawn from shadow energy
- **Blood Price** — Heal self by spending 15 HP; scales with INT
- **Condemnation** — Mark a target; they take bonus shadow damage from all sources for duration
- **Profane Strike** — Melee skill: dark-aspected, +shadow damage on hit
- **Dark Ward** — Short-duration damage reduction from a shadow barrier

**Visual:**
- Holy glow is replaced by a faint dark aura
- Armor appears unchanged but its light-reflecting quality shifts to absorbing
- Smite animations replaced with shadow-tinted equivalents

## Narrative Implications

The faith organizations that produced the Paladin will react. Most will formally excommunicate the character and notify allied cities. The Fallen Paladin loses access to temple services, Paladin trainers, and the social credibility their original class conferred. They gain, in exchange, the grudging respect of Evil-aligned factions who take a certain satisfaction in the corruption of something holy.

The most interesting case is the Fallen Paladin who knows what happened and is trying to understand it. The [Burning Road](twice_tempered.md) exists for them — but it requires confronting what specifically went wrong, which is harder than it sounds.
