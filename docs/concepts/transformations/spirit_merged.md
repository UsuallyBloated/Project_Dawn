# Spirit Merged
*Earned Permanent — Any Alignment — The Staying*

## Overview
A Shaman's relationship with ancestor spirits is supposed to be transactional: you call, they answer, the conversation ends, they return to wherever they go. The Spirit Merged is a Shaman who called so often, held the connection so long, that the spirit on the other end of the bond decided the arrangement was more convenient than returning. They are still there. Not controlling, not possessing — present. A semi-visible overlay on the Shaman's form, a second voice in the decision-making that the Shaman can consult without ritual.

This is not always comfortable. The ancestor spirit has opinions. They express those opinions. They remember things the Shaman doesn't and forget things the Shaman considers important. But the power available to a Shaman who is never fully alone is substantially greater than the power available to one who has to call their advisors from scratch every time.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Any
- **Class:** Shaman
- **Level:** 28
- **Trigger:** Extended use of ancestor-spirit communion over lifetime; the bond deepens past the point of normal separation; no specific quest

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Spirit Echo** — Passive: each Shaman spell or ability has a small chance to trigger a second cast at reduced effectiveness; the spirit adds its contribution
- **Always Consulted** — The ancestor spirit's presence makes the Shaman immune to fear effects and significantly resistant to mind control; the spirit holds
- **Enhanced HoTs** — Healing-over-time spells from the Shaman are strengthened; the spirit directs additional restorative energy
- **Ancestral Memory** — Access to knowledge the ancestor holds; bonus to certain dialogue options and lore checks; some quest lines open

**Visual:**
- The ancestor spirit is semi-visible as a faint overlay on the character at all times — not dramatic, but present to those who look
- The spirit's visual form reflects the Shaman's race and history; it appears as a figure from the ancestral tradition
- In combat, the spirit becomes more defined briefly when the echo triggers

## Narrative Implications

Other Shamans can see the spirit and recognize what it means. Among Shaman communities this transformation carries significant weight — it represents either a profound deepening of the ancestral practice or a failure of proper ritual hygiene, depending on the tradition. Minotaur Shamans, whose practice is already about recovery and carrying, tend to regard it as an achievement. Some Ogre traditions find it unsettling.

Non-Shamans who notice the spirit will have varied reactions depending on their relationship to the supernatural.
