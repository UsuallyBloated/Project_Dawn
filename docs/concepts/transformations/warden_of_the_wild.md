# Warden of the Wild
*Earned Permanent — Exalted — The Forest's Trust*

## Overview
The Warden of the Wild is what the natural world offers to those who have spent a career in its service and reached a moral state it recognizes as genuinely trustworthy. Not the institutional approval of a temple — the forest doesn't have a temple. This is something older: the accumulated acknowledgment of a thousand small choices made in the right direction, in the right places, over a very long time.

A Warden does not control nature. They have earned a relationship with it that other people haven't. Animals are not pets; they are neighbors who have decided the Warden is safe. The trees don't fight for the Warden; they just don't interfere. Blight and poison in the Warden's presence are slightly less effective, the way an immune system is slightly less effective against a familiar pathogen — the world has learned what this person is.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Exalted (≥ 1500)
- **Classes:** Druid, Ranger
- **Level:** 25
- **Trigger:** Sustained Exalted alignment; healing threshold in outdoor/wilderness zones; no specific quest

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Animal Accord** — Non-hostile animals in the area will not attack the Warden unprovoked; animal mobs ignore the character unless attacked
- **Nature's Resilience** — Immune to nature-sourced damage (poison, disease, natural fire from forest fires; does not include spell-fire)
- **Commune** — Brief communication with an animal in range (intelligence/quest utility; not full language)
- **Wild Sense** — Passive: increased perception in outdoor/wilderness zones; hidden traps and hidden creatures detected at greater range
- **Warden's Mending** — Druid and Ranger healing spells gain a bonus in wilderness zones; the land assists

**Visual:**
- No dramatic transformation — the Warden looks like themselves but the forest behaves differently around them
- Flowers and plants at the character's footsteps in natural zones (subtle, not theatrical)
- Animals in the area orient toward the character rather than away

## Narrative Implications

The Warden is a quieter transformation than the Exalted (Paladin/Cleric version) — no glow, no dramatic visual marker. NPCs who know what to look for (Wood Elves, certain Druids, experienced Rangers) will recognize it. Others will notice that the forest seems unusually calm around this person without being able to say why.

The Warden's standing with Wood Elf communities is the highest any non-Wood Elf can achieve. Some Wood Elf elders who have been resistant to outside contact will engage openly with a confirmed Warden.
