# Fallen Cleric
*Alignment Drift — Evil tier — Healing Inverted*

## Overview
The Fallen Cleric is, in some ways, the most disturbing of the drift transformations. The Paladin fell from a martial tradition — the loss is power. The Cleric fell from a healing tradition — the loss is mercy. What replaces it is not simply damage. It is the specific inversion of care: abilities that drain rather than restore, that poison rather than purify, that raise the dead rather than preserve the living.

The Cleric's deep connection to their deity made them the most effective healer in the game. That same depth of connection, directed into darkness, makes the Fallen Cleric uniquely effective at the specific kind of cruelty that knows exactly what a body needs in order to do the opposite.

Most Clerics who reach Evil did so through deliberate faction choice — serving an evil organization, repeatedly harming people they were positioned to help, or casting repeatedly in the service of destructive ends. A few got here through grief. The alignment system doesn't ask how.

## Requirements
- **Class:** Cleric
- **Trigger:** Alignment score reaches Evil tier (≤ -1501)
- **Reversibility:** Reverses if alignment returns to Good or higher. Very difficult from this tier.

## Effects

**Lost:**
- All healing spells (Minor/Major Heal, Complete Heal, HoTs, Resurrection)
- Holy damage and turning abilities
- Divine Ward and all protection-school abilities

**Gained:**
- **Dark Mending** — Heals self (only) via necrotic drain from current target; cannot heal others
- **Curse of Weakness** — DoT that reduces target's damage output while ticking
- **Pestilence** — Disease-type DoT; stacks with other DoTs
- **Raise Servant** — On killing a humanoid enemy, briefly animate their corpse as a temporary (60s) minion; cannot control, it simply attacks nearest enemy
- **Desecrate** — AoE ground effect that slows and weakens enemies within it; visual: necrotic ground

**Visual:**
- Robes shift to darker coloring, not dramatically — more like the color has been drawn out
- Holy symbol dims or cracks visually
- Healing animations replaced with draining particle effects

## Narrative Implications

The Fallen Cleric's deity has not necessarily abandoned them — some deity relationships survive this, expressed as silence rather than withdrawal. Others sever completely. The character may retain or lose their connection to a specific divine narrative depending on which deity they served and how that deity regards apostasy.

Temple communities react with horror rather than the complicated respect that Evil factions show a Fallen Paladin. The Cleric was the healer. They were the person people trusted with their lives in the worst moments. The fall is personal to everyone who knew them.
