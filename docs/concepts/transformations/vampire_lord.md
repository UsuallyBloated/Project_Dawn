# Vampire Lord
*Earned Permanent — Evil — The Long Night, Stage Three*

## Overview
The Vampire Lord is what the [Hunger](the_hunger.md) was pointing toward. The infection fully taken, the transformation complete — the character is no longer living in the ordinary sense. They are something old and dangerous wearing a familiar face: all of their skills and knowledge intact, now powered by a body that feeds on the living and does not die easily.

This is not a costume. The Vampire Lord has sunlight vulnerability that makes outdoor daytime zones genuinely hazardous. The feeding requirement intensifies. Social access to Good-aligned cities is essentially closed. What opens instead is a range of vampire-specific abilities, interactions, and questlines that do not exist for any other transformation — an entirely distinct layer of the game, accessible only from here.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Evil (≤ -1501)
- **Classes:** Blood Mage, Shadow Knight
- **Level:** 25
- **Prerequisites:** Must have passed through [The Hunger](the_hunger.md) and chosen to embrace at the Reckoning

## The Long Night Arc (Path to This Transformation)
1. **The Hunger** (level 18) — the infection takes hold passively
2. **The Reckoning** — encounter with the NPC who recognizes the condition; choose to embrace or resist
3. **The Rite of Consumption** — a quest chain requiring the character to feed on a specific type of prey, survive a sunlit encounter in a way that demonstrates commitment, and acquire an item of vampiric focus (a fang, a black chalice, depending on questline version)
4. **The Vampire Lord transformation** — fires at quest completion

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Lifedrain** — Passive on all auto-attacks; small percentage of damage dealt is returned as HP
- **Charm** — Dominate a humanoid NPC temporarily (not a player); they follow and serve briefly. Usable once per hour.
- **Mist Form** — Brief incorporeal state; cannot attack or be attacked; useful for repositioning or escaping
- **Blood Frenzy** — At low HP, damage output increases significantly; the hunger empowers the desperate
- **Ancient Resilience** — High resistance to physical damage; the undead body does not break easily

**Sunlight vulnerability:**
- Outdoor zones in daytime deal continuous damage (moderate, not immediately lethal; meaningful pressure)
- Daytime outdoor combat is viable but costly
- Indoor zones and nighttime are fully functional; some abilities are stronger at night

**Thirst mechanic (intensified):**
- Must feed more frequently than at Hunger stage
- Going without feeding too long produces severe debuffs and eventually a compelled-feeding state

**Visual:**
- The transformation is visible — pallor, still eyes, an unmistakable quality of being something that was once alive
- In darkness, eyes reflect light in a distinct red-orange
- Fang teeth become prominent

## Social Effects
- Good-aligned city access: closed; guards attack on sight or near-sight
- Neutral city access: heavily restricted; some cities tolerate the character if they don't feed openly
- Evil faction cities: full access; certain vampire-specific NPCs and questlines open
- Vampire Lord status is legible to experienced NPCs (Witch Hunters, certain Clerics) at a distance

## Notes
- **Reversibility:** Not reversible through normal play. The Reckoning's resist option is the only exit from the arc; once Vampire Lord is reached, no reversal exists.
- Vampire Lord and Revenant are mutually exclusive — both occupy the undead-body transformation space.
