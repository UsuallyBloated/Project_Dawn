# The Hunger
*Earned Permanent — Evil — Stage One of the Long Night*

## Overview
The Hunger is not the destination. It is the door before the door — the point at which a character's accumulated use of life-drain magic has left a mark that doesn't close. The vampiric infection, in whatever form it takes in this world, has found purchase. The character is not yet a Vampire Lord. They are something in between: still mostly what they were, but aware of a new and persistent need, and noticing that sunlight has become uncomfortable.

Most characters who reach The Hunger will progress to [Vampire Lord](vampire_lord.md). Some stop here — either by choice (the Reckoning quest offers an exit) or by circumstances. Living at The Hunger stage is possible but uncomfortable. The thirst mechanic demands feeding; ignoring it produces escalating stat debuffs.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Evil (≤ -1501)
- **Classes:** Any, but Blood Mage and Shadow Knight accumulate the trigger threshold fastest
- **Level:** 18
- **Trigger:** Accumulated lifedrain spell casts above a threshold combined with Evil alignment; the threshold is crossed quietly — no announcement

## Effects

**Gained:**
- Minor lifesteal on all attacks (small percentage; noticeable over time)
- **Thirst mechanic:** Must feed (drain HP from an enemy) periodically or suffer escalating STR/AGI/INT debuffs
- Reduced sunlight tolerance — bright outdoor zones cause discomfort (visual effect, very minor damage; not disabling)
- NPCs in cities begin reacting with suspicion; some notice something is wrong before they can articulate it

**Retained from pre-transformation:**
- All class abilities unchanged
- No mechanical losses yet — this is the infection taking hold, not the full change

**Visual:**
- Eyes occasionally catch light in an unusual way in darkness
- Skin takes on a very faint pallor — only noticeable up close
- Expression in idle animations shifts slightly; something watchful

## The Reckoning (Exit Point)
At stage two of [The Long Night](vampire_lord.md) arc, the character encounters a specific NPC (a Witch Hunter, a scholar of vampirism, or a priest depending on zone) who recognizes what's happening and offers a choice:

**Embrace:** Continue to [Vampire Lord](vampire_lord.md). The Hunger is a step toward something worse and more powerful.

**Resist:** A quest chain to purge the infection — expensive, difficult, and requiring items only available in specific locations. Success cures The Hunger completely and unlocks a Witch Hunter faction questline as reward.

If neither choice is made, the thirst mechanic continues and The Hunger persists indefinitely. It will not progress to Vampire Lord without the character choosing to embrace.

## Notes
- This transformation is designed to feel like a warning and an invitation simultaneously
- Characters who don't notice it for a while (possible if they're in combat-heavy play) may have an interesting Reckoning moment when they finally do
