# Elemental Fusion
*Earned Permanent — Neutral — They Came Without Being Called*

## Overview
Magicians and Sorcerers summon elementals. That is the relationship: the caster calls, the elemental arrives, the caster directs, the elemental is dismissed. The Elemental Fusion transformation is what happens when that cycle runs long enough, in enough volume, that the boundary dissolves. The elemental didn't leave the last time. Or rather — it left, but some of it stayed. And then the next summoning left a little more. And eventually the caster is carrying a quantity of elemental essence that is functionally structural.

The Fusion is specific to one school — whatever elemental type the caster has used most over their career. Fire fusion, ice fusion, or lightning fusion are the most common paths. Each produces a distinct set of changes and cannot be combined.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Neutral (-299 to 299)
- **Classes:** Magician, Sorcerer
- **Level:** 26
- **Trigger:** Summoning and dismissing elemental pets in very high lifetime volume; the boundary dissolves at a threshold determined by the most-summoned school

## Effects — Fire Fusion
- **Fire Resistance** — immune to fire damage
- **Flame Aura** — passive: melee attackers take brief fire damage on hit
- **Intensified Fire** — all fire-school spells deal +20% damage
- **Visual:** Eyes glow amber; skin has faint heat-shimmer; fire effects burn brighter and longer

## Effects — Ice Fusion
- **Cold Resistance** — immune to cold damage
- **Chilling Presence** — passive: enemies in melee range accumulate a chill stack reducing movement speed
- **Intensified Ice** — all ice-school spells deal +20% damage
- **Visual:** Breath is always visible; fingertips have frost at the edges; ice spell effects are deeper blue-white

## Effects — Lightning Fusion
- **Lightning Resistance** — immune to lightning damage
- **Static Field** — passive: occasionally discharges a brief arc to the nearest enemy (very short range)
- **Intensified Lightning** — all lightning-school spells deal +20% damage
- **Visual:** Eyes occasionally flicker with static; hair moves as if in mild electric field; lightning effects have a sharper, whiter character

## Mutual Effects (All Fusion Types)
- The element in question no longer counts as external — the caster is part of it, and it is part of them
- The aligned elemental pet summon is stronger and arrives faster (the element already inside answers the call)
- Other elemental types are unaffected

## Narrative Implications

Elemental Fusion is the transformation that arcane scholars write papers about, because it's the most mechanically legible. Unlike the Void-Touched (which involves something from outside known categories), Elemental Fusion is the result of a quantifiable process applied to documented elemental theory. Gnomish Academy theorists have attempted to induce it deliberately. The results have been approximately forty percent successful and sixty percent catastrophic.

Sorcerers who fuse tend to consider it an actualization — the bloodline power they always carried meeting the element they've always worked with. Magicians who fuse tend to consider it an experimental result, and begin immediately trying to understand the mechanism.
