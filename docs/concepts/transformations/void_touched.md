# Void-Touched
*Earned Permanent — Neutral to Evil — The Door Left Open*

## Overview
The arcane magic that Wizards, Sorcerers, and Magicians work with does not originate in the natural world or the divine. It comes from somewhere else — a place that academic tradition calls the Void, the Beyond, the Unstructured. Most casters draw from it carefully: a controlled channel, a defined opening, a contained transaction. The Void-Touched caster is someone who opened that channel enough times that it stopped closing fully.

There is no dramatic moment of transformation. The caster notices, eventually, that something feels different. The spells flow more easily. The dreams are unusual. The eyes have changed. Something passed through the aperture in both directions, and what came back was most of them but not all of them, and what came through with them is now resident.

This is not possession. The Void-Touched caster retains full agency. The resident something is not a personality — it is a quality, like a dye that has permeated the cloth. It makes the arcane more accessible and makes some other things noticeably harder to reach.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Neutral to Evil (-299 to -1501+)
- **Classes:** Wizard, Sorcerer, Magician
- **Level:** 22
- **Trigger:** Volume threshold of arcane spells cast over lifetime, without significant holy or nature magic to counterbalance; the Void notices sustained traffic and leans in

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Arcane Damage +15%** — All arcane-school spells deal increased damage
- **Void Resonance** — Passive: small chance on any arcane spell to trigger a secondary uncontrolled discharge (additional damage to target, or brief AoE; unpredictable but beneficial)
- **Resist Arcane** — Significant resistance to arcane-school damage
- **Void Step** — Passive: small chance to partially phase through a melee attack (not a guaranteed evade; more like a flicker)

**Complications:**
- **Occasional uncontrolled discharge** — as above; the secondary effect can sometimes affect allies if poorly positioned
- **Holy and nature magic less effective when received** — healing from holy sources is slightly less potent on the Void-Touched character (the void-quality interferes with clean divine connection)

**Visual:**
- Eyes shift to a deep starfield-black — pupils expand until no iris is visible; in darkness the eyes faintly shimmer
- Arcane spell effects have a slightly different character — a deeper color, edges that blur oddly
- In bright light the character's shadow is occasionally a half-second slow

## Narrative Implications

Academically-oriented NPCs (Elven scholars, Gnome arcane theorists) find the Void-Touched fascinating and slightly alarming. The transformation represents either a theoretical achievement (true arcane saturation) or a catastrophic research accident, depending on who you ask.

Witch Hunters view it with professional concern — this is adjacent to what they hunt, and the distinction between "harmlessly marked" and "actively dangerous" is a matter of ongoing debate in their order.

The Void-Touched caster is typically uninterested in this discourse. The spells work better. That's most of what they were here for.
