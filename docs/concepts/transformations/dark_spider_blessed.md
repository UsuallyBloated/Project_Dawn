# Dark Spider-Blessed
*Race-Specific — Dark Elf — Evil — The Goddess Looked*

## Overview
The spider-goddess that Dark Elf temples serve is not theoretical. She has favorites. The Dark Spider-Blessed is one of them — a Dark Elf who has served the temple with enough distinction, aligned with enough precision to the goddess's particular aesthetic (power acquired, power applied, power maintained through cunning rather than force), that the goddess's attention has settled on them as a deliberate investment.

This is not an honor freely given. The temple's recognition of the Blessed involves a political process that the Blessed has probably been navigating for some time. The goddess's direct mark — the web-affinity changes, the silk ability, the climbing — comes separately from the temple's approval, and the two do not always arrive together.

## Requirements
- **Race:** Dark Elf
- **Alignment:** Evil (≤ -1501)
- **Classes:** Enchanter, Shadow Knight, Blood Mage
- **Level:** 22
- **Trigger:** Sustained distinguished service to the Dark Elf temple hierarchy, combined with the right class and Evil alignment; the temple formally recognizes the character; the goddess confirms with a mark

## Effects

**Gained:**
- **Web Sense** — Passive: detect invisible and hidden creatures at greater range; the spider's awareness of vibration translates
- **Silk Snare** — Active ability: fire a silk-strand root that holds a target briefly; physical root effect, not magic
- **Surface Climb** — Can traverse vertical surfaces at normal movement speed; negligible in most combat but significant for positioning and exploration
- **Temple Authority** — Social standing in Vel'Sharath and Dark Elf settlements dramatically elevated; prices improve, dialogue options expand, certain quests become available
- **Arachnid Resilience** — Passive: resistance to poison

**Visual:**
- Spider-silk fine lines appear at the wrists and neck — not dramatic, but visible to the knowing eye
- Eyes take on a faint multi-faceted quality in certain light
- Movement has a subtle deliberate quality — the Blessed walks like something that is always in the right position

## Narrative Implications

Within Dark Elf society, the Blessed is elevated and watched simultaneously. Elevated because the goddess's mark is real social capital. Watched because anything the goddess has marked becomes a political variable, and in Dark Elf politics nothing with variable value goes unmonitored.

Other races react with the specific discomfort of encountering a spider-linked entity, which is a deeply instinctive response that does not require cultural context.
