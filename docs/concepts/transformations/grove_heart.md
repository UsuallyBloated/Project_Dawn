# Grove Heart
*Earned Permanent — Exalted — The Other Half of the Bargain*

## Overview
Druids speak of the forest as a living entity with its own slow awareness. Most speak of it metaphorically. The Grove Heart transformation is what happens when it turns out to be literally true, and the forest has been paying attention long enough to offer something back.

The Druid who reaches Grove Heart has healed enough, protected enough, cared for enough of the living world at Exalted alignment that the forest's awareness has recognized them as kin — not as an outsider who tends the garden, but as part of the garden. The transformation is not dramatic. There is no moment of thunder. The Druid simply notices, during a quiet moment in a forest they've walked through a hundred times, that the forest is slightly more present than it was. And then more present. And then it has always been present like this, and they are inside that presence rather than outside it.

## Requirements
- **Alignment:** Exalted (≥ 1500), sustained
- **Class:** Druid
- **Level:** 30
- **Trigger:** Healing a total threshold of HP in outdoor/natural zones; communing with natural sites; Exalted alignment maintained. No specific quest — the forest decides when conditions are met.

## Effects

**In Natural Zones (outdoor, wilderness, forest terrain):**
- **Living Regen** — Passive HP regeneration even in combat; the land contributes to survival
- **Animal Calm** — Non-hostile animals are non-hostile; animal mobs do not aggro the Grove Heart unless attacked
- **Blight Resistance** — Blight, poison, and disease effects in the zone are weakened by the Grove Heart's presence; applies to nearby allies at reduced strength
- **Nature's Mending** — Druid healing spells receive a significant bonus in natural zones; the land assists

**Always Active:**
- **Woodland Passage** — Movement through dense forest terrain is unimpeded (no movement penalty from forest terrain type)
- **Root Sense** — Detect living and undead creatures through connected root systems; works within a radius in natural zones

**Visual:**
- Flowers bloom briefly at footsteps in natural zones (subtle, not theatrical)
- The character's appearance shifts over time: a slight bark texture at the skin's edges, visible only in certain light; moss and small growing things at the collar and shoulders
- Animals in the area orient toward the Grove Heart rather than away

## Narrative Implications

Wood Elf communities respond to a Grove Heart with the closest thing to full acceptance they extend to outsiders. It is the only transformation that produces this specific reaction — because the forest speaking for someone is more legible to Wood Elves than any institutional credential.

The Grove Heart and the [Warden of the Wild](warden_of_the_wild.md) are related but distinct. The Warden is recognized by the forest. The Grove Heart is partly in the forest. The distinction matters to NPCs who understand the difference, and it matters to the character's experience of the world.
