# Redeemed Shadow Knight
*Alignment Drift — Exalted tier — The Penitent Blade*

## Overview
A Shadow Knight reaching Exalted tier has done something that most people — including most Shadow Knights — would have said was impossible. They started at -1600. They climbed more than three thousand alignment points through a path of deliberate, sustained good action, while maintaining a class whose every spell and skill was built for evil. Every holy deed cost them efficiency. Every good act went against the grain of their entire power structure. They did it anyway.

The transformation that results is not the same as a Paladin. The Redeemed Shadow Knight carries the scar of what they were. Their holy abilities are not given freely — they are earned through guilt made productive. The aesthetic is penitent, not triumphant. They are not a Paladin who was always going to be this. They are a Shadow Knight who fought themselves for long enough that something changed.

This is the rarest of all the drift transformations to see in practice, and the most narratively resonant when it happens.

## Requirements
- **Class:** Shadow Knight
- **Trigger:** Alignment score reaches Exalted tier (≥ 1500)
- **Reversibility:** Reverses if alignment drops below Good. The climb back is long.

## Effects

**Lost:**
- All dark spells (Shadow Bolt, Harm Touch, Fear, and all Shadow Knight dark-school abilities)
- Dark Strike and Harm Touch skills
- Lifetap passive

**Gained:**
- **Radiant Bolt** — Holy damage ranged attack
- **Holy Mantle** — Damage absorb shield drawn from holy energy
- **Sacrificial Mend** — Heal an ally by spending 15 HP; scales with WIS
- **Redeemed Strike** — Melee skill: holy-aspected, undead take bonus damage
- **Penitent's Touch** — Brief touch-range holy damage + minor self-heal; the only ability that references the old power

**Visual:**
- Dark aura becomes a faint gold-white
- Armor takes on a worn, slightly damaged quality — not broken, just used hard for a long time
- The character's bearing changes; something in the posture is different

## Narrative Implications

The Redeemed Shadow Knight is not welcomed by the faith community the way a Paladin is. They didn't come from within. They came from the outside and fought their way here, and the institutions that produce Paladins from birth don't entirely trust the route. Some temples will receive them with complicated grace. Others maintain their doors are closed on principle.

Evil-aligned factions treat them with the specific contempt reserved for traitors. Where a Fallen Paladin earns a certain satisfaction from their former allies, the Redeemed Shadow Knight earns nothing from theirs.

What they earn instead is the specific standing of someone who changed completely, against all reasonable expectation, and is still here. That is not nothing.
