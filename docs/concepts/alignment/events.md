# Alignment Events
*What actions change alignment, and by how much.*

The alignment score runs from -2000 (most Evil) to +2000 (most Exalted). Actions add or subtract from the running total. Tier boundaries are crossed when the score crosses ±300, ±1500.

**Momentum rule:** the further a character is from center, the smaller the per-action impact in that direction. Moving from Neutral toward Evil is faster than moving from Bad toward Evil. The extremes resist further movement — reaching and staying at ±2000 is deliberate work.

---

## Combat Kills

The alignment of the killed target determines the delta. Killing Good characters pulls score down; killing Evil characters pushes score up. Killing Neutral characters has no alignment impact.

| Target Alignment Tier | Delta on Kill | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Exalted | −80 | Killing a recognized holy figure; significant negative event |
| Good | −30 | Standard Good-aligned NPC, player, or creature |
| Neutral | 0 | No alignment change |
| Bad | +10 | Marginal positive; Bad-tier isn't unambiguously Evil |
| Evil | +25 | Standard positive kill |
| Evil (named/boss) | +50 | Named Evil enemies carry more weight |
| Undead (Evil-aligned) | +20 | Sanctioned destruction; slightly less than living Evil |
| Summoned creature (Evil) | +15 | The creature itself is less culpable than the summoner |
| Innocent (non-combatant) | −60 | NPC not engaged in combat, not flagged as threat |

**PvP kills:** Use the killed player's alignment tier, same as NPC table. Killing Evil-aligned players gives +25. Killing Good-aligned players gives −30. This creates meaningful PvP consequences without locking PvP out.

## Spell Casting (Per Cast)

Not every spell shifts alignment — only schools with an inherent moral weight. Healing and holy magic accumulate slowly positive; shadow and necromantic accumulate slowly negative.

| Spell Type | Delta per Cast | Applies To |
|---|---|---|
| Holy damage (offensive) | +1 | Paladin, Cleric, Shadow Knight (Redeemed) |
| Healing (target: ally or self) | +1 | Cleric, Paladin, Druid, Shaman, Blood Mage, and others |
| Shadow damage | −1 | Shadow Knight, Blood Mage, Necromancer, Witch Hunter |
| Necromantic (summon, animate) | −2 | Necromancer |
| Blood drain (HP → mana/damage) | −1 | Blood Mage |
| Fear effect | −1 | Necromancer |
| Charm (long duration) | −1 | Enchanter, Bard |
| Banishment (vs. undead/demons) | +2 | Witch Hunter, Cleric |

These are small, but they accumulate over a session. A Necromancer spending an hour in combat shifts meaningfully without a single dramatic choice.

## Quest Completion

Quest reward alignment deltas are set per-quest. Typical ranges:

| Quest Category | Delta Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Quest for Good-aligned faction | +20 to +100 | Scales with quest significance |
| Quest for Evil-aligned faction | −20 to −100 | Scales with quest significance |
| Neutral faction quest | 0 to ±10 | May have small secondary moral choices embedded |
| Deliver item to Good temple | +15 | |
| Deliver item to Evil faction | −15 | |
| Turn in Evil artifact for destruction | +30 | |
| Possess an Evil artifact (equip) | −10 | One-time on first equip |
| Complete a redemption quest arc | +150 to +500 | Specific quest chains; designed as alignment-shift content |
| Complete a corruption quest arc | −150 to −500 | Specific quest chains; designed as Evil-path content |

## Decay

Alignment does not naturally decay back toward center over time. The record is permanent unless actively changed through subsequent actions. A player who spent six months becoming Evil cannot sit idle and gradually become Neutral — they have to choose to act differently.

This is intentional. The alignment record reflects what was done, not what might be done next.
