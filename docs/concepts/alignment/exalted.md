# Exalted
*Score ≥ 1500 — Touched by the divine — The world bends*

## Overview

Exalted is not a tier most characters reach. It requires sustained, deliberate goodness over a very long time — not a single heroic act, but a pattern of choices that the world accumulates into something it can no longer ignore. The divine (whichever gods or forces the character's class answers to) has noticed. The world reflects that noticing.

An Exalted character does not announce themselves. They don't need to. Something in their bearing, their light, the way undead flinch at their approach — all of it communicates what they are before they speak. NPCs who are theologically sophisticated recognize it immediately. NPCs who are not recognize it as something, even if they can't name it.

The tier is demanding to reach and easy to lose. A sustained pattern of Evil acts will drop a character from Exalted to Good faster than it took them to ascend. The math is asymmetric by design — climbing is slow, falling is fast.

## Score Range

**1500 to 2000.** The cap is 2000 but functionally unreachable through normal play — sustained Exalted alignment is the practical ceiling.

## Classes That Start Here

None. No class starts at Exalted. It is always earned.

## Races That Tend Here

No race is locked to Exalted. The races who most commonly reach it through their class choices are those playing Paladin and Cleric — the classes with the strongest upward alignment pull. Elf and Halfling players reaching Exalted is considered unremarkable by their communities. An Ogre or Troll reaching it is considered a miracle by everyone, including the Ogre.

## NPC & World Reactions

**Good-aligned settlements:** Merchants offer discounts. Guards lower weapons. Temple clergy engage immediately and with reverence. Some NPCs will approach the player unprompted to ask for help or blessing.

**Neutral settlements:** Recognizable deference. The rare vendor will refuse service on principle (some people distrust the conspicuously holy) but this is the exception. Most NPCs are respectful.

**Evil-aligned settlements:** Hostile. Exalted characters cannot enter most Evil faction cities without being attacked. Dark Elf cities, Ogre tribal holds, and certain undead-controlled zones are effectively locked.

**Undead enemies:** Passive proximity effect — undead within a radius take continuous minor holy damage from the character's presence. Weak undead may flee rather than engage.

## Mechanical Effects

- Paladin and Cleric spells function at **full effectiveness**
- Holy damage bonus applies where relevant
- Undead proximity aura (see above)
- Certain quests and NPCs only available at Exalted tier
- Transformation triggers become accessible (see below)

## Narrative Implications

Exalted characters are the world's saints in the making. The faith organizations that produce Paladins and Clerics monitor alignment carefully — an Exalted Cleric is a candidate for canonization in some traditions, or a pilgrimage destination in others. This brings attention that is not always welcome. Not every faction wants a living symbol of righteousness walking their territory.

For non-holy classes reaching Exalted — a Warrior, a Ranger, a Bard — the reaction is different and in some ways more interesting. These characters carry no institutional backing. They are simply people who chose Good repeatedly until something changed. Some faiths try to recruit them. Others distrust goodness that operates outside their framework.
