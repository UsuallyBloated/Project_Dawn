# Cleric
*Primary Healer / Support — Any Alignment — Deity*

## Overview
The healer in the classical sense. Regardless of the deity served — good, neutral, or shadow-aligned — the Cleric's role is to keep allies alive. Their offensive spells are respectable but secondary to their healing function. The restriction to blunt weapons is theological, not physical: bladed weapons draw blood, and the Cleric's covenant prohibits it.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Chain (plate access at higher levels)
- **Weapons:** Blunt weapons only — maces, hammers, staves, warhammers; shields
- **Primary Resource:** Mana primary, stamina for Holy Strike
- **Pet:** None

| Greater Heal | Self-heal | +120 HP | 55 | 2.5s |

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Ogre, Troll, Minotaur, Vah Shir, Kobold, Half-Ogre

**Blocked:** Fae (follow no mortal gods); Iksar (follow necromantic tradition rather than clerical orders).

## Playstyle
In solo play, alternate Smite and Holy Strike while using Healing Light when HP drops. Greater Heal covers emergency or high-damage situations. In group play, mana conservation is the primary discipline — hold Healing Light and Greater Heal for genuine need; only use Smite and Divine Wrath when healing demand is low. Greater Heal's 2.5s cast requires anticipation — cast it before the target is already at critical HP.
