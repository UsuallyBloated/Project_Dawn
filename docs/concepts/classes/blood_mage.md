# Blood Mage
*Dark Caster — Neutral to Evil — Life force*

## Overview
Suffering as fuel. The Blood Mage converts life force — enemy and self — into spell power. Their sustain comes from Life Drain (damaging an enemy to heal themselves) rather than from any external healer. Hemorrhage is their signature finisher: the highest shadow damage value of any single spell in the game, at a significant mana cost and a 2.0s cast window. Unlike the Necromancer, the Blood Mage has no pet — they are self-contained, fueled by whatever living things are available.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Cloth/light leather
- **Weapons:** Ritual blades (athames), staves
- **Primary Resource:** Mana; some mechanics interact with HP as a secondary resource
- **Pet:** None

## Skills
None — pure spellcaster.

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Dark Elf, Half-Elf, Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Kobold, Half-Ogre

**Blocked:** All Good-aligned races (Elf, Halfling, Dwarf — the practice is incompatible with their convictions); Gnome (find life-force mechanics philosophically incoherent, not morally objectionable — they simply cannot take the practice seriously enough to master it); Fae (biology incompatible with the magic); Minotaur, Felhari, Wood Elf.

## Playstyle
Open with Crimson Bolt for fast pressure; weave Life Drain throughout the fight for sustained self-healing; Blood Bolt for consistent mid-tier damage; hold Hemorrhage for the finishing burst when the target is below half HP. The Blood Mage self-sustains better than any other cloth caster — Life Drain offsets incoming damage across long encounters. Their weakness is opener burst: Hemorrhage requires a 2.0s cast time, so they cannot spike a target down quickly and are vulnerable to being interrupted during that window.

## Spell Unlock Schedule

Spells are purchased from Blood Mage guild vendors in neutral or evil-aligned cities, or extracted from ritual texts recovered in the field. Most spells in this list are not sold openly — the vendor needs to know who they're dealing with.
