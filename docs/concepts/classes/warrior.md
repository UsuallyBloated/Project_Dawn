# Warrior
*Melee Tank — Any Alignment — Martial training*

## Overview
The oldest discipline. Warriors have no magic, no divine favor, and no spirit bond — only training, endurance, and the capacity to absorb punishment that would break any other class. They are the backbone of any group: capable of drawing enemy attention and surviving it indefinitely. Their strength comes from nothing but practice and will.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Plate — heaviest available
- **Weapons:** All melee weapons and shields; two-handers for solo, sword-and-board for tanking
- **Primary Resource:** Stamina only — no mana
- **Pet:** None

## Spells
None.

## Skills

| Skill | Dmg Multiplier | Stamina | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slash | 1.5x | 8 | 3s |
| Shield Bash | 1.2x | 15 | 8s |
| Pummel | 1.0x | 12 | 5s |

## Race Availability
**Available:** All races.

No cultural, physiological, or alignment barrier prevents any race from pursuing martial discipline.

## Playstyle
Slash drives consistent damage at low stamina cost. Pummel provides an interrupt. Shield Bash creates knockback for positional control. No mana means no downtime waiting for regeneration — pace is set entirely by stamina recovery. In group play, the Warrior keeps enemies facing away from allies; in solo play, survival requires managing skill cooldowns to outpace incoming damage.
