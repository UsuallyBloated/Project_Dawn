# Monk
*Unarmed Martial — Any Alignment — Inner discipline*

## Overview
The body is the weapon. The Monk's power comes from inner discipline, and metal armor disrupts the energy flow that training cultivates — they cannot wear it and retain their abilities. Their damage scales with mastery rather than equipment, making them unusually gear-independent. The combo Tiger Claw → Roundhouse → Flying Kick is the core rotation, each skill escalating in stamina cost, cooldown, and multiplier.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Cloth or light wraps only — no metal
- **Weapons:** Fists, hand wraps; some staves and short blunt weapons
- **Primary Resource:** Stamina
- **Pet:** None

## Spells
None.

## Skills

| Skill | Dmg Multiplier | Stamina | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tiger Claw | 1.2x | 10 | 3s |
| Roundhouse | 1.6x | 15 | 6s |
| Flying Kick | 2.2x | 20 | 10s |

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Elf, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Kel`varath, Felhari

**Blocked:** Ogre, Troll, Minotaur, Half-Ogre (mass and temperament incompatible with the training); Fae (body cannot meet the physical demands); Dark Elf (the arrogance the training cannot overcome); Gnome (the body will not cooperate with what the mind attempts).

## Playstyle
Open with Tiger Claw (lowest cost, fastest cooldown), follow with Roundhouse when it becomes available, hold Flying Kick for burst moments or finishers. No mana, no heals, no defensive spells — the Monk survives by dealing damage faster than the enemy. They are fragile in situations where they cannot attack continuously, and exceptional when they can stay in melee range and cycle the full rotation.
