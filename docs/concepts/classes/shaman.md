# Shaman
*Spirit Caster / Buffer-Debuffer — Any Alignment — Ancestor spirits*

## Overview
The Shaman speaks with the dead of their lineage and draws on their accumulated knowledge and strength. This is not universal religion — it requires an active, living tradition of ancestor-veneration that only specific cultures maintain. Their damage is spirit-typed, their healing leans toward HoT, and their Primal Strike gives more melee viability than a pure caster. They are not the best at any single role; they are the most consistent across all of them.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Chain/leather
- **Weapons:** Clubs, primitive weapons, spears, staves
- **Primary Resource:** Both mana and stamina
- **Pet:** None (spirit communication, not spirit control)

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Minotaur, Vah Shir, Kobold, Half-Ogre

**Blocked:** All races without active ancestor-spirit traditions. Gnome ancestor spirits actively resist the summons. Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, and Fae have incompatible spiritual frameworks.

## Playstyle
Spirit Bolt for quick instant-cast pressure; Primal Strike for melee-integrated DPS when stamina permits; Ancestral Strike for burst when mana is the free resource; Mending for emergency self-heals; Healing Wave for sustained recovery that continues regenerating for 18s after the cast. The Shaman rotation is flexible — lean on Primal Strike when mana is low, go pure-caster when stamina is spent.
