# Ranger
*Archer / Nature Hybrid — Any Alignment — Skill + nature*

## Overview
The best ranged fighter in the game. True Shot provides a ranged burst option no other class matches at distance. Their nature spells are minimal — enough to mark targets and self-heal, not enough to qualify as a caster. Double Strike reflects two-weapon melee competency when forced close. The Ranger's strength is range control: hitting hard from a distance the enemy cannot easily close.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Leather/chain
- **Weapons:** Bows (primary), swords and axes (melee fallback), quivers
- **Primary Resource:** Both mana and stamina
- **Pet:** None

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Elf, Wood Elf, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Minotaur, Fae, Vah Shir

**Blocked:** Dark Elf (no tradition of open-field nature hunting); Ogre, Troll, Half-Ogre (too large and loud for ranged precision work); Iksar (close-combat martial tradition); Gnome (insufficient physical frame for effective bow draw); Kobold.

## Playstyle
Open at range with Hunter's Mark, follow with True Shot for the burst, maintain distance while auto-attacking. Nature's Cure covers self-healing during kiting. Double Strike handles close-combat when the enemy reaches melee range. The Ranger's primary challenge is maintaining range — they are significantly weaker once an enemy is on top of them.
