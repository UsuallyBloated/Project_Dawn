# Magician
*Arcane DPS (Generalist) — Any Alignment — Study (generalist)*

## Overview
The most accessible arcane class. The Magician draws on a broad pool of learned spells across multiple damage types — arcane, frost, fire, lightning — without specializing deeply in any of them. Their Heal spell gives more self-sufficiency than any other pure caster. Design target: elemental pet summoning (air, water, fire, earth elementals as combat companions or utility tools) — not yet implemented.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Cloth
- **Weapons:** Staves, wands
- **Primary Resource:** Mana
- **Pet:** Elemental constructs (design target — not yet implemented)

## Spells

| Spell | Type | Damage/Heal | Mana | Cast |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arcane Missile | Arcane dmg | 20 | 15 | Instant |
| Frost Bolt | Ice dmg | 30 | 20 | Instant |
| Fireball | Fire dmg | 50 | 30 | 1.5s |
| Lightning Strike | Lightning dmg | 80 | 40 | 2.0s |
| Heal | Self-heal | +40 HP | 25 | 1.0s |

## Skills
None — pure spellcaster.

## Race Availability
**Available:** All races except Ogre.

**Blocked:** Ogre — INT floor too low for arcane study.

## Playstyle
Arcane Missile and Frost Bolt for instant-cast pressure; Fireball for mid-tier invested damage; Lightning Strike as the burst finisher. Heal provides self-sustain when HP drops — no other pure caster has this option. Mana efficiency is the only constraint: spend instants freely, hold Lightning Strike for decisive moments. The Magician's generalist pool means they adapt to any target type but never have the highest output against any specific one.
