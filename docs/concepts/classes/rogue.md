# Rogue
*Stealth / Burst DPS — Any Alignment — Skill*

## Overview
No magic. No divine favor. Just positioning, timing, and the right blade in the right place. The Rogue's ceiling is defined entirely by their ability to get behind the target and stay there. Backstab is the signature: a 3.0x multiplier that requires rear positioning — from the front, it is just Slash. Evade buys a window to reposition or escape when things go wrong.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Leather
- **Weapons:** Daggers, short swords, hand crossbows — nothing two-handed
- **Primary Resource:** Stamina
- **Pet:** None

## Spells
None.

## Skills

| Skill | Dmg Multiplier | Stamina | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slash | 1.5x | 8 | 3s |
| Backstab | 3.0x | 20 | 6s |
| Evade | — (self) | 10 | 12s |

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Kel`varath, Fae, Felhari, Kobold

**Blocked:** Ogre, Troll, Minotaur, Half-Ogre — size and gait incompatible with the precision and stealth the discipline requires.

## Playstyle
Maintain rear positioning at all times — Backstab at 3.0x is the entire damage model. Losing position cuts output nearly in half; the correct response is Evade to create space and reposition, not to fight from the front. Slash fills gaps between Backstab cooldowns. No mana means no resource management other than stamina — the Rogue is always ready if they can reach the right angle.

## Combat Arts Unlock Schedule

Combat arts are trained at Thieves' Guild halls or independent specialists. Rear-position arts require flanking; stealth arts are unusable in heavy armor.
