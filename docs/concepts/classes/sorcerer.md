# Sorcerer
*Arcane DPS (Innate) — Any Alignment — Innate bloodline*

## Overview
Born with it. The Sorcerer's power cannot be removed, unlearned, or depleted by preparation — it is part of their biology. Unlike the Wizard, they require no spellbook and no preparation cycles. Unlike the Magician, their spell pool is narrower — they know fewer spells, but those they know are always available. The class suits players who want consistent arcane output without the Wizard's preparation overhead.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Cloth
- **Weapons:** Staves
- **Primary Resource:** Mana
- **Pet:** None

## Spells
| Spell | Type | Damage | Mana | Cast | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arcane Burst | Arcane dmg | 30 | 18 | Instant | 3s |
| Bloodfire | Fire dmg | 40 | 22 | Instant | 4s |
| Void Lance | Arcane dmg | 55 | 28 | 1.0s | 6s |
| Tempest Bolt | Lightning dmg | 65 | 35 | 1.5s | 10s |
| Soul Surge | Arcane dmg | 80 | 45 | 2.0s | 14s |

Two instant-cast spells give the Sorcerer flexibility the Wizard lacks. Peak single-hit (Soul Surge at 80) sits meaningfully below Wizard peak (Meteor at 130).

## Skills
None — pure spellcaster.

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Troll, Iksar, Fae, Vah Shir, Kobold, Half-Ogre

**Blocked:** Ogre, Minotaur — the bloodline power exists in these races but manifests as uncontrolled destructive episodes rather than usable magic.

## Playstyle
Engage immediately — no preparation required. Rotate Arcane Burst and Bloodfire on cooldown for baseline pressure; escalate with Void Lance and Tempest Bolt on longer cooldowns; spend Soul Surge against high-value targets. Sustained damage output exceeds the Wizard over a long session because the Sorcerer never hits a dry spellbook. Single-target burst ceiling is lower, but consistency across multiple pulls without rest is the Sorcerer's edge. Pairs well in groups that move fast and don't allow the Wizard time to re-memorize between encounters.
