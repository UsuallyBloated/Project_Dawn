# Bard
*Support / Hybrid DPS — Any Alignment — Music magic*

## Overview
The most social class. The Bard's power comes from performance — voice, instrument, and the ability to move a room. Their spells lean on sound-based damage (Dissonance, Chorus of Misery), self-healing (Battle Hymn), and crowd control (Siren's Song — a 30-second charm that temporarily converts an enemy into a combat ally). In groups, their buffs and debuffs multiply everyone else's effectiveness. Swindler's Strike is their stamina-fueled melee contribution.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Chain/leather
- **Weapons:** Instruments (functional tool, not cosmetic), one-handed swords, daggers
- **Primary Resource:** Both mana and stamina
- **Pet:** Temporary — Siren's Song charm (30s; enemy converted to ally)

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Minotaur, Fae, Vah Shir, Kobold

**Blocked:** Ogre, Troll (voice and fine motor control incompatible with the performance demands); Iksar (no musical tradition); Half-Ogre (insufficient fine control for instrument performance).

## Playstyle
Solo: Siren's Song turns a 1v1 into a 2v1 — let the charmed enemy do the work while using Dissonance and Chorus of Misery from range, healing with Battle Hymn as needed. Group: the Bard's primary value is in support effects that expand significantly with level. Swindler's Strike fills stamina-side gaps. Charm management — re-applying before the 30s expires without pulling aggro — is the highest-skill-ceiling element of the class.
