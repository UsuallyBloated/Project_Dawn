# Witch Hunter
*Anti-Magic Specialist — Good preferred — Training*

## Overview
Hunters of corrupted spellwork. The Witch Hunter's conviction is vocational, not religious — they are not Paladin-adjacent in motivation, just in alignment tendency. They are trained to detect, expose, and eliminate magical threats. Their spells work against all enemies but deal bonus effectiveness against magical and summoned targets. Silver Bolt extends their reach into ranged combat.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Chain/leather
- **Weapons:** Crossbows, one-handed swords, silver weapons
- **Primary Resource:** Both mana and stamina
- **Pet:** None

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Vah Shir, Half-Ogre

**Blocked:** All Evil-aligned races (they are the hunted, not the hunter); Minotaur (no institutional connection to the discipline); Fae (relationship to magic too entangled with the class's targets); Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Dark Elf, Kobold.

## Playstyle
Lead with Expose (strips magical defenses), follow with Witchfire and Silver Bolt for sustained damage, and hold Banishment as the heavy-cast finisher — expensive, but holy-typed for extra effectiveness against summoned and enchanted targets. Rite of Warding covers moderate self-healing. Against non-magical targets, the Witch Hunter is a competent but not exceptional combatant; against magical targets, they significantly outperform their apparent power tier.
