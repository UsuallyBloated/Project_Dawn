# Shadow Knight
*Dark Tank — Evil preferred — Dark faith / undeath*

## Overview
The inverted Paladin. Where the Paladin draws power from light and life, the Shadow Knight draws from a dark patron — an entity of death, shadow, or entropy. Life force is a resource to drain and repurpose. Lifetap defines the class: it damages an enemy and heals the Shadow Knight simultaneously, enabling sustained solo play no other armored fighter can match. If alignment shifts toward Good, abilities transform into the Redeemed Shadow Knight variant.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Plate
- **Weapons:** Heavy swords, scythes, dark weapons — often two-handed for maximum drain throughput
- **Primary Resource:** Both mana and stamina
- **Pet:** Ritual undead (minor; design target — not yet implemented as permanent companion)

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Dark Elf, Half-Elf, Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Minotaur, Kobold, Half-Ogre

**Blocked:** All Good-aligned races (Elf, Halfling, Dwarf — cannot commit to genuine darkness); Gnome, Wood Elf, Fae (no tradition of dark patronage in their cultures); Vah Shir (oath traditions incompatible with shadow covenant).

## Playstyle
Harm Touch is the burst spike — 2.5x at high stamina cost on a long cooldown, held for decisive moments. Dark Strike maintains steady stamina DPS. Lifetap is the sustain engine — use it throughout drawn-out fights to offset incoming damage without a healer. Siphon opens at range; Dark Shroud is the heavy-cast dump when mana permits. The Shadow Knight's greatest strength is self-sufficiency in solo content.

## Shadow Knight (Redeemed — Alignment Variant)
A Shadow Knight who has turned from their dark patron. Alignment shifts toward Good. Class identity remains martial, but abilities shift to holy-adjacent equivalents.
