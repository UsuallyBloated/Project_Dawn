# Enchanter
*Crowd Control / Support — Any Alignment — Mind magic*

## Overview
The best crowd control in the game. The Enchanter does not primarily damage enemies — they neutralize them. Mesmerize stops a target cold; Charm converts one to a combat ally for 60 seconds (the highest-duration charm in the game); Rune provides a mana-efficient absorb shield. Their arcane damage is the worst among casters, but it exists — Color Spray and Cascade of Stars provide output when control is not needed. No other class matches their ceiling in multi-enemy encounters.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Cloth
- **Weapons:** Staves
- **Primary Resource:** Mana
- **Pet:** Temporary — Charm (60s; the longest charm duration in the game)

## Skills
None — pure spellcaster.

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Half-Elf, Fae

**Blocked:** Dwarf, Ogre, Troll, Iksar, Minotaur, Vah Shir, Kobold, Half-Ogre — requires sustained concentration and social intelligence that these physiologies and cultures do not support.

## Playstyle
Against a single target: Charm to convert, let the charmed enemy fight while dealing Color Spray damage from range, re-apply Charm before the 60s expires. Against multiple targets: Mesmerize the dangerous ones, handle them one at a time. Rune is instant-cast and cheap — keep it up as a buffer before HP damage begins. Cascade of Stars is the expensive single-target finisher when control is not needed. The Enchanter has a very high ceiling and an extremely demanding skill floor — the class falls apart under panic and excels under calm.
