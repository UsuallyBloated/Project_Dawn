# Paladin
*Holy Warrior — Good Alignment only — Divine faith*

## Overview
Faith made martial. A Paladin's power flows from genuine devotion to a good-aligned deity. The discipline requires both the physical readiness of a Warrior and the spiritual commitment of a Cleric — slower to peak than either, and more capable than either when faith and sword align. If alignment shifts to Evil through in-world choices, abilities transform into the Fallen Paladin variant.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Plate
- **Weapons:** Swords, maces, shields — no evil-associated weapons
- **Primary Resource:** Both mana and stamina
- **Pet:** None

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Elf, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Felhari, Half-Ogre

**Blocked:** All Evil-aligned races (cannot perform genuine good faith); Fae (follow no mortal religious hierarchies); Dark Elf, Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Minotaur, Kobold (alignment or cultural tradition incompatible with good-faith covenant).

## Playstyle
The Paladin operates between tank and off-healer. Divine Blow maintains stamina DPS; Holy Shield provides a defensive cooldown. Righteous Fire is the instant-cast damage option at range; Judgment is the heavy-cast finisher. Crusader's Mend covers moderate self-healing; Lay on Hands is the emergency heal, held for critical moments. Managing both mana and stamina simultaneously is the primary skill challenge.

## Fallen Paladin (Alignment Variant)
A Paladin whose alignment has shifted to Evil. Not a separate class — a state reached through in-world choices that replaces abilities with shadow-flavored equivalents.
