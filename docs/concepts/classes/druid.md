# Druid
*Nature Caster / Off-Healer — Any Alignment — The living world*

## Overview
Power drawn from the ongoing fact of living things — not a deity, not ancestors. Druids damage (Wrath, Call Lightning), control (Entangle), and heal over time (Regrowth). Their signature is sustained versatility: not the fastest or the hardest, but they do not run dry on long encounters. The restriction to leather armor reflects the tradition's requirement that the body remain open to the world it draws from.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Leather
- **Weapons:** Staves, clubs, nature-adjacent weapons — nothing forged in fire
- **Primary Resource:** Mana
- **Pet:** None

## Spells

| Spell | Type | Damage/Effect | Mana | Cast |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wrath | Nature dmg | 25 | 20 | Instant |
| Entangle | Nature DoT | 10 + 5/s for 18s | 18 | Instant |
| Call Lightning | Nature dmg | 65 | 35 | 1.5s |
| Regrowth | Nature HoT | +5 HP/s for 18s | 25 | 1.0s |

## Skills
None — the Druid's body is the channel, not the weapon.

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Elf, Wood Elf, Gnome, Halfling, Dwarf, Half-Elf, Minotaur, Fae, Vah Shir

**Blocked:** Dark Elf (severed from living nature by generations underground); Ogre, Troll (no tradition of nature attunement); Iksar (martial-necromantic tradition, not nature-based); Kobold (culture and lifespan incompatible); Half-Ogre.

## Playstyle
Open with Entangle to root the target in DoT damage, follow with Wrath for instant pressure, cast Regrowth when HP begins to drop, and use Call Lightning as the heavy finisher when the target is locked in place. Entangle is unusually mana-efficient for the damage it produces over 18 seconds — it is the preferred sustained damage spell. Regrowth stacks with incoming heals and continues regenerating after the cast completes.
