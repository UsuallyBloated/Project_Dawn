# Beast Master
*Melee Hybrid / Pet — Any Alignment — Bond with nature and animal spirits*

## Overview
The Beast Master is a melee fighter permanently bonded to a spirit warder — a living animal companion that cannot be dispelled, dismissed, or permanently killed. Unlike the Necromancer's summoned skeletons or the Enchanter's charmed victims, the warder is not a tool; it is a partner. It levels with the character, accumulates shared combat history, and fights with coordinated intent, not commanded obedience.

The Beast Master's magic is thin and borrowed from Shaman-adjacent spirit-work: minor self-heals, a debuffing shriek, a haste buff, and direct spirit damage. Their power comes from the two-body combat unit — a coordinated attacker who occupies multiple positions simultaneously.

## Combat Profile
- **Armor:** Medium leather and chain — nothing that masks scent or restricts movement
- **Weapons:** Claws, fist weapons, hand wraps, short melee weapons, some spears
- **Primary Resource:** Both stamina (skills) and mana (spells)
- **Pet:** Permanent spirit warder — levels with character; not summoned, always present; recalled if killed

## Race Availability
**Available:** Human, Elf, Wood Elf, Half-Elf, Ogre, Troll, Kel`varath, Minotaur, Felhari, Kobold, Half-Ogre

**Blocked:**
- **Dark Elf** — severed from living nature; the warder-bond cannot form in soil that no longer answers them
- **Gnome** — the analytical frame that makes them excellent engineers forecloses the instinctual surrender the bond requires
- **Halfling** — their relationship to animals is domestic and agrarian; the warder-bond draws on predator instinct, not keeper instinct
- **Dwarf** — stone culture; deep respect for the living world, but no tradition of spirit-bonding with beasts
- **Fae** — their magic is illusory and mind-based; the beast-spirit bond operates on an incompatible channel, and their form does not suit the melee demands of the class

## The Warder System
The warder is a permanent companion, not a summon:
- Present alongside the character at all times; cannot be unsummoned in combat
- Has its own HP pool, healed by Warder's Mend and out-of-combat regeneration
- If reduced to 0 HP, it retreats into spirit-space and returns at reduced HP after a short delay — it is never permanently lost
- Scales in damage, HP, and accuracy as the Beast Master levels
- The warder's type (wolf, great cat, boar, serpent, etc.) is chosen at character creation and does not change — it is a spirit-bond, not a summoned creature type

## Playstyle
The Beast Master fights as a two-body unit. Open with Primal Bond to accelerate both attack speeds into a fight. Use Claw Rake on cooldown for personal DPS; save Warder's Fury for burst moments when both attacker and warder strike simultaneously. Feral Shriek slows enemies who are pulling away, keeping them in range of both fighters. Spirit Mend and Warder's Mend handle sustain — prioritize the warder's HP when it takes focus; prioritize self-healing when it doesn't. Spirit Strike is the ranged fallback during approach or kiting.

The warder attacks the Beast Master's current target automatically. Warder's Fury is the only mid-fight command that changes warder behavior.

## Lore Notes
The Beast Master tradition predates any organized magical school. It is not taught — it is recognized, in individuals whose connection to animal spirits is already present before formal training begins. A Beast Master and their warder share sensory impressions at range; experienced Beast Masters report knowing when their warder has found prey before any outward signal. The academic consensus is that this is passive spiritual resonance. Beast Masters consider the explanation beside the point.
