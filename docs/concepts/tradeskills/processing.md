# Processing

Processing skills convert raw gathered materials into usable components. They are the middle layer of the economy — no production skill works without them. Processing is rarely glamorous, but a bottleneck processor can hold significant economic power in a player-driven economy.

Processing combines happen at fixed stations: a forge for Smelting, a tanning rack for Tanning, a workbench for Rope Making. The stations exist in towns and crafting hubs; some high-level players may acquire personal station housing items.

---

## Smelting

**Requires:** Forge station | **Prerequisites:** None | **Feeds Into:** Blacksmithing, Armorsmithing, Weaponsmithing, Jewel Crafting (gold)

Convert raw ore into workable ingots. One of the most fundamental steps in the production chain — nearly every metal item requires smelted ingots. Higher Smelting skill increases ingot yield per ore (at skill 100+, a skilled smelter pulls 1.2–1.5 ingots per ore on average vs. 1.0 at skill 0). At skill 150+, alloy combinations unlock.

### Smelt Recipes

| Input | Min Skill | Output | Trivial At |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2× Copper Ore | 0 | 1 Copper Ingot | 40 |
| 2× Tin Ore | 15 | 1 Tin Ingot | 50 |
| 1× Copper Ore + 1× Tin Ore | 30 | 1 Bronze Ingot | 65 |
| 2× Iron Ore | 60 | 1 Iron Ingot | 120 |
| 2× Silver Ore | 90 | 1 Silver Ingot | 150 |
| 2× Gold Ore | 120 | 1 Gold Ingot | 170 |
| 2× Mithril Ore | 160 | 1 Mithril Ingot | 200 |
| 2× Adamantite Ore | 185 | 1 Adamantite Ingot | — (Grandmaster only) |

**Alloys (skill 150+):**
| Combination | Output | Property |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Mithril + 1 Silver | Moonmetal Ingot | Light, resistant to arcane; premium Witch Hunter gear |
| 1 Adamantite + 1 Gold | Sunsteel Ingot | Holy-imbue compatible; Paladin high-tier armor base |
| 1 Iron + 1 Obsidian (crushed) | Shadowiron Ingot | Dark-imbue compatible; Shadow Knight armor base |

**Charcoal:** A by-product of any Smelting action — leftover fuel. Feeds Baking (kiln fuel) and Pottery (kiln firing). Low value but eliminates the need for Loggers to supply Bakers directly.

**Racial bonuses:** Dwarf receives minor +XP rate from underground forge affinity.

## Tanning

**Requires:** Tanning Rack station | **Prerequisites:** None (but Skinning provides the raw materials) | **Feeds Into:** Leatherworking, Tailoring (high-quality lining)

Convert raw hides and pelts into workable leather. Tanning skill determines leather quality from a given hide — a Grandmaster Tanner produces excellent leather from a wolf hide; a novice produces rough scraps. Higher skill also reduces material loss (failed tans destroy the hide).

### Tanning Recipes

| Input | Min Skill | Output | Quality Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scraps (rat, rabbit) | 0 | Rough Leather Scraps | Practice; used in Leatherworking 0–30 |
| Standard Hide (wolf, deer) | 15 | Standard Leather | Bread-and-butter Leatherworking material |
| Heavy Pelt (bear) | 40 | Thick Leather | Medium armor base |
| Scale Leather (serpent) | 60 | Cured Scale | Preserves the scale texture; scale armor |
| Silkpelt (big cat) | 80 | Silked Leather | Premium; soft; tailoring upper tier |
| Drake Scale | 120 | Tempered Drake Scale | Partial magic resistance baked in |
| Shadow Hide | 150 | Shadow Leather | Retains darkness-affinity; Shadow Weaving compatible |
| Dragon Scale | 185 | Legendary Dragon Leather | Legendary armor base; +enchanting bonus when used |

**Tanning oil:** Required for all Standard tier and above combines. Produced from fish oil (Fishing → Cooking intermediate), rendered animal fat (Cooking by-product), or purchased from vendor. Tanning without oil produces Rough output regardless of skill.

**Bleaching:** Optional step at skill 80+. Bleached leather produces a white or natural-tone variant — purely cosmetic, but high-value trade item for tailors who want appearance flexibility.

## Rope Making

**Requires:** Workbench | **Prerequisites:** None | **Feeds Into:** Masonry (scaffolding), Sailing (rigging), Animal Husbandry (lead ropes), Trapping (Deadfall component)

Braid plant fiber and sinew into rope and cordage. One of the most overlooked processing skills — demand is consistent and unglamorous. Rope is a component in construction, sailing, and several tier-2 trap recipes. Nobody thinks about rope until they need it.

### Materials

| Input | Min Skill | Output | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3× Plant Fiber (Herbalism by-product) | 0 | Thin Cord | Low-tier traps, binding components |
| 2× Sinew (Skinning by-product) | 20 | Bowstring | Fletching (required for finished bows) |
| 4× Plant Fiber + 1× Sinew | 40 | Standard Rope | Masonry scaffolding, Sailing rigging, Cage Traps |
| 3× Heavy Fiber (rare Herbalism) + 2× Sinew | 80 | Heavy Rope | Deadfall traps, ship anchor lines |
| 2× Shadow Hide Strip + 2× Plant Fiber | 120 | Shadow Cord | Shadow Weaving loom thread; Rogue equipment binding |
| 2× Dragon Sinew + 2× Mithril Wire (Blacksmithing by-product) | 170 | Legendary Bowstring | +15% bow range; permanent; Ranger end-game item |

**Plant fiber:** By-product of Herbalism harvests at skill 20+. Every herb gather has a chance to include fiber — the plant's stems and stalks left after the useful parts are stripped. Not worth anything alone; critical for Rope Making.

**Mithril Wire:** By-product of Blacksmithing combines at skill 160+ — the fine waste strands left when working mithril. Feeds the Legendary Bowstring recipe above.

**Economic note:** Rope has no glamour but steady demand. A player who maintains a Rope Making business can sell to Masons, Sailors, Rangers (bowstrings), and Trappers without overlap with any major competitor tradeskill. Underappreciated.
