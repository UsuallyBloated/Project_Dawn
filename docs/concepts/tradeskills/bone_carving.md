# Bone Carving

*Exclusive — Ogre and Troll primary. Minotaur via Ancestor Vessel transformation only.*

## Overview

Bone Carving works monster bones, tusks, horns, and hardened biological materials into weapons, trinkets, fetishes, and ritual objects. The output is primitive in aesthetic and surprisingly effective in practice. A Grandmaster Bone Carver's weapon does not look like a Dwarf's forge work. It looks like something that came out of the deep wilderness with its own history.

This is the crafting tradition of cultures that didn't have forges. The Ogre tribes, the Troll clan-groups, the Minotaur ancestors — they made their best weapons from what the world gave them after they killed it. The results were not inferior. They were different.

**Sinew and hide:** Several Bone Carving recipes use sinew (Skinning/Rope Making by-product) and rough hide strips for binding. A Troll who also has Skinning is fully self-supplied.

## Ancestral Resonance (Minotaur Ancestor Vessel)

The Ancestor Vessel transformation is the only path for Minotaurs to access Bone Carving. When a Minotaur carves bone, the output carries **Ancestral Resonance** — a property not present in Ogre or Troll-crafted items.

**Ancestral Resonance:** Items with this property carry embedded lore about the creature the bone came from. On equip, the wearer receives a brief vision or text fragment about the creature's history (lore delivery mechanic). At higher skill, the resonance adds passive bonuses:
- Fetishes with Ancestral Resonance: +10% effectiveness of Shaman spirit spells for the wearer.
- Weapons with Ancestral Resonance: +5% damage against the creature type the bone came from.
- A Minotaur Grandmaster carving Dragon Bone produces a weapon that deals +5% bonus damage to dragons, permanently.

This is a character-specific lore mechanic. The vision the Minotaur carver sees when working Ancestral Bone is intended to be a fragment of that creature's final moments — not gameplay-critical but atmospheric.

## Racial Character

**Ogre Bone Carving:** Massive. Intended to be wielded by something with 17 STR. Ogre-carved weapons are intimidating objects — large bones, tusks the size of short swords, crude but deliberately imposing. Ogre fetishes are not decorative; they are trophies.

**Troll Bone Carving:** Unsettling. Trolls work with a different cultural framework — bones are collected from things they've survived, not just killed. A Troll Bone Carver often incorporates their own shed materials (bone chips from old wounds, hardened hide) alongside monster bones. Troll-carved items sometimes look partially alive.

**Minotaur Bone Carving:** Sacred. The Ancestor Vessel Minotaur carves with the intention of honoring the creature. The work is slower, more considered, and the result carries the weight of a ritual act. A Minotaur-carved Dragon Bone Totem is not a trophy — it is a memorial.

## Economic Position

Bone Carving competes with Blacksmithing and Leatherworking in the physical damage and melee armor markets. At equivalent tiers, the outputs are roughly comparable in stat values. The distinction is:
- Bone weapons and trinkets are not available from standard vendors.
- Their appearance is distinct — no two Bone Carvers produce identical-looking items.
- Some materials (Dragon Bone, Void-Touched Bone, Intact Tusk) are rare enough that Grandmaster Bone Carving products are supply-constrained regardless of skill.

In a mature server economy, a Grandmaster Ogre or Troll Bone Carver serves a niche that no other tradeskill can replicate. The customers are mostly other Ogres and Trolls, but the end-tier products attract collectors and power-seekers from any race willing to carry something that looks like it came off a killing floor.
