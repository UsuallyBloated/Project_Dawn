# Shadow Weaving

*Exclusive — Dark Elf only.*

## Overview

Shadow Weaving is the Dark Elf exclusive textile art — the craft of weaving shadow-infused cloth that carries innate stealth properties. It bridges Tailoring and dark magic into a single tradeskill that produces cloth and clothing unavailable from any other source.

Shadow Weaving is not Tailoring with a dark aesthetic. It is a separate skill. A Dark Elf who is a Grandmaster Tailor starts Shadow Weaving at 0. The techniques are fundamentally different: Tailoring works with light, the thread, and the stitch. Shadow Weaving works with the spaces between threads — the voids, the dark intervals, the negative space that light skips across rather than penetrating.

The output of Shadow Weaving looks like cloth woven from darkness. Because it is.

---

## Access

**Dark Elf only.** No other race can learn Shadow Weaving at any skill level. It is not teachable to non-Dark Elves, purchasable by them, or derivable from any other tradeskill path.

Dark Elves do not receive a separate racial XP bonus for Shadow Weaving — they already have exclusive access. The skill is the bonus.

## Materials

Shadow Weaving uses materials not found in standard Tailoring:

| Material | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shadow Fiber | Herbalism from shadow biome plants (skill 80+) | The primary weaving thread; light-absorbing |
| Darkwood Thread | Logging (Darkwood, skill 150) → Woodworking (pulp) | Structural component; prevents cloth from fraying |
| Shadow Hide Strip | Skinning (Shadow Hide, skill 150) | Binding and trim; retains darkness affinity |
| Night Essence | Rare Herbalism (Nightshade concentrate, Alchemy skill 50+ by-product) | Dye component; deepens stealth effect |
| Void Thread | Void Bloom → Alchemy processing (skill 170) | End-tier thread; near-invisible cloth |
| Moonsilk | Moonpetal → specialized processing (Shadow Weaving 130) | Rare component; iridescent, shifts with light |
| Soul Shard Fragment | Necromantic Scribing by-product; Grave Robbing rare | End-tier binding; gives cloth a faint resonance |

**Shadow Fiber:** The critical bottleneck. Shadow biome plants that yield Shadow Fiber are specific to zones with persistent low-light or shadow-magic conditions. Dark Elves are the primary Herbalists in these zones due to race affinity and cultural territory — they effectively control the supply chain from root up.

**Darkwood Loom:** Shadow Weaving requires a Darkwood Loom as the crafting station. This is a Woodworking product (skill 150, Darkwood). A Dark Elf who also takes Logging and Woodworking can produce their own loom; others must purchase it.

### Armor

All Shadow Weaving armor carries a base **Stealth Bonus** — a passive reduction to the character's detectable presence. This is not the same as the Rogue's Hide skill or Camouflage (Ranger spell) — it is a persistent passive that stacks with those effects. A fully-geared Shadow Weave character in stealth is significantly harder to detect than an equivalently-skilled character in standard cloth.

## Stealth Mechanics Integration

The Stealth Bonus from Shadow Weave gear interacts with:

- **Rogue's Sneak/Hide skill** (design target): Stacks additively.
- **Ranger's Camouflage spell**: Stacks multiplicatively (both effects apply; the combined result is stronger than either alone).
- **Enemy detection radius**: Reduced by Stealth Bonus value. A +25 Stealth character is detected at significantly shorter range than a +0 character using the same movement behavior.

**Cloak of Passing specific:** At walk speed, the wearer does not break stealth effects — normally, stealth breaks on any movement above a slow walk threshold. The Cloak changes this. Running still breaks it. A character in the Cloak can reposition, take cover, or approach at walk speed while remaining hidden. This is a significant tactical item for Rogues, Shadow Knights, and dark casters who rely on approach.

## Cultural Context

Shadow Weaving is an ancient Dark Elf tradition predating the underground exile. In the surface age, Dark Elves wove with the shadows of ancient forests. After the exile, they adapted to weave with cave darkness, deep tunnel shadow, and eventually the shadow magic that permeates their home zones.

The craft is not secret — Dark Elves don't hide it. It simply requires materials that exist only where Dark Elves live and a racial intuition for shadow that cannot be taught to those who don't carry it. Other races know what Shadow Weave cloth is. They simply cannot make it.

**In the player economy:** Shadow Weave cloth is one of the few materials where the supply chain is effectively Dark Elf controlled end-to-end. Dark Elves gather Shadow Fiber, produce Shadow Cloth, and are the only ones who can turn it into finished armor. A Dark Elf Shadow Weaver can sell finished garments to non-Dark Elf stealth characters (Rogues, Rangers with Camouflage builds) at prices they set, because no competition exists.
