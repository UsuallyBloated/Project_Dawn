# Enchanting

*Arcane — Available to all races and classes. Elf racial bonus.*

## Overview

Enchanting imbues existing items with magical properties using reagents and runes. Unlike Runeforging (which bakes the effect in during creation) or Runecarving (which physically inscribes a rune), Enchanting applies a magical overlay — a persistent effect bound to the item's surface through concentrated arcane work.

The same item can have one Enchantment. Applying a second destroys the first. Enchantments can theoretically be dispelled by certain very high-level magical effects — this distinguishes them from Runeforged bonuses, which cannot.

Enchanting is the most accessible path to magical item enhancement. Any character willing to invest in the skill can access it. The Elf racial bonus makes Elven Enchanters the most efficient practitioners, but they hold no exclusivity.

---

## How It Works

Enchanting requires three inputs:
1. **The item** to be enchanted (weapon, armor, jewelry, or focus item).
2. **The reagent** that determines the enchantment type.
3. **The binding agent** — a magical catalyst that fixes the effect (changes by tier).

Higher skill = stronger enchantment bonus values and access to higher-tier reagents. Enchanting a high-level item at low skill produces a weak version of the effect. The item's quality tier also matters — Enchanting a Crude item produces a worse result than Enchanting a Superior base.

### Binding Agents by Tier

| Tier | Binding Agent | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Apprentice | Arcane Dust | Vendor purchase; low cost |
| Journeyman | Crystallized Mana | Alchemy product (skill 60) |
| Artisan | Enchanter's Flux | Alchemy product (skill 100) |
| Master | Pure Arcane Essence | Alchemy product (skill 150) |
| Grandmaster | Void Catalyst | Legendary drop or Alchemy Grandmaster |

## Alignment Effects on Enchanting

Enchanting is not split by alignment the way Alchemy is. However, certain reagent types are only accessible to specific alignments:

- **Holy Water** (reagent for Holy Weapon, Grand Ward) — only produced in Good-aligned temples by Clerics. Evil-aligned characters cannot purchase it or produce it. A Holy Weapon enchantment is structurally inaccessible to Evil characters.
- **Void Crystal** (reagent for Shadow Touch, Illusion Bind) — found only in end-game dungeon Mining and Grave Robbing. Not alignment-gated, but Evil characters have naturally better access via Grave Robbing.

**Transformation-specific effects:**
- **Fae-Old (Fae transformation):** Enchanting effectiveness increases. Illusion-imbued items last longer and have slightly stronger effects. Fae-Old is the only transformation that directly amplifies Enchanting output.
- **Fallen Paladin:** Gains the ability to imbue shadow effects on formerly holy gear. The Enchanting window will offer shadow reagent options where it previously offered holy ones.
- **Redeemed Shadow Knight:** Gains holy-imbue option. Holy Water reagent becomes accessible to purchase despite character history.

## Racial Bonus

**Elf:** +XP rate. Additionally, Elf-produced Enchantments at the same skill level have 5% stronger bonus values — a subtle but cumulative advantage at Grandmaster tier.

---

## Economic Position

Enchanting occupies a specific market slot: it upgrades existing items rather than producing new ones. This means Enchanters depend on other crafters and adventurers for their input supply. An Enchanter who can't find quality base items to work on is limited in output.

The reverse is also true: other crafters want their output enchanted. A Blacksmith who partners with a Grandmaster Enchanter can sell finished gear at significantly higher prices than either could alone.

**Disenchanting (design target):** A potential mechanic — applying Enchanting skill to break down an enchanted item to recover its reagents (partially). This would create a recycling loop for outdated enchanted gear and give Enchanters a secondary income stream. Not yet fully designed; note here for future consideration.

## Faction Context

**Good-aligned temples:** The primary source of Holy Water and holy-imbue training. Enchanting recipes for holy weapons are only taught here. Standing requirement: Friendly+.

**Dark Elf guilds:** Source of Void Crystal and shadow-imbue training. Evil-aligned Enchanting recipes. Standing requirement varies.

**Elven settlements:** Elven master Enchanters offer the highest Enchanting skill cap recipes and the finest reagent supplies. Access requires faction standing with the relevant Elven community.

**Gnomish Cogsworth:** Stocks Crystal Lenses (Glassblowing product) that are used in some high-tier Enchanting recipes — the Gnome demand for precision optics overlaps with Enchanting.
