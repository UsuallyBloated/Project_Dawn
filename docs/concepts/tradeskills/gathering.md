# Gathering

Gathering skills extract raw materials from the world. Nodes are fixed spawn points in zones — ore veins in cave systems and mountains, herb patches in fields and marshes, timber stands in forests. Nodes deplete on harvest and respawn on a timer. Multiple players can harvest the same node, but yield degrades after the first harvester.

**Gathering skill mechanics:** Skill score affects (1) passive detection range — the minimap or track skill shows more nodes at higher skill; (2) yield per node; (3) minimum skill gate on higher-tier nodes; (4) rare material chance. A skill 0 character can attempt any node but gets poor yield and finds no rare variants.

## Mining

**Tools:** Pickaxe | **Feeds Into:** Smelting, Blacksmithing, Jewel Crafting, Pottery

The primary metal and gem source. Ore veins appear in cave systems, mountain zones, and dungeon floors. Higher-tier ore has a minimum skill requirement — attempting to mine adamantite at skill 10 produces nothing.

### Ore Tiers

| Ore | Min Skill | Zone Type | Smelts Into | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Copper Ore | 0 | Surface caves, shallow dungeons | Copper Ingot | Most plentiful early ore |
| Tin Ore | 25 | Mid-tier caves | Tin Ingot | Rarely used alone |
| Iron Ore | 60 | Deep caves, mountain zones | Iron Ingot | Workhorse ore for mid-tier smithing |
| Silver Ore | 90 | Mountain cave veins | Silver Ingot | Effective vs. undead and magical creatures; Witch Hunter demand |
| Gold Ore | 120 | Deep mountain, dungeon rare spawns | Gold Ingot | Jewel Crafting and Enchanting reagent |
| Mithril Ore | 160 | High mountain, dungeon upper floors | Mithril Ingot | Highest standard armor tier |
| Adamantite Ore | 185 | End-game zones only | Adamantite Ingot | Legendary tier; rare spawn |

### Coal

| Item | Min Skill | Node Location | Used In |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coal | 0 | Coal seam nodes — shallow caves, mine tunnels | Smelting (fuel for all ingot recipes) |

Coal seams appear in the same cave systems as ore veins but are their own distinct node type. A skill-0 miner can harvest them. Higher Mining skill does not improve coal yield — it is purely a fuel resource.

**Bronze:** Smelting copper + tin at skill 30 produces Bronze Ingots — stronger than copper alone. First alloy in the game; guards and town militia gear is often bronze.

**Gem nodes:** Rare spawn within ore veins at Mining skill 50+. Yield directly to Jewel Crafting. Types by rarity: Jasper → Bloodstone → Sapphire → Emerald → Diamond. Higher skill increases both gem find chance and gem tier.

**Racial bonuses:**
- **Dwarf:** +XP gain rate; bonus gem chance in ore veins.
- **Ogre:** +XP gain rate (raw driving force on the pick; Ogres don't chip, they gouge).

## Logging

**Tools:** Axe | **Feeds Into:** Woodworking, Fletching, Scribing (paper pulp from bark)

Timber harvesting from trees in forested zones. Different species yield different wood types with different properties. Ancient trees in deep-forest zones require high skill to fell cleanly — low-skill attempts produce splintered, low-quality timber or nothing at all.

### Wood Types

| Wood | Min Skill | Zone | Properties |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pine | 0 | Common forests, hillsides | General-purpose; cheap; plentiful |
| Oak | 30 | Temperate forests | Dense and durable; best for shields |
| Birch | 50 | Northern forests, elevated zones | Light and straight-grained; good for arrow shafts and bows |
| Ash | 80 | Ancient forests | Strong and flexible; premium bow wood; Ranger preference |
| Ironwood | 120 | Deep forest, dungeon groves | Near-metal hardness; weapon hafts, heavy staves |
| Darkwood | 150 | Shadow biomes, cursed groves | Used in Shadow Weaving looms; dark caster staves and focus items |
| Ancientwood | 185 | The oldest forest zones — marked on no map | Legendary staves, Druid ritual objects; unreproducible items |

**Bark pulp:** By-product of Logging at skill 40+. Feeds Scribing (paper production). A single logging yield includes bark strips alongside the timber.

## Herbalism

**Tools:** None (bare hands; an herb pouch in inventory increases yield stack size) | **Feeds Into:** Alchemy, Cooking, Brewing, Scribing (plant-based inks), Incense Crafting

The most class-diverse gathering skill in the game. Herb nodes appear as ground-level spawns in fields, forests, marshes, dungeons, and cave systems. Each plant has specific biome and condition requirements — some grow only at night, some only in rain, a few only within detection range of undead.

### Herb Reference

| Herb | Min Skill | Biome / Condition | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bloodmoss | 0 | Common fields, forest edges | White Alchemy (healing base) |
| Bitterroot | 0 | Marshes, riverbanks | White Alchemy (antidotes) |
| Feverfew | 0 | Meadows, road-side verges | White Alchemy (healing, used alongside Bloodmoss) |
| Wild Mushroom | 0 | Shaded forest floors, damp hollows | Cooking (Mushroom Bread) |
| Wild Berries | 0 | Forest edges, hedgerows | Cooking (Berry Tart) |
| Wormwood | 10 | Marshes, disturbed soil near settlements | White Alchemy (antidotes, Mana Potion base) |
| Nightshade | 25 | Forests, night-only spawn | Black Alchemy (poison base) |
| Silverleaf | 40 | Temperate fields, hillsides | White Alchemy (mana restoration) |
| Shimmerbloom | 60 | High elevation, mountain meadows | White Alchemy (stat elixirs) |
| Swampweed | 60 | Marshes, bog zones | Brewing; Cooking (spice and flavor) |
| Rotting Fungi | 80 | Dungeon floors, cave walls, near undead | Black Alchemy (disease and plague) |
| Deeproot | 90 | Deep cave systems, underground gardens | Alchemy; Incense Crafting (powerful ritual smoke) |
| Dragonwort | 120 | Volcanic zones, magma-adjacent caves | Fire Alchemy; Enchanting fire-imbue reagent |
| Moonpetal | 140 | Open ground, night-only — wilts at dawn | White Alchemy superior tier (Superior Healing Draught) |
| Grave Flower | 160 | Near burial sites, undead-heavy zones | Necromantic Scribing; Black Alchemy (curse reagent) |
| Spirit Sage | 170 | Ancient burial grounds, spirit-touched areas | Shaman rituals; White Alchemy spirit elixirs |
| Void Bloom | 185 | End-game zones; Lich-corrupted areas | Legendary Alchemy; Necromantic Scribing |

**Identifying quality:** Wood Elf racial bonus — can assess an herb node before harvesting: Poor / Standard / Excellent. Other races pick and discover quality after.

**Warden of the Wild (Druid transformation):** Hidden herb nodes become visible. These produce rare reagents and legendary-tier alchemical components not available to lower mastery Herbalists.

**Racial bonuses:**
- **Wood Elf:** +XP rate.
- **Druid class:** +XP rate.

## Skinning

**Tools:** Skinning Knife (must be in inventory) | **Feeds Into:** Leatherworking, Tailoring (fur lining), Tanning, Rope Making (sinew by-product)

Strip hides and pelts from slain creatures. The creature must be dead. Fire-damaged bodies yield Charred Leather — functional but reduced quality, and most Leatherworking recipes reject it for the better tiers. Skinning skill determines hide quality and access to exotic creature types.

**Sinew:** By-product of any skinning action at skill 30+. Sinew feeds Rope Making and Fletching (bowstrings).

### Hide Tiers

| Source | Min Skill | Output | Quality Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rat, rabbit | 0 | Scraps | Practice leather; low-tier pouches |
| Wolf, deer | 15 | Standard Hide | Light armor, belts, basic pouches |
| Bear | 40 | Heavy Pelt | Medium leather armor |
| Serpent, lizard | 60 | Scale Leather | Scale-pattern armor; unique appearance |
| Big cat (Felhari hunting grounds) | 80 | Silkpelt | Premium leather; Tailoring upper tier |
| Drake, wyvern | 120 | Drake Scale | High-tier scale armor; partial magic resistance |
| Creature of shadow (undead beasts, spectral creatures) | 150 | Shadow Hide | Shadow Weaving feed; Leatherworking with stealth-adjacent properties |
| Dragon (end-game) | 185 | Dragon Scale | Legendary armor material; Enchanting reagent |

**Racial bonuses:**
- **Troll:** +XP rate; bonus chance to find rare hide variant (albino, banded, spotted — valuable to traders and tailors for appearance).

## Fishing

**Tools:** Fishing Rod (equips to weapon slot), Bait (consumed per cast) | **Feeds Into:** Cooking, Alchemy (fish oil, aquatic reagents)

The most passive gathering skill. The player equips a rod, casts at a water node, and waits for a catch event. Rod quality and bait type affect what can be caught. Higher Fishing skill increases catch tier, reduces wait time, and opens saltwater and deepwater zones.

### Fishing Zones

| Zone Type | Min Skill | Catch Types | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Freshwater ponds | 0 | Common fish (Cooking), River Shell (Pottery) | Widely available |
| Rivers | 20 | River trout, Crayfish (Cooking + Alchemy oil) | Good early food source |
| Coastal saltwater | 50 | Sea fish, Coral (Jewel Crafting, Enchanting) | Coral is an Enchanting reagent |
| Deep ocean | 90 | Rare fish (tier-3 food buffs), Shark Hide (Leatherworking) | Requires a boat or dock |
| Underwater caves | 120 | Cave creatures, bioluminescent reagents (Alchemy, Enchanting) | Requires dive ability or Enduring Breath |
| Deepwater (Troll exclusive) | 150 | Abyssal catches; deep-sea creatures | See below |

**Deepwater Fishing (Troll exclusive):** Trolls can fish in genuinely dangerous, lightless deep water. The catches include creatures other players cannot reach — abyssal fish, deep-sea serpents, phosphorescent jellyfish with rare alchemical properties. Some of the rarest Cooking and Alchemy ingredients exist only on Troll fishing tables. This is a quiet, enormous economic advantage for Troll crafters.

## Trapping

**Tools:** Trap Kit (consumed on set; recovered on success) | **Feeds Into:** Skinning (indirect), Cooking (meat), Leatherworking (small hides)

Set mechanical traps in the field and return to collect them. The trap waits for a target to trigger it; the player retrieves the catch on a later visit. Higher skill allows setting more traps simultaneously, reduces time-to-catch, and opens access to larger or more exotic prey.

### Trap Types

| Trap | Skill | Target | Products |
|---|---|---|---|
| Snare Trap | 0 | Rabbits, small rodents | Rabbit Pelt, Rabbit Meat |
| Spring Trap | 30 | Fox, badger | Fox Pelt (premium small hide), Meat |
| Bear Trap | 60 | Wolf, mountain goat | Wolf Pelt, Goat Hide, Bone (Bone Carving feed) |
| Cage Trap | 80 | Exotic small creatures, birds | Rare pelts; live animals (feed into Animal Husbandry) |
| Deadfall | 120 | Larger prey (bear-size) | Heavy Pelt, large Bone components |

**Ranger affinity:** Ranger-built traps inflict 15% bonus damage when the trapped creature breaks free and engages. Rangers also receive +XP rate for Trapping.

**Multi-trap limit:** Skill 0–49: 2 traps simultaneously. Skill 50–99: 4. Skill 100–149: 6. Skill 150+: 8. Trap kit cost scales with trap tier.

## Quarrying

**Tools:** Maul and Chisel | **Feeds Into:** Masonry, Pottery (crushed limestone as clay substitute)

Extract large stone blocks from quarry nodes in mountain and cliff zones. Quarrying produces heavier, bulkier output than Mining — primarily used in player housing, fortification construction, and large-scale masonry projects. Fossil inclusions (lore items) and rare stone types appear as bonuses at higher skill.

### Stone Types

| Stone | Min Skill | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|
| Flint | 0 | Knapped into Flint Arrowheads (Fletching) |
| Limestone | 0 | Masonry basics; Pottery substitute |
| Granite | 40 | Heavy masonry, dungeon construction |
| Marble | 80 | Premium housing; high vendor value |
| Obsidian | 120 | Decorative; Enchanting reagent (volcanic magic alignment) |
| Ruinstone | 160 | Taken from ancient dungeon walls; contains carvings — Scribing can transcribe them; Necromantic Scribing uses them directly |

**Fossil inclusions:** At skill 100+, quarrying rarely yields fossil fragments — bones and impressions of extinct creatures embedded in stone. These are lore items with vendor value or use in Necromantic Scribing.

**Racial bonuses:**
- **Ogre:** +XP rate; produces larger blocks per action (structural advantage for housing projects).
- **Dwarf:** Minor bonus from underground experience.

## Grave Robbing

**Alignment required:** Neutral or Evil | **Tools:** Shovel | **Feeds Into:** Necromantic Scribing, Black Alchemy, Bone Carving

Looting burial sites — crypts, cemeteries, ancient battlefields, dungeon necropolises — for rare reagents, bone components, cursed objects, and dark alchemical materials.

Mechanically, Grave Robbing is a **gathering skill** — the player targets a burial node, performs the action, receives materials. The distinction is moral and social: committing Grave Robbing near settlements permanently damages faction standing with any good-aligned or law-based faction that witnesses it or finds the evidence. Items from grave nodes in inventory near city gates can trigger guard inspection events.

**Alignment gate:** Attempting Grave Robbing at Good alignment fails. The character physically cannot complete the action.

### Burial Site Yields

| Site Type | Min Skill | Yield |
|---|---|---|
| Common grave | 0 | Bone Chips, Burial Cloth, Old Coin |
| Warrior's grave | 30 | Weapon Fragment, Iron Burial Token, Bone (medium) |
| Mage's tomb | 60 | Arcane Residue, Dark Runestone, Spell Shard |
| Ancient burial mound | 90 | Ancestral Bone (Bone Carving feed), Cursed Relic, Black Alchemical Reagent |
| Necromancer's crypt | 120 | Lich Dust, Death Rune, Soul Shard |
| Legendary hero's tomb | 160 | Hero's Remains (unique — each tomb once only), Dark Crown Components |

**Lich transformation:** Grave Robbing cap raises to 300. Lich characters passively sense hidden burial sites within a large detection radius — unmarked graves and forgotten crypts invisible to other players become nodes on their map.

**A note on the skill's place in the world:** Grave Robbing is not socially invisible. NPCs who witness it react. Players who discover that another player has the skill may react. The skill is mechanically useful and thematically consistent with evil caster play — but it carries weight. This is intentional.
