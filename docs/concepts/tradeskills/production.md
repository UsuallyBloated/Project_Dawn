# Production

Production skills combine processed components into finished goods — armor, weapons, clothing, jewelry, and structural materials. These are the tradeskills most visible to the player character: the output is worn, wielded, or built.

Most production skills require a crafting station (forge, loom, workbench). Output quality scales with skill level — a Grandmaster Blacksmith produces weapons with higher base damage than the same recipe at skill 50. The quality suffix system: Crude → Standard → Fine → Superior → Masterwork. Only Grandmaster-level crafters reliably produce Masterwork items.

---

## Blacksmithing

**Requires:** Forge station | **Prerequisites:** Smelting (recommended; ingots can be purchased) | **Cap:** 200 base; 250 for Dwarves

The foundational production skill. Blacksmithing produces metal weapons, metal armor components, and the intermediate parts that Armorsmithing and Weaponsmithing specialize into. A generalist Blacksmith can make everything; specialists make better versions of their chosen category.

### Weapon Recipes (Sample)

| Item | Materials | Min Skill | Trivial At |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper Dagger | 1 Copper Ingot | 0 | 40 |
| Iron Short Sword | 2 Iron Ingots | 60 | 120 |
| Iron Longsword | 3 Iron Ingots + 1 Wood Haft | 70 | 130 |
| Silver-Edged Blade | 2 Iron Ingots + 1 Silver Ingot | 100 | 160 |
| Mithril Sword | 3 Mithril Ingots + 1 Ironwood Haft | 160 | 210 (above base cap — Dwarf only) |
| Adamantite Greatsword | 4 Adamantite Ingots + 1 Ancientwood Haft | 185 | Grandmaster |

### Armor Recipes (Sample)

| Item | Materials | Min Skill | Trivial At |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iron Chain Coif | 3 Iron Ingots | 65 | 125 |
| Iron Plate Chestpiece | 5 Iron Ingots | 80 | 140 |
| Silver Mail Shirt | 4 Silver Ingots | 110 | 170 |
| Mithril Plate | 6 Mithril Ingots | 165 | — (Dwarf cap) |

**Arrowheads:** Blacksmithing produces arrowheads as small components fed into Fletching. Standard iron arrowheads at skill 50; silver-tipped at skill 90; mithril at skill 160.

**Runeforging prerequisite:** Blacksmithing 150 is the gate to unlock Runeforging for Dwarves. See [runeforging.md](runeforging.md).

**Racial bonus:**
- **Dwarf:** +XP rate; cap raised to 250; access to Dwarven-pattern recipes (broader shoulders, heavier construction, unique appearance).

## Armorsmithing

**Requires:** Forge station | **Prerequisites:** Blacksmithing 50 | **Cap:** 200

Armorsmithing is a specialization of Blacksmithing focused on plate, chain, and ring armor. Armorsmithing recipes require the same ingot inputs as equivalent Blacksmithing recipes but produce higher-quality armor — better AC values, more resistance slots, better weight distribution.

The distinction: Blacksmithing can make a functional iron plate chest. Armorsmithing makes the better version of that same chest. At Grandmaster level, an Armorsmith's plate outperforms a Blacksmith's by a measurable margin.

### Specialty Products

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Full Plate Set | Only Armorsmiths can produce complete matched sets (bonus for wearing full sets: +AC) |
| Ring Mail | Armorsmithing-only weave; lighter than chain, more flexible |
| Jousting Plate | Ceremonial heavy armor; no combat role but high vendor value |
| Field Repair Kit | Tool item — allows basic armor repair in the field without a forge |

**Armorsmithing vs. Leatherworking:** Armorsmiths work metal; Leatherworkers work hides. A plate Warrior spec goes to the Armorsmith; a Ranger or Rogue spec goes to the Leatherworker.

## Weaponsmithing

**Requires:** Forge station | **Prerequisites:** Blacksmithing 50 | **Cap:** 200

Weaponsmithing specializes in bladed and blunt metal weapons — swords, axes, hammers, maces. Like Armorsmithing, it produces better versions of the weapons a Blacksmith could make. The gap is most significant at Masterwork tier: a Weaponsmith's Masterwork longsword has +10% base damage vs. a Blacksmith's equivalent.

### Specialty Products

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Two-Handed Weapons | Large weapons require Weaponsmithing 80+ — Blacksmithing cannot produce them above Crude quality |
| Serrated Edge | Weaponsmithing technique; adds small Bleed DoT on hit to any bladed weapon |
| Balanced Grip | Reduces stamina cost of weapon skills by 5% (permanent enchanting-free bonus) |
| Ritual Blade | High-skill Weaponsmithing; specific shape used by Shaman and Cleric for ceremonies |

## Tailoring

**Requires:** Loom or Sewing Kit | **Prerequisites:** None | **Cap:** 200

Produce cloth armor, robes, bags, and decorative garments. Tailoring uses cloth (from Herbalism — plant fibers woven into cloth) and hides (fur lining and trim from Skinning/Tanning). The gap between cloth armor and leather armor is appearance and caster preference — robes have mana bonuses baked in on higher tiers.

### Products (Sample)

| Item | Materials | Min Skill | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cloth Tunic | 4 Cloth | 0 | Starting caster armor equivalent |
| Reinforced Cloth Robe | 6 Cloth + 2 Leather Strips | 50 | Minor physical resistance added |
| Silk Robe of Clarity | 4 Silk Cloth + 2 Shimmerbloom reagent | 110 | +5 MP regen; caster demand item |
| Mage's Cowl | 3 Silk Cloth + 1 Enchanting reagent | 130 | +INT bonus (Tailoring + Enchanting combo item) |
| Halfling Travel Coat | 4 Cloth + 2 Rabbit Pelt | 60 | Halfling-exclusive pattern; +movement speed minor |
| Shadow Robe | 4 Shadow Cloth (Shadow Weaving output) | 150 | Stealth bonus; Dark Elf Tailoring demand |

**Bags:** Tailoring produces inventory bags. Higher skill = more bag slots. Skill 0: 4-slot bag. Skill 100: 8-slot. Skill 200: 12-slot (Grandmaster bags are rare, high-value trade items).

## Leatherworking

**Requires:** Workbench | **Prerequisites:** Tanning 25 | **Cap:** 200

Craft leather and hide armor, belts, quivers, and pouches. The primary armor tradeskill for Ranger, Rogue, Monk, and Bard. Leatherworking uses cured leather from Tanning as its primary input.

### Products (Sample)

| Item | Materials | Min Skill | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rough Leather Vest | 3 Rough Leather | 0 | Basic starting armor |
| Standard Leather Armor | 5 Standard Leather + 2 Rope | 40 | Core Ranger/Rogue armor |
| Scale Mail Vest | 4 Cured Scale + 2 Standard Leather | 80 | Good armor with unique appearance |
| Silkpelt Armor | 4 Silked Leather + 2 Standard Leather | 110 | Light, premium; Rogue and Monk preference |
| Drake Scale Armor | 5 Tempered Drake Scale + 3 Standard Leather | 140 | Partial magic resistance (built-in) |
| Quiver (12-slot) | 2 Standard Leather + 1 Rope | 30 | Ranger demand — holds arrows |
| Quiver (20-slot) | 4 Silked Leather + 2 Rope | 100 | Grandmaster quiver; high Ranger demand |

**Belts and slots:** Leatherworking produces belts that grant additional pouch slot(s) to the paperdoll. Grandmaster belts offer up to 3 extra pouches — significant inventory advantage.

## Woodworking

**Requires:** Workbench | **Prerequisites:** None | **Cap:** 200

Craft bows, staves, shields, and furniture from timber. Woodworking is one of the few tradeskills that produces every weapon type for a single class — Rangers buy their bows here.

### Products (Sample)

| Item | Materials | Min Skill | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crude Wooden Staff | 1 Pine | 0 | Starting caster weapon |
| Ash Shortbow | 2 Ash Wood + 1 Bowstring | 80 | Solid Ranger weapon |
| Ash Longbow | 3 Ash Wood + 1 Bowstring | 100 | +15% range vs. shortbow |
| Ironwood Stave | 2 Ironwood | 120 | Wizard/Magician preference |
| Ancientwood Focus Staff | 2 Ancientwood + 1 Enchanting Reagent | 185 | Legendary caster weapon |
| Wooden Tower Shield | 3 Oak + 2 Iron Ingots (Blacksmithing bind) | 70 | Warrior off-hand |
| Darkwood Loom | 3 Darkwood | 150 | Station item; required for Shadow Weaving |

**Bow mechanic note:** Woodworking bows require a Bowstring (Rope Making output) to be functional. An unstrung bow is a production intermediate, not a usable item.

**Arrow Shaft:** Woodworking produces arrow shafts as Fletching components. Birch shafts at skill 50; Ash at skill 80; Ironwood at skill 120.

## Fletching

**Requires:** Workbench (or field-crafting with Traveling Kit) | **Prerequisites:** None | **Cap:** 200

Craft arrows, bolts, and quivers. Fletching is the Ranger's self-sufficiency skill — a Ranger who has Fletching, Woodworking, and Blacksmithing can produce their own ammunition entirely without buying from vendors.

**Ranger-crafted arrows:** Any arrow made by a Ranger character has a small proc chance to trigger Hunter's Eye briefly on hit (a self-buff that normally has a 45-second cooldown). This proc does not trigger the cooldown — it is a passive benefit only available on Ranger-crafted ammunition.

### Arrow Types

| Arrow | Components | Min Skill | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard Arrow | Arrow Shaft + Iron Arrowhead + Feather Fletching | 0 | Base damage |
| Broadhead Arrow | Arrow Shaft + Razor Arrowhead + Stiff Feathers | 40 | 0.9× damage; applies Bleed DoT on hit (5 dmg/3s for 15s) |
| Silver-Tipped Arrow | Arrow Shaft + Silver Arrowhead + Blessed Feathers | 90 | 1.2× damage vs. undead/magical creatures |
| Armor-Piercing Arrow | Arrow Shaft + Mithril Arrowhead + Stiff Feathers | 150 | Ignores 20% of target armor |
| Incendiary Arrow | Arrow Shaft + Iron Arrowhead + Alchemist's Fire (Alchemy) | 110 | Applies Fire DoT on hit (8 dmg/3s for 12s) |
| Shadow Arrow | Arrow Shaft + Iron Arrowhead + Shadow Leather Strip | 140 | Applies Shadow DoT; reduced detection on hit |
| Legendary Straight | Ancientwood Shaft + Mithril Arrowhead + Legendary Bowstring | 185 | Maximum range; 1.4× damage |

**Bolt variants:** Crossbow bolts follow the same recipe pattern. Witch Hunters specifically use silver-core bolts (silver arrowhead equivalent) against magical and transformed targets.

**Racial bonus:**
- **Wood Elf:** +XP rate; minor accuracy bonus on Wood Elf-fletched arrows (tight-woven fletching from forest bird feathers).

## Jewel Crafting

**Requires:** Jeweler's Workbench | **Prerequisites:** Mining 40 (for gem access) | **Cap:** 200

Cut gems and craft rings, amulets, necklaces, and trinkets. Jewel Crafting is the primary source of stat-boosting accessories. The output occupies ring, neck, and trinket slots — areas that Blacksmithing and Leatherworking don't cover.

### Gem Cutting

Raw gems from Mining must be cut before use in jewelry. Cutting improves value and unlocks the gem's stat bonus.

| Raw Gem | Min Skill | Cut Output | Bonus When Set |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jasper | 0 | Cut Jasper | +2 STR |
| Bloodstone | 30 | Cut Bloodstone | +3 CON |
| Sapphire | 60 | Cut Sapphire | +3 INT or +3 WIS |
| Emerald | 90 | Cut Emerald | +3 AGI or +3 DEX |
| Diamond | 140 | Cut Diamond | +5 to chosen stat |

### Jewelry Recipes (Sample)

| Item | Materials | Min Skill | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper Ring | 1 Copper Ingot | 0 | Slot filler; low bonus |
| Iron Ring of Strength | 1 Iron Ingot + 1 Cut Jasper | 60 | +2 STR |
| Silver Necklace | 1 Silver Ingot + wire (Blacksmithing) | 90 | Base for gem setting |
| Gold Amulet of Clarity | 1 Gold Ingot + 1 Cut Sapphire | 130 | +5 INT, +3 MP regen |
| Diamond Ring of Power | 1 Gold Ingot + 2 Cut Diamond | 160 | +10 INT |
| Enchanter's Focus Pendant | 1 Gold Ingot + 1 Cut Diamond + Enchanting Reagent | 175 | +8 INT, +CC duration 10% |

## Pottery

**Requires:** Potter's Wheel + Kiln station | **Prerequisites:** None | **Cap:** 200

Shape clay into containers, ceremonial urns, and utility items. Clay comes from Mining (crushed limestone) or riverbank clay nodes. Pottery is primarily a support tradeskill — it produces the vials, flasks, and containers used by Alchemy, Glassblowing, and Cooking.

### Products (Sample)

| Item | Materials | Min Skill | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clay Vial (10-stack) | 1 Clay | 0 | Alchemy container (replaces purchased Empty Vial) |
| Ceramic Flask | 2 Clay | 20 | Better container; holds larger Alchemy outputs |
| Cooking Pot | 4 Clay + Kiln firing | 40 | Station item for personal use |
| Storage Urn | 5 Clay + Kiln firing | 70 | Housing item; 20-slot container |
| Ritual Urn | 3 Clay + 1 Bone Dust | 90 | Shaman ritual component; also vendor-valuable |
| Siege Pot (empty) | 6 Clay + Iron Banding | 120 | Combines with Alchemy fill to become siege weapon |

**Alchemy economy:** Potters who mass-produce Clay Vials provide a direct economic link to Alchemists — purchased Empty Vials have a markup; Pottery-made vials cost only raw materials and time. A dedicated Potter + Alchemist duo produces potions cheaper than solo Alchemists who buy containers.

## Masonry

**Requires:** Quarrying output | **Prerequisites:** Quarrying 30 | **Cap:** 200

Shape quarried stone into building components for player housing, fortification, and NPC construction contracts. Masonry operates at a larger physical scale than other production skills — output items are bulky and usually delivered to a build site rather than traded in stacks.

### Products (Sample)

| Item | Materials | Min Skill | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Limestone Block | 2 Limestone | 0 | Housing wall component |
| Granite Slab | 2 Granite | 40 | Floor tiles; durable housing material |
| Marble Column | 3 Marble | 80 | Decorative housing; high vendor value |
| Fortification Stone | 4 Granite + 1 Iron Banding | 100 | Defensive wall construction |
| Obsidian Tile | 2 Obsidian | 120 | Luxury floor tile; mage tower aesthetic |
| Carved Ruinstone Block | 2 Ruinstone | 160 | Dungeon construction / necromantic aesthetics |

**Racial bonus:**
- **Ogre:** +XP rate; produces extra blocks per action (raw physical advantage — an Ogre shapes stone faster).

## Runecarving

**Requires:** Carving Set (portable) | **Prerequisites:** None | **Cap:** 200

Inscribe runes onto existing weapons and armor to add permanent bonuses. Runecarving is post-craft application — the item already exists; the rune is carved into it afterward. This is the accessible version of rune-work; Runeforging (Dwarf exclusive) does it during the forge process and produces superior results, but Runecarving is available to any skilled enough crafter.

Each item can hold one carved rune. Carving a second rune over an existing one destroys the first. Runecarving cannot be undone.

### Rune Types

| Rune | Carved On | Min Skill | Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rune of Striking | Weapon | 40 | +3 base damage |
| Rune of Warding | Armor | 40 | +3 AC |
| Rune of Speed | Boots or gloves | 60 | +5% movement speed |
| Rune of Clarity | Helm or ring | 80 | +3 MP regen |
| Rune of Fortitude | Armor (chest) | 90 | +10 max HP |
| Rune of Power | Weapon | 110 | +5 base damage |
| Rune of the Shadow | Weapon or cloak | 130 | +2 shadow damage; works with shadow spells |
| Grand Rune of Striking | Weapon | 160 | +10 base damage; requires Masterwork weapon base |
| Ancient Rune (Ruinstone) | Any | 180 | Varies — transcribed from dungeon carvings via Scribing |

**Racial bonus:**
- **Dark Elf:** +XP rate (ancient arcane tradition; Dark Elf rune-work predates written language).

**Runeforging vs. Runecarving:** Runecarving is applied after the item is complete. Runeforging (Dwarf exclusive) weaves the rune into the metal during the forge process — stronger result, more permanent, higher cap. See [runeforging.md](runeforging.md).

## Taxidermy

**Requires:** Taxidermy Kit (portable) | **Prerequisites:** Skinning 30 | **Cap:** 200

Mount creature remains as trophies and craft trinkets from preserved biological material. Taxidermy bridges the gap between the hunting economy (Skinning, Trapping) and housing/accessory markets. A mounted trophy is a housing item; a crafted trinket is an equipped passive bonus.

### Products

| Item | Materials | Min Skill | Slot / Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mounted Wolf Head | Wolf Head + Mounting Board + Preservation Oil | 30 | Housing | Decorative; lore-tagged with zone kill location |
| Mounted Bear Trophy | Bear Head + Mounting Board | 50 | Housing | High vendor value; guild hall aesthetic |
| Bone Fetish (minor) | Small Bone × 3 + Monster Fang | 40 | Trinket | +2 CON |
| Hunter's Tooth Necklace | Monster Fang × 5 + Sinew | 70 | Neck | +4 AGI; hunter aesthetic |
| Trophy Claw | Drake Claw × 2 + Preservation Oil | 100 | Trinket | +5 STR; visually impressive |
| Dire Wolf Pelt Mount | Dire Wolf Pelt + Backing + Preservation Oil | 110 | Housing | Rare; high housing value |
| Dragon Trophy | Dragon Scale × 3 + Dragon Bone | 185 | Housing | Legendary housing item; signals end-game achievement |
| Wyvern Skull Mount | Wyvern Skull + Mounting Frame | 150 | Housing | Unique appearance per wyvern type (color variation) |

**Preservation Oil:** Taxidermy requires Preservation Oil as a consumable (produced via Alchemy skill 30+). Without it, some recipes degrade — the mount loses luster after time. Alchemy-supplied Preservation Oil is a steady demand item for Taxidermists.

**Kill location tag:** Mounted trophies retain the zone and approximate location where the creature was killed. A trophy inspect shows: *"Grey Wolf — killed in the Thornwood Forest, Eastern Highlands."* This is the game's most personal lore object — entirely player-authored by the act of hunting.

## Tideweaving

**Requires:** Tideweaving Loom (coastal station; can be purchased in port towns) | **Prerequisites:** Fishing 30 | **Cap:** 200

A coastal craft that uses sea plants, shells, coral, and bioluminescent material gathered from the ocean to produce light armor and decorative jewelry. Tideweaving is not Tailoring — it works with wet, living material that requires different technique. The output has a distinct coastal aesthetic: iridescent, water-resistant, and faintly luminous in dim light.

Tideweaving fills the niche between cloth armor and leather armor with a third option that has its own resistances (better in water/wet environments, slightly worse in fire/desert zones).

### Materials

| Material | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sea Kelp | Herbalism (coastal zones) | Primary fiber |
| Shell Fragment | Fishing (coastal), Quarrying (beach nodes) | Structural component |
| Coral Branch | Fishing (coastal saltwater, skill 50+) | Premium material |
| Bioluminescent Roe | Fishing (underwater caves) | Rare; produces glowing cloth |
| Abyssal Ink | Deepwater Fishing (Troll exclusive) | Darkens dye permanently; unique appearance |
| Pearl | Fishing rare drop (deep ocean) | Jewelry quality material |

### Products

| Item | Materials | Min Skill | Stats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kelp Wraps | 4× Sea Kelp + Shell Fragment | 0 | Light armor; water resistance +5% |
| Shell Vest | 6× Sea Kelp + Shell Fragment × 4 | 30 | Light armor; +3 AC; water resistance +10% |
| Coral Mail | 5× Coral Branch + Sea Kelp × 3 | 70 | Medium light armor; +8 AC; elemental resistance +5% |
| Tideweave Robe | 6× Sea Kelp + Bioluminescent Roe × 2 | 90 | Caster cloth; glows faintly; +5 INT; water resistance +15% |
| Pearl Ring | 1× Pearl + Shell Fragment | 60 | Ring; +4 WIS or INT (player's choice on craft) |
| Coral Necklace | 2× Coral Branch + Pearl | 100 | Neck; +6 WIS; +5 MP regen |
| Abyssal Garb | 6× Sea Kelp + Abyssal Ink + Coral Branch × 3 | 160 | Troll-ingredient-dependent; darkest variant; +12 AC, +8 AGI, underwater breath +50% |

**Water resistance:** Tideweave gear reduces damage from water-aligned spells and environmental drowning rate. This makes it the preferred armor in aquatic dungeon zones.

**Racial bonus:** Troll (+XP rate; Abyssal Ink supply for black-dye variants). Kel`varath (+XP rate; aquatic affinity).
