# Alchemy

*Consumables — Available to all alignments; recipes split by alignment branch*

## Overview

Alchemy converts herbs, animal parts, mineral components, and aquatic reagents into potions, elixirs, poisons, and vials. It is the most versatile consumable tradeskill — its output covers every category of temporary effect in the game: healing, mana restoration, stat amplification, damage-over-time, debuffs, and poison weapon coatings.

One tradeskill. Two recipe books. Which branch you can access depends on your alignment score.

---

## White Alchemy

**Alignment:** Good or Neutral (score ≥ -299)

White Alchemy produces healing potions, antidotes, stat-enhancing elixirs, and mana restoratives. It is the primary reason Druids, Clerics, and Shamans invest in the tradeskill — self-supplied potions are substantially cheaper than vendor alternatives and stackable with Cleric/Druid healing.

## Black Alchemy

**Alignment:** Evil or Neutral (score ≤ 299)

Black Alchemy produces weapon poisons, disease vials, curse bottles, and reagents for Necromantic Scribing. It is the primary tradeskill for Necromancers, Blood Mages, and shadow-aligned characters who want to inflict DoTs or supply dark crafting.

Black Alchemy shares the same tradeskill skill score as White Alchemy — they are one skill, not two. Alignment determines recipe availability. A Neutral character who crafts both sides of the recipe book has access to the most powerful Alchemy outputs in the game.

## Alignment Shift Mechanic

When a character's alignment score crosses into a branch they previously had access to:
- **Good → Neutral:** Retains both branches (Neutral has access to both).
- **Neutral → Evil (≤ -300):** Loses access to White Alchemy recipes above Minor Healing Draught and Minor Mana Tonic. The body makes good things wrong.
- **Evil → Neutral:** Regains full White Alchemy access.
- **Good → Evil directly (rapid fall):** Loses White Alchemy in one crossing.

**Stash rule:** Potions crafted before an alignment shift remain in inventory and still work. You just can't make more.

A Necromancer who began as Neutral and maintained Neutral alignment indefinitely can remain a dual-branch Alchemist. Neutral is not a safe default — it requires active maintenance when playing dark classes.

## Racial and Faction Context

**Druid:** White Alchemy trainers at reduced cost in Ranger/Druid wilderness settlements. Warden of the Wild expands reagent access.

**Shaman:** Spirit Sage and deep ritual components available through Ancestor spirit-contact mechanics — Shaman Alchemy has additional lore recipes not listed here that require Shaman-specific ritual knowledge.

**Good-aligned temples:** White Alchemy trainers above skill 100. Enchanting of holy potion bottles (cosmetic but high vendor value).

**Dark Elf guilds and Necromancer halls:** Black Alchemy trainers. Necromancer's Ink recipe is only sold here — it is not derivable through trial and error.

**Kobold tunnel markets:** Unique reagents from deep-tunnel flora not found elsewhere. Several mid-tier Black Alchemy recipes use Kobold-sourced components as alternatives to above-ground herbs.

## Tools and Stations

- **Alchemy Kit (portable):** Allows Apprentice and Journeyman recipes anywhere. Consumed slowly — eventually needs replacement.
- **Alchemy Table (station):** Required for Artisan and above. Found in towns, crafting guilds, and player housing.
- **Mortar and Pestle:** Intermediate — produces powdered reagents used in several tier-2+ recipes. Portable.
- **Vials:** Clay Vials (Pottery) and Glass Vials (Glassblowing) are interchangeable below skill 100. Above 100, some recipes specifically require Glass Vials (precision output). Grand Vials (Glassblowing skill 140+) are required for Legendary tier.
