# Runeforging

*Exclusive — Dwarf only. Requires Blacksmithing 150.*

## Overview

Runeforging is the fusion of metalsmithing and runic inscription into a single action. Where Runecarving applies runes to a finished item as a second step, Runeforging weaves the rune into the metal during the forge process. The rune becomes part of the item's physical structure — inseparable, more powerful, and more permanent.

This is the Dwarf endgame crafting path. It is the final step in the chain: Mining → Smelting → Blacksmithing → Runeforging. No other race has access at any skill level.

**Why Dwarves:** The technique requires an understanding of metal at a structural level — knowing how a rune's geometry distributes heat stress, how its pathways align with grain flow in the cooling ingot. Dwarves developed this knowledge over generations of underground forge work. It is not magic that anyone could learn if they tried hard enough. It is a synthesis of cultural knowledge and physical instinct that no one outside a Dwarven hold has ever successfully replicated.

---

## How It Works

Standard Blacksmithing: forge item from ingots, then optionally apply a rune via Runecarving as a separate step.

Runeforging: the rune is specified before forging begins. The inscription is carved into the mold or pressed into the cooling metal at a precise moment. The result is a weapon or armor piece with the rune physically embedded — it cannot be removed, overwritten, or dispelled.

**Runecarving vs. Runeforging comparison:**

| Property | Runecarving | Runeforging |
|---|---|---|
| When applied | After the item is completed | During the forge process |
| Removable | Yes — carving a new rune destroys the old one | No — permanently fused |
| Strength | Standard bonus values | 30–50% stronger than equivalent Runecarving rune |
| Dispel-resistant | No | Yes — magical dispel cannot remove a Runeforged bonus |
| Who can do it | Any skilled Runecarver | Dwarf only |
| Max rune capacity | 1 rune per item | 1 primary + 1 secondary (Grandmaster only) |

## Runeforging Recipes

Runeforging is not a separate combine window — it is a modified Blacksmithing combine. When a Dwarf with Blacksmithing 150+ and Runeforging skill attempts a metal weapon or armor combine, they have the option to incorporate a rune. Selecting a rune adds it to the combine requirements.

### Secondary Runes (Grandmaster, skill 190–200)

Grandmaster Runeforgers can embed a second, lesser rune alongside the primary. The secondary rune must be of lower power tier than the primary.

| Secondary Rune | Effect |
|---|---|
| Forging Mark | Weapon deals 2 extra fire damage (always) |
| Warding Glyph | +3 AC |
| Durability Seal | Item degrades at half normal rate |
| Maker's Brand | Item is permanently marked with the crafter's name (cosmetic; historical record) |

The Maker's Brand is worth noting. A Grandmaster Runeforger can permanently mark their work. In a player-driven economy, a known Dwarf smith's Maker's Brand becomes a quality marker. The brand is visible on item inspection. This is the closest the game gets to item provenance.

## Crystal Components

Runeforging requires crystals as the physical medium for rune encoding. Crystals are a Mining by-product at skill 120+ (rare chance from any ore vein) and a rare Quarrying find.

| Crystal | Alignment | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Red Crystal | Fire | Mining deep volcanic zones |
| Blue Crystal | Ice | Mining high-elevation caves |
| Clear Crystal | Lightning | Mining at peak elevation nodes |
| Earth Crystal | Physical | Quarrying granite nodes |
| Wind Crystal | Movement | Mining Mithril veins (rare by-product) |
| Fire/Ice/Shadow Crystal | Elemental | End-game zone mining |
| Void Crystal | Shadow/Dark | End-game dungeon mining; Grave Robbing rare find |
| Grand Crystal | Legendary | Adamantite Mining rare by-product; boss encounter drop |

## Training and Faction Context

**Dwarven holds (faction):** The only trainers for Runeforging. The technique is not sold in other settlements and is not derivable without instruction. First-generation knowledge requires either being born in a Dwarven hold culture or having sufficient faction standing to be taught.

**Faction standing required:** Friendly (300+) standing with the relevant Dwarven hold faction to access Runeforging trainers above skill 150. Skill 190–200 recipes are taught only at Allied standing (750+).

**Runeforged gear as status:** A piece of Runeforged armor or a Runeforged weapon is identifiable on inspection. In settlements, Runeforged gear marks the owner as someone with the resources and connections to obtain it. This is social capital, not just mechanical advantage.

## The Endgame Chain

The full Dwarf crafting chain is:
1. **Mining** (ore) → **Smelting** (ingots) → **Blacksmithing** (item) + **Runeforging** (rune embedded) = Runeforged item
2. **Brewing** → **Dwarven Grand Stout** (requires Runeforged Brewing Vessel, itself a Runeforging product at skill 170)
3. **Crystal Mining** (rare drops from end-game nodes) → Crystal stock for legendary rune tier

The Runeforged Brewing Vessel is worth a note: it's a station item that a Dwarf produces for their own brewery. Functionally, it's an upgraded Brewing Barrel — but it enables the Grand Stout recipe that non-Runeforged barrels cannot. A Dwarf who completes the full chain (Blacksmithing to Runeforging, Brewing to Grand Stout) is self-sufficient in both the best combat gear and the best consumable buffs in the game.

Nobody else can replicate this. That is the point.
