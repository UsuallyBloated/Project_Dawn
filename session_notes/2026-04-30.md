## Stun / Root / Slow
`scripts/enemy.gd` — new state-effect methods all guard on `is_dead`:
- `stun(duration)` — sets `_stun_timer`; physics loop skips movement AND attacking while > 0; emits `Combat.enemy_stunned`
- `root(duration)` — sets `_root_timer`; blocks movement in `_tick_chase()` but allows attacking
- `snare(amount, duration)` — reduces move_speed by `amount` fraction
- `apply_attack_slow(amount, duration)` — scales attack cooldown by `(1.0 + amount)`
- `silence(duration)` — blocks spell casting while `_silence_timer > 0`
